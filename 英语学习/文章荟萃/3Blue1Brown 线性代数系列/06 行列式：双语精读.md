## 面积的缩放（Scaling Area）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| One thing that turns out to be surprisingly useful for understanding a given transformation is to measure exactly how much a given transformation stretches and squishes things. More specifically, to measure the factor by which the area of a given region increases or decreases. | 要理解一个给定的变换，有件事出奇地有用：精确测量这个变换把东西拉伸（stretch）、压扁（squish）了多少。更具体地说，就是测量某个给定区域的面积增大或减小的倍数。 |
| For example, the matrix $\begin{bmatrix}3&0\\0&2\end{bmatrix}$ scales $\hat i$ by a factor of $3$, and scales $\hat j$ by a factor of $2$. How can we tell the factor by which space gets stretched? Focus your attention on the $1\times 1$ square whose bottom sits on $\hat i$, and whose left side sits on $\hat j$. After the transformation, this turns into a $2\times 3$ rectangle. | 比如，矩阵 $\begin{bmatrix}3&0\\0&2\end{bmatrix}$ 把 $\hat i$ 缩放了 3 倍，把 $\hat j$ 缩放了 2 倍。那我们怎么知道空间被拉伸了多少倍？把注意力集中到那个 $1\times 1$ 的正方形上：它的底边贴着 $\hat i$，左边贴着 $\hat j$。变换之后，它变成了一个 $2\times 3$ 的矩形。 |
| Since this region started with area $1$, and ended up with area $2\times 3=6$, we can say the linear transformation has scaled its area by a factor of $6$. | 由于这块区域起初面积为 $1$，最后面积为 $2\times 3=6$，我们可以说，这个线性变换把它的面积缩放了 6 倍。 |
| So even though this transformation smooshes things about, it seems to leave areas unchanged. Actually, though, if you know how much the area of that one unit square changes, it tells you how the area of _any_ region in space changes. For starters, notice that whatever happens to one square in the grid happens to any other square in the grid, no matter the size of that other square. This is because grid lines remain parallel and evenly spaced. | 所以，尽管这个变换把东西揉来揉去，它似乎没有改变面积。不过实际上，只要你*知道*那一个单位正方形的面积变了多少，你就知道了空间中*任何*区域的面积会怎么变。首先要注意：网格里任何一个正方形发生的事，同样发生在网格里任何其他正方形上，无论后者有多大。这是因为网格线保持平行且等距。 |
| Then, any shape that's _not_ a grid square can be approximated by grid squares, with arbitrarily good approximations if you use small enough grid squares. So since the areas of all those tiny grid squares are being scaled by a certain amount, the area of the blob as a whole will be scaled by that amount. | 进而，任何*不是*网格正方形的形状，都可以用网格正方形来近似（approximate）——只要把网格取得足够小，近似就能要多好有多好。既然所有那些微小网格正方形的面积都被缩放了同一个倍数，那团「墨迹」整体的面积也会被缩放同样的倍数。 |
| This special scaling factor, the factor by which a linear transformation changes areas, is called the "determinant" of that transformation. | 这个特殊的缩放倍数——线性变换改变面积的倍数——就叫作该变换的「行列式」（determinant）。 |
| I'll show how to compute the determinant of transformation using its matrix later on in this video, but understanding what it represents is much more important than the computation. | 在这支视频的后面，我会展示如何用矩阵算出行列式；但理解行列式*代表*什么，远比计算本身重要。 |
| For example, the determinant of a transformation would be $3$ if that transformation increases the area of a region by a factor of 3. | 比如，如果某个变换把一个区域的面积增大到 3 倍，那它的行列式就是 $3$。 |
| The determinant of a transformation is $\frac12$ if it squishes down all areas by a factor of 1/2. | 如果某个变换把所有面积都压扁到 1/2 倍，那它的行列式就是 $\frac12$。 |
| **What is the determinant of the following transformation?** | （译注：原文此处是一道互动测验的题干，配图见官网原页。）**下面这个变换的行列式是多少？** |
| This parallelogram has a height of $2$ and a width of $2$, so the unit square was scaled by $2\times 2=4$. | （译注：上题的答案解析。）这个平行四边形高为 $2$、宽为 $2$，所以单位正方形被缩放了 $2\times 2=4$ 倍。 |
| The determinant of a 2D transformation is $0$ if it squishes all of space onto a line, or even onto a single point, since the area of every region would then become 0. | 如果一个二维变换把整个空间压到一条直线上、甚至压到一个点上，那么它的行列式就是 $0$，因为此时每个区域的面积都变成了 0。 |
| That last one is especially important; checking if the determinant of a given matrix is $0$ will give a way of computing whether or not the transformation associated with a matrix squishes everything into a smaller dimension. You'll see in the next few chapters why this is a useful thing to think about. For now I just want to lay down the visual intuition, which in and of itself is a beautiful thing to think about. | 最后这种情况尤其重要：检查一个给定矩阵的行列式是否为 $0$，就给了我们一种计算途径，来判断与矩阵相应的变换是否把一切压进了更低的维度。接下来几章你会看到，为什么这是个值得思考的有用问题。眼下，我只想先立起这份视觉直觉——它本身就是一件美妙的思考对象。 |

> - **squish** /skwɪʃ/ —— 压扁。3b1b 的高频口语词；smoosh /smuːʃ/ 是它的口语变体。
> - **factor** /ˈfæktə/ —— 倍数。by a factor of 6 缩放 6 倍。
> - **region** /ˈriːdʒən/ —— 区域。the area of a given region。
> - **approximate** /ˈæprɒksɪmeɪt/ —— 近似（动词）。be approximated by grid squares。
> - **arbitrarily** /ˌɑːbɪˈtrɛərəli/ —— 任意地。arbitrarily good approximations。
> - **parallelogram** /ˌpærəˈleləɡræm/ —— 平行四边形。
> - *in and of itself* —— 就其本身而言。a beautiful thing in and of itself。

## 负的行列式（Negative determinant）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Actually, what I've said so far is not quite right. The full concept of the determinant allows for negative values, but what does the idea of scaling an area by a negative amount mean? | 其实，我前面说的并不完全对。行列式的完整概念允许负值；可是「把面积缩放一个负的倍数」是什么意思呢？ |

$$
\det\left(
\begin{bmatrix}
\color{green}1 & \color{red}2 \\
\color{green}3 & \color{red}4
\end{bmatrix}
\right)
=
\color{orange}-2
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| This has to do with the idea of orientation. For example, notice how normally $\hat j$ is to the left of $\hat i$. | 这和「定向」（orientation）的概念有关。比如，注意平时 $\hat j$ 位于 $\hat i$ 的左边。 |
| However, after this transformation now $L(\hat j)$ is on the right of $L(\hat i)$. | 然而，经过这个变换之后，$L(\hat j)$ 现在跑到了 $L(\hat i)$ 的右边。 |
| If you thought of 2D space as a sheet of paper, a transformation like that one seems to turn over that sheet to the other side. Any transformations that do this are said to "invert the orientation of space". Whenever this happens, the determinant will be negative. The absolute value of the determinant still tells you the factor by which areas have been scaled. | 如果你把二维空间想成一张纸，那么像这样的变换，就仿佛把那张纸翻到了另一面。凡是这样做变换的，都说它「反转了空间的定向」（invert the orientation of space）。每当这种情况发生，行列式就会是负的。行列式的绝对值仍然告诉你面积被缩放了多少倍。 |

$$
\det\left(
\begin{bmatrix}
1.5 & 1 \\
2 & -2
\end{bmatrix}
\right)
=
-5
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| **What does the determinant of the matrix** $\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\3&4\end{bmatrix}$ **tell us about the space?** | （译注：原文此处是一道自由问答题，下为官网给出的参考回答。）**矩阵 $\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\3&4\end{bmatrix}$ 的行列式，告诉我们空间的什么信息？** |
| Since $\det\left(\begin{bmatrix}1 &2 \\3 &4\end{bmatrix}\right)=-2$, we know this transformation flips space and doubles the area. | 由于 $\det\left(\begin{bmatrix}1 &2 \\3 &4\end{bmatrix}\right)=-2$，我们知道这个变换翻转了空间，并把面积加倍。 |
| Why would the idea of negative area be a natural way to describe orientation flipping? Well, think about the series of transformations you get by slowly letting $\hat i$ get closer and closer to $\hat j$. As $\hat i$ gets closer, all of the areas in space are getting squished more and more, meaning the determinant approaches $0$. Once $\hat i$ lines up with $\hat j$, the determinant is $0$. Then if $\hat i$ continues the way it was going, doesn't it feel kind of natural for the determinant to keep decreasing into negative numbers? | 为什么「负面积」会是描述定向反转的一种自然方式？嗯，想象一下：让 $\hat i$ 慢慢地越来越靠近 $\hat j$，你会得到一系列变换。随着 $\hat i$ 越靠越近，空间里所有面积都被压得越来越扁，也就是说行列式趋近 $0$。一旦 $\hat i$ 与 $\hat j$ 对齐，行列式就是 $0$。那如果 $\hat i$ 继续沿原来的方向走下去，行列式继续下降、跌进负数，是不是感觉挺自然的？ |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 负的行列式
> - **orientation** /ˌɔːriənˈteɪʃn/ —— 定向、朝向。invert the orientation of space。
> - **invert** /ɪnˈvɜːt/ —— 反转、倒置。
> - **absolute value** —— 绝对值。the absolute value of the determinant。
> - **flip** /flɪp/ —— 翻转。flips space and doubles the area。
> - **approach** /əˈprəʊtʃ/ —— 趋近。the determinant approaches 0。
> - **sheet** /ʃiːt/ —— 一张（纸）。a sheet of paper。
> - *turn over* —— 翻过去。turn over that sheet to the other side。
> - *line up with* —— 与……对齐。once $\hat i$ lines up with $\hat j$。

## 三维情形（In Three Dimensions）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Okay, so that's the understanding of the determinant in two-dimensions, what do you think it should mean for three dimensions? It also tells you how much the transformation scales things, but this time, it tells you how much _volumes_ get scaled. | 好，这就是二维情形下对行列式的理解；那你觉得它在三维里应该意味着什么？它同样告诉你这个变换把东西缩放了多少，只不过这一次，它告诉你的是*体积*（volume）被缩放了多少。 |
| Just as in two dimensions, where this is easiest to think about by focusing on one particular square with area $1$, and watching only what happens to it. In three dimensions it helps to focus your attention on the specific $1\times 1\times 1$ cube whose edges are resting on the basis vectors $\hat i$, $\hat j$ and $\hat k$. | 就像二维里最容易的思考方式是盯住那个面积为 $1$ 的特定正方形、只看它身上发生的事。在三维里，能帮你集中注意力的对象，是那个特定的 $1\times 1\times 1$ 立方体，它的棱分别架在基向量 $\hat i$、$\hat j$ 和 $\hat k$ 上。 |
| After the transformation, that cube might get warped into a slanty cube. That shape has the best name ever: _parallelepiped_. A name made even more delightful when your professor has a thick Russian accent. | 变换之后，那个立方体可能会被扭歪成一个斜斜的立方体。这个形状拥有史上最棒的名字：*平行六面体*（parallelepiped）。如果你的教授还操着一口浓重的俄国口音，这名字就更好玩了。 |
| （译注：原文此处有一条脚注：「Whenever I read the word "parallelepiped" I hear the voice of one of my favorite professors, Yakov Eliashberg.」——每当读到 parallelepiped 这个词，我耳边都会响起我最喜欢的教授之一 Yakov Eliashberg 的声音。） | （译注：此行是原文脚注内容的对照，脚注标记已按体例删除。） |

$$
\det\left(
\begin{bmatrix}
\color{green}-1.0 & \color{red}0.0 & \color{blue}0.05 \\
\color{green}0.15 & \color{red}1.0 & \color{blue}0.05 \\
\color{green}0.0 & \color{red}0.25 & \color{blue}1.0
\end{bmatrix}
\right)
=
\begin{matrix}
\text{Volume of this} \\
\text{parallelepiped}
\end{matrix}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Since this cube starts with a volume of $1$, and the determinant gives the factor by which _any_ volume is scaled, you can think of the determinant as simply being the volume of the parallelepiped that this cube turns into. | 由于这个立方体起初体积为 $1$，而行列式给出*任何*体积被缩放的倍数，你干脆可以把行列式就想成：这个立方体变成的那个平行六面体的体积。 |
| A determinant of zero would mean that all of space is squished onto something with zero volume, meaning a flat plane, a line, or in the most extreme case into a single point at the origin. | 行列式为零则意味着：整个空间被压到了某个零体积的东西上——一个平坦的平面、一条直线，或者最极端的情况下，压到原点处的单个点上。 |
| The above unit cube transforms into a flat parallelepiped, also known as a parallelogram. | 上面的单位立方体就变成了一个扁平的平行六面体——它还有个更常见的名字：平行四边形。 |

$$
\det\left(
\begin{bmatrix}
\color{green}1.0 & \color{red}0.0 & \color{blue}1.0 \\
\color{green}0.5 & \color{red}1.0 & \color{blue}1.5 \\
\color{green}1.0 & \color{red}0.0 & \color{blue}1.0
\end{bmatrix}
\right)
=
0
$$

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 三维情形
> - **volume** /ˈvɒljuːm/ —— 体积。how much volumes get scaled。
> - **cube** /kjuːb/ —— 立方体。a $1\times 1\times 1$ cube。
> - **warp** /wɔːp/ —— 扭歪、使变形。get warped into a slanty cube。
> - **slanty** /ˈslɑːnti/ —— 歪斜的（口语）。a slanty cube。
> - **parallelepiped** /ˌpærəleˈlepɪped/ —— 平行六面体。本课最难的单词，也是最好笑的梗。
> - **delightful** /dɪˈlaɪtfl/ —— 令人愉快的。made even more delightful。
> - **accent** /ˈæksent/ —— 口音。a thick Russian accent。
> - *in the most extreme case* —— 在最极端的情况下。

## 三维中的负行列式（Negative determinants in 3D）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| What about negative determinants in three dimensions, what would that mean? | 那三维里的负行列式呢？那又意味着什么？ |
| One way to describe orientation in three-dimensions is with the right-hand rule: Point the forefinger of your right hand in the direction of $\hat i$, stick out your middle finger in the direction of $\hat j$, and notice how when you point up your thumb, it is in the direction of $\hat k$. | 描述三维定向的一种办法是右手定则（right-hand rule）：把右手食指指向 $\hat i$ 的方向，中指朝 $\hat j$ 的方向伸出，然后注意，当你竖起大拇指时，它正指向 $\hat k$ 的方向。 |
| If you can still do this after the transformation, orientation has not changed, and the determinant is positive. Otherwise, if after the transformation it only makes sense to do that with your left hand, orientation has been flipped, and the determinant is negative. | 如果变换之后你还能这么做，说明定向没有改变，行列式为正。否则，如果变换之后只有用左手做才说得通，说明定向被翻转了，行列式为负。 |
| **What is the sign of the determinant for the following transformation?** | （译注：原文此处是一道互动测验的题干，候选为 Positive / Negative 两个选项，配图见官网原页。）**下面这个变换的行列式，符号是什么？** |
| Point your left index finger towards yourself. This finger represents the green arrow $\hat i$. Stick out your left middle finger halfway. This finger represents the red arrow $\hat j$. Your left thumb will naturally point in the direction of the blue arrow $\hat k$. Since this works with your left hand, the determinant must be negative. | （译注：上题的答案解析，答案为 Negative。）把左手食指朝你自己指向自己。这根手指代表绿色的箭头 $\hat i$。左手中指伸出一半。这根手指代表红色的箭头 $\hat j$。你的左手拇指会自然指向蓝色箭头 $\hat k$ 的方向。既然要用左手才成立，行列式必然为负。 |

> - **forefinger** /ˈfɔːfɪŋɡə/ —— 食指。又作 index finger。
> - **middle finger** —— 中指。
> - **thumb** /θʌm/ —— 大拇指。point up your thumb。
> - **represent** /ˌreprɪˈzent/ —— 代表。This finger represents the green arrow。
> - *stick out* —— 伸出。stick out your middle finger。
> - *make sense* —— 说得通。it only makes sense to do that with your left hand。

## 二维（Two Dimensions）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| This is the formula for a $2\times 2$ matrix: | 这是 $2\times 2$ 矩阵的公式： |

$$
\det\left(
\begin{bmatrix}
\color{green}a&\color{red}b \\
\color{green}c&\color{red}d
\end{bmatrix}
\right)
=
\color{green}a\color{red}d
\color{currentColor}-
\color{red}b\color{green}c
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Here's part of an intuition for this formula. Let's say the terms $\color{red}b$ and $\color{green}c$ were zero. Then the term $\color{green}a$ tells you how much $\hat i$ is stretched in the $x$-direction, and the term $\color{red}d$ tells you how much $\hat j$ is stretched in the $y$-direction. So if the other terms were zero, it should make sense that $\color{green}a\color{currentColor}\cdot\color{red}d$ gives the area of the rectangle that our favorite unit square turns into, like the $\begin{bmatrix}3&0\\0&2\end{bmatrix}$ example from earlier. | 这里给出这条公式的一部分直觉。假设 $b$ 和 $c$ 两项是零。那么 $a$ 这一项告诉你 $\hat i$ 在 $x$ 方向上被拉伸了多少，$d$ 这一项告诉你 $\hat j$ 在 $y$ 方向上被拉伸了多少。所以，如果其余各项为零，那 $a\cdot d$ 给出的正是我们最爱的那个单位正方形所变成的矩形的面积——就像前面 $\begin{bmatrix}3&0\\0&2\end{bmatrix}$ 那个例子一样。 |
| Even if only one of $b$ or $c$ are zero, you'll have a parallelogram with base $a$ and height $d$, so the area will still be $\color{green}a\color{currentColor}\cdot\color{red}d$. | 哪怕 $b$、$c$ 中只有一个为零，你得到的也是底为 $a$、高为 $d$ 的平行四边形，所以面积仍然是 $a\cdot d$。 |
| Loosely speaking, if both $b$ and $c$ are nonzero, the term $\color{red}b\color{currentColor}\cdot\color{green}c$ tells you how much this rectangle is stretched or squished in the diagonal direction. It shows how much $\hat i$ is changed in the $y$-direction, and how much $\hat j$ is changed in the $x$-direction. ## 矩阵乘法（Matrix Multiplication）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Here's a kind of fun question to think about: If you multiply two matrices together, the determinant of the resulting matrix is the same as the product of the determinants of the original two matrices. | 来思考一个挺好玩的问题：如果你把两个矩阵相乘，所得矩阵的行列式，等于原来那两个矩阵的行列式之积。 |

$$
\det(
\color{blue}M_1
\color{purple}M_2
\color{currentColor})=\det(
\color{blue}M_1
\color{currentColor})\det(
\color{purple}M_2
\color{currentColor})
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| If you tried to justify this numerically, it would be a horrible mess. **But can you explain why in just one sentence?** | 如果你试图从数值上论证这一点，那会是一团可怕的乱麻。**但你能只用一句话解释这是为什么吗？** |
| The determinant shows how much area scales after the matrix's linear transformation. | （译注：原文此处是一道自由问答题，下为官网给出的参考回答。）行列式显示的是：矩阵的线性变换作用之后，面积被缩放了多少。 |
| Since applying two matrices sequentially leads to a new linear transformation, it doesn't matter if we first apply $M_2$ which scales space by $\det(M_2)\cdot A$ and then apply $M_1$ which scales space by $\det(M_1)\cdot\det(M_2)\cdot A$ or if we apply both of them which scales space by $\det(M_1M_2)\cdot A$. | 既然先后应用两个矩阵会导向一个新的线性变换，那么「先应用把空间缩放 $\det(M_2)\cdot A$ 的 $M_2$，再应用把空间缩放 $\det(M_1)\cdot\det(M_2)\cdot A$ 的 $M_1$」，与「一次性应用把空间缩放 $\det(M_1M_2)\cdot A$ 的两个矩阵」，两者并无区别。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 矩阵乘法
> - **product** /ˈprɒdʌkt/ —— 乘积。the product of the determinants。
> - **horrible** /ˈhɒrəbl/ —— 可怕的。a horrible mess。
> - **mess** /mes/ —— 乱糟糟的一团。
> - **numerically** /njuːˈmerɪkli/ —— 从数值上。justify this numerically。
> - **sequentially** /sɪˈkwenʃəli/ —— 相继地、依次地。applying two matrices sequentially。
> - **justify** /ˈdʒʌstɪfaɪ/ —— 论证、说明其合理性。
> - *lead to* —— 导向、导致。leads to a new linear transformation。
