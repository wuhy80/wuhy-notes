## 开篇（Introduction）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| In the last chapter, we talked about how to compute a three-dimensional cross product of two vectors, $\vec{\mathbf{v}} \times \vec{\mathbf{w}}$. It's this funny thing where you write a matrix whose second column has the coordinates of $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$, whose third columns has the coordinates of $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$, but the entries of the first column, weirdly, are the basis vectors $\hat{\imath}$, $\hat{\jmath}$ and $\hat{k}$, where you pretend they are numbers for the sake of computations. | 上一章我们讲了如何计算两个向量的三维叉积 $\vec{\mathbf{v}} \times \vec{\mathbf{w}}$。它是个滑稽的东西：你写一个矩阵，第二列放 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 的坐标，第三列放 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 的坐标，可第一列的条目，怪就怪在，居然是基向量 $\hat{\imath}$、$\hat{\jmath}$ 和 $\hat{k}$——而为了做计算，你假装它们是数字。 |

$$
\left[\begin{array}{l}v_1 \\v_2 \\v_3\end{array}\right] \times\left[\begin{array}{l}w_1 \\w_2 \\w_3\end{array}\right]=\operatorname{det}\left(\left[\begin{array}{lll}\hat{\imath} & v_1 & w_1 \\\hat{\jmath} & v_2 & w_2 \\\hat{k} & v_3 & w_3\end{array}\right]\right)
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| If you just chug along with the computation, ignoring this weirdness, you get some constant times $\hat{\imath}$, plus some constant times $\hat{\jmath}$, plus some constant times $\hat{k}$, which defines a new 3d vector. | 如果你不管这套怪异之处、闷头把计算推下去，你会得到某个常数乘 $\hat{\imath}$，加上某个常数乘 $\hat{\jmath}$，再加上某个常数乘 $\hat{k}$——这就定义出一个新的三维向量。 |

$$
\vec{\mathbf{p}} = \hat{\imath} \underbrace{\left(v_2 w_3-v_3 w_2\right)}_{\text {Some number }}+\hat{\jmath} \underbrace{\left(v_3 w_1-v_1 w_3\right)}_{\text {Some number }}+\hat{k} \underbrace{\left(v_1 w_2-v_2 w_1\right)}_{\text {Some number }}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| From here, students are typically told to just believe that the resulting vector has the following geometric properties: | 讲到这里，学生们通常被要求直接相信：所得向量具有下面这些几何性质： |
| Its length equals the area of the parallelogram defined by $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$. | 它的长度等于由 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 确定的平行四边形的面积。 |
| It points in a direction perpendicular to $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$, | 它指向一个垂直于 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 的方向， |
| This direction obeys the right-hand rule, in the sense that if you point your forefinger along $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$, and your middle finger along $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$, then stick out your thumb, it will point in the direction of the new vectors. | 这个方向服从右手定则——意思是，如果你把食指指向 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$，中指指向 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$，然后竖起大拇指，它就会指向新向量的方向。 |
| There are some brute-force computational ways to confirm these facts, but I want to share with you a really elegant line of reasoning. This leverages a bit of background, though, so I'm assuming everyone has read chapter 5 on the determinant, and chapter 9 where I introduce the idea of duality. So, you know, go back and take a look if needed. | 确认这些事实有一些暴力硬算的办法，但我想给你分享一条真正优雅的推理路线。不过这要借一点背景知识：我假定每个人都读过讲行列式的第 5 章，以及我引入对偶性（duality）思想的第 9 章。所以，你懂的，需要的话就回去看看。 |

## 在变换之光下（Under the light of transformations）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| As a reminder, the idea of duality is that anytime you have a linear transformation from some space to the number line, it is associated with a unique vector in that space, in the sense that performing the linear transformation is the same as taking a dot product with that vector. | 提醒一下，对偶性（duality）的想法是：任何时候你有一个从某个空间到数轴（number line）的线性变换，它都与该空间中一个唯一的向量相关联——意思是，执行这个线性变换，等同于与那个向量做点积。 |
| Any time you have a 2d-to-1d linear transformation it's associated with some vector. | （译注：图注。）任何时候你有一个从二维到一维的线性变换，它都与某个向量相关联。 |
| Numerically, it's because one of those transformations is described by a matrix with just one row, where each column tells you which number the basis vectors land on. And multiplying this matrix by some vector $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ is computationally identical to taking the dot product between $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and the vector you get by turning that matrix on its side. | 数值上，原因在于：这类变换由一个只有一行的矩阵描述，矩阵的每一列告诉你基向量落在哪个数上。而这个矩阵乘以某个向量 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$，在计算上完全等同于取 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 与「把矩阵转成侧躺」所得向量之间的点积。 |
| The takeaway is that when you're out in the mathematical wild and you find a linear transformation to the number line, you will be able to match it to some vector, which is called the "dual vector" of the transformation, so that performing that linear transformation is the same as taking the dot product with that vector. | 要点是：当你在数学的野外漫游、发现一个到数轴的线性变换时，你总能把它匹配到某个向量——这个向量叫作该变换的「对偶向量」（dual vector）——使得执行这个线性变换等同于与那个向量做点积。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 在变换之光下
> - **duality** /djuːˈæləti/ —— 对偶性。the idea of duality。
> - **number line** —— 数轴。a linear transformation to the number line。
> - **unique** /juˈniːk/ —— 唯一的。a unique vector in that space。
> - **takeaway** /ˈteɪkəweɪ/ —— 要点、带走的结论。The takeaway is that...
> - *be associated with* —— 与……相关联。it is associated with a unique vector。
> - *turn on its side* —— 把……转成侧躺（矩阵躺倒）。turning that matrix on its side。

## 这个想法（The idea）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| The cross product gives us a really slick example of this process in action. It takes some effort, but it's definitely worth it. What I'm going to do is define a certain linear transformation from three dimensions to the number line, and it will be defined in terms of two vectors $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$. Then, when we associate that transformation with its dual vector in 3d space, that dual vector will be the cross product of $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$. Understanding that transformation will make clear the connection between the geometry and the computation of the cross product. | 叉积给了我们一个特别利落的实例，让上面这套流程真刀真枪地跑一遍。它要花些力气，但绝对值得。我要做的是：定义一个从三维到数轴的特定线性变换，它由两个向量 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 来定义。然后，当我们把这个变换与它在三维空间中的对偶向量联系起来时，那个对偶向量就将是 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 的叉积。理解了这个变换，叉积的几何与计算之间的联系就会变得清清楚楚。 |
| To back up a bit, remember that in two dimensions, computing the 2d version of the cross product of vectors $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ involves taking the determinant of a matrix whose columns contain the coordinates of those vectors. There's no nonsense with basis vectors stuck in the matrix, just an ordinary determinant returning a number. Geometrically, this gives us the area of the parallelogram spanned out by those two vectors, with the possibility of being negative depending on the orientation of the vectors. | 先退一步说。记住，在二维里，计算向量 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 的二维版叉积，取的是这样一个矩阵的行列式：它的列放着这两个向量的坐标。矩阵里没有卡着基向量那种乱七八糟的东西，只是一个返回数字的普通行列式。在几何上，这给出的是这两个向量张成的平行四边形的面积，并可能为负，取决于向量的定向。 |
| If you didn't already know the 3d cross product, you might imagine that it involves taking 3 separate 3d vectors, $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$, $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$, and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$, making their coordinates the columns of a 3x3 matrix, and computing the determinant of that matrix. As you know from chapter 5, geometrically this would give the volume of a parallelepiped spanned out by those 3 vectors, with the plus or minus sign of your result depending on their right-hand rule orientation of the three vectors. | 如果你本来不知道三维叉积，你可能会想象它是这样的：取 3 个独立的三维向量 $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$、$\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$，把它们的坐标做成一个 3x3 矩阵的列，然后算这个矩阵的行列式。你在第 5 章已经知道，在几何上这会给出这 3 个向量张成的平行六面体的体积，结果的加减号取决于这三个向量的右手定则定向。 |
| Of course, you all know this is not the 3d cross product, since the actual 3d cross product takes two vectors and spits out a vector, it doesn't take in three vectors and spit out a number. But this idea actually gets us really close to what the real cross product is. Consider the first vector $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ to be a variable, while $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ are fixed. | 当然，你们都知道这并不是三维叉积，因为真正的三维叉积是吃进两个向量、吐出一个向量，而不是吃进三个向量、吐出一个数。但这个想法其实已经把我们带到了离真正的叉积非常近的地方。把第一个向量 $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ 看成变量（variable），而 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 固定不变。 |
| What we have, then, is a function from three dimensions to the number line. You input some vector $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$, and you get a number by taking the determinant of a matrix whose first column is $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$, and whose other two columns are the constant vectors $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$. Geometrically, the meaning of this function is that for any input vector $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$, you consider the parallelepiped defined by this vector, $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$, then return its volume, with a plus or minus sign depending on orientation. What's more, this function is linear. | 那么我们手里就有一个从三维到数轴的函数：你输入某个向量 $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$，通过取一个矩阵的行列式得到一个数——这个矩阵的第一列是 $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$，另外两列是常向量 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$。在几何上，这个函数的意义是：对任何输入向量 $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$，你考虑由这个向量、$\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 确定的平行六面体，然后返回它的体积，正负号取决于定向。而且，这个函数是线性的。 |
| So here we are, out in the mathematical wild with a linear transformation that outputs numbers. The idea of duality should be perking up in your mind. What we're going to do is find the matrix that describes this transformation, which will be a 1x3 matrix since the transformation goes from three dimensions to one. In other words, we're looking for a 1x3 matrix such that multiplying this matrix by some vector $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ gives the same result as plugging in $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ to the first column of a 3x3 matrix whose other two columns have the coordinates of $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$, and computing the determinant. | 于是，我们又一次站在数学的野外，手里握着一个输出数字的线性变换。对偶性的念头应该在你脑子里竖起耳朵了。我们要做的，是找到描述这个变换的矩阵——它会是一个 1x3 矩阵，因为这个变换从三维到一维。换句话说，我们在找一个 1x3 矩阵，使得「这个矩阵乘以某个向量 $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$」与「把 $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ 塞进一个 3x3 矩阵的第一列（另外两列放着 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 的坐标）再算行列式」给出相同的结果。 |
| If you think in terms of duality and turn this matrix on its side, we're looking for a special 3d vector, that I'll call $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$, such that taking the dot product between $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$ and any other vector $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ gives the same result as plugging in $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ to the first column of a matrix and computing the determinant. | 如果你用对偶性的思路，把这个矩阵转成侧躺，那么我们在找的就是一个特殊的三维向量——我叫它 $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$——使得「$\vec{\mathbf{p}}$ 与任何其他向量 $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ 的点积」与「把 $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ 塞进矩阵第一列再算行列式」给出相同的结果。 |
| I'll get to the geometry of this in just a moment, but right now let's dig in and think about what this means computationally. Taking the dot product between $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ will give | 几何部分我马上就讲，但现在先一头扎进去，想想这在计算上意味着什么。取 $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$ 与 $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ 的点积会给出 |

$$
(\text{something}) \cdot x + (\text{something}) \cdot y + (\text{something}) \cdot z
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Those somethings are the coordinates of $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$. When you compute the determinant on the right, you can organize it to look like | 那些 something 就是 $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$ 的坐标。当你算右边的行列式时，可以把它整理成 |

$$
(v_2 w_3 - v_3 w_2) \cdot x + (v_3 w_1 - v_1 w_3) \cdot y + (v_1 w_2 - v_2 w_1) \cdot z
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| This shows what those "somethings" are, and hence what the coordinates of the vector $\mathbf{p}$ are. | 这就显示出那些「something」是什么，从而显示出向量 $\mathbf{p}$ 的坐标是什么。 |
| So the answers to what those "somethings" are will give the coordinates of the vector $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$ that we're looking for. | 所以，回答「那些 something 是什么」，就给出了我们要找的向量 $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$ 的坐标。 |
| But this should feel very familiar to anyone who's actually worked through a cross product computation before: Collecting the constant terms that are multiplied by $x$, $y$ and $z$ like this is no different from plugging in the symbols $\hat{\imath}$, $\hat{\jmath}$ and $\hat{k}$, and seeing which coefficients aggregate on each of these terms. It's just that plugging in $\hat{\imath}$, $\hat{\jmath}$ and $\hat{k}$ is a way of signaling that we should interpret these three coefficients as coordinates of a vector. | 但任何一个真正动手算过叉积的人，对这一幕都应该感到非常眼熟：像这样把乘在 $x$、$y$ 和 $z$ 上的常数项分别归拢起来，与塞进符号 $\hat{\imath}$、$\hat{\jmath}$ 和 $\hat{k}$、再看每一项上聚集了哪些系数，并没有什么不同。只不过，塞进 $\hat{\imath}$、$\hat{\jmath}$ 和 $\hat{k}$ 是在发出信号：我们应该把这三个系数解读成一个向量的坐标。 |
| In other words, this funky computation can be thought of as the answer to a certain question: "What vector $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$ has the special property that when you take a dot product between $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$, it gives the same result as plugging in $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ to the first column of a matrix whose other two columns have the coordinates of $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$, then computing the determinant?" | 换句话说，这个古怪的计算可以被想成某个问题的答案：「哪个向量 $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$ 具有这样的特殊性质：当你取 $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$ 与 $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ 的点积时，结果与把 $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ 塞进一个矩阵的第一列（另外两列是 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 的坐标）再算行列式相同？」 |
| Now for the cool part, which ties this all together with the geometric understanding of the cross product I showed earlier. Let's ask that same question again, but this time we're going to try to answer it geometrically, instead of computationally: "What 3d vector $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$ has the property that when you take a dot product between $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$ and some other vector $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$, it gives the same value as if you took the signed volume of the parallelepiped defined by this vector $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ along with $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$?" | 现在到最酷的部分了，它把这一切与前面展示过的叉积几何理解绑在了一起。让我们把同一个问题再问一遍，但这一次试着从几何上、而不是从计算上回答它：「哪个三维向量 $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$ 具有这样的性质：当你取 $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$ 与某个其他向量 $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ 的点积时，结果等于你取由 $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ 连同 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 确定的平行六面体的带符号体积？」 |
| Even though we are drawing the vector $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ as $\left[\begin{array}{c}-2 \\ 0 \\ 3\end{array}\right]$ in the figure which gives the parallelepiped a tangible volume, still think about $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ as a variable that is free to change. | （译注：图注。）尽管图中把向量 $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ 画成了 $\left[\begin{array}{c}-2 \\ 0 \\ 3\end{array}\right]$，好让平行六面体有一个摸得着的体积，你仍然要把 $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ 想成一个可以自由变化的变量。 |
| Remember, the geometric interpretation of the dot product between a vector $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$ and some other vector is to project that other vector onto $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$, and multiply the length of the projection by the length of $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$. | 记住，向量 $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$ 与某个其他向量的点积，其几何解读是：把那个向量投影到 $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$ 上，再拿投影的长度乘以 $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$ 的长度。 |
| Here's one way to think of the volume of the parallelepiped formed by $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$, $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$, and $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$: take the area of the parallelogram defined by $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$, and multiply it not by the length of $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$, but by the component of $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ which is perpendicular to that parallelogram. | 思考由 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$、$\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ 形成的平行六面体的体积，有一种办法：取由 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 确定的平行四边形的面积，乘的不是 $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ 的长度，而是 $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ 中垂直于那个平行四边形的分量。 |
| In other words, consider, the way our linear function works on a given vector is to project that vector onto the line perpendicular to both $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$, then multiply the length of the projection by the area of the parallelogram spanned by $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$. But this is the same thing as taking a dot product between $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ and a vector perpendicular to $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ with a length equal to the area of the parallelogram! | 换句话说，想一想我们的线性函数作用在给定向量上的方式：把那个向量投影到同时垂直于 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 的直线上，再拿投影的长度乘以 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 张成的平行四边形的面积。但这恰恰等于取 $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ 与「一个垂直于 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$、长度等于平行四边形面积的向量」的点积！ |
| If you choose the appropriate direction for that vector, the cases where the dot product is negative will be the same as those when the right-hand-rule orientation of $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$, $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ is negative. For example, a quick way to reverse the orientation is to swap $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ which inverts the volume of the parallelepiped. | 只要给那个向量选好合适的方向，点积为负的那些情形，就会恰好是 $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$、$\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 的右手定则定向为负的那些情形。比如，反转定向的一个快捷方式就是交换 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$，这会让平行六面体的体积变号。 |
| This means we just found a vector $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$ so that taking a dot product between $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$ and some vector $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ is the same thing as computing the determinant of a 3x3 matrix whose columns contain the coordinates of that vector, $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$! | 这意味着，我们刚刚找到了一个向量 $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$，使得「$\vec{\mathbf{p}}$ 与某个向量 $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ 的点积」与「计算一个 3x3 矩阵的行列式（其列含那个向量、$\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 的坐标）」是同一件事！ |
| So the answer we found computationally earlier using the notational trick must correspond to this vector. This is the fundamental reason why the computation and the geometric interpretation are related! | 所以，前面借助记号花招从计算上找到的答案，必定对应于这个向量。这就是计算与几何解释之所以相关的根本原因！ |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 这个想法

> - **variable** /ˈveəriəbl/ —— 变量。Consider the first vector $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ to be a variable。

| To sum up what just happened, we defined a linear transformation from 3d space to the number line defined in terms of the vectors $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$. | 来总结一下刚才发生的事：我们定义了一个从三维空间到数轴的线性变换，它由向量 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 定义。 |
| 3d to 1d Linear Transformation | （译注：图题。）三维到一维的线性变换。 |
| Then we went through two different ways to think about the dual vector of this transformation, the vector such that applying the transformation is the same as taking a dot product with this vector. | 然后我们用两种不同的方式思考了这个变换的对偶向量——那个向量，使得应用这个变换等同于与它做点积。 |
| Dual Vector of Transformation | （译注：图题。）变换的对偶向量。 |
| On the one hand, thinking about the dual vector from a computational approach leads us to the trick of plugging in the symbols $\hat{\imath}$, $\hat{\jmath}$, and $\hat{k}$ to the first column of a matrix and computing the determinant. | 一方面，从计算的路子思考对偶向量，会把我们引到那个花招：把符号 $\hat{\imath}$、$\hat{\jmath}$ 和 $\hat{k}$ 塞进矩阵的第一列，再算行列式。 |
| But thinking geometrically, we can deduce that this dual vector must be perpendicular to $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$, with a length equal to the area of the parallelogram spanned out by those two vectors. Also, its direction will be determined by the right-hand rule. | 但从几何上思考，我们可以推出：这个对偶向量必定垂直于 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$，长度等于这两个向量张成的平行四边形的面积；而且，它的方向将由右手定则确定。 |
| For both approaches, finding the dual vector of this linear transformation gives us a much deeper understanding of the result of the cross product between two vectors $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ than just the formula. | 对这两条路子来说，找到这个线性变换的对偶向量，都让我们对两个向量 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 之间叉积结果的理解，比光看公式深刻得多。 |
