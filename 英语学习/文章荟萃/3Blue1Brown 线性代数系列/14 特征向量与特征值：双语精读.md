## 一个例子（An example）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| To start, consider some linear transformation in two dimensions, like the one shown here. | 先考虑某个二维的线性变换，就像这里展示的这个。 |
| It moves the basis vector $\hat{\imath}$ to the coordinates $\left[\begin{array}{c}3 \\ 0\end{array}\right]$, and $\hat{\jmath}$ to $\left[\begin{array}{c}1 \\ 2\end{array}\right]$, so it's represented with a the matrix $\left[\begin{array}{c}3 & 1 \\ 0 & 2\end{array}\right]$. Now, focus on what it does to any individual vector, and think about the span of that vector, which is the line passing through the origin and its tip. | 它把基向量 $\hat{\imath}$ 移到坐标 $\left[\begin{array}{c}3 \\ 0\end{array}\right]$，把 $\hat{\jmath}$ 移到 $\left[\begin{array}{c}1 \\ 2\end{array}\right]$，所以它由矩阵 $\left[\begin{array}{c}3 & 1 \\ 0 & 2\end{array}\right]$ 表示。现在，聚焦它对任何一个单独向量的作用，并想一想那个向量的张成（span）——也就是穿过原点和向量尖端的那条直线。 |
| Most vectors will get knocked off that line during the transformation. It would seem pretty coincidental if the place where a vector lands happens to be somewhere on that line. But some special vectors do remain on their own span, meaning the effect the matrix has on such a vector is to just stretch or squish it, like a scalar. | 大多数向量会在变换中被撞离那条直线。如果向量落下的位置恰好还在那条线上，看上去就相当巧合。但确实有一些特殊的向量会留在自己张成的空间上，也就是说，矩阵对这种向量的作用只是像标量（scalar）一样把它拉伸或压扁。 |
| For this specific example, the basis vector $\hat{\imath}$ is one such vector with this special property. The span of $\hat{\imath}$ is the $x$-axis, and from the first column of the matrix we can see that $\hat{\imath}$ moves over to $3$ times itself, still on that $x$-axis. What's more, because of the way linear transformations work, any other vector on the $x$-axis is also just stretched by a factor of $3$, and hence remains on its own span. | 在这个具体的例子里，基向量 $\hat{\imath}$ 就是具有这种特殊性质的向量之一。$\hat{\imath}$ 的张成是 $x$ 轴，而从矩阵第一列可以看到，$\hat{\imath}$ 移到了自身的 $3$ 倍处，仍在那条 $x$ 轴上。更进一步，由于线性变换的运作方式，$x$ 轴上的任何其他向量也都只是被拉伸 $3$ 倍，因此同样留在自己张成的空间上。 |
| A slightly sneakier vector that remains on its own span during this transformation is $\left[\begin{array}{c}1 \\ -1\end{array}\right]$. It ends up getting stretched out by a factor of $2$. And again, linearity will imply that any other vector on the diagonal line spanned by this one will just get stretched out by a factor of $2$. | 这个变换里另一个不那么显眼、却同样留在自己张成空间上的向量，是 $\left[\begin{array}{c}1 \\ -1\end{array}\right]$。它最终被拉伸了 $2$ 倍。同样地，线性性意味着由它张成的那条对角线上的任何其他向量，也都只会被拉伸 $2$ 倍。 |
| For this transformation, those are all the vectors with this special property of staying on their span: Those on the $x$-axis that get stretched by a factor of $3$, and those on this diagonal line that are stretched by a factor of $2$. Any other vector will get rotated somewhat during the transformation, knocked off of the line it spans. | 对这个变换而言，具有「留在自己张成空间上」这一特殊性质的向量就这些了：$x$ 轴上被拉伸 $3$ 倍的那些，以及这条对角线上被拉伸 $2$ 倍的那些。任何其他向量都会在变换中被稍微旋转，被撞离它张成的直线。 |
| These special vectors are called the "eigenvectors" of the transformation, and each eigenvector has associated with it what's called an "eigenvalue", which is the factor by which it's stretched or squished during the transformation. In this example, vectors on the $x$-axis are eigenvectors with eigenvalue $3$, while those on the diagonal line are eigenvectors with eigenvalue $2$. | 这些特殊的向量叫作该变换的「特征向量」（eigenvectors），而每个特征向量都附带一个所谓的「特征值」（eigenvalue），即在变换中它被拉伸或压扁的倍数。在这个例子里，$x$ 轴上的向量是特征值为 $3$ 的特征向量，对角线上的向量则是特征值为 $2$ 的特征向量。 |
| Of course, there's nothing special about stretching vs. squishing, or the fact that these eigenvalues happen to be positive. In another example you could have an eigenvector with eigenvalue $-\frac{1}{2}$, meaning that vector gets flipped and squished by a factor of $\frac{1}{2}$, but the important part is that it stays on the line that it spans out, without being rotated off of it. | 当然，「拉伸还是压扁」并没什么特殊之处，这些特征值恰好为正也并非要害。换一个例子，你完全可能遇到特征值为 $-\frac{1}{2}$ 的特征向量，意思是那个向量被翻了个面、又被压扁到 $\frac{1}{2}$ 倍；但关键的部分在于：它仍留在它张出的那条直线上，没有被旋转出去。 |
| Eigenvector with eigenvalue $-\frac{1}{2}$. | （图注）特征值为 $-\frac{1}{2}$ 的特征向量。 |

## 三维旋转轴（3d rotational axis）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| For a glimpse of why this might be a useful thing to think about, consider some three-dimensional rotation. If you can find an eigenvector for this rotation, a vector which remains on its own span, what you have found is the axis of rotation. | 要初窥这件事为什么可能有用，请考虑某个三维旋转。如果你能为这个旋转找到一个特征向量——一个保持在自身张成空间上的向量——那你找到的就是旋转轴（axis of rotation）。 |
| It's much easier to think about a 3d rotation in terms of an axis of rotation, and an angle by which it is rotating, rather than thinking about the full 3x3 matrix associated with such a transformation. | 用「旋转轴加旋转角」来思考三维旋转，比盯着与这种变换相伴的那整个 $3\times3$ 矩阵要容易得多。 |
| In this case, the corresponding eigenvalue would have to be $1$, since 3d rotations don't stretch or squish anything. | 在这种情形下，相应的特征值必然是 $1$，因为三维旋转不拉伸、也不压扁任何东西。 |
| This is a pattern that shows up a lot in linear algebra: With any linear transformation described by a matrix, you could understand what it's doing by reading off the columns of this matrix as the landing spots for the basis vectors. But often a better way to get at the heart of what that transformation actually does, less dependent on your particular coordinate system, is to find the eigenvectors and eigenvalues. | 这是线性代数中一再出现的模式：对一个由矩阵描述的线性变换，你当然可以把矩阵的列读成基向量的落点，以此来理解它在做什么；但要想触及那个变换真正的核心，往往更好的办法是找出特征向量与特征值——这样更不依赖于你碰巧选用的坐标系。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 三维旋转轴

> - **axis** /ˈæksɪs/ —— 轴。the axis of rotation。
> - **rotation** /rəʊˈteɪʃn/ —— 旋转。consider some three-dimensional rotation。
> - **landing spot** —— 落点。the landing spots for the basis vectors。
> - *read off* —— 直接读出。reading off the columns of this matrix。
> - *get at the heart of* —— 触及……的核心。get at the heart of what that transformation actually does。

## 计算评注（Notes on computation）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| We won't cover the full details on methods for computing eigenvectors and eigenvalues here, but we'll try to give an overview of the computational ideas that are most important for conceptual understanding. | 我们不会在这里讲尽计算特征向量与特征值的方法细节，但会试着概述那些对概念理解最重要的计算思想。 |
| Symbolically, here's what the idea of eigenvectors and eigenvalues looks like. $A$ is the matrix representing some transformation, with $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ as an eigenvector, and $\lambda$ (lambda) is a number, namely the corresponding eigenvalue. | 用符号写出来，特征向量与特征值的想法长这个样子：$A$ 是表示某个变换的矩阵，$\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 是一个特征向量，而 $\lambda$（lambda）是一个数，即相应的特征值。 |
| What this expression is saying is that the matrix-vector product $A$ times $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ gives the same result as scaling the eigenvector $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ by the eigenvalue lambda. | 这个表达式说的是：「矩阵 $A$ 乘向量 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$」这个矩阵-向量乘积，与「用特征值 lambda 缩放特征向量 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$」给出同样的结果。 |
| Finding the eigenvectors and eigenvalues of a matrix $A$ comes down to finding values of $\mathbf{v}$ and $\lambda$ that make this expression true. It's a little awkward to work with at first, because the left-hand-side represents matrix vector multiplication, while the right-hand-side is scalar-vector multiplication. Let's start by rewriting that right-hand-side as some kind of matrix vector multiplication, using a matrix which has the effect of scaling vectors by a factor of lambda. | 求矩阵 $A$ 的特征向量与特征值，归结起来就是找出让这个表达式成立的 $\mathbf{v}$ 与 $\lambda$ 的取值。一开始用它做运算会有点别扭，因为左边代表矩阵-向量乘法，而右边却是标量-向量乘法。我们先把右边改写成某种矩阵-向量乘法：用一个能把向量按 lambda 倍缩放的矩阵。 |
| The columns of such matrix will represent what happens to each basis vector, and each basis vector is simply multiplied by lambda, so this matrix will have the number lambda down the diagonal with zeros everywhere else. The common way to write this matrix is as lambda times $I$, where $I$ is the identity matrix with $1$s down the diagonal. | 这种矩阵的列表示每个基向量身上发生了什么，而每个基向量都只是被乘上 lambda，所以这个矩阵在对角线上是 lambda、其余地方全是零。这个矩阵通常写作 lambda 乘 $I$，其中 $I$ 是对角线全为 $1$ 的单位矩阵（identity matrix）。 |
| With both sides looking like matrix-vector multiplication, we can subtract off the right-hand side and factor out the $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$. So we have a new matrix, $\left(A - \lambda I\right)$, and we're looking for a vector $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ such that this new matrix times $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ gives the zero vector. | 现在两边看起来都是矩阵-向量乘法了，我们可以把右边减掉，并把 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 提出来。于是得到一个新矩阵 $\left(A - \lambda I\right)$，我们要找的，是让「这个新矩阵乘 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$」给出零向量的向量 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$。 |
| Now, this will always be true when $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ is the zero vector, but that's boring, we want a non-zero eigenvector. | 当然，$\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 是零向量时这永远成立，但那很无聊，我们想要的是非零的特征向量。 |

## 行列式为零（Zero Determinant）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| As viewers of chapter 5 and chapter 6 will know, the only way it's possible for the product of a matrix with a nonzero vector to become zero is if the transformation associated with that matrix squishes space into a lower dimension. And that squishification corresponds to a zero determinant of the matrix. | 看过第 5、6 章的观众会知道：矩阵与一个非零向量的乘积要想变成零，唯一的可能是与该矩阵相应的变换把空间压进了更低的维度，而这种压扁正对应着矩阵行列式为零。 |
| To be concrete, let's say your matrix is $\left[\begin{array}{c} 2 & 2 \\ 1 & 3 \end{array}\right]$, and imagine subtracting off the variable amount lambda from each diagonal entry. | 说点具体的：设你的矩阵是 $\left[\begin{array}{c} 2 & 2 \\ 1 & 3 \end{array}\right]$，想象从每个对角元里减去可变的量 lambda。 |
| Imagine tweaking lambda, turning a knob to change its value. As that value of lambda changes, the matrix changes, and so the determinant of that matrix changes. | 想象你在微调 lambda，像拧旋钮一样改变它的值。lambda 的值一变，矩阵随之而变，矩阵的行列式也随之而变。 |
| The goal is to find a value of lambda that will make the determinant zero, meaning the tweaked transformation squishes space. In this case, the sweet spot comes when $\lambda = 1$. | 目标是找到让行列式恰好为零的那个 lambda 值——意味着被调过的变换会把空间压扁。在这个例子里，最佳点出现在 $\lambda = 1$。 |
| Remember that the lambda is tied to the matrix, so if we use another matrix, the lambda might not be $1$. To unravel what that means, when $\lambda=1$, the matrix $A - \lambda \cdot I$ squishes space onto a line. That means there's a nonzero vector $\mathbf{v}$ such that $(A-\lambda\cdot I)\cdot \mathbf{v}=0$. | 记住，lambda 是和矩阵绑定的，所以换一个矩阵，lambda 就未必是 $1$。把这件事理清楚：当 $\lambda=1$ 时，矩阵 $A - \lambda \cdot I$ 把空间压到一条直线上，这意味着存在一个非零向量 $\mathbf{v}$，使得 $(A-\lambda\cdot I)\cdot \mathbf{v}=0$。 |
| The reason we care about that is because it means $A \cdot \mathbf{v} = \lambda \cdot \mathbf{v}$, which you can read as saying the vector $\mathbf{v}$ is an eigenvector of $A$, staying on its own span during the transformation $A$. In this example, the corresponding eigenvalue is $1$, so it actually just stays fixed in place. | 我们关心这一点，是因为它意味着 $A \cdot \mathbf{v} = \lambda \cdot \mathbf{v}$——你可以把它读作：向量 $\mathbf{v}$ 是 $A$ 的特征向量，在变换 $A$ 中保持在自身张成的空间上。本例中相应的特征值是 $1$，所以它实际上原地不动。 |

$$
\begin{aligned}
A \vec{\mathbf{v}} & =\lambda \vec{\mathbf{v}} \\ \rule{0pt}{2em}
A \vec{\mathbf{v}}-\lambda I \vec{\mathbf{v}} & =0 \\ \rule{0pt}{2em}
(A-\lambda I) \vec{\mathbf{v}} & =0 \\ \rule{0pt}{2em}
\operatorname{det}(A-\lambda I) & =0
\end{aligned}
$$

## 特征多项式（Characteristic polynomial）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| This expression, where we take a given matrix minus lambda times the identity and set its determinant equal to zero, thinking of lambda as a variable, is fundamental to eigenvalues. This is the kind of thing we mentioned in the introduction. If you didn't have a solid grasp of the determinant, and why it relates to linear systems having non-zero solutions, an expression like this would feel completely out of the blue. | 这个表达式——取给定矩阵减去 lambda 乘单位矩阵、再令其行列式为零，并把 lambda 当作变量——对特征值来说是根基性的东西。这就是我们在开篇提到的那类事：如果你对行列式没有扎实的把握，也不明白它为什么与「线性方程组有非零解」相关，这样的表达式就会显得完全没头没脑。 |
| Let's revisit the example from the start, with the matrix $\left[\begin{array}{c} 3 & 1\\ 0 & 2 \end{array}\right]$. | 我们回到开头的例子，矩阵 $\left[\begin{array}{c} 3 & 1\\ 0 & 2 \end{array}\right]$。 |
| To find if a value $\lambda$ is an eigenvalue, subtract it from the diagonals of this matrix, and compute the determinant. | 要判断某个值 $\lambda$ 是不是特征值，就把它从这个矩阵的对角线里减掉，然后算行列式。 |

$$
\operatorname{det}\left(\left[\begin{array}{cc}3-\lambda & \mathbb{1} \\ 0 & 2-\lambda\end{array}\right]\right)
$$
