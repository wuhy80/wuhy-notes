| Remember, the type of answer you get here can depend on whether or not the transformation squishes all of space into a lower dimension. That is, if it has zero determinant. ## 与基向量做点积会怎样？（What about dot products with basis vectors?）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| As a first pass, let's show an idea that is wrong, but in the right direction. | 作为第一次尝试，先来看一个错误、但方向正确的想法。 |
| The $x$-coordinate of this mystery input vector is what you get by taking its dot product with $\hat{\imath}$. Likewise the $y$-coordinate is what you get by dotting it with $\hat{\jmath}$. | 这个神秘输入向量的 $x$ 坐标，就是它与 $\hat{\imath}$ 做点积得到的数。同样，$y$ 坐标就是它与 $\hat{\jmath}$ 做点积得到的数。 |
| Maybe you hope that after the transformation, the dot products with the transformed version of the mystery vector with the transformed versions of the basis vectors will also be these coordinates $x$ and $y$. | 也许你希望：变换之后，「变换后的神秘向量」与「变换后的基向量」做点积，得到的也还是这些坐标 $x$ 和 $y$。 |
| That'd be fantastic, because we know the transformed versions of each of these vectors. There's just one problem with this: it's not at all true! For most linear transformations, the dot product before and after the transformation will be very different. | 那可就太妙了，因为我们知道这些向量各自变换后的样子。只是这有一个问题：它完全不对！对大多数线性变换来说，变换前后的点积会非常不同。 |
| For example, you could have two vectors generally pointing in the same direction, with a positive dot product, which get pulled away from each other during the transformation, in such a way that they then have a negative dot product. | 比如，两个向量可能大体指向同一方向、点积为正，却在变换中彼此拉开，以至于之后点积变成负的。 |
| Likewise, if things start off perpendicular, with dot product zero, like the two basis vectors, there's no guarantee that they will stay perpendicular after the transformation, preserving that zero dot product. | 同样，如果两个东西起初垂直、点积为零——就像那两个基向量——也没法保证它们在变换之后仍然垂直、保住那个零点积。 |
| In the example we were looking at, dot products certainly aren't preserved. They tend to get bigger, since most vectors are getting stretched. In fact, transformations which do preserve dot products are special enough to have their own name: Orthonormal transformations. These are the ones which leave all the basis vectors perpendicular to each other with unit lengths. | 在我们刚才看的例子里，点积显然没有被保持。它们倾向于变大，因为大多数向量都被拉伸了。事实上，确实保持点积的变换非常特别，特殊到有自己的名字：标准正交变换（orthonormal transformations）。这类变换让所有基向量保持相互垂直，且长度都是单位长度。 |
| You often think of these as rotation matrices. They correspond to rigid motion, with no stretching, squishing or morphing. | 你常把它们想成旋转矩阵。它们对应的是刚性运动：没有拉伸、压扁或变形。 |
| Solving a linear system with an orthonormal matrix is very easy: Since dot products are preserved, taking the dot product between the output vector and all the columns of your matrix will be the same as taking the dot products between the input vector and all the basis vectors, which is the same as finding the coordinates of the input vector. | 用标准正交矩阵解线性方程组非常容易：既然点积被保持，取「输出向量与矩阵各列」的点积，就等同于取「输入向量与各基向量」的点积，而这等同于找出输入向量的坐标。 |
| In that very special case, $x$ would be the dot product of the first column with the output vector, and $y$ would be the dot product of the second column with the output vector. | 在那个非常特殊的情形里，$x$ 就是第一列与输出向量的点积，$y$ 就是第二列与输出向量的点积。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 与基向量做点积会怎样？
> - **mystery** /ˈmɪstri/ —— 神秘的、未知的。this mystery input vector。
> - **preserve** /prɪˈzɜːv/ —— 保持。dot products certainly aren't preserved。
> - **orthonormal** /ˌɔːθəˈnɔːml/ —— 标准正交的。Orthonormal transformations。
> - **rigid** /ˈrɪdʒɪd/ —— 刚性的。rigid motion。
> - **morph** /mɔːf/ —— 变形。stretching, squishing or morphing。
> - **fantastic** /fænˈtæstɪk/ —— 极好的、妙极了。That'd be fantastic。
> - *first pass* —— 第一次尝试。As a first pass。
> - *pull away from* —— 拉开、拽离。get pulled away from each other。

## 一个更好的思路（A Better Approach）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Now, even though this idea breaks down for most linear systems, it points us in the direction of something to look for: Is there an alternate geometric understanding for the coordinates of our input vector which remains unchanged after the transformation? | 现在，尽管这个想法对大多数线性方程组都会失效，它却给我们指出了该去找什么：对输入向量的坐标，有没有另一种几何理解，在变换之后保持不变？ |
| If your mind has been mulling over determinants, you might think of this clever idea: Take the parallelogram defined by the first basis vector, $\hat{\imath}$, and the mystery input vector $\left[\begin{array}{c} x \\ y \end{array}\right]$. The area of this parallelogram is its base, $1$, times the height perpendicular to that base, which is the $y$-coordinate of our input vector. | 如果你的脑子一直在琢磨行列式，你可能会想到这个聪明的点子：取由第一个基向量 $\hat{\imath}$ 和神秘输入向量 $\left[\begin{array}{c} x \\ y \end{array}\right]$ 确定的平行四边形。这个平行四边形的面积，等于它的底 $1$ 乘以垂直于底的高——也就是我们输入向量的 $y$ 坐标。 |
| The area of this parallelogram is sort of a screwy roundabout way to describe the vector's $y$-coordinate; it's a wacky way to talk about coordinates, but run with us. | 用平行四边形的面积来描述向量的 $y$ 坐标，是个挺怪异的迂回做法；这么谈坐标是够怪的，但请跟我们一路走到底。 |
| Actually, to be more accurate, you should think of the signed area of this parallelogram, in the sense described by the determinant video. That way, a vector with negative $y$-coordinate would correspond to a negative area for this parallelogram. | 说得更准确些，你应该想的是这个平行四边形的带符号面积（signed area），就是行列式那一章讲的那种意义。这样，一个 $y$ 坐标为负的向量，就对应这个平行四边形的一个负面积。 |
| Symmetrically, if you look at the parallelogram spanned by the vector and the second basis vector, $\hat{\jmath}$, its area will be the $x$-coordinate of the vector. Again, it's a strange way to represent the $x$-coordinate, but you'll see what it buys us in a moment. | 对称地，如果你看由这个向量和第二个基向量 $\hat{\jmath}$ 张成的平行四边形，它的面积将是这个向量的 $x$ 坐标。再说一遍，这是一种表示 $x$ 坐标的奇怪方式，但你马上就会看到它给我们换来什么。 |
| Here's what this would look like in three-dimensions: Ordinarily the way you might think of one of a vector's coordinates, say its $z$-coordinate, would be to take its dot product with the third standard basis vector, $\hat{k}$. But instead, consider the parallelepiped it creates with the other two basis vectors, $\hat{\imath}$ and $\hat{\jmath}$. | 在三维里这会是什么样子：通常，你可能这样想向量的某个坐标，比如它的 $z$ 坐标——取它与第三个标准基向量 $\hat{k}$ 的点积。但换一种方式，考虑它与其他两个基向量 $\hat{\imath}$ 和 $\hat{\jmath}$ 一起生成的平行六面体。 |
| If you think of the square with area $1$ spanned by $\hat{\imath}$ and $\hat{\jmath}$ as the base of this guy, its volume is the same as its height, which is the third coordinate of our vector. | 如果你把由 $\hat{\imath}$ 和 $\hat{\jmath}$ 张成的那个面积为 $1$ 的正方形想成这个家伙的底，那么它的体积就等于它的高——正是我们向量的第三个坐标。 |
| Likewise, the wacky way to think about any other coordinate of this vector is to form the parallelepiped between this vector and all the basis vectors other than the one you're looking for, and get its volume. | 同样，思考这个向量任何其他坐标的怪办法是：在这个向量与「除你正在找的那个之外的所有基向量」之间生成平行六面体，然后取它的体积。 |
| Or, rather, we should talk about the signed volume of these parallelepipeds, in the sense described in the determinant video, where the order in which you list the three vectors matters and you're using the right-hand rule. That way negative coordinates still make sense. | 或者更确切地说，我们应该谈这些平行六面体的带符号体积，就是行列式那一章讲的那种意义：列出三个向量的顺序很重要，并且用的是右手定则。这样一来，负坐标仍然说得通。 |

## 把它带进输出空间（Follow this into the output space.）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Okay, so why think of coordinates as areas and volumes like this? As you apply some matrix transformation, the areas of the parallelograms don't stay the same, they may get scaled up or down. **But**, and this is a key idea of determinants, all these areas get scaled by the same amount. Namely, the determinant of our transformation matrix. | 好，那为什么要把坐标想成这样的面积和体积？在你应用某个矩阵变换时，这些平行四边形的面积不会保持不变，它们可能被放大或缩小。**但是**——这是行列式的一个关键想法——所有这些面积都被缩放同一个倍数。具体地说，就是我们变换矩阵的行列式。 |
| For example, if you look the parallelogram spanned by the vector where your first basis vector lands, which is the first column of the matrix, and the transformed version of $\left[\begin{array}{c} x \\ y \end{array}\right]$, what is its area? | 比如，如果你看「由你的第一个基向量的落点（即矩阵第一列）与 $\left[\begin{array}{c} x \\ y \end{array}\right]$ 的变换后版本」张成的那个平行四边形，它的面积是多少？ |
| Well, this is the transformed version of that parallelogram we were looking at earlier, whose area was the $y$-coordinate of the mystery input vector. So its area will be the determinant of the transformation multiplied by that value. | 嗯，这正是我们先前看的那个平行四边形变换后的版本——它的面积曾是神秘输入向量的 $y$ 坐标。所以它的面积将是变换的行列式乘以那个值。 |
| The $y$-coordinate of our mystery input vector is the area of this parallelogram, spanned by the first column of the matrix and the output vector, divided by the determinant of the full transformation. | 我们神秘输入向量的 $y$ 坐标，就是「由矩阵第一列与输出向量张成的这个平行四边形的面积」除以「整个变换的行列式」。 |

$$
y=\frac{\text { Area }}{\operatorname{det}(A)}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| And how do you get this area? Well we know the coordinates for where the mystery input vector lands, that's the whole point of a linear system of equations. So create a matrix whose first column is the same as that of our matrix, and whose second column is the output vector, and take its determinant. | 那这块面积怎么求？嗯，我们知道神秘输入向量落在哪儿的坐标——这正是一整个线性方程组的意义所在。所以，构造一个矩阵：第一列与我们原来的矩阵相同，第二列是输出向量，然后取它的行列式。 |
| Look at that; just using data from the output of the transformation, namely the columns of the matrix and the coordinates of our output vector, we can recover the $y$-coordinate of our mystery input vector. | 看啊：只凭变换输出的数据——也就是矩阵的列和输出向量的坐标——我们就能还原出神秘输入向量的 $y$ 坐标。 |
| Likewise, the same idea can get you the $x$-coordinate. Look at that parallelogram we defined early which encodes the $x$-coordinate of the mystery input vector, spanned by the input vector and $\hat{\jmath}$. The transformed version of this guy is spanned by the output vector and the second column of the matrix, and its area will have been multiplied by the determinant of the matrix. | 同样，同一个点子也能拿到 $x$ 坐标。看我们早先定义的那个平行四边形，它编码了神秘输入向量的 $x$ 坐标，由输入向量和 $\hat{\jmath}$ 张成。这个家伙变换后的版本，由输出向量和矩阵第二列张成，而且它的面积会被乘上矩阵的行列式。 |
| The $x$-coordinate of our mystery input vector is this area divided by the determinant of the transformation. Similar to what we did before, you can compute the area of that output parallelogram by creating a new matrix whose first column is the output vector, and whose second column is the same as the original matrix. | 我们神秘输入向量的 $x$ 坐标，就是这个面积除以变换的行列式。与之前类似，你可以构造一个新矩阵：第一列是输出向量，第二列与原矩阵相同——用它的行列式算出那块输出平行四边形的面积。 |
| Again, just using data from the output space, the numbers we see in our original linear system, we can recover the $x$-coordinate of our mystery input vector. This formula for finding the solutions to a linear system of equations is known as Cramer's rule. | 再一次，只凭输出空间的数据——我们原始线性方程组里看到的那些数——我们就能还原出神秘输入向量的 $x$ 坐标。这个求线性方程组解的公式，就是著名的克莱姆法则。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 把它带进输出空间
> - **namely** /ˈneɪmli/ —— 也就是、具体地说。Namely, the determinant of our transformation matrix。
> - **recover** /rɪˈkʌvə/ —— 还原出、找回。we can recover the $y$-coordinate。
> - **encode** /ɪnˈkəʊd/ —— 编码。encodes the $x$-coordinate of the mystery input vector。
> - **divide** /dɪˈvaɪd/ —— 除、除以。divided by the determinant of the full transformation。
> - **guy** /ɡaɪ/ —— 家伙（口语指代）。The transformed version of this guy。
> - *scale up or down* —— 按比例放大或缩小。they may get scaled up or down。

## 合理性检验（Sanity check）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Here, just to sanity check ourselves, plug in the numbers here. The determinant of that top altered matrix is $4+2=6$ and the bottom determinant is $2$, so the $x$-coordinate should be $3$. | 这里，光做一遍合理性检验（sanity check）：把数代进来。上面那个改动过的矩阵，行列式是 $4+2=6$；下面的行列式是 $2$，所以 $x$ 坐标应该是 $3$。 |

$$
x=\frac{\text { Area }}{\operatorname{det}(A)}=\frac{\operatorname{det}\left(\left[\begin{array}{rr}
4 & -1 \\
2 & 1
\end{array}\right]\right)}{\operatorname{det}\left(\left[\begin{array}{rr}
2 & -1 \\
0 & 1
\end{array}\right]\right)}=\frac{(4)(1)-(-1)(2)}{(2)(1)-(-1)(0)}=\frac{6}{2}=3
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| And indeed, looking back at that input vector we started with, it's $x$-coordinate is $3$. | 的确，回头看那个我们出发时的输入向量，它的 $x$ 坐标正是 $3$。 |
| Likewise, Cramer's rule suggests the $y$-coordinate should be $\frac{4}{2} = 2$ and that is indeed the $y$-coordinate of the input vector we started with here. | 同样，克莱姆法则提示 $y$ 坐标应该是 $\frac{4}{2} = 2$，而这正是我们这里出发的那个输入向量的 $y$ 坐标。 |

$$
y=\frac{\text { Area }}{\operatorname{det}(A)}=\frac{\operatorname{det}\left(\left[\begin{array}{ll}
2 & 4 \\
0 & 2
\end{array}\right]\right)}{\operatorname{det}\left(\left[\begin{array}{rr}
2 & -1 \\
0 & 1
\end{array}\right]\right)}=\frac{(2)(2)-(4)(0)}{(2)(1)-(-1)(0)}=\frac{4}{2}=2
$$

> - **sanity check** —— 合理性检验、常识校验。just to sanity check ourselves。
> - **plug in** —— 代入。plug in the numbers here。
> - **altered** /ˈɔːltəd/ —— 改动过的。that top altered matrix。
> - **indeed** /ɪnˈdiːd/ —— 确实。And indeed, looking back at that input vector。
> - *look back at* —— 回头看。looking back at that input vector we started with。
