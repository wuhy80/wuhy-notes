## 变换的复合（Composition of Transformations）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Oftentimes, you find yourself wanting to describe the effects of applying one linear transformation, then applying another. For example, maybe you want to describe what happens when you rotate the plane $90^\circ$ counterclockwise, then to apply a shear. The overall effect here, from start to finish, is another linear transformation, distinct from the rotation and the shear. This new linear transformation is commonly called the "composition" of the two separate transformations we applied. | 很多时候，你会发现自己想描述的是「先做一个线性变换，再做另一个」的效果。比如，你想描述「先把平面逆时针旋转 $90^\circ$，再来一个剪切」会发生什么。这从头到尾的整体效果，本身又是另一个线性变换，不同于那个旋转、也不同于那个剪切。这个新的线性变换，通常叫做我们施加的那两个变换的「复合」（composition）。 |
| Like any linear transformation, it can be described with a matrix all of its own by following $\hat i$ and $\hat j$. | 像任何线性变换一样，只要跟着 $\hat i$ 和 $\hat j$ 走，它就能用属于自己的一个矩阵来描述。 |
| In this example, the ultimate landing spot for $\hat i$ after both transformations is $\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix}$, so make this the first column of a matrix. Likewise, $\hat j$ ultimately ends up at $\begin{bmatrix}-1\\0\end{bmatrix}$, so make this the second column of a matrix. This new matrix captures the overall effect of applying a rotation then a shear, but as one single action rather than two successive ones. | 在这个例子里，经过两次变换后 $\hat i$ 的最终落点是 $\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix}$，就把它作为矩阵的第一列；同样，$\hat j$ 最终停在 $\begin{bmatrix}-1\\0\end{bmatrix}$，就把它作为矩阵的第二列。这个新矩阵一举捕捉到「先旋转后剪切」的整体效果——是作为一次单独的动作，而不是两次相继的动作。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 复合
> - **composition** /ˌkɒmpəˈzɪʃn/ —— 复合。本课的核心词，指「先做这个、再做那个」的整体变换。
> - **counterclockwise** /ˌkaʊntəˈklɒkwaɪz/ —— 逆时针地。
> - **distinct** /dɪˈstɪŋkt/ —— 不同的、有区别的。distinct from the rotation and the shear。
> - **ultimate** /ˈʌltɪmət/ —— 最终的。the ultimate landing spot。
> - **capture** /ˈkæptʃə/ —— 捕捉、刻画。captures the overall effect。
> - **successive** /səkˈsesɪv/ —— 相继的。two successive ones。
> - *from start to finish* —— 从头到尾。
> - *all of its own* —— 属于它自己专属的。

## 复合即乘法（Composition is Multiplication）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Here's one way to think about that new matrix. If you were to take some vector and pump it through the rotation then the shear, the long way to compute where it lands by first multiplying on the left by the rotation matrix, then multiplying the result on the left by the shear matrix. This is, numerically speaking, what it _means_ to apply a rotation then a shear to a given vector. But whatever you get should be the same as just multiplying this new composition matrix that we found by the vector, no matter what vector you chose, since this new matrix is supposed to capture the same overall effect as the rotation-then-shear action. | 关于那个新矩阵，有一种思考方式。如果你取某个向量，先把它灌过旋转、再灌过剪切，那么「笨办法」是：先从左边乘旋转矩阵，再把结果从左边乘剪切矩阵，算出它落在哪里。从数字上说，这就是「对给定向量先旋转后剪切」的*含义*。但无论你选什么向量，得到的结果都应该等于直接用我们找到的那个新复合矩阵去乘这个向量——因为这个新矩阵要捕捉的，正是「先旋转后剪切」这个动作的整体效果。 |

## 结合律（Associativity）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| I remember when I first took linear algebra, there was a homework problem that asked to prove that matrix multiplication is associative. That means if you have three matrices, A, B and C, and multiply them all, it shouldn't matter if you first compute A times B, then multiply the result by C, or if you first multiply B times C, then multiply the result by the matrix A on the left. In other words, does it matter where you put the parentheses. | 我记得自己第一次上线性代数课时，有道作业题要求证明矩阵乘法满足结合律（associative）。意思是：如果你有三个矩阵 A、B、C，要把它们全乘起来，那么先算 A 乘 B、再把结果乘 C，还是先算 B 乘 C、再把结果从左边乘矩阵 A，不应有区别。换句话说，括号放哪儿要紧吗？ |

$$
(AB)C\stackrel{?}{=}A(BC)
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Now, if you try to work through this numerically, like I did back then, it's horrible! And unenlightening for that matter. But when you think of matrix multiplication as applying one transformation after another, this property is trivial, can you see why? | 好吧，如果你试图从数字上推导它——就像我当年那样——那可太惨了！而且说实话毫无启发性。但当你把矩阵乘法想成「先做一个变换、再做另一个」，这条性质就是显而易见的。你能看出为什么吗？ |
| What it's saying is that if you first apply (C then B), then A, it's the same as applying C, (then B then A). I mean, there's nothing to prove! You're just applying three things one after the other, all in the same order! | 它说的是：如果你先施加（先 C 后 B）、再施加 A，和先施加 C、再施加（先 B 后 A），是同一回事。我是说，根本没什么可证的！你不过是把三样东西按同一个先后顺序一个接一个地施加而已！ |
| This might feel like cheating, but it's not. It's an honest-to-goodness proof that matrix multiplication is associative. And even better than that, it's a _good explanation_ for why that property should be true. | 这可能感觉像在耍赖，但并不是。这是一个如假包换的、证明矩阵乘法满足结合律的证明。而且比这更好的是，它还*解释了为什么*这条性质理应为真。 |
