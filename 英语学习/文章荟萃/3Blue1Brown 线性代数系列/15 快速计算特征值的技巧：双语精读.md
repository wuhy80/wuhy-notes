## 开篇（Introduction）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| This is a video for anyone who already knows what eigenvalues and eigenvectors are, and who might enjoy a quick way to compute them in the case of 2x2 matrices. If you're unfamiliar with eigenvalues, take a look at the previous chapter which introduces them. | 这支视频面向所有已经知道什么是特征值（eigenvalues）与特征向量（eigenvectors）、而且可能乐于见到 $2\times2$ 矩阵情形下一种快速算法的人。如果你对特征值还不熟悉，请先看看介绍它们的上一章。 |
| You can skip ahead if you just want to see the trick, but if possible we'd like you to rediscover it for yourself, so let's lay down a little background. | 如果你只想直接看这个技巧，可以跳到后面；但如果可以的话，我们希望你由自己重新发现它，所以我们先铺一点点背景。 |
| As a quick reminder, if the effect of a linear transformation on a given vector is to scale it by some constant, we call that vector an eigenvector of the transformation, and we call the relevant scaling factor the corresponding eigenvalue, often denoted with the letter lambda, $\lambda$. | 快速回顾一下：如果线性变换对某个给定向量的作用是把它按某个常数缩放，我们就把这个向量叫作该变换的特征向量，并把相应的缩放因子叫作对应的特征值，通常记作字母 lambda，$\lambda$。 |
| When you write this as an equation and rearrange a little, what you see is that if a number $\lambda$ is an eigenvalue of a matrix $A$, then the matrix $(A - \lambda I)$ must send some nonzero vector, namely the corresponding eigenvector, to the zero vector, which in turn means the determinant of this modified matrix must be $0$. | 当你把它写成一个方程并稍作整理，你会看到：如果数 $\lambda$ 是矩阵 $A$ 的特征值，那么矩阵 $(A - \lambda I)$ 必然会把某个非零向量——也就是相应的特征向量——送到零向量上，而这反过来意味着这个改动过的矩阵的行列式必须是 $0$。 |

$$
\begin{aligned}
A \vec{\mathbf{v}} & =\lambda I \vec{\mathbf{v}} \\ \rule{0pt}{1.25em}
A \vec{\mathbf{v}}-\lambda I \vec{\mathbf{v}} & =\vec{\mathbf{0}} \\ \rule{0pt}{1.25em}
(A-\lambda I) \vec{\mathbf{v}} & =\vec{\mathbf{0}} \\ \rule{0pt}{1.25em}
\operatorname{det}(A-\lambda I) &= 0
\end{aligned}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| That's a bit of a mouthful, but again, we're assuming this is review for anyone reading. | 这话有点绕口，但再说一遍，我们假设这对任何读者都是复习。 |
| The usual way to compute eigenvalues and how most students are taught to carry it out, is to subtract a variable lambda off the diagonals of a matrix and solve for when the determinant equals $0$. For example, when finding the eigenvalues of the matrix $\left[\begin{array}{ll} 3 & 1 \\ 4 & 1 \end{array}\right]$ this looks like: | 计算特征值的常规做法——也是大多数学生被教的做法——是从矩阵的对角线里减去一个变量 lambda，然后解「行列式何时等于 $0$」。比如，求矩阵 $\left[\begin{array}{ll} 3 & 1 \\ 4 & 1 \end{array}\right]$ 的特征值时，写出来是这样： |

$$
\operatorname{det}\left(\left[\begin{array}{cc}
3-\lambda & 1 \\
4 & 1-\lambda
\end{array}\right]\right)=(3-\lambda)(1-\lambda)-(1)(4) = 0
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| This always involves a few steps to expand this and simplify it to get a clean quadratic polynomial, known as the "characteristic polynomial" of the matrix. The eigenvalues are the roots of this polynomial, so to find them you apply the quadratic formula, which typically requires one or two more steps of simplification. | 这总是免不了几步展开和化简，才能得到一个干净利落的二次多项式——也就是矩阵的「特征多项式」（characteristic polynomial）。特征值是这个多项式的根，所以求特征值你要套用二次公式（quadratic formula），这通常又需要一两步额外的化简。 |

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{det}\left(\left[\begin{array}{cc}
3-\lambda & 1 \\
4 & 1-\lambda
\end{array}\right]\right) & =(3-\lambda)(1-\lambda)-(1)(4) \\ \rule{0pt}{1.5em}
& =\left(3-4 \lambda+\lambda^2\right)-4 \\ \rule{0pt}{1.5em}
& =\lambda^2-4 \lambda-1=0 \\ \rule{0pt}{2.0em}
\lambda_1, \lambda_2 & = \frac{4 \pm \sqrt{4^2-4(1)(-1)}}{2} =2 \pm \sqrt{5}
\end{aligned}
$$

|---|---|
| This process isn't _terrible_, but at least for 2x2 matrices, there's a much more direct way to get at this answer. If you want to rediscover this trick, there are three relevant facts you'll need to know, each of which is worth knowing in its own right and can help with other problem-solving. | 这个过程谈不上*糟糕*，但至少对 $2\times2$ 矩阵而言，有一条直接得多的路能通向这个答案。如果你想自己重新发现这个技巧，需要知道三个相关的事实，每一条本身都值得知道，而且对其他解题也有帮助。 |
| 1. The trace of a matrix, which is the sum of these two diagonal entries, is equal to the sum of the eigenvalues. | 一、矩阵的迹（trace），也就是这两个对角元之和，等于特征值之和。 |

$$
\operatorname{tr}\left(\left[\begin{array}{cc}a & b \\ c & d\end{array}\right]\right)=a+d=\lambda_1+\lambda_2
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Or another way to phrase it, more useful for our purposes, is that the mean of the two eigenvalues is the same as the mean of these two diagonal entries. | 换一种说法——对我们的目的更有用的一种：两个特征值的均值，等于这两个对角元的均值。 |

$$
\frac{1}{2} \operatorname{tr}\left(\left[\begin{array}{cc}a & b \\ c & d\end{array}\right]\right)=\frac{a+d}{2}=\frac{\lambda_1+\lambda_2}{2}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| 2. The determinant of a matrix, our usual $ad-bc$ formula, equals the product of the two eigenvalues. | 二、矩阵的行列式——我们熟悉的 $ad-bc$ 公式——等于两个特征值的乘积。 |

$$
\operatorname{det}\left(\left[\begin{array}{ll}a & b \\ c & d\end{array}\right]\right)=a d-b c=\lambda_1 \lambda_2
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| This should make sense if you understand that an eigenvalue describes how much an operator stretches space in a particular direction and that the determinant describes how much an operator scales areas (or volumes). | 如果你明白「特征值描述的是一个算子（operator）在特定方向上把空间拉伸多少，而行列式描述的是一个算子把面积（或体积）缩放多少」，这一条就应该说得通。 |
| 3. (We'll get to this...) | 三、（我们马上会讲到这个……） |
| Before getting to the third fact, notice how you can essentially read these first two values out of the matrix. Take this matrix $\left[\begin{array}{ll}8 & 4 \\ 2 & 6\end{array}\right]$ as an example. Straight away you can know that the mean of the eigenvalues is the same as the mean of $8$ and $6$, which is $7$. | 在讲第三个事实之前，注意你基本上可以把前两个值直接从矩阵里读出来。拿矩阵 $\left[\begin{array}{ll}8 & 4 \\ 2 & 6\end{array}\right]$ 作例子：你立刻就能知道，特征值的均值等于 $8$ 和 $6$ 的均值，也就是 $7$。 |

$$
m = \frac{\lambda_1 + \lambda_2}{2} = 7
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Likewise, most linear algebra students are well-practiced at finding the determinant, which in this case is $8 \cdot 6 - 4 \cdot 2 = 48 - 8$, so you know the product of our two eigenvalues is $40$. | 同样地，大多数线性代数学生对求行列式已经练得滚瓜烂熟——本例中是 $8 \cdot 6 - 4 \cdot 2 = 48 - 8$——于是你知道我们两个特征值的乘积是 $40$。 |

$$
p = \lambda_1 \lambda_2= 40
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Take a moment to see how you can derive what will be our third relevant fact, which is how to recover two numbers when you know their mean and product. | 花点时间看看，你能不能推导出我们的第三个相关事实：已知两个数的均值和乘积，如何把这两个数找回来。 |
| Focus on this example. You know the two values are evenly spaced around $7$, so they look like $7$ plus or minus something; let's call it $d$ for distance. | 盯住这个例子。你知道这两个值均匀分布在 $7$ 两侧，所以它们形如 $7$ 加减某个数；就叫它 $d$ 吧，代表距离（distance）。 |
| You also know that the product of these two numbers is $40$. | 你还知道这两个数的乘积是 $40$。 |

$$
40 = (7+d)(7-d)
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| To find $d$, notice how this product expands nicely as a difference of squares. This lets you cleanly solve for $d$: | 要求 $d$，注意这个乘积按平方差（difference of squares）展开得非常漂亮。这让你能干净利落地解出 $d$： |

$$
\begin{aligned}
40 & = (7+d)(7-d) \\ \rule{0pt}{1.25em}
40 & = 7^2-d^2 \\ \rule{0pt}{1.25em}
d^2 & =7^2-40 \\ \rule{0pt}{1.25em}
d^2 & =9 \\ \rule{0pt}{1.25em}
d & =3
\end{aligned}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| In other words, the two values for this very specific example work out to be $4$ and $10$. | 换句话说，对这个非常具体的例子，两个值算出来是 $4$ 和 $10$。 |
| But our goal is a quick trick and you wouldn't want to think through this each time, so let's wrap up what we just did in a general formula. | 但我们的目标是一个快速技巧，你不会想每次都把这套推理走一遍，所以我们把刚才做的事打包成一个一般公式。 |
| For any mean, $m$, and product, $p$, the distance squared is always going to be $m^2 - p$. This gives the third key fact, which is that when two numbers have a mean and a product, you can write those two numbers as $m \pm \sqrt{m^2 - p}$. | 对任何均值 $m$ 与乘积 $p$，距离的平方总是 $m^2 - p$。这就给出第三个关键事实：当两个数有均值和乘积时，你可以把这两个数写成 $m \pm \sqrt{m^2 - p}$。 |
| This is decently fast to rederive on the fly if you ever forget it and it's essentially just a rephrasing of the difference of squares formula, but even still it's a fact worth memorizing. In fact, Tim from Acapella Science wrote us a quick jingle to make it a little more memorable. | 就算你忘了，现场重新推导它也相当快，而且它本质上只是平方差公式的另一种说法；尽管如此，它仍是一个值得记住的事实。事实上，来自 Acapella Science 的 Tim 给我们写了一段顺口的小调（jingle），让它更好记一些。 |

So again, start writing out the formula, but writing $5$ in place of $m$: | 这里再用矩阵 $\left[\begin{array}{ll}2 & 7 \\ 1 & 8\end{array}\right]$ 试一次。这次特征值的均值等于 $2$ 和 $8$ 的均值，即 $5$。同样地，先写出公式，但把 $m$ 的位置写成 $5$： |

$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| You see what we mean about how you can basically just write down the eigenvalues while staring at the matrix? It's typically just the tiniest bit of simplifying at the end. | 你明白我们说的「盯着矩阵就基本能把特征值直接写下来」是什么意思了吧？通常只是最后要做一丁点化简。 |
| What are the eigenvalue(s) of the matrix $\left[\begin{array}{ll}2 & 3 \\ 2 & 4\end{array}\right]$? | （译注：原文此处是一道互动测验的题干，四个候选为 $3 \pm \sqrt{11}$、$3 \pm \sqrt{7}$、$3 \pm \sqrt{2}$、$3 \pm \sqrt{10}$，正确答案是 $3 \pm \sqrt{7}$；原题在页面上附有如下的解析推导。）矩阵 $\left[\begin{array}{ll}2 & 3 \\ 2 & 4\end{array}\right]$ 的特征值是什么？ |

$$
\begin{aligned}
A & =\left[\begin{array}{ll}
2 & 3 \\
2 & 4
\end{array}\right] \\ \rule{0pt}{1.5em}
m & =\frac{2+4}{2}=3 \\ \rule{0pt}{1.25em}
p & =\operatorname{det}(A)=8-6=2 \\ \rule{0pt}{1.25em}
\lambda_{1,} \lambda_2 & =3 \pm \sqrt{3^2-2} \\ \rule{0pt}{1.25em}
& =3 \pm \sqrt{7}
\end{aligned}
$$

| The mean of the diagonal in all three cases is $0$, so the mean of the eigenvalues in all cases is $0$, making our formula look especially simple. | 三种情形里对角线的均值都是 $0$，所以各种情形下特征值的均值都是 $0$，这让我们的公式显得格外简单。 |
| What about the products of the eigenvalues, the determinants? For the first one, it's $0 - 1$ or $-1$. The second also looks like $0 - 1$, though it takes a moment more to see because of the complex numbers. And the final one looks like $-1 - 0$. So in all three cases, the eigenvalues are $±1$. | 那么特征值的乘积，也就是行列式呢？第一个是 $0 - 1$，即 $-1$。第二个看起来也是 $0 - 1$，只是因为含有复数（complex numbers），要多花一点时间才能看出来。最后一个是 $-1 - 0$。所以三种情形下，特征值都是 $±1$。 |
| Although in this case you don't even really need the formula to find two values evenly spaced around zero whose product is $-1$. | 不过在这个例子里，要找出「均匀分布在零两侧、乘积为 $-1$」的两个数，你其实连公式都不太需要。 |
| If you're curious, in the context of quantum mechanics, these matrices correspond with observations you might make about the spin of a particle in the $x$, $y$ or $z$-direction, and the fact that these eigenvalues are $±1$ corresponds with the idea that the values for the spin you would observe would be entirely in one direction or entirely in another, as opposed to something continuously ranging in between. | 满足一下好奇心：在量子力学的语境里，这些矩阵对应着你对粒子沿 $x$、$y$ 或 $z$ 方向自旋（spin）可能做的观测；而这些特征值是 $±1$ 这一事实，对应着「你观测到的自旋值要么完全朝一个方向、要么完全朝另一个方向，而不是在两者之间连续取值」这一观念。 |
| Maybe you'd wonder how exactly this works, and why you'd use 2x2 matrices with complex numbers to describe spin in three dimensions. Those would be valid questions, just beyond the scope of what we're talking about here. | 你也许会想问：这究竟是怎么运作的？为什么在三维里描述自旋要用带复数的 $2\times2$ 矩阵？这些是好问题，只是超出了我们这里谈论的范围。 |
| You know it's funny, this section is supposed to be about a case where 2x2 matrices are not just toy examples or homework problems, but actually come up in practice, and quantum mechanics is great for that. However, the example kind of undercuts the whole point we're trying to make. For these specific matrices, if you use the traditional method with characteristic polynomials, it's essentially just as fast, and might actually faster. | 说来有趣：这一节本该展示一个 $2\times2$ 矩阵不只是玩具例子或课后题、而是真会在实践中出现的场景，量子力学正是绝佳素材。然而，这个例子却有点拆了我们想说的台。对这几个特定的矩阵，如果你用特征多项式的传统方法，速度基本上一样快，甚至可能更快。 |
| For the first matrix, the relevant determinant directly gives you a characteristic polynomial of $\lambda^2 - 1$, which clearly has roots of plus and minus $1$. Same answer for the second matrix. And for the last, forget about doing any computations, traditional or otherwise, it's already a diagonal matrix, so those diagonal entries are the eigenvalues! | 对第一个矩阵，相关的行列式直接给你特征多项式 $\lambda^2 - 1$，它的根显然是正负 $1$。第二个矩阵答案相同。至于最后一个，管它传统方法还是别的什么算法，统统免了——它已经是对角矩阵，那些对角元就是特征值！ |
| However, the example is not totally lost on our cause, where you would actually feel the speed up is the more general case where you take a linear combination of these matrices and then try to compute the eigenvalues. | 不过，这个例子对我们的主张并非全然无用：真正能让你感到提速的，是更一般的情形——把这些矩阵做一个线性组合（linear combination），然后再去算特征值。 |
| We might write this as $a$ times the first one, plus $b$ times the second, plus $c$ times the third. | More specifically, you should assume this vector is normalized, meaning $a^2 + b^2 + c^2 = 1$. ## 与二次公式的关系（Relation to the quadratic formula）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| To be clear, using the mean-product formula is the same thing as finding roots of the characteristic polynomial; it has to be. In fact, this formula is a nice way to think about solving quadratics in general and some viewers of the channel may recognize this. | 说清楚一点：使用均值-乘积公式与求特征多项式的根，是一回事；它不可能不是。实际上，这个公式为一般性地解二次式提供了一个很好的思路，频道的部分观众可能认得出来。 |
| If you're trying to find the roots of a quadratic given its coefficients, you can think of that as a puzzle where you know the sum of two values, and you know their product, and you're trying to recover the original two values. | 如果你要在给定系数的情况下求某个二次式的根，你可以把它想成一道谜题：你知道两个值的和，也知道它们的乘积，试着把原来那两个数找回来。 |
| Specifically, if the polynomial is normalized so that the leading coefficient is $1$, then the mean of the roots is $-1/2$ times the linear coefficient, for the example on screen that would be $5$, and the product of the roots is even easier, it's just the constant term. From there, you'd apply the mean product formula to find the roots. | 具体而言，如果多项式被归一化到首项系数（leading coefficient）为 $1$，那么根的均值是 $-1/2$ 乘一次项系数——屏幕上这个例子里就是 $5$——而根的乘积更容易，就是常数项（constant term）。接下来，你用均值-乘积公式去求根。 |
| Now, you could think of the mean product formula as being a lighter-weight reframing of the traditional quadratic formula. But the real advantage is that the terms have more meaning to them. | 你可以把均值-乘积公式看作传统二次公式的一个更轻量的重新表述。但真正的优势在于：这些项承载着更多的含义。 |
| The whole point of this eigenvalue trick is that because you can read out the mean and product directly from the matrix, you can jump straight to writing down the roots without thinking about what the characteristic polynomial looks like. But to do that, we need a version of the quadratic formula where the terms carry some kind of meaning. | 这个特征值技巧的全部要点在于：因为均值和乘积可以直接从矩阵里读出，你可以直接跳到把根写下来，不用去想特征多项式长什么样。但要做到这一点，我们需要一个「各项都带有某种含义」版本的二次公式。 |
| What are the eigenvalue(s) of the matrix $\left[\begin{array}{ll}3 & 1 \\ 5 & 7\end{array}\right]$? | （译注：原文此处是一道互动测验的题干，四个候选为 $5$、$3$ 和 $7$、$2$ 和 $8$、$4$ 和 $6$，正确答案是 $2$ 和 $8$；原题在页面上附有如下的解析推导。）矩阵 $\left[\begin{array}{ll}3 & 1 \\ 5 & 7\end{array}\right]$ 的特征值是什么？ |

$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_1, \lambda_2 & =5 \pm \sqrt{5^2-16} \\ \rule{0pt}{1.25em}
& =5 \pm \sqrt{25-16} \\ \rule{0pt}{1.25em}
& =5 \pm \sqrt{9} \\ \rule{0pt}{1.25em}
& =2,8
\end{aligned}
$$
