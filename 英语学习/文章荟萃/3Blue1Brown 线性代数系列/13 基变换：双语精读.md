## 开篇（Introduction）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| If we have a vector sitting here in 2d space, we have a standard way to describe it with coordinates. In this case, the vector has coordinates $\left[\begin{array}{c} 3 \\ 2  \end{array}\right]$, which means going from its tail to its tip involves moving $3$ units to the right, and $2$ units up. | 如果我们有一个停在二维空间里的向量，我们有一套描述它的标准坐标方法。在这个例子里，向量的坐标是 $\left[\begin{array}{c} 3 \\ 2  \end{array}\right]$，意思是从尾走到箭头，需要向右移动 $3$ 个单位，再向上移动 $2$ 个单位。 |
| Now, the more linear-algebra oriented way to describe coordinates is to think of each of those numbers as a scalar, a thing that stretches or squishes vectors. You think of the first coordinate as scaling $\hat{\imath}$, the vector with length one pointing to the right, while the second coordinate scales $\hat{\jmath}$, the vector with length one pointing straight up. The tip-to-tail sum of these two scaled vectors is what the coordinates are meant to describe. | 而用更线性代数的方式描述坐标，是把每个数都想成一个标量（scalar）——一种拉伸或压扁向量的东西。你把第一个坐标想成缩放 $\hat{\imath}$（长度为 1、指向右边的向量），第二个坐标缩放 $\hat{\jmath}$（长度为 1、指向正上方的向量）。这两个缩放后的向量头尾相接的和，正是坐标想要描述的东西。 |
| You can think of these two special vectors as encapsulating all the implicit assumptions of our coordinate system: the fact that the first number indicates rightward motion, that the second indicates upward motion, exactly how far a unit of distance is; all of that is tied up in the choice of $\hat{\imath}$ and $\hat{\jmath}$ as the vectors which our scalar coordinates are meant scale. The specific way that we translate a vector into a pair of numbers is called a "coordinate system", and these two special vectors $\hat{\imath}$ and $\hat{\jmath}$ are called the "basis" vectors of standard coordinate system. | 你可以把这两个特殊的向量想成封装了我们坐标系统全部隐含假设的东西：第一个数表示向右运动、第二个数表示向上运动、一个单位距离究竟有多远——这一切都系于「选择 $\hat{\imath}$ 和 $\hat{\jmath}$ 作为我们的标量坐标所缩放的向量」这个决定。把一个向量翻译成一对数的特定方式，叫作一个「坐标系统」（coordinate system）；而这两个特殊的向量 $\hat{\imath}$ 和 $\hat{\jmath}$，就叫标准坐标系统的「基」（basis）向量。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 开篇

> - **encapsulate** /ɪnˈkæpsjuleɪt/ —— 封装、囊括。encapsulating all the implicit assumptions。
> - **implicit** /ɪmˈplɪsɪt/ —— 隐含的。the implicit assumptions of our coordinate system。
> - **tip-to-tail** /ˈtɪp tə ˌteɪl/ —— 头尾相接的。The tip-to-tail sum of these two scaled vectors。
> - **basis** /ˈbeɪsɪs/ —— 基。复数 bases /ˈbeɪsiːz/；the "basis" vectors。
> - *be tied up in* —— 系于、缠结在。all of that is tied up in the choice of...。

## 另一套坐标系（Alternate system）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| What we'd like to talk about here is the idea of using a different set of basis vectors. For example, let's say you have a friend, Jennifer, who uses a different set of basis vectors, which I'll call $\vec{\mathbf{b}_1}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{b}_2}$. Her first basis vector, $\vec{\mathbf{b}_1}$, points to the right and up a bit, and her second basis vector, $\vec{\mathbf{b}_2}$, points left and up. | 我们这里想谈的，是使用另一组基向量的想法。比如，假设你有个朋友 Jennifer，她用一组不同的基向量，我把它叫作 $\vec{\mathbf{b}_1}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{b}_2}$。她的第一个基向量 $\vec{\mathbf{b}_1}$ 指向右、略偏上，第二个基向量 $\vec{\mathbf{b}_2}$ 指向左上。 |
| Now take another look at the vector we showed earlier, the one we would describe with coordinates $\left[\begin{array}{c} 3 \\ 2 \end{array}\right]$ using our basis vectors $\hat{\imath}$ and $\hat{\jmath}$. | 现在再看看我们之前展示的那个向量——用我们的基向量 $\hat{\imath}$ 和 $\hat{\jmath}$，我们会用坐标 $\left[\begin{array}{c} 3 \\ 2 \end{array}\right]$ 描述它。 |
| Jennifer would actually describe this vector with coordinates$\left[\begin{array}{c} 5/3 \\ 1/3 \end{array}\right]$. What this means is that the particular way to get that vector using her two basis vectors is to scale $\vec{\mathbf{b}_1}$ by $5/3$, scale $\vec{\mathbf{b}}_2$ by $1/3$, then to add them together. | Jennifer 实际上会把这个向量描述成坐标 $\left[\begin{array}{c} 5/3 \\ 1/3 \end{array}\right]$。意思是：用她的两个基向量凑出这个向量的特定方式，是把 $\vec{\mathbf{b}_1}$ 缩放 $5/3$ 倍、把 $\vec{\mathbf{b}}_2$ 缩放 $1/3$ 倍，再把它们加起来。 |
| In a little bit we'll show you how you can figure out those two numbers. In general, whenever Jennifer uses coordinates to describe a vector, she thinks of the first coordinate as scaling $\vec{\mathbf{b}}_1$, and the second coordinate as scaling $\vec{\mathbf{b}}_2$, and she adds the results. | 稍后我们会展示你怎么算出这两个数。一般地，每当 Jennifer 用坐标描述一个向量时，她把第一个坐标想成缩放 $\vec{\mathbf{b}}_1$，第二个坐标想成缩放 $\vec{\mathbf{b}}_2$，然后相加。 |
| What she gets will typically be completely different from the vector we think of as having those coordinates. | 她得到的向量，通常与我们认为拥有那些坐标的向量完全不同。 |
| To be a little more precise, her first basis vector, $\vec{\mathbf{b}}_1$, is something we would describe with coordinates $\left[\begin{array}{c} 2 \\ 1 \end{array}\right]$, and her second basis vector, $\vec{\mathbf{b}}_2$, is something we would describe as $\left[\begin{array}{c} -1 \\ 1 \end{array}\right]$. | 说得更精确一点：她的第一个基向量 $\vec{\mathbf{b}}_1$，在我们看来坐标是 $\left[\begin{array}{c} 2 \\ 1 \end{array}\right]$；她的第二个基向量 $\vec{\mathbf{b}}_2$，在我们看来是 $\left[\begin{array}{c} -1 \\ 1 \end{array}\right]$。 |
| It's important to realize that from her perspective, in her system, those vectors have coordinates $\left[\begin{array}{c} 1 \\ 0 \end{array}\right]$ and $\left[\begin{array}{c} 0 \\ 1 \end{array}\right]$. They are what define the meaning of the coordinates $\left[\begin{array}{c} 1 \\ 0 \end{array}\right]$ and $\left[\begin{array}{c} 0 \\ 1 \end{array}\right]$ in her world. | 重要的是要意识到：从她的视角、在她的系统里，那些向量的坐标是 $\left[\begin{array}{c} 1 \\ 0 \end{array}\right]$ 和 $\left[\begin{array}{c} 0 \\ 1 \end{array}\right]$。正是它们，定义了她的世界里坐标 $\left[\begin{array}{c} 1 \\ 0 \end{array}\right]$ 和 $\left[\begin{array}{c} 0 \\ 1 \end{array}\right]$ 的含义。 |
| So in effect, we are speaking different languages. We're all looking at the same vectors in space, but Jennifer uses different words and numbers to describe them. | 所以实际上，我们在说不同的语言。我们看的都是空间里同样的向量，但 Jennifer 用不同的词和数来描述它们。 |

But this grid is just a construct, a way to visualize our coordinate system, so it depends on our choice of basis vectors. Space itself has no intrinsic grid. | 关于我们在这里如何表示事物，先快速说一句：在画二维空间时，我们通常用方形的网格。但这个网格只是一个构造物（construct），一种把坐标系统可视化的方式，所以它取决于我们对基向量的选择。空间本身没有内在的网格。 |
| Jennifer might draw her own grid, which would be an equally made up construct, meant as nothing more than a visual tool to help follow what her coordinates mean. | Jennifer 也可能画出她自己的网格，那同样是人为编造的构造物，不过是一个帮你看懂她的坐标含义的视觉工具。 |

## 基变换矩阵（Change of basis matrix）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| A fairly natural question to ask at this point is how we translate between coordinate systems. For example, if Jennifer describes a vector as $\left[\begin{array}{c} -1 \\ 2 \end{array}\right]$, what would its coordinates be in our system. How do you translate from her language to ours? | 到这一步，一个相当自然的问题是：我们如何在坐标系统之间翻译？比如，如果 Jennifer 把一个向量描述为 $\left[\begin{array}{c} -1 \\ 2 \end{array}\right]$，它在我们系统里的坐标是什么？你怎么把她的语言翻译成我们的语言？ |
| Well, what her coordinates are saying is that this vector is $-1 \vec{\mathbf{b}}_1 + 2 \vec{\mathbf{b}}_2$. From our perspective, $\vec{\mathbf{b}}_1$ has coordinates $\left[\begin{array}{c} 2 \\ 1 \end{array}\right]$, and $\vec{\mathbf{b}}_2$ has coordinates $\left[\begin{array}{c} -1 \\ 1 \end{array}\right]$. When we scale and add these representations of her basis vectors in our coordinates, we get $\left[\begin{array}{c} -4 \\ 1 \end{array}\right]$. So that's how we would describe the vector she thinks of as $\left[\begin{array}{c} -1 \\ 2 \end{array}\right]$. | 嗯，她的坐标说的是：这个向量是 $-1 \vec{\mathbf{b}}_1 + 2 \vec{\mathbf{b}}_2$。在我们的视角里，$\vec{\mathbf{b}}_1$ 的坐标是 $\left[\begin{array}{c} 2 \\ 1 \end{array}\right]$，$\vec{\mathbf{b}}_2$ 的坐标是 $\left[\begin{array}{c} -1 \\ 1 \end{array}\right]$。当我们用我们的坐标缩放并相加她的基向量的这些表示时，得到 $\left[\begin{array}{c} -4 \\ 1 \end{array}\right]$。这就是我们会如何描述她心中那个 $\left[\begin{array}{c} -1 \\ 2 \end{array}\right]$ 的向量。 |
| This process, of scaling each of her basis vectors by the corresponding coordinates of $\left[\begin{array}{c} -1 \\ 2 \end{array}\right]$ and adding them together might feel familiar: It's matrix-vector multiplication, with a matrix whose columns represent Jennifer's basis vectors. | 「把她的每个基向量按 $\left[\begin{array}{c} -1 \\ 2 \end{array}\right]$ 的对应坐标缩放、再把它们相加」这个过程，可能让你觉得眼熟：这就是矩阵与向量相乘，用的矩阵的列表示 Jennifer 的基向量。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 基变换矩阵
> - **translate** /trænzˈleɪt/ —— 翻译、转译。how we translate between coordinate systems。
> - **corresponding** /ˌkɒrəˈspɒndɪŋ/ —— 对应的。the corresponding coordinates。
> - **representation** /ˌreprɪzenˈteɪʃn/ —— 表示。these representations of her basis vectors。
> - **matrix-vector multiplication** —— 矩阵与向量相乘。It's matrix-vector multiplication。
> - 纯中文说明：作者把「坐标翻译」类比成语言翻译——Jennifer 说她的语言，我们说我们的语言，基变换矩阵就是词典。

## 这如何是一个变换（How this is a transformation）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| In fact, once you understand matrix-vector multiplication as applying a certain linear transformation, say by watching what might be the most important video in this series, chapter 3, there's a pretty intuitive way to think about what's going on here. | 事实上，一旦你理解了矩阵向量乘法是应用某个线性变换——比如看过可能是这个系列里最重要的一支视频、第 3 章——对这里发生的事就有一个相当直观的理解方式。 |
| A matrix whose columns represent Jennifer's basis vectors can be thought of as a transformation that moves our basis vectors, $\hat{\imath}$ and $\hat{\jmath}$, the things we think of when we say $\left[\begin{array}{c} 1 \\ 0 \end{array}\right]$ and $\left[\begin{array}{c} 0 \\ 1 \end{array}\right]$, to Jennifer's basis vectors, the things she thinks of when she says $\left[\begin{array}{c} 1 \\ 0 \end{array}\right]$ and $\left[\begin{array}{c} 0 \\ 1 \end{array}\right]$. | 一个列代表 Jennifer 基向量的矩阵，可以想成一个变换：它把我们的基向量 $\hat{\imath}$ 和 $\hat{\jmath}$——也就是我们说 $\left[\begin{array}{c} 1 \\ 0 \end{array}\right]$ 和 $\left[\begin{array}{c} 0 \\ 1 \end{array}\right]$ 时心里想的那个东西——移到 Jennifer 的基向量上，也就是她说 $\left[\begin{array}{c} 1 \\ 0 \end{array}\right]$ 和 $\left[\begin{array}{c} 0 \\ 1 \end{array}\right]$ 时她心里想的那个东西。 |
| For example, let's walk through what it means to take the vector that we think of as having coordinates $\left[\begin{array}{c} -1 \\ 2 \end{array}\right]$, and applying this transformation. Before the linear transformation we're thinking of this vector as a certain linear combination of basis vectors, $-1\hat{\imath} + 2\hat{\jmath}$. | 比如，让我们走一遍：取那个我们以为坐标是 $\left[\begin{array}{c} -1 \\ 2 \end{array}\right]$ 的向量，应用这个变换，是什么意思。在线性变换之前，我们把这个向量想成基向量的某个线性组合，$-1\hat{\imath} + 2\hat{\jmath}$。 |
| How we think of $\left[\begin{array}{c} -1 \\ 2 \end{array}\right]$ | （译注：图注。）我们如何理解 $\left[\begin{array}{c} -1 \\ 2 \end{array}\right]$。 |

| How Jennifer thinks of $\left[\begin{array}{c} -1 \\ 2 \end{array}\right]$ | （译注：图注。）Jennifer 如何理解 $\left[\begin{array}{c} -1 \\ 2 \end{array}\right]$。 |

## 从我们的到她的（Going from ours to hers）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| What about if you want to go the other way around? In the example we used earlier in this chapter, we had a vector with coordinates $\left[\begin{array}{c} 3 \\ 2 \end{array}\right]$ in our system. | 那如果想反着来呢？在本章前面用过的例子里，我们有一个在我们系统里坐标为 $\left[\begin{array}{c} 3 \\ 2 \end{array}\right]$ 的向量。 |
| How do we know that it would have coordinates $\left[\begin{array}{c} 5/3 \\ 1/3 \end{array}\right]$ in Jennifer's system? | 我们怎么知道它在 Jennifer 系统里的坐标是 $\left[\begin{array}{c} 5/3 \\ 1/3 \end{array}\right]$？ |
| Well, you start with that change of basis matrix that translates Jennifer's language into ours, then you take its inverse. Remember, the inverse of a transformation is a new transformation that corresponds to playing that first one backwards. | 嗯，你从那个把 Jennifer 语言翻译成我们语言的基变换矩阵出发，然后取它的逆。记住，一个变换的逆是一个新变换，对应着把原来那场变换倒着放。 |
| In this case, the inverse of the change of basis matrix that has Jennifer's basis as its columns ends up working out to have columns $\left[\begin{array}{c} 1/3 \\ -1/3 \end{array}\right]$, and $\left[\begin{array}{c} 1/3 \\ 2/3 \end{array}\right]$. | 在这个例子里，以 Jennifer 的基为列的那个基变换矩阵，它的逆算出来列是 $\left[\begin{array}{c} 1/3 \\ -1/3 \end{array}\right]$ 和 $\left[\begin{array}{c} 1/3 \\ 2/3 \end{array}\right]$。 |

$$
\left[\begin{array}{cc}
2 & -1 \\
1 & 1
\end{array}\right]^{-1}=\left[\begin{array}{cc}
1 / 3 & 1 / 3 \\
-1 / 3 & 2 / 3
\end{array}\right]
$$

|---|---|
| So for example, to see what the vector $\left[\begin{array}{c} 3 \\ 2 \end{array}\right]$ looks like in Jennifer's system, we multiply this inverse change of basis matrix by $\left[\begin{array}{c} 3 \\ 2 \end{array}\right]$, which works out to be $\left[\begin{array}{c} 5/3 \\ 1/3 \end{array}\right]$. | 所以比如，要看向量 $\left[\begin{array}{c} 3 \\ 2 \end{array}\right]$ 在 Jennifer 的系统里长什么样，我们就把这个逆基变换矩阵乘以 $\left[\begin{array}{c} 3 \\ 2 \end{array}\right]$，算出来是 $\left[\begin{array}{c} 5/3 \\ 1/3 \end{array}\right]$。 |
| In practice, especially when you're working in more than two dimensions, you'd use a computer to compute the matrix which represents this inverse, but for a small matrix, like $M = \begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix}$, the inverse can be computed by swapping along one diagonal, negating the other, and dividing by the determinant. | 实际操作中，尤其当你在超过二维的空间里工作时，你会用计算机来算表示这个逆的矩阵；但对小矩阵，比如 $M = \begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix}$，逆可以这样算：沿一条对角线对调、另一条对角线取负，再除以行列式。 |

$$
M ^{-1} =  \frac{1}{\det(M)} \begin{bmatrix} d & -b \\ -c & a \end{bmatrix}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| In Olive's language, defined by the basis $\vec{\mathbf{b}}_1 = \left[\begin{array}{c} 2 \\ 3 \end{array}\right]$ and $\vec{\mathbf{b}}_2 = \left[\begin{array}{c} -3 \\ 2 \end{array}\right]$, how would they describe the vector that we would describe with the coordinates $\left[\begin{array}{c} -5 \\ -1 \end{array}\right]$? | （译注：原文此处是一道互动测验的题干，候选为四个二维列向量，正确答案为 $\left[\begin{array}{c} -1 \\ 1 \end{array}\right]$；官网给出的解法为下面的计算过程。）在 Olive 的语言——由基 $\vec{\mathbf{b}}_1 = \left[\begin{array}{c} 2 \\ 3 \end{array}\right]$ 与 $\vec{\mathbf{b}}_2 = \left[\begin{array}{c} -3 \\ 2 \end{array}\right]$ 定义——里，他们会如何描述我们用坐标 $\left[\begin{array}{c} -5 \\ -1 \end{array}\right]$ 描述的那个向量？ |

$$
\begin{aligned}
{\left[\begin{array}{cc}
2 & -3 \\
3 & 2
\end{array}\right]^{-1}\left[\begin{array}{l}
-5 \\
-1
\end{array}\right] } & =
{\left[\begin{array}{cc}
2 / 13 & 3 / 13 \\
-3 / 13 & 2 / 13
\end{array}\right] \\ \rule{0pt}{2.5em}
\left[\begin{array}{l}
-5 \\
-1
\end{array}\right] } \\ \rule{0pt}{2.5em}
& =-5\left[\begin{array}{c}
2 / 13 \\
-3 / 13
\end{array}\right]-1\left[\begin{array}{l}
3 / 13 \\
2 / 13
\end{array}\right] \\ \rule{0pt}{2.5em}
& =\left[\begin{array}{l}

15 / 13
\end{array}\right]-\left[\begin{array}{l}
3 / 13 \\
2 / 13
\end{array}\right] \\ \rule{0pt}{2.5em}
& =\left[\begin{array}{c}
-13 / 13 \\

\end{array}\right] \\ \rule{0pt}{2.5em}

\end{array}\right]
\end{aligned}
$$

## 以旋转为例（Follow Rotation）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Consider some linear transformation, like a $90$ degree counterclockwise rotation. In our coordinate system, we represent this with the matrix $\left[\begin{array}{c} 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{array}\right]$. The first column tells us that $\hat{\imath}$ goes to $\left[\begin{array}{c} 0 \\ 1 \end{array}\right]$, and the second column tells us that $\hat{\jmath}$ goes to $\left[\begin{array}{c} -1 \\ 0 \end{array}\right]$. | 考虑某个线性变换，比如 $90$ 度逆时针旋转。在我们的坐标系统里，我们用矩阵 $\left[\begin{array}{c} 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{array}\right]$ 表示它。第一列告诉我们 $\hat{\imath}$ 变到 $\left[\begin{array}{c} 0 \\ 1 \end{array}\right]$，第二列告诉我们 $\hat{\jmath}$ 变到 $\left[\begin{array}{c} -1 \\ 0 \end{array}\right]$。 |
| This representation is heavily tied up in our choice of basis vectors from the fact that we're following $\hat{\imath}$ and $\hat{\jmath}$ in the first place, to the fact that we record their landing spots in our own coordinate system. How would Jennifer describe a $90$ degree rotation of space? | 这个表示与我们对基向量的选择深度捆绑：从「我们一开始就在跟踪 $\hat{\imath}$ 和 $\hat{\jmath}$」，到「我们用我们自己的坐标系统记录它们的落点」。那 Jennifer 会怎么描述空间的 $90$ 度旋转？ |
| You might be tempted to just translate the columns of our rotation matrix into Jennifer's language, but this is not quite right. ## 这个过程（The process）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Here's a common way to think about how this is done: Start with any vector, written in Jennifer's language. Rather than trying to follow what happens to it in terms of her language, first translate it into our language using the change of basis matrix, the one whose columns represent her basis vectors in our language. This gives the same vector written in our language. | 思考怎么做到这一点，有一个常见方式：从任何向量开始，用 Jennifer 的语言写出。与其追着它在她的语言里发生什么，不如先用基变换矩阵——列表示「用我们语言写出的她的基向量」的那个——把它翻译成我们的语言。这给出同一个向量、用我们的语言写出。 |
| Then apply the transformation matrix to it by multiplying on the left. This tells us where that vector lands, but in our language. | 然后，按惯例左乘，对它应用变换矩阵。这告诉我们那个向量落到哪儿，但是在我们的语言里。 |
| As a last step, apply the inverse change of basis matrix, multiplied on the left as usual, to get this transformed vector in Jennifer's language. | 最后一步，照常左乘逆基变换矩阵，得到这个变换后的向量在 Jennifer 语言里的版本。 |
| Since we could do this to any vector written in her language, first applying the change of basis matrix, then the transformation then the inverse change of basis, that composition of three matrices gives us the transformation matrix in Jennifer's language. It takes in a vector in her language, and spits out the transformed version of that vector in her language. | 既然我们可以对任何用她的语言写出的向量都这么做——先应用基变换矩阵，再应用变换，然后应用逆基变换——这三个矩阵的复合就给了我们 Jennifer 语言下的变换矩阵。它吃进一个她的语言里的向量，吐出那个向量变换后的版本、仍然用她的语言。 |
| For this example, where Jennifer's basis vectors look like $\left[\begin{array}{c} 2 \\ 1 \end{array}\right]$ and $\left[\begin{array}{c} -1 \\ 1 \end{array}\right]$ to us, and we're translating a $90$ degree rotation, the product of these three matrices, if you work through it, has columns $\left[\begin{array}{c} 1/3 \\ 5/3 \end{array}\right]$ and $\left[\begin{array}{c} -2/3 \\ -1/3 \end{array}\right]$. So if Jennifer multiplies that matrix by the coordinates of a vector in her system, it will return the $90$ degree rotated version of her vector, expressed in her coordinate system. | 在这个例子里，Jennifer 的基向量在我们看来是 $\left[\begin{array}{c} 2 \\ 1 \end{array}\right]$ 和 $\left[\begin{array}{c} -1 \\ 1 \end{array}\right]$，我们翻译的是 $90$ 度旋转；这三个矩阵的乘积，如果你动手算一遍，列是 $\left[\begin{array}{c} 1/3 \\ 5/3 \end{array}\right]$ 和 $\left[\begin{array}{c} -2/3 \\ -1/3 \end{array}\right]$。于是，如果 Jennifer 把那个矩阵乘以她系统里一个向量的坐标，它会返回她的向量旋转 $90$ 度后的版本，并且用她的坐标系统表示。 |
| In general, whenever you see an expression like $A^{-1}MA$, it suggests a mathematical sort of empathy. The middle matrix represents a transformation as you see it, the outer two matrices represent the empathy, this shift in perspective, and the full matrix product represents that same transformation as someone else sees it. | 一般地，每当你看到 $A^{-1}MA$ 这样的表达式，它暗示着一种数学上的换位思考（empathy）：中间的矩阵表示你所看到的变换，外面两个矩阵表示那种共情、那种视角切换，而完整的矩阵乘积表示同一场变换在别人眼中的样子。 |
| For those of you wondering why we care about using alternate coordinate systems, the next chapter on eigenvectors and eigenvalues will give a really important example of this. See you then! | 如果你们好奇我们为什么要在乎使用别的坐标系统，下一章的本征向量与本征值（eigenvectors and eigenvalues）会给出一个非常重要的例子。到时候见！ |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 这个过程
> - **composition** /ˌkɒmpəˈzɪʃn/ —— 复合。that composition of three matrices。
> - **empathy** /ˈempəθi/ —— 共情、换位理解。a mathematical sort of empathy。
> - **successive** /səkˈsesɪv/ —— 相继的。composing successive transformations。
> - **express** /ɪkˈspres/ —— 表达、表示。expressed in her coordinate system。
> - *multiply on the left* —— 左乘。apply the transformation matrix by multiplying on the left。
> - *spit out* —— （口语）吐出、输出。spits out the transformed version。
