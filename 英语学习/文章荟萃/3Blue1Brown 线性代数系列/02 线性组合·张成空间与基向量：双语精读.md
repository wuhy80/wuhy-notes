## 开篇：坐标的另一种看法（Introduction）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| In the last chapter, along with the ideas of vector addition and scalar multiplication, we described vector coordinates, where there's this back-and-forth between pairs of numbers and two-dimensional vectors. | 上一章里，除了向量加法和标量乘法这两个概念，我们还讲了向量坐标（vector coordinates）——也就是一对数与二维向量之间的那种来回对应。 |
| Now it's possible that vector coordinates were already familiar to many of you, but there's another interesting way to think about these coordinates, which is central to linear algebra. When you have a pair of numbers meant to describe a vector, like $(3, -2)$, think of each coordinate as a scalar, meaning think about how each one stretches or squishes vectors. | 也许向量坐标对你们中许多人来说早已不新鲜，但还有另一种理解坐标的有趣方式，它对线性代数来说至关重要。当你手里有一对用来描述向量的数，比如 $(3, -2)$ 时，把每个坐标都想成一个标量（scalar）——也就是说，想想它们各自如何拉伸或压扁向量。 |
| In the $xy$-coordinate system, there are two special vectors. The one pointing to the right with length $1$, commonly called "i hat" $\hat{\imath}$ or "the unit vector in the $x$-direction". The other one is pointing straight up with length $1$, commonly called "j hat" $\hat{\jmath}$ or "the unit vector in the $y$-direction". | 在 $xy$ 坐标系里，有两个特殊的向量。一个指向右方、长度为 $1$，通常叫「i 帽」（i hat）$\hat{\imath}$，或者「$x$ 方向的单位向量」；另一个竖直向上、长度为 $1$，通常叫「j 帽」（j hat）$\hat{\jmath}$，或者「$y$ 方向的单位向量」。 |
| Now, think of the $x$-coordinate as a scalar that scales $\hat{\imath}$, stretching it by a factor of $3$, and the $y$-coordinate as a scalar that scales $\hat{\jmath}$, flipping it and stretching it by a factor of $2$. In this sense, the vector that these coordinates describe is the sum of two scaled vectors. | 现在，把 $x$ 坐标想成一个缩放 $\hat{\imath}$ 的标量，把它拉伸到 3 倍；把 $y$ 坐标想成一个缩放 $\hat{\jmath}$ 的标量，把它翻转并拉伸到 2 倍。从这个角度看，这组坐标所描述的向量，就是两个缩放后的向量之和。 |
| This idea of adding together two scaled vectors is a surprisingly important concept. Those two vectors $\hat{\imath}$ and $\hat{\jmath}$ have a special name: Together they are called the "basis" of the coordinate system. What this means is that when you think about coordinates as scalars, the basis vectors are what those scalars actually scale. | 把两个缩放过的向量加在一起——这个想法重要得出人意料。$\hat{\imath}$ 和 $\hat{\jmath}$ 这两个向量有一个专门的名字：它们合起来被称为坐标系的「基」（basis）。意思是说，当你把坐标想成标量时，基向量（basis vectors）才是这些标量真正缩放的对象。 |
| There's also a more technical definition of basis, but we'll get to that later. Framing our familiar coordinate system in terms of these two special basis vectors raises an interesting and subtle point: We could choose a different pair of basis vectors and get a perfectly reasonable _new_ coordinate system. | 「基」还有一个更技术化的定义，我们稍后再讲。用这两个特殊的基向量来重新框定我们熟悉的坐标系，会引出一个微妙而有趣的点：我们完全可以选择另一对基向量，得到一个同样合情合理的*新*坐标系。 |

> - **unit vector** —— 单位向量。长度为 1 的向量。
> - **basis** /ˈbeɪsɪs/ —— 基。复数 bases /ˈbeɪsiːz/，坐标系赖以搭建的地基向量。
> - **flip** /flɪp/ —— 翻转。乘负标量时向量调头。
> - **technical** /ˈteknɪkl/ —— 技术性的、严格意义上的。a more technical definition 更严格的定义。
> - **subtle** /ˈsʌtl/ —— 微妙的、不易察觉的。an interesting and subtle point。
> - **frame** /freɪm/ —— 框定、把……放进框架。framing ... in terms of ... 用……来表述……。
> - *back-and-forth* —— 来回对应、双向转换。

## 换一组基向量（Choosing Different Basis Vectors）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| For example, take some vector pointing up and to the right, along with a vector pointing down and to the right. | 举个例子，取一个指向右上方的向量，再取一个指向右下方的向量。 |
| Take a moment to think about all the different vectors you can get by choosing two scalars, using each to scale one of the vectors, then adding them. Which two-dimensional vectors can you reach by altering your choice of scalars? | 花点时间想想：任选两个标量，分别用来缩放这两个向量，再把它们相加——这样你能得到的所有不同向量有哪些？改变标量的选择，你能够到哪些二维向量？ |
| The answer is, for these two vectors, you can describe _every_ possible two-dimensional vector this way, and it's a good puzzle to contemplate why. A new pair of basis vectors like this still gives you a way to go back and forth between pairs of numbers and two-dimensional vectors, but the association is definitely different from the version you get using the standard basis of $\hat{\imath}$ and $\hat{\jmath}$. | 答案是：对这两个向量来说，*每一个*可能的二维向量都可以这样描述出来；至于为什么，是个值得琢磨的好谜题。这样一对新的基向量，依然给了你在「数对」与「二维向量」之间来回对应的办法，但这种对应关系，和用 $\hat{\imath}$、$\hat{\jmath}$ 这组标准基（standard basis）得到的版本明显不同。 |
| $\left[\begin{array}{c}-0.8 \\ 1.3\end{array}\right] \neq\left[\begin{array}{c}3.1 \\ -2.9\end{array}\right]$ | （译注：下方配图的图注。）$\left[\begin{array}{c}-0.8 \\ 1.3\end{array}\right] \neq\left[\begin{array}{c}3.1 \\ -2.9\end{array}\right]$——同一个向量，在新旧两套基下写出的坐标并不相同。 |
| We'll go into much more detail on this point in a [later chapter](https://www.3blue1brown.com/lessons/change-of-basis), describing the relationship between different coordinate systems, but for now we just want you to appreciate that any way to describe vectors numerically depends on your choice of basis vectors. | 这一点，我们会在[后面的章节](https://www.3blue1brown.com/lessons/change-of-basis)里详细展开，讲清楚不同坐标系之间的关系；但眼下只希望你领会：任何用数字描述向量的办法，都取决于你对基向量的选择。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 换基

> - **contemplate** /ˈkɒntəmpleɪt/ —— 沉思、琢磨。a good puzzle to contemplate。
> - **association** /əˌsəʊʃiˈeɪʃn/ —— 对应关系。the association is different。
> - **appreciate** /əˈpriːʃieɪt/ —— 领会、体会到（此处不是「感激」）。
> - **standard basis** —— 标准基。指 $\hat{\imath}$、$\hat{\jmath}$ 这一组默认基。
> - *go back and forth* —— 来回转换。
> - *for now* —— 眼下、暂时。

## 线性组合（Linear Combinations）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Any time you're scaling two vectors and adding them like this, it's called a "linear combination" of those two vectors. | 每当你像这样缩放两个向量再把它们相加，就叫做这两个向量的「线性组合」（linear combination）。 |
| Where does the word "linear" come from here? What does this have to do with lines? Well, when you multiply a scalar by a vector, it changes the magnitude of that vector. Multiplying every real number by the vector produces an infinite line that passes through the origin and the point defined by the vector. | 这里的「线性」（linear）一词从何而来？它跟「直线」有什么关系？是这样：标量乘向量时，改变的是向量的长度（magnitude）。让每个实数都去乘这个向量，就会产生一条无穷长的直线，它穿过原点和这个向量所定义的那个点。 |
| So a linear combination of two vectors is a method of combining these two lines. For most pairs of vectors, if you let both scalars range freely and consider every possible vector you could get, you will be able to reach every possible point on the plane. Every two-dimensional vector is within your grasp. | 所以，两个向量的线性组合，就是把这两条线合并起来的一种方法。对大多数向量对来说，如果让两个标量自由取值、考虑你能得到的所有可能向量，你将能够到平面上每一个可能的点。每一个二维向量都在你的掌握之中。 |
| However, if your two original vectors happen to line up, the lines produced by the scalar multiplication will be the same line, so adding them together can't yield a vector outside of that line. | 然而，如果两个初始向量恰好排在同一条线上，数乘产生的两条线就是同一条线，于是不管怎么相加，都产生不出这条线之外的向量。 |
| There's a third possibility too: Both your vectors could be the $0$ vector, in which case you'll just be stuck at the origin. | 还有第三种可能：两个向量都是零向量 $0$，这种情况下你就只能困在原点了。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 线性组合
> - **linear combination** —— 线性组合。缩放后相加的标准术语，本课核心。
> - **magnitude** /ˈmæɡnɪtjuːd/ —— 大小、长度。标量乘法改变的是 magnitude。
> - **infinite** /ˈɪnfɪnət/ —— 无穷的。an infinite line 一条无穷长的直线。
> - **yield** /jiːld/ —— 产生、给出。can't yield a vector outside that line。
> - **grasp** /ɡrɑːsp/ —— 掌握、抓住。within your grasp。
> - *range freely* —— 自由取值。let both scalars range freely。
> - *line up* —— 排成一条线（正好与 linear 呼应）。

## 张成空间（Span）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| The set of all possible vectors you can reach with linear combinations of a given pair of vectors is called the "span" of those two vectors. Restating what we just saw in this lingo, the span of most pairs of 2D vectors is all vectors in 2D space, but when they line up, their span is all vectors whose tip sit on a certain line. | 仅用一组给定向量的线性组合就能到达的所有可能向量的集合，叫做这两个向量的「张成空间」（span）。用这套行话重述我们刚看到的现象：大多数二维向量对的张成是二维空间中的全部向量；但当它们排在同一条线上时，它们的张成就是所有箭尖坐在某条特定直线上的向量。 |
| Remember how we said linear algebra revolves around vector addition and scalar multiplication? The span of two vectors is basically a way of asking what are all the possible vectors you can reach using these two by only using those fundamental operations of vector addition and scalar multiplication. | 还记得我们说过，线性代数围绕着向量加法和标量乘法吗？两个向量的张成，基本上就是在问：只靠向量加法和标量乘法这两个基本运算，用这两个向量你究竟能到达哪些所有可能的向量。 |
| Of all the answers, $\vec{\mathbf{v}} = \left[\begin{array}{c}2 \\ 3\end{array}\right]$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}} = \left[\begin{array}{c}-1 \\ -1.5\end{array}\right]$ are the only two vectors which lie on the same line as eachother. | （译注：原文此处是一道互动测验的答案解析，题目在官网原页面上——问 $a\vec{\mathbf{v}} + b\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 的哪组向量只产生落在同一条直线上的结果。）在所有选项里，$\vec{\mathbf{v}} = \left[\begin{array}{c}2 \\ 3\end{array}\right]$ 与 $\vec{\mathbf{w}} = \left[\begin{array}{c}-1 \\ -1.5\end{array}\right]$ 是唯一一对彼此共线的向量。 |
| These two vectors line up because $\begin{bmatrix}-6\\ 4\end{bmatrix}=-2\begin{bmatrix}3\\ -2\end{bmatrix}$, meaning their span is the line going through the origin and the tips of the vectors. If the two vectors did not lie on the same line, their span would be the full 2d plane. Alternatively, if both vectors were the zero vector their span would be the point at the origin. | （译注：原文此处是一道互动测验的答案解析，题目在官网原页面上——问 $\begin{bmatrix}3\\ -2\end{bmatrix}$ 与 $\begin{bmatrix}-6\\ 4\end{bmatrix}$ 的张成是什么。）这两个向量共线，因为 $\begin{bmatrix}-6\\ 4\end{bmatrix}=-2\begin{bmatrix}3\\ -2\end{bmatrix}$，也就是说它们的张成是穿过原点和这两个向量箭尖的那条直线。如果两个向量不共线，它们的张成就是整个二维平面。再或者，如果两个向量都是零向量，它们的张成就是原点那一个点。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 张成空间
> - **span** /spæn/ —— 张成（空间）。可达向量集合的标准术语，名词动词皆可。
> - **reach** /riːtʃ/ —— 够到、到达。vectors you can reach with linear combinations。
> - **set** /set/ —— 集合。the set of all possible vectors。
> - **restate** /ˌriːˈsteɪt/ —— 换个说法重述。restate ... in this lingo。
> - **alternatively** /ɔːlˈtɜːnətɪvli/ —— 或者、另一种可能是。
> - *revolve around* —— 围绕……展开。
> - *all vectors whose tips sit on a certain line* —— 箭尖坐在某条直线上的全体向量，span 的几何说法。

## 向量与点（Vectors vs Points）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| This is a good time to talk about how people commonly think about vectors as points. It gets very crowded to think about a whole collection of vectors sitting on a line, and more crowded still to think about all two-dimensional vectors all at once, filling up the plane. | 现在正好谈谈人们通常如何把向量想成点。让一整族向量都挤在一条线上去想，已经够拥挤了；要把所有二维向量一次性全想一遍、铺满整个平面，那就更拥挤了。 |
| So when dealing with collections of vectors like this, it's common to represent each one just with a point in space, the point at the tip of this vector. That way, if you want to think about every possible vector whose tip sits on a certain line, just think about that line itself. | 所以，对付这样的向量集合时，常见的做法是用空间里的一个点来代表每个向量——就是这个向量箭尖所在的那个点。这样一来，如果你想思考「箭尖坐在某条特定直线上的所有可能向量」，直接想那条直线本身就行。 |
| Likewise, to think about all possible two-dimensional vectors, conceptualize each one as the point where its tip sits. Then to think about all of them all at once, you can just think about the infinite flat sheet that is two-dimensional space, leaving the arrows out of it. | 同理，要思考所有可能的二维向量，就把每一个概念化为它的箭尖所在的那个点。而要一次性把它们全想完，你只需要想那张无穷大的平坦薄片——也就是二维空间本身——把箭头都省掉。 |
| In general, if you're thinking of a vector on its own, think of it as an arrow, and if you're thinking of a collection, it's convenient to think of them as points. | 一般来说，单独想一个向量时，把它想成箭头；想一个集合时，把它们想成点会更方便。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 向量与点
> - **crowded** /ˈkraʊdɪd/ —— 拥挤的。文中形容满屏箭头难以思考。
> - **collection** /kəˈlekʃn/ —— 集合、一群。a collection of vectors。
> - **represent** /ˌreprɪˈzent/ —— 表示、代表。
> - **conceptualize** /kənˈseptʃuəlaɪz/ —— 概念化、在脑中成形。
> - **convenient** /kənˈviːniənt/ —— 方便的。
> - *flat sheet* —— 平坦薄片，文中指二维平面。
> - *all at once* —— 一次性地、全部一起。

## 三维空间中的张成（Span in 3D）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| The idea of span gets more interesting if we start thinking about vectors in three-dimensional space. For example, if you take two vectors in three-dimensional space that are not pointing in the same direction, what does it mean to take their span? | 如果开始思考三维空间里的向量，「张成」这个概念就更有意思了。比如说，取两个三维空间里并不指向同一方向的向量，取它们的张成是什么意思？ |
| Well, their span is the collection of all possible linear combinations of those two vectors, meaning all possible vectors you get by scaling each of the two you start with in some way, then adding them together. | 它们的张成，就是这两个向量所有可能线性组合的集合——也就是说，把初始的两个向量各自以某种方式缩放、再相加，你能得到的所有可能向量。 |
| You can imagine turning two knobs to change the two scalars defining the linear combination, adding the scaled vectors and following the tip of the resulting vector. That tip traces out some kind of flat sheet cutting through the origin of three-dimensional space. | 你可以想象转动两个旋钮来改变定义线性组合的两个标量，把缩放后的向量相加，并盯着合成向量的箭尖。那个箭尖会描出某种穿过三维空间原点的平坦薄片。 |
| This flat sheet is the span of the two vectors. Or more precisely, the set of all possible vectors whose tips sit on this flat sheet is the span of your two vectors. Isn't that a beautiful mental image? | 这张平坦薄片就是这两个向量的张成。更精确地说，箭尖坐在这张薄片上的所有可能向量的集合，才是你这两个向量的张成。这难道不是一幅漂亮的脑内图景吗？ |
| What happens if you add on a third vector, and consider the span of all three vectors? A linear combination of three vectors is defined pretty much the same way as for two: Choose three scalars, use them to scale each of your vectors, then add them all together. And again, the span of these vectors is the set of all possible linear combinations. | 如果再加第三个向量、考虑这三个向量的张成，又会怎样？三个向量的线性组合，定义跟两个向量时几乎一样：选三个标量，用它们分别缩放三个向量，然后全部相加。同样地，这些向量的张成就是所有可能线性组合的集合。 |
| The linear combination of $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$, $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$, and $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$: $a\vec{\mathbf{v}} + b\vec{\mathbf{w}} + c\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ | （图注）$\vec{\mathbf{v}}$、$\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 与 $\vec{\mathbf{u}}$ 的线性组合：$a\vec{\mathbf{v}} + b\vec{\mathbf{w}} + c\vec{\mathbf{u}}$。 |
| Two things can happen when we add a third vector: | 加入第三个向量后，有两种可能： |
| 1. If your third vector happens to be sitting on the span of the first two, then the span doesn't change, you're sort of trapped on that same flat sheet. In other words, adding a scaled version of the third vector to linear combinations of the first two doesn't give you access to any new vectors. | 1. 如果第三个向量恰好就坐在前两个向量的张成上，那么张成不变——你算是被困在了同一张平坦薄片上。换句话说，把第三个向量的缩放版加进前两个向量的线性组合里，不会让你够到任何新向量。 |
| 2. If you just randomly choose a third vector, it's almost certainly not sitting on the span of the first. Since it's then pointing in a separate direction, it unlocks access to every possible three-dimensional vector! The way to think about this is that as you scale the new third vector, it moves around the span of the first two to sweep it through all of space. | 2. 如果你随机挑一个第三向量，那它几乎肯定不坐在前者的张成上。既然它指向一个不同的方向，它就解锁了通往每一个可能的三维向量的通道！理解方式是：当你缩放这个新的第三向量时，它在前两者的张成上四处移动，把这张薄片扫过整个空间。 |
| It's kind of like you're making full use of the three freely-changing scalars that you have at your disposal to access the full three dimensions of space. | 这就好比你在充分利用手头三个可自由变化的标量，去够到空间的全部三个维度。 |
| In the case where the third vector was sitting on the span of the first two, or the case where two vectors happen to line up, we want some terminology to describe the fact that at least one of these vectors is redundant, not adding anything to our span. Whenever this happens, where you have multiple vectors, and you could remove one without reducing their span, the relevant terminology is to say they are "linearly dependent". | 在「第三向量坐在前两个的张成上」或者「两个向量恰好共线」的情形里，我们想有个术语来描述「这些向量里至少有一个是多余的、对张成毫无贡献」这件事。每当出现这种情况——你手里有一组向量，去掉其中一个也不会缩小它们的张成——相应的术语就说它们「线性相关」（linearly dependent）。 |
| Another way of phrasing this would be to say that one of the vectors can be expressed as a linear combination of the others. That is, it's already in the span of the other two. | 换一种说法：其中一个向量可以被表示为其他向量的线性组合。也就是说，它本来就在另外两个向量的张成里。 |
| On the other hand, if each vector really does add another dimension to the span, they are said to be "linearly independent". | 反过来，如果每个向量都真的给张成添上了新的维度，就说它们「线性无关」（linearly independent）。 |
