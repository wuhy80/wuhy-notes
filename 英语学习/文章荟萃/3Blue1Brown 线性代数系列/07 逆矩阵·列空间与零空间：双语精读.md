## 线性方程组（Linear Systems of Equations）

| 原文 | 译文 |

| You have a hint by now of how linear algebra is useful for describing the manipulation of space, which is useful for things like computer graphics and robotics. When I say "system of equations", I mean you have a list of variables you don't know and a list of equations relating them. | 到现在你已经有点感觉了：线性代数在描述空间的操纵上很有用，这对计算机图形学、机器人学之类的东西都很管用。但线性代数之所以适用面更广、几乎任何技术学科都离不开它，一个主要原因在于：它让我们能求解某些方程组（system of equations）。我说「方程组」，意思是你手上有一串不知道的变量（variable），和一串把它们联系起来的方程。 |

| The typical way to organize this sort of special system of equations is to throw all the variables on the left and to put any lingering constants on the right. | You might notice that this looks a lot like matrix-vector multiplication. ## 逆变换（Inverses）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Let's start with the most likely case, where the determinant is nonzero and space does not get squished onto a zero-area line. In this case, there will always be one and only one vector $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{x}}$ that lands on $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{v}}$, which you can find by playing the transformation in reverse. | 从最可能的情形开始：行列式非零，空间没有被压到零面积的直线上。这种情况下，总会有且只有一个向量 $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{x}}$ 落在 $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{v}}$ 上；把它找出来的办法，是把变换倒着放一遍。 |
| Following where $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{v}}$ goes as we rewind the tape like this, you will find the vector $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{x}}$ such that $A\overrightarrow{\mathbf{x}}=\overrightarrow{\mathbf{v}}$. | 像这样把录像带倒回去、跟着 $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{v}}$ 走，你就会找到满足 $A\overrightarrow{\mathbf{x}}=\overrightarrow{\mathbf{v}}$ 的那个向量 $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{x}}$。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 逆变换

> - **rewind** /ˌriːˈwaɪnd/ —— 倒带。as we rewind the tape。
> - **nonzero** /ˌnɒnˈzɪərəʊ/ —— 非零的。the determinant is nonzero。
> - **zero-area** /ˌzɪərəʊˈeəriə/ —— 零面积的。a zero-area line。

## 逆矩阵（Inverse Matrix）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| When you play the transformation in reverse, it actually corresponds with a separate linear transformation, commonly called the inverse of $A$, denoted as $A^{-1}$. For example, if $A$ was a counterclockwise rotation by $90^\circ$, the inverse of $A$ would be a clockwise rotation by $90^\circ$. | 把变换倒着放，其实对应着另一个独立的线性变换，通常叫作 $A$ 的逆（inverse），记作 $A^{-1}$。比如，如果 $A$ 是逆时针旋转 $90^\circ$，那 $A$ 的逆就是顺时针旋转 $90^\circ$。 |
| If $A$ was a rightward shear that pushed $\hat j$ one unit to the right, the inverse of $A$ would be leftward a shear that pushed $\hat j$ one unit to the left. | 如果 $A$ 是把 $\hat j$ 向右推一格的右向剪切（shear），那 $A$ 的逆就是把 $\hat j$ 向左推一格的左向剪切。 |
| In general, $A^{-1}$ is the unique transformation with the property that if you apply the transformation $A$, and follow it with the transformation $A$ inverse, you end up back where you started. | 一般地，$A^{-1}$ 是满足下面性质的唯一变换：如果你先应用变换 $A$，紧接着应用变换 $A$ 的逆，你就会回到起点。 |
| What is the inverse matrix for a $180^\circ$ rotation? $\begin{bmatrix}-1&0\\0&-1\end{bmatrix}$ | （译注：原文此处是一道互动测验的题干，四个候选矩阵见官网原页，正确答案是 $\begin{bmatrix}-1&0\\0&-1\end{bmatrix}$。）一个 $180^\circ$ 旋转的逆矩阵是什么？$\begin{bmatrix}-1&0\\0&-1\end{bmatrix}$ |
| The $180^\circ$ rotation matrix is its own inverse! That should make sense because applying the transformation twice will get you back to where you started. | （译注：上题的答案解析。）这个 $180^\circ$ 旋转矩阵是它自己的逆！这应该说得通：把变换应用两次，你就回到了起点。 |
| Since applying one transformation after another is captured algebraically with matrix multiplication, the core property of this reverse transform $A^{-1}$ is that $A^{-1}$ times $A$ equals the matrix that corresponds to doing nothing. The transformation which does nothing is called the "identity" transformation. It leaves $\hat i$ and $\hat j$ where they are, unmoved, so its columns are $\begin{bmatrix}1\\0\end{bmatrix}$ and $\begin{bmatrix}0\\1\end{bmatrix}$. | 既然「先后应用两个变换」在代数上由矩阵乘法捕捉，那么这个反向变换 $A^{-1}$ 的核心性质就是：$A^{-1}$ 乘 $A$，等于「什么都不做」所对应的矩阵。什么都不做的变换叫「恒等」（identity）变换：它让 $\hat i$ 和 $\hat j$ 待在原地、纹丝不动，所以它的列是 $\begin{bmatrix}1\\0\end{bmatrix}$ 和 $\begin{bmatrix}0\\1\end{bmatrix}$。 |

$$
A^{-1}A=\begin{bmatrix}
\color{green}1&\color{red}0 \\
\color{green}0&\color{red}1
\end{bmatrix}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| There are computational methods to compute this inverse matrix. In the case of two dimensions there's a commonly taught formula, which I have to admit, I can never remember. Once you find this inverse, which in practice you'd do with a computer, you can solve your equation by multiplying this inverse matrix by $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{v}}$. And again, what that means geometrically is that you're playing the transformation in reverse and following $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{v}}$ to end up at $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{x}}$. | 计算逆矩阵是有计算方法的。二维情形有一条常被讲授的公式——不瞒你说，我从来记不住它。一旦找到这个逆（实践中你会用电脑来找），把它乘上 $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{v}}$，方程就解出来了。再强调一遍，这在几何上的意思是：你把变换倒着放，跟着 $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{v}}$ 走，最终到达 $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{x}}$。 |

$$
\begin{align*}
\color{green}A^{-1}
\color{currentColor}A
\color{purple}\overrightarrow{\mathbf{x}}
\color{currentColor}&=
\color{green}A^{-1}
\color{orange}\overrightarrow{\mathbf{v}}
\\
\color{purple}\overrightarrow{\mathbf{x}}
\color{currentColor}&=
\color{green}A^{-1}
\color{orange}\overrightarrow{\mathbf{v}}
\end{align*}
$$

| This nonzero determinant case corresponds with the idea that if you have two unknowns and two linear equations, it's almost certainly the case that there is a single unique solution. | 行列式非零这种情形，对应着这样一层意思：如果你有两个未知数、两条线性方程，那么几乎可以肯定，存在唯一的解。 |

## 更高维度（Higher Dimensions）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| This idea also makes sense in higher dimensions when the number of equations equals the number of unknowns. Again, the system of equations can be translated to the geometric interpretation where you have some transformation $A$ and some vector $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{v}}$, and you're looking for the vector $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{x}}$ that lands on $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{v}}$. | 当方程数等于未知数个数时，这个想法在高维同样说得通。同样地，方程组可以翻译成几何解读：你有一个变换 $A$ 和一个向量 $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{v}}$，你在找落在 $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{v}}$ 上的向量 $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{x}}$。 |

$$
\color{purple}\overrightarrow{\mathbf{x}}
\color{currentColor}=\color{orange}\overrightarrow{\mathbf{v}}
\\
\color{currentColor}
\begin{aligned}
\color{currentColor}2\color{green}x\color{currentColor}+5\color{red}y\color{currentColor}+3\color{blue}z\color{currentColor}&=-3 \\
\color{currentColor}4\color{green}x\color{currentColor}+0\color{red}y\color{currentColor}+8\color{blue}z\color{currentColor}&=0 \\
\color{currentColor}1\color{green}x\color{currentColor}+3\color{red}y\color{currentColor}+0\color{blue}z\color{currentColor}&=2
\end{aligned}
\to

\underbrace{
\begin{bmatrix}
2&5&3\\
4&0&8\\
1&3&0
\end{bmatrix}
}_{\large A\vphantom{\hat{A}}}

\underbrace{
\begin{bmatrix}
\color{green}x \\
\color{red}y \\
\color{blue}z
\end{bmatrix}
}_{\large \color{purple}\overrightarrow{\mathbf{x}}}
=
\underbrace{
\begin{bmatrix}
-3 \\
0 \\
2
\end{bmatrix}
}_{\large \color{orange}\overrightarrow{\mathbf{v}}}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Just like in 2D, we can play a 3D transformation in reverse to find where the vector $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{x}}$ came from when it landed on $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{v}}$. | 和二维一样，我们可以把三维变换倒着放，找出向量 $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{x}}$ 落到 $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{v}}$ 之前是从哪儿来的。 |
| As long as the transformation for $A$ doesn't squish all of space into a lower dimension, meaning its determinant is not zero, there will be an inverse transformation $A^{-1}$. The inverse transformation has the property that if you first do $A$, then you do $A^{-1}$, it's the same as doing nothing. Multiplying that reverse transformation matrix by $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{v}}$, you get the answer $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{x}}$. | 只要 $A$ 的变换没有把整个空间压进更低的维度——也就是行列式非零——就会存在逆变换 $A^{-1}$。这个逆变换的性质是：先做 $A$、再做 $A^{-1}$，等于什么都没做。把那个反向的变换矩阵乘上 $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{v}}$，就得到答案 $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{x}}$。 |

$$
\det(A)\neq0\quad\to\quad A^{-1}\text{ exists}
$$

> - **interpretation** /ɪnˌtɜːprɪˈteɪʃn/ —— 解读。the geometric interpretation。
> - **exist** /ɪɡˈzɪst/ —— 存在。$A^{-1}$ exists。
> - *as long as* —— 只要。as long as the transformation doesn't squish space。
> - *translate to* —— 翻译成。translated to the geometric interpretation。

## 不可逆性（Irreversibility）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Up to this point, we've only mentioned the case when the determinant isn't zero. But when the determinant _is_ zero, and the transformation associated with the system of equations squishes space into a smaller dimension, there is no inverse. You cannot unsquish a line to turn it into a plane. At least, that's not something a _function_ can do. | 到目前为止，我们只提了行列式非零的情形。但当行列式*确实*为零、与方程组相应的变换把空间压进更小的维度时，逆是不存在的。你没法把一条直线「解压」回一个平面。至少，这不是*函数*能做到的事。 |
| That would require transforming each vector into a whole line of vectors, but functions can only take a single input to a single output. | 那需要把每个向量变换成整整一条线上的向量；但函数只能把单个输入对应到单个输出。 |
| Similarly, for 3 equations and 3 unknowns, there will be no inverse if the corresponding transformation squishes 3D space onto a plane, onto a line, or onto a point. Those all correspond to a determinant of zero, since any region is squished onto a zero-volume region. | 类似地，对三条方程、三个未知数来说，如果相应的变换把三维空间压到某个平面上、某条直线上、或某个点上，逆就不存在。这些全都对应行列式为零，因为任何区域都被压成了零体积的区域。 |
| Does $A^{-1}$ exist when $A=\begin{bmatrix}3&-1\\-9&3\end{bmatrix}$? | （译注：原文此处是一道互动测验的题干，候选为「Yes / No」，正确答案为 No。）当 $A=\begin{bmatrix}3&-1\\-9&3\end{bmatrix}$ 时，$A^{-1}$ 存在吗？ |
| The columns of this matrix are linearly dependent $-3\begin{bmatrix}-1\\3\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}3\\-9\end{bmatrix}$, so the determinant of $A$ is zero and $A^{-1}$ does not exist. | （译注：上题的答案解析。）这个矩阵的列线性相关（linearly dependent）：$-3\begin{bmatrix}-1\\3\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}3\\-9\end{bmatrix}$，所以 $A$ 的行列式为零，$A^{-1}$ 不存在。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 不可逆性
> - **irreversibility** /ˌɪrɪˌvɜːsəˈbɪləti/ —— 不可逆性。本节标题。
> - **unsquish** /ʌnˈskwɪʃ/ —— 「解压」、把压扁的还原（squish 的反义口语造词）。
> - **single** /ˈsɪŋɡl/ —— 单个的。a single input to a single output。
> - **linearly dependent** —— 线性相关的。the columns are linearly dependent。
> - *up to this point* —— 到目前为止。
> - *correspond to* —— 对应于。Those all correspond to a determinant of zero。

## 列空间（Column Space）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| It's still possible that a solution exists even when there is no inverse, it's just that when your transformation squishes space onto, say, a line, you have to be lucky enough to have the vector $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{v}}$ live somewhere on that line. | 就算没有逆，解仍然可能存在——只不过，当你的变换把空间压到（比如说）一条直线上时，你得足够走运，让向量 $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{v}}$ 恰好住在这条线上的某个地方。 |
| You might notice that some of these zero-determinant cases feel much more restrictive than others. Given a $3\times 3$ matrix, it seems much harder for a solution to exist when it squishes space onto a line compared to when it squishes things onto a plane. | 你可能注意到，同样是行列式为零，有些情形感觉比另一些限制强得多。给定一个 $3\times 3$ 矩阵，如果它把空间压到一条直线上，解存在的难度看起来远大于把它压到平面上的情形。 |
| We have some language that's a bit more specific than just saying zero-determinant. When the output of a transformation is a line, meaning it is one-dimensional, we say the transformation has a rank of $1$. | 我们有一些比光说「行列式为零」更精确的说法。当变换的输出是一条直线——也就是一维的——我们就说这个变换的秩（rank）是 $1$。 |
| If all the vectors land on some two-dimensional plane, we say the transformation has a rank of $2$. So the word "rank" means the number of dimensions in the output of a transformation. | 如果所有向量都落在某个二维平面上，我们就说这个变换的秩是 $2$。所以「秩」这个词，指的就是变换输出的维数。 |
| So for instance, in the case of $2\times 2$ matrices, rank $2$ is the best it can be, it means the basis vectors continue to span the full 2D space and the determinant is nonzero. But for $3\times 3$ matrices, rank $2$ means things have collapsed, but not as much as they would have for a rank $1$ transformation. | 比如说，对 $2\times 2$ 矩阵而言，秩 $2$ 已是上限，它意味着基向量仍张成（span）完整的二维空间、行列式非零。但对 $3\times 3$ 矩阵而言，秩 $2$ 意味着空间塌了，只是没有塌到秩 $1$ 的变换那种程度。 |
| If a 3D transform has a non-zero determinant and its output fills all of 3D space, it has a rank of $3$. This set of all possible outputs for your matrix, whether it's a line, a plane, or 3D space; is called the "column space" of your matrix. You can probably guess where the name comes from; the columns of your matrix tell you where the basis vectors land and the span of those transformed basis vectors gives you all possible outputs. In other words, the column space is the span of the columns of your matrix. | 如果一个三维变换的行列式非零、输出填满整个三维空间，它的秩就是 $3$。你的矩阵所有可能输出的集合——无论是一条直线、一个平面，还是三维空间——叫作这个矩阵的「列空间」（column space）。你大概能猜到这名字的来历：矩阵的列告诉你基向量落在哪儿，而这些变换后基向量的张成，就给出所有可能的输出。换句话说，列空间就是矩阵各列的张成。 |
| So a more precise definition of rank is that it's the number of dimensions in the column space. When this rank is as high as it can be, equaling the number of columns in the matrix, the matrix is called "full rank". | 所以秩更精确的定义是：列空间的维数。当秩达到它能达到的最大值——等于矩阵的列数——这个矩阵就叫「满秩」（full rank）。 |
| What is the rank of this matrix? $\begin{bmatrix}1&-2&4\\-2&4&-8\\5&-10&20\end{bmatrix}$ | （译注：原文此处是一道互动测验的题干，候选为 Rank 0 / Rank 1 / Rank 2 / Rank 3（full rank），正确答案是 Rank 1。）这个矩阵的秩是多少？$\begin{bmatrix}1&-2&4\\-2&4&-8\\5&-10&20\end{bmatrix}$ |
| The columns of this matrix can be expressed as $\begin{bmatrix}1\\-2\\5\end{bmatrix}=-\frac12\begin{bmatrix}-2\\4\\-10\end{bmatrix}=\frac14\begin{bmatrix}4\\-8\\20\end{bmatrix}$. Since they are all linearly dependent and form a line, the rank is $1$. | （译注：上题的答案解析。）这个矩阵的列可以表示为 $\begin{bmatrix}1\\-2\\5\end{bmatrix}=-\frac12\begin{bmatrix}-2\\4\\-10\end{bmatrix}=\frac14\begin{bmatrix}4\\-8\\20\end{bmatrix}$。既然它们全都线性相关、形成一条直线，秩就是 $1$。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 列空间
> - **rank** /ræŋk/ —— 秩。the number of dimensions in the output。
> - **column space** —— 列空间。the span of the columns of your matrix。
> - **span** /spæn/ —— 张成。the basis vectors continue to span the full 2D space。
> - **restrictive** /rɪˈstrɪktɪv/ —— 限制性强的。much more restrictive than others。
> - **collapse** /kəˈlæps/ —— 塌缩。things have collapsed。
> - **full rank** —— 满秩。equaling the number of columns。
> - *say* —— 比如说（口语插入语）。squishes space onto, say, a line。

## 零空间（Null Space）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Notice the zero vector will always be included in the column space since linear transformations must keep the origin fixed in place. | 注意，零向量永远会包含在列空间里，因为线性变换必须让原点固定在原地。 |
| For a full-rank transformation, the only vector that lands at the origin is the zero vector itself. But for matrices that aren't full rank, which squish to a smaller dimension, you can have a whole bunch of vectors land on zero. If a 2D transformation squishes space onto a line, there is a separate line in a different direction full of vectors that get squished on the origin. | 对满秩变换来说，落在原点的向量只有零向量自己。但对不满秩的矩阵——也就是压到更小维度的那些——你可以有一大堆向量落到零上。如果一个二维变换把空间压到一条直线上，那么在另一个方向上，会有一条独立的直线，装满了被压到原点的向量。 |
| If a 3D transformation squishes space onto a plane, there is a line full of vectors that land on the origin. | 如果一个三维变换把空间压到一个平面上，就会有一条装满「落到原点的向量」的直线。 |
| If a 3D transformation squishes all of space onto a line, there is a whole _plane_ full of vectors that land on the origin. | 如果一个三维变换把整个空间压到一条直线上，就会有整整一个*平面*，装满落到原点的向量。 |
| This set of vectors that land on the origin is called the "null space" or the "kernel" of your matrix. It's the space of vectors that becomes null, in the sense that they land on the zero vector. In terms of the linear system of equations, if $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{v}}$ happens to be the zero vector, the null space gives you all possible solutions to the equation. | 这些落到原点的向量的集合，叫作你矩阵的「零空间」（null space）或「核」（kernel）。它是「变成零（null）」的向量所构成的空间——所谓变成零，是说它们落在零向量上。放到线性方程组的语境里：如果 $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{v}}$ 恰好是零向量，那么零空间就给出了方程所有可能的解。 |
| What does the null space of this matrix look like? $\begin{bmatrix}1&-2&4\\-2&4&-8\\5&-10&20\end{bmatrix}$ | （译注：原文此处是一道互动测验的题干，候选为「仅零向量 / 一条直线 / 一个平面 / 整个三维空间」，正确答案是「一个平面」。）这个矩阵的零空间长什么样？$\begin{bmatrix}1&-2&4\\-2&4&-8\\5&-10&20\end{bmatrix}$ |
| The columns of this matrix can be expressed as $\begin{bmatrix}1\\-2\\5\end{bmatrix}=-\frac12\begin{bmatrix}-2\\4\\-10\end{bmatrix}=\frac14\begin{bmatrix}4\\-8\\20\end{bmatrix}$. Since they are all linearly dependent and form a line, the rank is $1$. This means an entire plane gets squished to the origin. The dimensionality of the null space is inversely proportional to the rank of the matrix. $3\text{ (size of matrix)}-1\text{ (rank)}=2\text{ (size of null space)}$. | （译注：上题的答案解析。）这个矩阵的列可以表示为 $\begin{bmatrix}1\\-2\\5\end{bmatrix}=-\frac12\begin{bmatrix}-2\\4\\-10\end{bmatrix}=\frac14\begin{bmatrix}4\\-8\\20\end{bmatrix}$。既然它们全都线性相关、形成一条直线，秩就是 $1$。这意味着整个平面被压到了原点上。零空间的维数与矩阵的秩成反比：$3\text{（矩阵的尺寸）}-1\text{（秩）}=2\text{（零空间的尺寸）}$。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 零空间
> - **null space / kernel** —— 零空间 / 核。the space of vectors that becomes null。
> - **null** /nʌl/ —— 零、空。becomes null。
> - **a whole bunch of** —— 一大堆。a whole bunch of vectors。
> - **dimensionality** /ˌdɪmenʃəˈnæləti/ —— 维数。the dimensionality of the null space。
> - **inversely proportional** —— 成反比的。inversely proportional to the rank。
> - *happen to be* —— 恰好是。if $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{v}}$ happens to be the zero vector。
> - *in terms of* —— 放到……的语境里、就……而言。in terms of the linear system。

Otherwise, the ideas of column space and null space let us know when there is a solution, and what the set of all possible solutions can look like. | 以上就是从几何角度思考线性方程组的一份非常概览式的介绍。每个方程组都有一个线性变换与它对应。当那个变换有逆时，你可以用逆来解方程组；否则，列空间与零空间的想法让我们知道解何时存在、所有可能解的集合会长什么样。 |
