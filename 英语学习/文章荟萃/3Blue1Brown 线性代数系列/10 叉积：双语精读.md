## 二维（Two dimensions）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| We'll start in two dimensions. If you have two vectors, $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$, think about the parallelogram that they span out. What I mean by this is that if you take a copy of $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$, and move its tail to the tip of w, and you take a copy of $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$, and move its tail to the tip of $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$, the four vectors enclose a certain parallelogram. | 我们从二维讲起。如果你有两个向量 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$，想一想它们张成（span out）的那个平行四边形。我的意思是：取一份 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 的拷贝，把它的尾移到 w 的箭头处；再取一份 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 的拷贝，把它的尾移到 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 的箭头处——这四个向量就围出了一个平行四边形。 |
| The cross product of $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$, written with the x-shaped multiplication symbol ($\times$), is the area of this parallelogram. | $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 的叉积，写作 x 形的乘号（$\times$），就是这个平行四边形的面积。 |

| Notice, this means order matters. ## 用行列式计算（Compute with determinant）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Of course, you want to be able to compute this without someone telling you the area. This is where the determinant comes in from chapter 5. For the 2d cross product $\vec{\mathbf{v}} \times \vec{\mathbf{w}}$, you write the coordinates of $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ as the first column of a matrix, write the coordinates of $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ as the second column, then you compute the determinant. | 当然，你会希望在没有别人告诉你面积的情况下自己算出它。这就轮到第 5 章的行列式（determinant）登场了。要算二维叉积 $\vec{\mathbf{v}} \times \vec{\mathbf{w}}$，你把 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 的坐标写成矩阵的第一列，把 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 的坐标写成第二列，然后算出行列式。 |
| This is because a matrix whose columns represent $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ corresponds with a linear transformation that moves the basis vectors $\hat{\imath}$ and $\hat{\jmath}$ to $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$. | 这是因为，一个列代表 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 的矩阵，对应着一个把基向量 $\hat{\imath}$ 和 $\hat{\jmath}$ 移到 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 的线性变换。 |
| The determinant is all about measuring how areas change due to a transformation, and the prototypical area to look at is the unit square resting on $\hat{\imath}$ and $\hat{\jmath}$. After the transformation, that square turns into the parallelogram we care about. So the determinant, which generally measures the factor by which areas are changed, gives the area of this parallelogram, since it evolved from a square that started with area 1. | 行列式量的正是一个变换会让面积如何变化；而最值得看的那块典型面积，就是架在 $\hat{\imath}$ 和 $\hat{\jmath}$ 上的单位正方形。变换之后，那个正方形变成了我们关心的这个平行四边形。所以，一般用来测量面积变化倍数的行列式，给出的就是这个平行四边形的面积——因为它从一个面积为 1 的正方形演化而来。 |
| What's more, if $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ is a _clockwise_ rotation away from $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$, it means orientation is flipped during the transformation, which is what it means for a determinant to be negative. | 而且，如果 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 是从 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 出发的*顺时针*旋转，就说明定向在变换过程中被翻转了——这正是行列式为负的含义。 |
| Since the orientation is flipped, the determinant is negative. | （译注：图注。）由于定向被翻转了，行列式为负。 |
| Since the orientation isn't flipped, the determinant is positive. | （译注：图注。）由于定向没有被翻转，行列式为正。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 用行列式计算
> - **compute** /kəmˈpjuːt/ —— 计算。compute this without someone telling you the area。
> - **prototypical** /prəˈtɒtɪpɪkl/ —— 典型的、最具代表性的。the prototypical area to look at。
> - **flip** /flɪp/ —— 翻转。orientation is flipped during the transformation。
> - **evolve** /ɪˈvɒlv/ —— 演化、变来。since it evolved from a square that started with area 1。
> - *come in* —— 登场、派上用场。This is where the determinant comes in。
> - *rest on* —— 架在……之上。the unit square resting on $\hat{\imath}$ and $\hat{\jmath}$。

## 行列式示例（Determinant example）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| For example, if $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ has coordinates $\begin{bmatrix}-3\\1\end{bmatrix}$, and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ has coordinates $\begin{bmatrix}2\\1\end{bmatrix}$, the determinant is: | 比如，若 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 的坐标是 $\begin{bmatrix}-3\\1\end{bmatrix}$，$\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 的坐标是 $\begin{bmatrix}2\\1\end{bmatrix}$，那么行列式就是： |

$$
\operatorname{det}\left(\left[\begin{array}{cc}-3 & 2 \\ 1 & 1\end{array}\right]\right)=(-3)\cdot1 - 2\cdot1 = -5.00
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Evidently the area of the parallelogram formed by the two vectors is $5$, and since $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ is a clockwise rotation away from $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ it should make sense that this value is negative. | 显然，这两个向量形成的平行四边形面积是 $5$；又因为 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 是从 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 出发顺时针旋转得到的，这个值为负就说得通了。 |
| I'd recommend playing around with this notion a bit in your head to get an intuitive feel for the cross product. | 我建议你在脑子里把这个概念多摆弄摆弄，好对叉积找到一种直觉上的手感。 |
| What is result of of $\vec{\mathbf{v}} \times \vec{\mathbf{w}}$ where $\vec{\mathbf{v}} = \begin{bmatrix}1\\-2\end{bmatrix}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}} = \begin{bmatrix}4\\3\end{bmatrix}$? | （译注：原文此处是一道互动测验的题干，候选为 5 / −11 / 11 / 10 四个选项，正确答案为 11，配图见官网原页。）当 $\vec{\mathbf{v}} = \begin{bmatrix}1\\-2\end{bmatrix}$、$\vec{\mathbf{w}} = \begin{bmatrix}4\\3\end{bmatrix}$ 时，$\vec{\mathbf{v}} \times \vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 的结果是多少？ |
| To compute the cross product calculate the determinant of the transformation matrix. | （译注：上题的答案解析。）要计算叉积，就算出变换矩阵的行列式。 |

$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec{\mathbf{v}} \times \vec{\mathbf{w}} &=\operatorname{det}\left(\left[\begin{array}{cc}1 & 4 \\ -2 & 3\end{array}\right]\right) \\\rule{0pt}{2em}
\vec{\mathbf{v}} \times \vec{\mathbf{w}} &=1(3)-4(-2) \\\rule{0pt}{2em}
\vec{\mathbf{v}} \times \vec{\mathbf{w}} &=11
\end{aligned}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| We can gut check our answer by drawing the diagram, and seeing if $11$ feels like a reasonable area, and whether it makes sense that it's positive. | 我们可以做个直觉校验（gut check）：把图画出来，看看 $11$ 这个面积感觉上合不合理，以及它为正是否说得通。 |
| We know the cross product is equal to area of the parallelogram formed by the two vectors and since $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ is a counterclockwise rotation away from $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ we also know the result will be positive. | 我们知道，叉积等于这两个向量所形成的平行四边形的面积；由于 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 是从 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 出发逆时针旋转得到的，我们还知道结果会是正的。 |
| Which corresponds to the determinant of the transformation matrix that sends $\hat{\imath} \to \begin{bmatrix}1\\-2\end{bmatrix}$ and $\hat{\jmath} \to \begin{bmatrix}4\\3\end{bmatrix}$. | 这对应的是那个变换矩阵的行列式：它把 $\hat{\imath}$ 变到 $\begin{bmatrix}1\\-2\end{bmatrix}$，把 $\hat{\jmath}$ 变到 $\begin{bmatrix}4\\3\end{bmatrix}$。 |

## 性质（Properties）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| For example, you might notice that when two vectors are perpendicular, or close to being perpendicular, their cross product is larger than it would be if they were pointing in a very similar direction, because the area of that parallelogram is larger when its sides are perpendicular. | 比如，你可能会注意到：当两个向量相互垂直、或接近垂直时，它们的叉积要比它们指向非常相近的方向时更大，因为当平行四边形的边相互垂直时，它的面积更大。 |
| $\vec{\mathbf{v}} \times \vec{\mathbf{w}}$ is bigger when the vectors are more perpendicular to eachother and smaller when the vectors are pointing in a similar direction. | （译注：图注。）当两个向量彼此更接近垂直时，$\vec{\mathbf{v}} \times \vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 更大；当它们指向相近的方向时，$\vec{\mathbf{v}} \times \vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 更小。 |
| Something else you might notice is that if you scale up one of the vectors, perhaps multiplying $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ by $3$, then the area of that parallelogram is scaled up by a factor of three, so $(3\vec{\mathbf{v}}) \times \vec{\mathbf{w}} = 3 (\vec{\mathbf{v}} \times \vec{\mathbf{w}})$. | 你可能还会注意到另一件事：如果把其中一个向量放大，比如把 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 乘以 $3$，那么那个平行四边形的面积也会放大 3 倍，于是 $(3\vec{\mathbf{v}}) \times \vec{\mathbf{w}} = 3 (\vec{\mathbf{v}} \times \vec{\mathbf{w}})$。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 性质
> - **property** /ˈprɒpəti/ —— 性质。本节讲叉积的两条性质。
> - **perpendicular** /ˌpɜːpənˈdɪkjələ/ —— 垂直的。when two vectors are perpendicular。
> - **scale up** —— 放大、按比例放大。scale up one of the vectors。
> - **factor** /ˈfæktə/ —— 倍数。scaled up by a factor of three。
> - *point in* —— 指向。pointing in a very similar direction。

## 标准三维视角（Standard 3d View）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Now, even though everything we just did is a perfectly fine mathematical operation, what I just described is technically not the cross product. The true cross product is something that combines two 3d vectors to get a new 3d vector. | 不过，尽管我们刚才做的一切都是完全正当的数学运算，我刚才描述的那个东西，严格说来并不是叉积。真正的叉积，是把两个三维向量结合成一个新三维向量的东西。 |

$$
\left[\begin{array}{l}
v_1 \\
v_2 \\
v_3
\end{array}\right]
\times
\left[\begin{array}{l}
w_1 \\
w_2 \\
w_3
\end{array}\right]
=
\left[\begin{array}{l}
p_1 \\
p_2 \\
p_3
\end{array}\right]
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| You still consider the parallelogram defined by the two vectors, and the area of that parallelogram still plays a big role. To be concrete the area is $9$ units for the vectors shown here. | 你仍然考虑由这两个向量确定的平行四边形，那个平行四边形的面积也仍然扮演着重要角色。具体一点：对这里画出的向量，面积是 $9$ 个单位。 |
| The 3d cross product does not return a number, it returns a vector. This new vector's length will be the area of that parallelogram, which in this example is $9$, and the direction of that new vector is perpendicular to that parallelogram. | 三维叉积返回的不是一个数，而是一个向量。这个新向量的长度将是那个平行四边形的面积——在这个例子里是 $9$——而这个新向量的方向垂直于那个平行四边形。 |
| But which way? There are two possible vectors with length $9$ pointing perpendicular to a given plane. This is where the right-hand rule comes in. Point the forefinger of your right hand in the direction of $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$, and stick out your middle finger in the direction of $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$. Now when you point up your thumb, that's the direction of the cross product. | 但朝哪一边呢？长度为 $9$、垂直于给定平面的向量有两个可能。这就轮到右手定则（right-hand rule）出场了：把右手食指指向 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 的方向，中指朝 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 的方向伸出。这时当你竖起大拇指，它指的就是叉积的方向。 |
| For example, let $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ be the vector with length $2$ in the $z$ direction, and let $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ be the vector with length $2$ in the $y$ direction. The parallelogram they define in this simple example is actually a square, since they're perpendicular, and the area of that square is $4$. Using the right hand rule, their cross product should point in the negative $x$ direction, so this cross product is $-4 \hat{\imath}$. | 比如，设 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 是沿 $z$ 方向、长度为 $2$ 的向量，$\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 是沿 $y$ 方向、长度为 $2$ 的向量。在这个简单的例子里，它们确定的平行四边形其实是个正方形，因为它们相互垂直，而这个正方形的面积是 $4$。用右手定则，它们的叉积应该指向 $x$ 负方向，所以这个叉积是 $-4 \hat{\imath}$。 |
| What is $\vec{\mathbf{v}} \times \vec{\mathbf{w}}$ where $\vec{\mathbf{v}} = \left[\begin{array}{c}  2  \\  0  \\  0  \end{array}\right]$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}} = \left[\begin{array}{c}  0  \\  -3  \\  0  \end{array}\right]$? | （译注：原文此处是一道互动测验的题干，候选为四个三维列向量，正确答案为 $\left[\begin{array}{c} 0 \\ 0 \\ -6 \end{array}\right]$，配图见官网原页。）当 $\vec{\mathbf{v}} = \left[\begin{array}{c} 2 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{array}\right]$、$\vec{\mathbf{w}} = \left[\begin{array}{c} 0 \\ -3 \\ 0 \end{array}\right]$ 时，$\vec{\mathbf{v}} \times \vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 是多少？ |
| Using what we know about computing the cross product so far you can draw the parallelogram formed between $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$. | （译注：上题的答案解析。）用目前我们关于叉积计算的知识，你可以画出 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 之间形成的平行四边形。 |
| Which, in this case, is a rectangle with an area of $6$ units. So we know the resulting vector $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$ will have a length of $6$ and will be perpendicular to the $xy$-coordinate plane, so it will lie either on the positive $z$-axis or the negative $z$-axis. Applying the right-hand rule tell us that it is the vector pointing in the negative direction on the $z$-axis. | 在这个例子里，它是一个面积为 $6$ 个单位的矩形。于是我们知道，所得向量 $\vec{\mathbf{p}}$ 的长度是 $6$，并且垂直于 $xy$ 坐标平面，所以它要么躺在 $z$ 正半轴上，要么躺在 $z$ 负半轴上。应用右手定则告诉我们：它是指向 $z$ 轴负方向的那个向量。 |
| Writing this out gives us the following computation. | 把这些写出来，就得到下面的计算。 |

$$
\left[\begin{array}{c}  2  \\  0  \\  0  \end{array}\right]
\times
\left[\begin{array}{c}  0  \\  -3  \\  0  \end{array}\right]
=
\left[\begin{array}{c}  0  \\  0  \\  -6  \end{array}\right]
$$

> - **return** /rɪˈtɜːn/ —— 返回。The 3d cross product does not return a number。
> - **forefinger** /ˈfɔːfɪŋɡə/ —— 食指。Point the forefinger of your right hand。

## 计算（Computing）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| There is a formula you could memorize for this cross product: | 关于这个叉积，有一条你可以背下来的公式： |

$$
\left[\begin{array}{l}
v_1 \\
v_2 \\
v_3
\end{array}\right] \times\left[\begin{array}{l}
w_1 \\
w_2 \\
w_3
\end{array}\right]=\left[\begin{array}{l}
v_2 \cdot w_3-w_2 \cdot v_3 \\
v_3 \cdot w_1-w_3 \cdot v_1 \\
v_1 \cdot w_2-w_1 \cdot v_2
\end{array}\right]
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| However, it's common and easier to remember a certain process involving the 3d determinant. This process looks truly strange at first. Write a 3d matrix where the second column is the coordinates of $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$, and the third column is the coordinates $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$. Then for the first column, write in the basis vector $\hat{\imath}$, $\hat{\jmath}$, and $\hat{k}$. Then, you compute the determinant of this matrix. | 然而，更常见也更好记的，是某个涉及三维行列式的操作流程。这个流程乍看之下真是奇怪。写一个 3d 矩阵：第二列是 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 的坐标，第三列是 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 的坐标；然后在第一列，写上基向量 $\hat{\imath}$、$\hat{\jmath}$ 和 $\hat{k}$。接着，你算出这个矩阵的行列式。 |
| The silliness is probably clear here. What on earth does it mean to put a vector in as the entry to a matrix? Students are often told that this is just a notational trick; when you carry out all the computations as if $\hat{\imath}$, $\hat{\jmath}$ and $\hat{k}$ were numbers. Then you get some linear combination of those basis vectors, and the vector defined by that linear combination, students are told to just believe, is the unique vector perpendicular to $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ whose magnitude is the area of the appropriate parallelogram, and whose direction obeys the right-hand-rule. | 这里的荒唐之处大概一目了然：把一个向量当作矩阵的条目塞进去，到底算什么意思？学生们常被告知，这只是一个记号上的花招：当你假装 $\hat{\imath}$、$\hat{\jmath}$ 和 $\hat{k}$ 是数字、把全部计算照做下去。然后你会得到那些基向量的某个线性组合；而这个线性组合所定义的向量——学生们被要求只管相信——就是唯一一个垂直于 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 和 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$、大小等于相应平行四边形面积、方向服从右手定则的向量。 |
| And sure, in some sense this is a notational trick, but there is a reason for doing it. It's not just a coincidence that the determinant is once again important, and putting the basis vectors in these slots is not just random. To understand where this all comes from, it helps to use the idea of duality that I introduced in the last chapter. This concept is a bit heavy, though, so I'm putting it into a separate follow-on chapter for those of you curious to learn more. | 的确，从某种意义上说这是个记号花招，但这么做是有原因的。行列式再次变得重要，绝非巧合；把基向量放进这些格子里，也绝非随机为之。要理解这一切从何而来，得用上我在上一章介绍的对偶性（duality）思想。不过这个概念有点重，所以我把它单独放进了紧随其后的一章，给那些想学得更深的读者。 |
| Arguably, it falls outside the essence of linear algebra, so you can feel comfortable if you want to skip that next chapter and go straight to the lesson on Cramer's Rule. But for those of you willing to go a bit deeper, it's just really cool. | 可以说，它落在线性代数的本质之外；所以如果你想跳过下一章、直接去看克莱姆法则（Cramer's Rule）那一课，完全不必有心理负担。但对于愿意再往下走一步的读者，它真是酷极了。 |
