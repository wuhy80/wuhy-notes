| For instance, two vectors of length two dotted together looks like: | 比如，两个长度为 2 的向量点在一起，是下面这样： |

$$
\left[\begin{array}{l}1 \\ 2\end{array}\right] \cdot \left[\begin{array}{l}3 \\ 4\end{array}\right] = 1 \cdot 3+2 \cdot 4 = 11
$$

|---|---|

## 几何解释（Geometric Interpretation）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Luckily, this computation has a nice geometric interpretation. To think about the dot product of two vectors $\mathbf{v}$ and $\mathbf{w}$, imagine projecting $\mathbf{w}$ onto the line that passes through the origin and the tip of $\mathbf{v}$. | 幸运的是，这个计算有一个漂亮的几何解释。要想两个向量 $\mathbf{v}$ 和 $\mathbf{w}$ 的点积，就想象把 $\mathbf{w}$ 投影（project）到一条直线上——这条直线穿过原点和 $\mathbf{v}$ 的尖端。 |
| Multiply the length of this projection by the length of $\mathbf{v}$, and you have the dot product $\mathbf{v}$ dot $\mathbf{w}$. | 把这个投影的长度乘以 $\mathbf{v}$ 的长度，你就得到了点积 $\mathbf{v}$ 点乘 $\mathbf{w}$。 |
| When the projection of $\mathbf{w}$ is pointing in the opposite direction from $\mathbf{v}$, the dot product will actually be negative. | 当 $\mathbf{w}$ 的投影指向与 $\mathbf{v}$ 相反的方向时，点积实际上会是负的。 |
| Notice how this means the sign of a dot product tells you how much two vectors $\mathbf{v}$ and $\mathbf{w}$ align. | 注意，这就意味着：点积的符号告诉你，两个向量 $\mathbf{v}$ 和 $\mathbf{w}$ 有多对齐（align）。 |

## 顺序无关紧要（Order Doesn't Matter）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Geometric interpretation recap. | （译注：图注。）几何解释小结。 |
| This geometric interpretation is very asymmetric, in that it treats the two vectors very differently. When I first learned this I was surprised that order doesn't matter, that you could instead project $\mathbf{v}$ onto $\mathbf{w}$ and multiply the length of the projected $\mathbf{v}$ times the length of $\mathbf{w}$ and get the same result. | 这个几何解释是非常不对称的：它对两个向量的处理方式很不一样。我当年第一次学到这一点时很惊讶——顺序居然无关紧要：你完全可以反过来，把 $\mathbf{v}$ 投影到 $\mathbf{w}$ 上，把投影后的 $\mathbf{v}$ 的长度乘以 $\mathbf{w}$ 的长度，得到同样的结果。 |
| Even though this feels like a very different process, it produces the same result. | （译注：图注。）尽管这感觉起来是很不一样的过程，它给出的结果相同。 |
| Here's the intuition for why order doesn't matter: If $\mathbf{v}$ and $\mathbf{w}$ are the same length, we can leverage symmetry, since projecting $\mathbf{w}$ onto $\mathbf{v}$ and multiplying the length of the projection by the length of $\mathbf{v}$ is a complete mirror image of projection $\mathbf{v}$ onto $\mathbf{w}$ and multiplying the length of the projection by the length of $\mathbf{w}$. | 以下是「顺序为什么无关紧要」的直觉：如果 $\mathbf{v}$ 和 $\mathbf{w}$ 长度相同，我们就能利用对称性——把 $\mathbf{w}$ 投影到 $\mathbf{v}$ 上、再把投影长度乘以 $\mathbf{v}$ 的长度，与把 $\mathbf{v}$ 投影到 $\mathbf{w}$ 上、再把投影长度乘以 $\mathbf{w}$ 的长度，完全是彼此的镜像。 |
| Now, if you scale one of them, say $\mathbf{v}$, by some constant like $2$ so that they don't have equal length, the symmetry is broken, but let's think through how to interpret the dot product between this new vector ($2$ times $\mathbf{v}$) and $\mathbf{w}$. | 现在，如果你把其中之一——比如 $\mathbf{v}$——缩放某个常数倍，比如 $2$ 倍，让两者长度不再相等，对称性就被打破了。但我们来想清楚一下：该怎么理解这个新向量（$2$ 倍的 $\mathbf{v}$）与 $\mathbf{w}$ 之间的点积？ |
| If you think of $\mathbf{w}$ as projected onto $\mathbf{v}$, the dot product $(2\mathbf{v})$ dot $\mathbf{w}$ will be exactly twice the dot product $\mathbf{v}$ dot $\mathbf{w}$. This is because when you scale $\mathbf{v}$ by $2$, it doesn't change the length of the projection of $\mathbf{w}$, but it doubles the length of the vector that you're projected onto. | 如果你把 $\mathbf{w}$ 想成被投影到 $\mathbf{v}$ 上，那么点积 $(2\mathbf{v})$ 点乘 $\mathbf{w}$ 会恰好是点积 $\mathbf{v}$ 点乘 $\mathbf{w}$ 的两倍。这是因为当你把 $\mathbf{v}$ 缩放 $2$ 倍时，$\mathbf{w}$ 投影的长度没变，变的是你投影所落上去的那个向量的长度——它翻倍了。 |
| The length of projecting $\mathbf{w}$ onto $2\mathbf{v}$ stays the same, while the length of $2\mathbf{v}$ doubles. | （译注：图注。）把 $\mathbf{w}$ 投影到 $2\mathbf{v}$ 上，投影长度保持不变，而 $2\mathbf{v}$ 的长度翻倍。 |
| On the other hand, let's say you were thinking about $\mathbf{v}$ as getting projected onto $\mathbf{w}$. In that case, the length of the projection is the thing that gets scaled as we multiply $\mathbf{v}$ by $2$, while the length of the vector you're projecting onto stays constant, so the overall effect is still to just double the dot product. | 另一方面，假设你想的是让 $\mathbf{v}$ 被投影到 $\mathbf{w}$ 上。在这种看法下，当我们把 $\mathbf{v}$ 乘以 $2$ 时，被缩放的是投影的长度，而你投影所落上去的向量长度保持不变，所以整体效果仍然只是把点积加倍。 |
| The length of projecting $2\mathbf{v}$ onto $\mathbf{w}$ doubles, while the length of $\mathbf{w}$ stays the same. | （译注：图注。）把 $2\mathbf{v}$ 投影到 $\mathbf{w}$ 上，投影长度翻倍，而 $\mathbf{w}$ 的长度保持不变。 |
| So even though symmetry is broken, the effect this scaling has on the value of the dot product is the same under both interpretations. | 所以，尽管对称性被打破了，这个缩放对点积数值造成的影响，在两种理解下是一样的。 |
| There's also another big question other than the order: Why on earth does this numerical process of matching coordinates, multiplying pairs, and adding them together have anything to do with projection? To give a satisfactory answer, and to do full justice to the significance of the dot product, we need to unearth something deeper going on here, which often goes by the name "duality." Before getting to that, I need to spend some time talking about linear transformations from multiple dimensions to one dimension, which is the number line. | 除了顺序之外，还有另一个大问题：这个「配对坐标、逐对相乘、再相加」的数值过程，到底为什么会和投影扯上关系？要给出一个令人满意的答案，要充分阐明点积的重要性，我们需要挖掘出这里更深一层的东西——它通常被称作「对偶性」（duality）。在讲到那之前，我需要花点时间谈谈从多维到一维——也就是数轴——的线性变换。 |
| These are functions that take in 2d vectors and spit out numbers, but because the transformation is linear these functions are much more restricted than your run-of-the-mill function with a 2d input and a 1d output. | 这些函数吃进二维向量、吐出数字；但由于变换是线性的，比起你随手碰到的「二维输入、一维输出」的普通函数，它们要受限制得多。 |

> - **asymmetric** /ˌeɪsɪˈmetrɪk/ —— 不对称的。This geometric interpretation is very asymmetric。

|---|---|
| As with transformations in higher dimensions there are some formal properties that makes one of these functions linear. This was discussed in chapter 3, so definitely go check that out if you haven't already. | 和高维情形一样，也有一些形式化性质决定这类函数是不是线性的。这在第 3 章讨论过，如果你还没看过，一定要去读一读。 |
| Example linear transformation and properties from chapter 3. | （译注：图注。）来自第 3 章的线性变换示例及其性质。 |
| I'm going to purposefully ignore these properties here so as to not distract from our end goal, and instead focus on certain visual property that's equivalent to all the formal stuff. If you take any line of evenly spaced dots, and apply the transformation, a linear transformation will keep those dots evenly spaced once that land in the output space, which is the number line. | 这里我会有意忽略这些性质，免得分散我们对最终目标的注意力，转而聚焦于某个视觉上的性质——它与所有那些形式化的东西等价。如果你取任意一条等距点列，施加这个变换，线性变换会让这些点在落进输出空间——也就是数轴——之后仍然保持等距。 |
| If you imagine applying the linear transformation $L\left(\vec{\mathrm{v}}\right)$ to the collection of vectors represented by the yellow dots, the line of dots remains evenly spaced in the output space. | （译注：图注。）如果你想象把线性变换 $L\left(\vec{\mathrm{v}}\right)$ 应用到这些黄点所代表的向量集合上，这条点列在输出空间里仍然保持等距。 |
| Of course, this means that if there is some line of dots that is unevenly spaced, then the transformation is not linear. | 当然，这也意味着：如果某条点列落下去之后间距变得不均匀，那这个变换就不是线性的。 |
| As with the cases we've seen before, one of these linear transformations is completely determined by where $\hat{\imath}$ and $\hat{\jmath}$ land, but this time each one simply lands on a number. So when we record where they land as the columns of a matrix, each of those columns just has a single number. | 和我们之前见过的情况一样，这类线性变换完全由 $\hat{\imath}$ 和 $\hat{\jmath}$ 落在哪里决定，只不过这一次，每一个都落在一个数上。所以，当我们把它们的落点记录成矩阵的列时，每一列就只有一个数。 |
| This linear transformation function is a 1x2 matrix. | （译注：图注。）这个线性变换函数是一个 1×2 矩阵。 |
| Let's walk through an example of what it means to apply one of these transformations to a vector. Let's say you have a linear transformation that takes i hat to $2$, and j hat to $-1$. To follow where a vector with coordinates, say, $\left[\begin{array}{l}3 \\ 2\end{array}\right]$ ends up, think of breaking up this vector as $3$ times i hat plus $2$ times j hat. | 我们来完整走一遍例子，看看「把这类变换应用到一个向量上」是什么意思。假设你有一个线性变换，它把 i hat 变到 $2$、把 j hat 变到 $-1$。要追踪一个坐标为 $\left[\begin{array}{l}3 \\ 2\end{array}\right]$ 的向量最终落到哪里，就想成把这个向量拆成 $3$ 倍的 i hat 加上 $2$ 倍的 j hat。 |
| If we call our linear transformation $L$, we can use the linearity to figure out where our vector goes. | 如果把我们这个线性变换叫作 $L$，我们就能利用线性性算出我们的向量去哪儿了。 |

$$
L(3 \hat{\imath} + 2 \hat{\jmath}) = 3 L(\hat{\imath}) + 2 L(\hat{\jmath}) = 3(2) + 2(-1) = 4
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| When you do this calculation purely numerically, it's matrix vector multiplication. This numerical operation of multiplying a 1-by-2 matrix by a vector feels just like taking the dot product of two vectors. | 纯从数值上看，这个计算就是矩阵-向量乘法。「1×2 矩阵乘向量」这个数值运算，感觉起来就像在取两个向量的点积。 |

But this suggests something truly awesome for the geometric view: There's some kind of connection between linear transformations that take vectors to numbers, and vectors themselves. | 既然我们看的是数值表示，在向量和 1×2 矩阵之间来回倒腾，可能感觉像是件傻事。但这为几何视角暗示了某种真正令人惊叹的东西：「把向量变成数字的线性变换」与「向量本身」之间存在某种联系。 |

## 单位向量（Unit Vector）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Imagine that you don't already know that dot products relate to projection. Now, what I'm going to do here is take a copy of the number line and place it diagonally in space somehow, with the number $0$ sitting on the origin. There's some two-dimensional unit vector whose tip sits where the number $1$ on the number line is, and I want to give that guy a name: u hat. | 想象一下，你还不知道点积与投影有关。现在，我要做的是：取一条数轴的副本，以某种方式把它斜着放进空间里，让数字 $0$ 落在原点上。有一个二维单位向量（unit vector），它的尖端恰好停在数轴上数字 $1$ 所在的位置，我想给这家伙起个名字：u hat。 |
| This little guy plays an important role in what's about to happen, so just keep him in the back of your mind. If we project 2d vectors onto this diagonal number line, in effect we've just defined a function that takes 2d vectors to numbers. | 这个小家伙在接下来要发生的事情里扮演重要角色，所以先把他记在脑子后头。如果我们把二维向量投影到这条斜放的数轴上，实际上我们就刚刚定义了一个「把二维向量变成数」的函数。 |
| Some example vectors. | （译注：图注。）一些示例向量。 |
| The example vectors projected onto the line defined by $\hat{\mathbf{u}}$. | （译注：图注。）这些示例向量投影到 $\hat{\mathbf{u}}$ 所定义的直线上。 |
| What's more, this function is actually linear, since it passes our visual test that any line of evenly spaced dots remains evenly spaced once it lands on the number line. | 而且，这个函数实际上是线性的——因为它通过了我们的视觉检验：任何一条等距点列，落进数轴之后仍然保持等距。 |
| Just to be clear, even though I've embedded the number line in 2d space like this, the outputs of this function are numbers, not 2d vectors. You should think of a function that takes in two coordinates, and outputs a single coordinate. | 说清楚一点：尽管我像这样把数轴嵌进了二维空间，这个函数的输出是数，而不是二维向量。你应该把它想成一个吃进两个坐标、吐出单个坐标的函数。 |
| But the vector u hat is a two-dimensional vector, living in the input space, it's just situated in a way that overlaps with the embedding of the number line. We just defined a linear transformation from 2d vectors to numbers, so you can find some 1x2 matrix that describes this transformation. | 但向量 u hat 是一个二维向量，生活在输入空间里，只不过它摆放的位置恰好与数轴的嵌入相互重叠。我们刚刚定义了一个从二维向量到数的线性变换，所以你能找到一个描述它的 1×2 矩阵。 |
| To find that 1x2 matrix, let's zoom in on our diagonal number line setup and think about where i-hat and j-hat go, since their landing spots will be the columns of this matrix. | 要找到那个 1×2 矩阵，让我们把斜放数轴这套装置放大了看，想想 i hat 和 j hat 会跑到哪里去——它们的落点就是这个矩阵的两列。 |
| We can reason through this with a very elegant piece of symmetry: Since i-hat and u-hat are both unit vectors, projecting i-hat onto the line passing through u-hat looks completely symmetric to projecting u-hat onto the x-axis. So when we ask what number i-hat lands on in the projection, the answer will be the same as whatever number u-hat lands on when you project it onto the x-axis. | 我们可以用一段非常优雅的对称性来推理：由于 i hat 和 u hat 都是单位向量，「把 i hat 投影到穿过 u hat 的那条线上」与「把 u hat 投影到 x 轴上」看起来完全对称。所以当我们问 i hat 在这个投影里落在哪个数上时，答案就等于：把 u hat 投影到 x 轴上时，它落在哪个数上。 |
| But projecting u-hat on the x-axis just means taking the x-coordinate of u-hat, so by symmetry, the number where i-hat lands when projected onto this diagonal number line will be the x-coordinate of u-hat. | 而把 u hat 投影到 x 轴上，不过就是取 u hat 的 x 坐标。所以由对称性，i hat 投影到这条斜放数轴上所落的那个数，就是 u hat 的 x 坐标。 |
| Isn't that neat? For identical reasoning, the y-coordinate of u-hat gives us the number where j-hat lands when projected onto the number-line-copy. | 是不是相当巧妙？完全相同的推理：u hat 的 y 坐标，就给出 j hat 投影到这条数轴副本上所落的那个数。 |
| So the entries of the 1x2 matrix describing the projection transformation are going to be the coordinates of u-hat. Computing this projection transformation for arbitrary vectors in space requires multiplying those vectors by this matrix. | 于是，描述这个投影变换的 1×2 矩阵，其元素就是 u hat 的坐标。要对空间中的任意向量计算这个投影变换，就需要让这些向量乘上这个矩阵。 |
| But of course, that matrix-vector multiplication process is computationally identical to a dot product with u-hat. | 但当然了，那个矩阵-向量乘法过程，在计算上与「和 u hat 做点积」一模一样。 |
| This is why taking the dot product with a unit vector can be interpreted as projecting a vector onto the span of that unit vector, and taking its length. It's as if the vector u-hat is really a linear transformation disguised as a vector. | 这就是为什么「与单位向量做点积」可以理解为：把一个向量投影到那个单位向量所张成的直线上，再取投影的长度。这就好像 u hat 这个向量，其实是一个伪装成向量的线性变换。 |
| For example, let's say we took that unit vector u and scaled it up by a factor of 3. | 比如，假设我们把那个单位向量 u 放大到 3 倍。 |
| Numerically, each of its components gets multiplied by 3, so looking at the matrix associated with this vector, it takes i hat and j hat to 3 times the values where they landed before. | 数值上，它的每个分量都被乘以 3，所以看与这个向量相应的矩阵：它把 i hat 和 j hat 变到它们之前落点的 3 倍。 |
| Since this is linear, it implies more generally that the new matrix can be interpreted as projecting any vector onto the number-line-copy, then multiplying where it lands by 3. | 由于这是线性的，就可以更一般地说：这个新矩阵可以理解为——先把任意向量投影到数轴副本上，再把落点乘以 3。 |
| This is why the dot product with non-unit vectors can be interpreted as first projecting onto that vector, then scaling by the length of that vector. | 这就是为什么「与非单位向量做点积」可以理解为：先投影到那个向量上，再按那个向量的长度缩放。 |
| Using the last figure what is the dot product of the vector $\left[\begin{array}{c}-2 \\ 1\end{array}\right]$ and the unit vector scaled by three from above? | （译注：原文此处是一道互动选择题，候选为 1 / -3 / -1 / 3，正确答案为 $-3$；下为官网给出的答案解析。）用上一张图，向量 $\left[\begin{array}{c}-2 \\ 1\end{array}\right]$ 与上面那个放大到三倍的单位向量的点积是多少？ |
| From the diagram, the projection of $\left[\begin{array}{c}-2 \\ 1\end{array}\right]$ onto the number line aligned to our unit vector is $-1$. This means $\vec{\mathbf{v}} \cdot \hat{\mathbf{u}}=-1$. Therefore, using linearity, $\vec{\mathbf{v}} \cdot(3 \hat{\mathbf{u}})=-3$ | （译注：上题的答案解析。）从图上看，$\left[\begin{array}{c}-2 \\ 1\end{array}\right]$ 投影到与我们的单位向量对齐的那条数轴上，落在 $-1$。这意味着 $\vec{\mathbf{v}} \cdot \hat{\mathbf{u}}=-1$。因此，利用线性性，$\vec{\mathbf{v}} \cdot(3 \hat{\mathbf{u}})=-3$。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 单位向量
> - **unit vector** —— 单位向量。a two-dimensional unit vector。
> - **diagonally** /daɪˈæɡənəli/ —— 斜着地。place it diagonally in space。
> - **embed** /ɪmˈbed/ —— 嵌入。I've embedded the number line in 2d space。
> - **overlap** /ˌəʊvəˈlæp/ —— 重叠。overlaps with the embedding of the number line。
> - **elegant** /ˈelɪɡənt/ —— 优雅的。a very elegant piece of symmetry。
> - **identical** /aɪˈdentɪkl/ —— 完全相同的。For identical reasoning。
> - *zoom in* —— 放大、凑近细看。let's zoom in on our diagonal number line setup。
> - *in the back of your mind* —— 记在脑子后头（暗暗惦记）。keep him in the back of your mind。

## 对偶性（Duality）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Notice what happened here: We had a linear transformation from 2d space to the number line which was not defined in terms of numerical vectors or numerical dot products, it was defined by projecting space onto a diagonal copy of the number line. | 注意这里发生了什么：我们有一个从二维空间到数轴的线性变换，它不是用数值向量或数值点积定义的，而是通过「把空间投影到一条斜放的数轴副本上」来定义的。 |
| Because the transformation was linear, it was necessarily described by some 1x2 matrix, and since multiplying a 1x2 matrix by a 2d vectors is the same as turning that matrix on its side and taking a dot product, this transformation was, inescapably, related to some 2d vector. | 由于这个变换是线性的，它必然由某个 1×2 矩阵描述；又因为 1×2 矩阵乘二维向量，等同于把那个矩阵侧躺过来取一次点积，所以这个变换无可逃避地与某个二维向量相关。 |
| The lesson here is that any time you have one of these linear transformations whose output space is the number line, no matter how it was defined, there is some unique vector $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ corresponding to that transformation, in the sense that applying that transformation to some other vector $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ is the same as taking the dot product between $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$. This, to me, is utterly beautiful. It's an example of something in math called "duality". | 这里的教益是：任何时候你手里有一个输出空间为数轴的线性变换——不管它当初是怎么定义的——都有某个唯一的向量 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 与那个变换相对应，其含义是：把那个变换作用到另外某个向量 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 上，等同于取 $\vec{\mathbf{v}}$ 与 $\vec{\mathbf{w}}$ 之间的点积。在我看来，这美得彻头彻尾。它是数学里叫作「对偶性」（duality）的东西的一个例子。 |
| Duality shows up in many different ways and forms throughout math, and it's super tricky to actually define. Loosely speaking, it refers to situations where you have a natural-but-surprising correspondence between two types of mathematical things. For the linear algebra case you just learned about, you'd say the dual of a vector is the linear transformation it encodes, and the dual of a linear transformation from some space to one dimension is a certain vector in that space. | 对偶性在数学各处以许多不同的方式与形态现身，想真正给它下定义相当棘手。粗略地说，它指的是这样一类情形：两类数学事物之间存在一种自然却又出人意料的对应。对你刚学到的线性代数情形，你会说：一个向量的对偶（dual）是它所编码的线性变换；而一个从某空间到一维的线性变换，其对偶是该空间中的某个特定向量。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 对偶性
> - **duality** /djuˈæləti/ —— 对偶性。something in math called "duality"。
> - **inescapably** /ˌɪnɪˈskeɪpəbli/ —— 无可逃避地。this transformation was, inescapably, related to some 2d vector。
> - **utterly** /ˈʌtəli/ —— 彻底地、十足地。This, to me, is utterly beautiful。
> - **correspondence** /ˌkɒrəˈspɒndəns/ —— 对应（关系）。a natural-but-surprising correspondence。
> - **encode** /ɪnˈkəʊd/ —— 编码。the linear transformation it encodes。
> - *loosely speaking* —— 粗略地说。Loosely speaking, it refers to situations where ...。
> - *show up* —— 出现、现身。Duality shows up in many different ways and forms。

## 结语（Conclusion）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| To sum up, on the surface, the dot product is a very useful geometric tool for understanding projections, and for testing whether or not vectors tend to point in the same direction. That's probably the most important thing for you to remember about the dot product, but on a deeper level, dotting two vectors is a way to translate one of those vectors to the world of transformations. | 总结一下：表面上看，点积是一个非常有用的几何工具——用于理解投影，也用于检验两个向量是否大体指向同一方向。关于点积，这大概是你最该记住的东西；但在更深的层面上，对两个向量做点积，是把其中一个向量翻译到变换世界的一种方式。 |
| Again, numerically, this might feel like a silly point to emphasize. It's just two computations that happen to look similar. The reason I find this important is that in math, when you are dealing with a vector, once you get to know its personality, sometimes you realize that it's easier to understand it not as an arrow in space, but as the physical embodiment of a linear transformation (the gradient vector from multivariable calculus comes to mind). It's as if the vector is just a conceptual shorthand for a certain transformation since it's easier to think about an arrow in space than it is to think about a transformation where all that space is moved onto a number line. | 再说一遍，从数值上看，强调这一点可能显得有点傻：不过是两个碰巧长得像的计算罢了。我之所以觉得这一点重要，是因为在数学里，当你和一个向量打交道时，一旦你摸清了它的「性格」（personality），有时你会意识到：不把它理解成空间中的一支箭头，而是理解成一个线性变换的物理化身（embodiment），反而更容易懂（多元微积分里的梯度向量（gradient vector）就会浮上心头）。这就好像向量只是某个变换的概念速记（shorthand）——因为思考空间中的一支箭头，总比思考一个把整个空间都搬到数轴上的变换要容易。 |
| In the next chapter, you'll see another really good example of this duality in action as you and I get into the cross product. | 在下一章，当你我进入叉积（cross product）时，你会看到这种对偶性发挥作用的另一个绝佳例子。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 结语
> - **emphasize** /ˈemfəsaɪz/ —— 强调。a silly point to emphasize。
> - **personality** /ˌpɜːsəˈnæləti/ —— 性格、个性。once you get to know its personality。
> - **embodiment** /ɪmˈbɒdimənt/ —— 化身、体现。the physical embodiment of a linear transformation。
> - **shorthand** /ˈʃɔːthænd/ —— 速记、简写。a conceptual shorthand for a certain transformation。
> - **gradient vector** —— 梯度向量。the gradient vector from multivariable calculus。
> - *on the surface* —— 表面上。on the surface, the dot product is a very useful geometric tool。
> - *on a deeper level* —— 在更深的层面上。but on a deeper level, dotting two vectors is ...。
> - *come to mind* —— 浮上心头。the gradient vector ... comes to mind。
