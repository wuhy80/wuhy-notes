## 画出分子与分母（Plot numerator and denominator）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Let's graph the numerator, $\sin(\pi x)$, and the denominator, $x^2-1$, of our original function and focus on what's happening at $x=1$. | 我们把原函数的分子 $\sin(\pi x)$ 和分母 $x^2-1$ 分别画出来，聚焦 $x=1$ 处发生了什么。 |
| The point here is that $\sin(\pi x)$ and $x^2-1$ are both $0$ at that point, so they cross the $x$-axis. In the same spirit as plugging in a specific value near $1$, like $1.00001$, let's zoom in on that point and consider what happens a tiny nudge $dx$ away. | 这里的要点是：$\sin(\pi x)$ 和 $x^2-1$ 在那个点都等于 0，所以它们都穿过 $x$ 轴。本着与代入 $1$ 附近的特定值（比如 $1.00001$）同样的精神，让我们放大到那个点，想想在旁边一个微小轻推 $dx$ 之外会发生什么。 |
| The value of $\sin(\pi x)$ is bumped down by some $d(\sin(\pi x))$ and the value of $x^2-1$ is bumped up by some $d(x^2-1)$. | $\sin(\pi x)$ 的值被往下撞了一点，幅度是某个 $d(\sin(\pi x))$；$x^2-1$ 的值则被往上抬了一点，幅度是某个 $d(x^2-1)$。 |
| Take a moment to think about how the fraction $d(\sin (\pi x)) / d\left(x^{2}-1\right)$ reframes the initial problem which had previously looked like $0$ divided by $0$. Why might this lead us towards an exact answer rather than just an approximation? | （译注：原文此处是一道互动问答题，答案面板内容见下。）花点时间想想：分式 $d(\sin (\pi x)) / d\left(x^{2}-1\right)$ 是怎样重新框定这个起初看起来像 $0$ 除以 $0$ 的问题的？为什么它可能把我们引向精确答案，而不只是一个近似？ |
| First, this fraction is the same as evaluating the original function at $f(1 + dx)$. | （译注：上题的答案解析。）首先，这个分式就等于把原函数在 $f(1 + dx)$ 处取值。 |
| And second, by looking at the ratio of these two tangent lines, whose approximations of the numerator and denominator get better and better as $dx$ approaches $0$, we are able to reason about the _exact_ value this limit approaches. | 其次，通过看这两条切线之比——随着 $dx$ 趋近 $0$，它们对分子和分母的近似越来越好——我们能够推理出这个极限所趋近的*精确*值。 |
| Since both of these changes are proportional to the small nudge $dx$, we can go ahead and apply our knowledge of derivatives. Using the chain rule, we know that the change to the function $d(\sin(\pi x))$ should be around $\cos(\pi x) \cdot \pi \cdot dx$. Similarly, using the power rule, we know that the change to the function $x^2-1$ should be around $2 x \cdot dx$. | 由于这两个变化都与微小的轻推 $dx$ 成正比，我们可以放手应用导数的知识。用链式法则（chain rule）可知，函数的变化量 $d(\sin(\pi x))$ 应该在 $\cos(\pi x) \cdot \pi \cdot dx$ 上下；同样，用幂法则（power rule）可知，函数 $x^2-1$ 的变化量应该在 $2 x \cdot dx$ 上下。 |
| Since we are interested in the fraction of these two functions at the point at $x = 1$ we can substitute $x=1$ into both of these expressions and evaluate the fraction. | 由于我们关心的是这两个函数在 $x = 1$ 处构成的分式，可以把 $x=1$ 代入这两个表达式，再求出这个分式。 |
| What this means is that for values of $x$ which are some tiny value $dx$ away from $1$, the ratio $\sin(\pi x)/(x^2-1)$ is approximately $(-\pi \cdot dx) / (2 \cdot dx)$. The $dx$'s cancel, so that value is $-\pi/2$. Importantly, these approximations get more and more accurate for smaller and smaller choices of $dx$, this ratio $-\pi/2$ actually tells us the precise limiting value as $x$ approaches $1$. | 这意味着：对于离 $1$ 有某个微小距离 $dx$ 的那些 $x$ 值，比值 $\sin(\pi x)/(x^2-1)$ 近似于 $(-\pi \cdot dx) / (2 \cdot dx)$。$dx$ 互相抵消，所以这个值是 $-\pi/2$。重要的是，$dx$ 取得越小，这些近似就越精确——这个比值 $-\pi/2$ 实际上告诉了我们当 $x$ 趋近 $1$ 时的精确极限值。 |
| Remember, what that means is that the limiting height on our original graph is evidently exactly $-\pi/2$. | 记住，这就意味着：原图像上的极限高度显然恰好是 $-\pi/2$。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 画出分子与分母
> - **numerator** /ˈnjuːməreɪtə/ —— 分子。the numerator $\sin(\pi x)$。
> - **denominator** /dɪˈnɒmɪneɪtə/ —— 分母。the denominator $x^2-1$。
> - **nudge** /nʌdʒ/ —— 轻推。a tiny nudge $dx$ away（本系列关键词）。
> - **proportional** /prəˈpɔːʃnl/ —— 成比例的。proportional to the small nudge $dx$。
> - **reframe** /ˌriːˈfreɪm/ —— 重新框定、换个角度看。reframes the initial problem。
> - **substitute** /ˈsʌbstɪtjuːt/ —— 代入。substitute $x=1$ into both of these expressions。
> - **evidently** /ˈevɪdəntli/ —— 显然地。is evidently exactly $-\pi/2$。
> - *chain rule / power rule* —— 链式法则 / 幂法则。Using the chain rule… using the power rule…

## 推广（Generalize）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| What happened there is a little subtle, so let me show it again, but this time a little more generally. Instead of these two specific functions, which both equal $0$ at $x=1$, think of any two functions $f(x)$ and $g(x)$, which are both $0$ at some common value $x = a$. | 刚才发生的事有点微妙，所以让我再演示一遍，这次更一般一些。别想这两个在 $x=1$ 处都等于 0 的特定函数了，设想任意两个函数 $f(x)$ 和 $g(x)$，它们在某个共同的值 $x = a$ 处都等于 0。 |
| The only constraint is that these have to be functions where you're able to take a derivative of them at $x = a$, meaning they each basically look like a line when you zoom in close enough to that value. | 唯一的约束是：这两个函数得让你能在 $x = a$ 处求导，也就是说，当你把放大倍数拉得足够近时，它们各自看起来基本像一条直线。 |
| Even though you can't compute $f$ divided by $g$ at this trouble point, since both equal zero, you can ask about this ratio for values of $x$ really really close to $a$, the limit as $x$ approach $a$. And it's helpful to think of those nearby inputs as a tiny nudge $dx$ away from $a$. | 虽然在这个麻烦点上你算不了 $f$ 除以 $g$——因为两者都等于零——但你可以对非常非常接近 $a$ 的那些 $x$ 值问这个比值是多少，也就是 $x$ 趋近 $a$ 时的极限。而把那些邻近的输入想成离 $a$ 一个微小轻推 $dx$ 之远，会很有帮助。 |
| The value of $f$ at that nudged point is approximately its derivative, $df/dx$ evaluated at $a$, times $dx$. Likewise, the the value of $g$ at that nudged point is approximately the derivative of $g$, evaluated at $a$, times $dx$. | （译注：原文 the the 系 the 之重复，照录。）$f$ 在那个被轻推的点上的值，近似等于它在 $a$ 处的导数 $df/dx$ 乘以 $dx$。同样，$g$ 在那个被轻推的点上的值，近似等于 $g$ 在 $a$ 处的导数乘以 $dx$。 |
| So near this trouble point, the ratio between the outputs of $f$ and $g$ is actually about the same as the derivative of $f$ at $a$, times $dx$, divided by the derivative of $g$ at $a$, times $dx$. These $dx$'s cancel, so the ratio of $f$ and $g$ near $a$ is about the same as the ratio between their derivatives. | 于是在这个麻烦点附近，$f$ 与 $g$ 的输出之比，实际上大约等于「$f$ 在 $a$ 处的导数乘以 $dx$」除以「$g$ 在 $a$ 处的导数乘以 $dx$」。这些 $dx$ 互相抵消，所以 $f$ 与 $g$ 在 $a$ 附近的比值，大约就是它们导数之比。 |
| Because both approximations get more accurate for smaller nudges, this ratio of derivatives gives the precise value for the limit. | （图注）由于轻推越小时两个近似都越精确，这个导数之比给出的就是极限的精确值。 |
| This is a _really_ handy trick for computing a lot of limits. If you come across an expression that seems to equal $0/0$ when you plug in some input, just try taking the derivative of the top and bottom expressions, and plugging in that trouble input. | 这是一个*非常*好用的技巧，能算出一大类极限。如果你碰到一个表达式，代入某个输入后似乎等于 $0/0$，那就试试把上面和下面的表达式分别求导，再代入那个麻烦的输入。 |
| This clever trick is called "**L'Hôpital's rule**". Interestingly, it was actually discovered by Johann Bernoulli, but L'Hopital was this wealthy dude who essentially paid Bernoulli for the rights to some of his mathematical discoveries. Academia was weird back then, but in a very literal way, it pays to understand these tiny nudges. | 这个聪明的技巧叫作「**L'Hôpital's rule**（洛必达法则）」。有意思的是，它其实是由约翰·伯努利（Johann Bernoulli）发现的，而洛必达是位有钱人，本质上是付钱给伯努利，买下了他某些数学发现的权利。那个年代的学术界很怪；不过从非常字面的意义上讲，理解这些微小轻推真的「有所回报」（it pays）。 |
| Now, you might remember that the definition of a derivative for any given function comes down to computing the limit of a fraction that looks like $0/0$, so you might think L'Hopital's rule gives a handy way to discover new derivative formulas. But that would be cheating, since presumably you don't yet know what the derivative on the numerator here is. | 不过你也许记得，任何给定函数的导数定义，归根结底就是算一个形如 $0/0$ 的分式的极限，所以你可能会想：洛必达法则岂不是提供了一条发现新导数公式的捷径？但那是作弊——因为十有八九，你此刻还不知道分子上那个导数是多少。 |
| When it comes to discovering derivative formulas, something we've been doing a fair amount this series, there is no systematic plug-and-chug method. But that's a good thing. When creativity is needed to solve problems like these, it's a good sign you're doing something real; something that might give you a powerful tool to solve future problems. | 说到发现导数公式——这个系列里我们已经干了不少回——并没有一套系统化的「代入硬算」方法。但这是件好事。当解决这类问题需要创造力时，那正说明你在做真正有价值的事；这种事可能会给你一件解决未来问题的强大工具。 |
| In the next lesson, I'll talk about what an integral is, as well as the fundamental theorem of calculus, which is another example of where limits are used to help give a clear meaning to a fairly delicate idea that flirts with infinity. | 下一课，我会讲积分是什么，以及微积分基本定理——它是又一个例子，展示极限如何帮助一个与无穷调情的、相当精巧的想法获得清晰的含义。 |

## 练习（Exercises）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| **What is the limit as $x$ approaches $0$ of the function $f(x) = \frac{\sin(x)}{x}$?** | （译注：原文此处是一道互动选择题，选项为 $-1$、$0$、$1$、Not defined，正确答案为第三个（$1$），解析见下。）**函数 $f(x) = \frac{\sin(x)}{x}$ 在 $x$ 趋近 $0$ 时的极限是多少？** |
| Observe that when substituting $x=$ into the limit expression it looks like $0$ divided by $0$. | （译注：上题的答案解析。原文 substituting $x=$ 处疑漏一个 $0$，照录。）观察到把 $x=$ 代入极限表达式时，它看起来像 $0$ 除以 $0$。 |

$$
\lim _{x \rightarrow 0} \frac{\sin(x)}{x} = \frac{0}{0}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Maybe your first thought is to graph the function and, right off the bat, the graph of the function looks like the value approaches $1$. But let's use what we've learned in this lesson to rigorously show that it really is $1$. | 你的第一反应也许是画出函数图像——一上来，图像看起来就像在趋近 1。但让我们用这一课学到的内容，严格地证明它确实就是 1。 |
| This looks like a good candidate for L'Hopital's rule - where we reframe the limit as the ratio of two function's, $g$ and $h$, tangent lines. | （译注：原文 two function's 系 two functions 之笔误，照录。）这看起来是洛必达法则的好苗子——我们把极限重新框定为两个函数 $g$ 和 $h$ 的切线之比。 |

$$
\lim _{x \rightarrow 0} \frac{\sin(x)}{x} = \lim _{x \rightarrow 0} \frac{g(x)}{h(x)}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Here, it's a good idea to check our constraint that we should be able to take the derivative of both of these function at the point the limit approaches. | （译注：原文 both of these function 系 both of these functions 之笔误，照录。）这里，最好先检查一下我们的约束条件：在这两个函数上，我们都应当能在极限所趋近的那个点求导。 |
| The further we zoom in the more these functions look like lines so our constraint checks out and we can go ahead and evaluate the ratio of the derivatives evaluated at the point $x=0$. | 放大得越深，这两个函数就越像直线，所以约束条件成立，我们可以放手去求在 $x=0$ 处取值的导数之比。 |

$$
\lim _{x \rightarrow 0} \frac{\sin(x)}{x} = \frac{\frac{dg}{dx}(0)}{\frac{dh}{dx}(0)}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| - The derivative of $\sin(x)$ is equal to $\cos(x)$ which evaluated at $0$ is $\cos(0)=1$. | （译注：以下两行为原文的项目符号列表，照录。）- $\sin(x)$ 的导数等于 $\cos(x)$，在 $0$ 处取值为 $\cos(0)=1$。 |
| - The derivative of $x$ is equal to $1$ which evaluated at $0$ will still be $1$. | - $x$ 的导数等于 $1$，在 $0$ 处取值仍是 $1$。 |

$$
\lim _{x \rightarrow 0} \frac{\sin(x)}{x} = \frac{\cos(0)}{1} = 1
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| This rigorously verifies that the limit of this function is indeed $1$ as $x$ approaches $0$ . | 这就严格地验证了：当 $x$ 趋近 $0$ 时，这个函数的极限确实是 $1$。 |
| **What is the limit of the function $\displaystyle f(x) = \frac{x^3 - 1}{x-1}$ as $x$ approaches $1$?** | （译注：原文此处是一道互动选择题，选项为 $1$、$-1$、$2$、$3$，正确答案为第四个（$3$），解析见下。）**函数 $\displaystyle f(x) = \frac{x^3 - 1}{x-1}$ 在 $x$ 趋近 $1$ 时的极限是多少？** |
| Substituting $1$ in for $x$ in the expression we can see that the limit looks like $0$ divided by $0$. | （译注：上题的答案解析。）把 $1$ 代入表达式中的 $x$，可以看到极限看起来像 $0$ 除以 $0$。 |

$$
\lim _{x \rightarrow 1} \frac{x^3 - 1}{x-1} = \frac{0}{0}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Of course, in practice you can go ahead and apply L'Hopital's rule, but here let's follow the same logic we laid out in this lesson. If we graph the expression we can see that the point at $x-1$ is undefined and appears to approach the value $3$. | 当然，实际操作中你可以直接套洛必达法则，但这里让我们沿用这一课铺开的同一套逻辑。如果把表达式画成图，可以看到 $x-1$ 处的点没有定义，并且显得在趋近值 $3$。 |
| Since these are polynomial functions, we know their derivatives exist at zero and so our constraint is satisfied. We can also plot the graph of the tangent lines. | 由于这些是多项式函数，我们知道它们的导数在该点存在，所以约束条件满足。我们还可以把切线的图像画出来。 |
| We can express this limit as the ratio of these tangent lines. | 我们可以把这个极限表示成这些切线之比。 |

$$
\lim _{x \rightarrow 1} \frac{x^3 - 1}{x-1} = \frac{\frac{dg}{dx}(1)}{\frac{dh}{dx}(1)}
$$

$$
\lim _{x \rightarrow 1} \frac{3x^2 dx}{1 \cdot dx} = \frac{3(1)^2}{1} = 3
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| So the limit of this function $\displaystyle f(x) = \frac{x^3 - 1}{x-1}$ as $x$ approaches $1$ is $3$. | 所以，函数 $\displaystyle f(x) = \frac{x^3 - 1}{x-1}$ 在 $x$ 趋近 $1$ 时的极限是 $3$。 |
