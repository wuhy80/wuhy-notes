## 速度与距离的关系（Relationship between velocity and distance）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| You might remember, in chapter 2 of this series, we were looking at the opposite situation, where you know a distance function, $s(t)$, and you want to figure out a velocity function from that. I showed how the derivative of your distance vs. time function gives you a velocity vs. time function. | 你可能还记得，在这个系列的第 2 章里，我们看到的是相反的情形：你知道距离函数 $s(t)$，想从中弄出速度函数。我当时展示了「距离-时间」函数的导数如何给出「速度-时间」函数。 |
| So in our current situation, where all we know is the velocity function, it should make sense that finding a distance vs. time function $s(t)$ comes down to asking what function has a derivative $-t^2 + 8t$. This is often described as finding the antiderivative of a function. | 所以在眼下这种只知道速度函数的情形里，下面这件事应该说得通：求「距离-时间」函数 $s(t)$，归根结底就是在问「什么函数的导数是 $-t^2 + 8t$」。这常被描述为求一个函数的反导数（antiderivative）。 |
| And indeed, that's what we'll end up doing, and you could even pause and try that right now for the function $v(t)=-t^2 + 8t$. | 的确，我们最后也会这么做；你甚至可以现在就暂停一下，对函数 $v(t)=-t^2 + 8t$ 亲手试试。 |
| But first, I want to spend the bulk of this article showing how this question is related to finding an area bounded by the velocity graph, because that helps to build an intuition for a whole class of what are called "integral problems" in math and science. | 但首先，我想用这篇文章的大部分篇幅，展示这个问题如何与「求速度图像所围的面积」联系起来，因为这有助于为数学和科学中一整类被称为「积分问题」的东西建立直觉。 |
| This figure highlights the area bounded under the curve formed by the function $v(t)=-t^2+8 t$. The vertical axis represents velocity measured in meters per second and the horizontal axis represents time. | （图注）这张图高亮了函数 $v(t)=-t^2+8 t$ 形成的曲线下所围的面积。纵轴代表以米每秒计的速度，横轴代表时间。 |
| To start off, notice this question would be much easier if the car was moving with a constant velocity, right? In that case, you could just multiply the velocity, in meters per second, by the amount of time passed, in seconds, and that gives you the number of meters traveled. | 先从简单处入手：注意，如果汽车以恒定速度行驶，这个问题会容易得多，对吧？那种情况下，你只要把以米每秒计的速度，乘以流逝的以秒计的时间，就得到了行驶的米数。 |
| Notice that you can visualize that distance as an area, and if visualizing distance as an area seems weird, I'm right there with you. It's just that on this plot, where the horizontal direction has units of seconds and the vertical direction has units of meters/second, units of area very naturally correspond to meters. But what makes our situation hard is that the velocity is not constant, it's incessantly changing at every instant. | 注意，你可以把这段距离直观地想象成一块面积；如果把距离想成面积显得很怪，我跟你感同身受。只不过在这张图上，横方向的单位是秒，纵方向的单位是米/秒，面积的单位便自然而然对应到米。但让我们处境变难的，是速度并非恒定——它在每个瞬间都不停地变。 |
| It would even be a lot easier if it only ever changed at a handful of points, maybe staying static for the first second, then suddenly discontinuously jumping to a constant 7 meters per second for the next second, and so on, with discontinuous jumps to portions of constant velocity. | 甚至，如果速度只在寥寥几个点上变化，事情也会容易得多：比如头一秒保持不动，然后突然不连续地跳到恒定的 7 米每秒维持一秒，如此继续——由不连续的跳跃串起一段段恒定速度。 |
| That might make it very uncomfortable for the driver, in fact, it's physically impossible, but it would make your calculations a lot more straightforward. You could compute the distance traveled on each interval by multiplying the constant velocity on that interval by the change in time. Then just add them all up. | 这可能会让司机非常难受——事实上这在物理上是不可能的——但它会让你的计算直白得多：你只要用每段上的恒定速度乘以该段的时间变化，算出每段行驶的距离，然后把它们全部加起来。 |
| So what we'll do is just approximate our velocity function as if it was constant on a bunch of different intervals. Then, as is common in calculus, we'll see how refining that approximation leads us to something precise. | 所以我们要做的，就是把速度函数近似成它在许多不同小区间上恒定。然后，正像微积分里常见的那样，我们会看到：不断改进这个近似，会把我们引向某个精确的东西。 |
| Notice as the we choose more and more sub-divisions that these approximations start to approach the true shape of the area bounded by the curve. | （图注）注意，随着我们选取越来越多的细分，这些近似开始逼近曲线所围面积的真实形状。 |
| Here, let's make this more concrete with some numbers. Chop up the time axis between 0 and 8 into many small intervals, each with some little width $dt$, like 0.25 seconds. Consider one of these intervals, like the one between $t=1$, and $1.25$. In reality, the car speeds up from 7 m/s to about 8.4 m/s during that time, which you can find by plugging in $t = 1$ and 1.25 to the equation for velocity. | 这里，让我们用一些数字把它说得更具体。把 0 到 8 之间的时间轴切成许多小区间，每个区间都有某个小宽度 $dt$，比如 0.25 秒。取其中一个区间，比如 $t=1$ 到 $1.25$ 之间。现实中，汽车在这段时间里从 7 m/s 加速到约 8.4 m/s；只要把 $t = 1$ 和 1.25 代入速度方程就能求出。 |
| We want to approximate the car's motion as if its velocity was constant on this interval. Again, the reason for doing that is that we don't really know how to handle anything other than constant velocity situations. You could choose this constant to be anything between $7$ and $8.4$, it doesn't really matter. | 我们想把汽车的运动近似成：它的速度在这个区间上是恒定的。再说一遍，之所以这么做，是因为除了恒定速度的情形，我们其实不知道怎么处理别的。你可以把这个常数取成 $7$ 到 $8.4$ 之间的任何值，那真的不要紧。（译注：原文此处有一条脚注，大意是——你可能会马上想到几处改进：取两个端点的平均、用右端点的值，甚至改用梯形；但不同的近似方式最终并不影响结果。） |
| All that matters is that our sequence of approximations, whatever they are, gets better and better as $dt$ gets smaller and smaller. Treating this car's journey as a bunch of discontinuous jumps in speed between small portions of constant velocity becomes a less wrong reflection of reality as we decrease the time between those jumps. | 真正要紧的是：不管我们的近似序列是什么样子，随着 $dt$ 越来越小，它们会越来越好。把这段旅程当成「一段段恒定速度之间的不连续跳变」，随着我们缩短那些跳变之间的时间，它对现实的刻画也就越来越不那么错。 |
| So, for convenience, let's just approximate the speed on each interval with whatever the true car's velocity is at the start of the interval; the height of the graph above the left side, which in this case is 7. | 于是，为方便起见，我们就用区间起点处汽车的真实速度来近似每个区间上的速度；也就是图像在左端上方的那个高度，在这个例子里是 7。 |
| So on this example interval, according to our approximation, the car moves $(7,\text{m/s})\cdot(0.25,\text{s})$. That's $1.75$ meters, nicely visualized as the area of this thin rectangle. This is a little under the real distance traveled, but not by much. And the same goes for every other interval: The approximated distance is $v(t) \cdot dt$, it's just that you plug in a different value of $t$ for each one, giving a different height for each rectangle. | 于是在这个示例区间上，按照我们的近似，汽车走了 $(7,\text{m/s})\cdot(0.25,\text{s})$，即 $1.75$ 米，很直观地对应这个细长矩形的面积。它比真实行驶距离略小一点，但差得不多。其他每个区间也都一样：近似距离是 $v(t) \cdot dt$，只不过每个区间代入不同的 $t$ 值，给出每个矩形不同的高度。 |

## 积分记号（Integral Notation）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| I'm going to write out an expression for the sum of the areas of all these rectangles in kind of a funny way. Take this symbol $\int$, which looks like a stretched "S" for sum, then put a $0$ at its bottom and an $8$ at its top to indicate that we're ranging over time steps between $0$ and $8$ seconds. | 我打算用一种有点滑稽的方式，写出所有这些矩形面积之和的表达式。取这个符号 $\int$——它看起来像一个拉长的「S」，代表 sum（求和）——然后在它的底端放一个 $0$、顶端放一个 $8$，表明我们遍历的是 $0$ 到 $8$ 秒之间的时间步。 |
| The amount we're adding up at each time step is $v(t) \cdot dt$ which, for each step, can be visualized as the rectangle of area on the graph. | 在每个时间步上我们加总的量是 $v(t) \cdot dt$，对每一步来说，它都可以在图上看成一个矩形的面积。 |
| Two things are implicit in this notation: First, the value $dt$ plays two roles: not only is it a factor in each quantity we're adding up, it also indicates the spacing between each sampled time step. So when you make $dt$ smaller, even though it decreases the area of each rectangle, it increases the total number of rectangles whose areas we're adding up, because if they are thinner it takes more of them to fill up that space. And second, the reason we don't use the usual sigma notation $\sum$ to indicate a sum is that this expression is technically not any particular sum for any particular choice of $dt$; it's meant to express whatever that sum approaches as $dt$ approaches 0. | 这个记号里隐含了两件事。第一，$dt$ 扮演两个角色：它不仅是我们加总的每个量里的一个因子，还标示了相邻采样时间步之间的间距。所以当你把 $dt$ 变小时，尽管每个矩形的面积变小了，但我们加总面积的矩形总数却变多了——因为它们更薄，就需要更多个才能填满那片空间。第二，我们不用通常的求和记号 $\sum$ 来表示求和，原因是：严格说来，这个表达式并不是对任何特定 $dt$ 取值的某个特定和式；它要表达的是，当 $dt$ 趋近 0 时，那个和式所趋近的值。 |
| As $dt$ approaches $0$ the area of the rectangle approaches the area bounded by the curve and the horizontal axis. | （图注）当 $dt$ 趋近 $0$ 时，矩形的总面积逼近曲线与横轴所围成的面积。 |
| Remember, smaller choices of $dt$ indicate closer approximations for our original question, _how far does the car go_? So this limiting value for the sum, the area under this curve, gives the precise answer to the question, in full unapproximated precision. | 记住，$dt$ 取得越小，就表示对我们最初的问题——*汽车走了多远*？——的近似越贴近。所以这个和式的极限值，也就是这条曲线下的面积，给出了问题的精确答案，是不掺任何近似的精度。 |
| Area under the curve is equal to the distance traveled. | （图注）曲线下面积等于行驶的距离。 |
| Now tell me that's not surprising. We have this pretty complicated idea of approximations that can involve adding up a huge number of very tiny things, and yet the value those approximations approach can be described so simply, as the area under a curve. | 现在你告诉我这不出奇。我们本来有一个相当复杂的近似想法，可能涉及把海量非常小的东西加起来；然而这些近似所趋近的值，却能用如此简单的方式描述——曲线下的面积。 |

$$
\underbrace{\int_0^8 v(t) d t}_{d t \rightarrow 0}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| This expression is called an "integral" of $v(t)$, since it brings all of its values together, it integrates them. | 这个表达式叫作 $v(t)$ 的「积分」（integral），因为它把 $v(t)$ 的所有值拢到了一起——它把它们整合（integrate）了起来。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 积分记号
> - **integrate** /ˈɪntɪɡreɪt/ —— 积分；整合。it integrates them；名词 integral。
> - **notation** /nəʊˈteɪʃn/ —— 记号、记法。Two things are implicit in this notation。
> - **implicit** /ɪmˈplɪsɪt/ —— 隐含的、不言明的。Two things are implicit in this notation。
> - **spacing** /ˈspeɪsɪŋ/ —— 间距。the spacing between each sampled time step。
> - **factor** /ˈfæktə/ —— 因子。a factor in each quantity we're adding up。
> - **unapproximated** /ˌʌnəˈprɒksɪmeɪtɪd/ —— 未取近似的。in full unapproximated precision（作者自造词）。
> - *range over* —— 遍历、取遍。we're ranging over time steps。
> - *sigma notation* —— Σ 求和记号。the usual sigma notation $\sum$。

## 面积函数的导数是原函数（Derivative of area function is the function）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| For our velocity example, think of this right endpoint as a variable, capital $T$. | 对我们的速度例子来说，把这个右端点想成一个变量——大写的 $T$。 |

## 基本定理（Fundamental theorem）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| More generally, anytime you want to integrate some function - and remember you think of as adding up the values $f(x) \cdot dx$ for inputs in a certain range then asking what that sum approaches as $dx$ approaches $0$ - the first step to evaluating that integral is to find an antiderivative, some other function, "capital $F(x)$", whose derivative is the thing inside the integral. Then the integral equals this antiderivative evaluated at the top bound, minus its value at the bottom bound. This fact is called the "fundamental theorem of calculus". | 更一般地，任何时候你想积分某个函数——记住，你把它想成：对某个范围内的输入加总 $f(x) \cdot dx$，然后问当 $dx$ 趋近 $0$ 时那个和趋近于什么——求这个积分的第一步，是找到一个反导数，某个别的函数「大写 $F(x)$」，它的导数恰好是积分号里面的东西。然后，积分就等于这个反导数在上界处的取值，减去它在下界处的取值。这个事实叫作「微积分基本定理」（fundamental theorem of calculus）。 |
| Here's what's crazy about this fact: The integral, the limiting value for the sum of all these thin rectangles, takes into account every single input on the continuum from the lower bound to the upper bound, that's why we use the word "integrate"; it brings them together. And yet, to actually compute it using the antiderivative, you look at only two inputs: the top bound and the bottom bound. It almost feels like cheating! Finding the antiderivative implicitly accounts for all the information needed to add up all the values between the lower bound and upper bound. | 这个事实疯狂的地方在于：积分——所有这些细长矩形之和的极限值——把从下界到上界这段连续统上的每一个输入都考虑了进去；这正是我们用 integrate（整合）这个词的原因：它把它们聚拢到一起。可真要用反导数去计算它时，你只看两个输入：上界和下界。简直像作弊！求反导数这件事，隐含地把「在下界与上界之间加总所有值」所需的信息全部考虑进来了。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 基本定理
> - **continuum** /kənˈtɪnjuəm/ —— 连续统。every single input on the continuum。
> - **implicitly** /ɪmˈplɪsɪtli/ —— 隐含地、暗含地。implicitly accounts for all the information。
> - **fundamental** /ˌfʌndəˈmentl/ —— 基本的。the "fundamental theorem of calculus"。
> - **crazy** /ˈkreɪzi/ —— 疯狂的、了不得的。Here's what's crazy about this fact。
> - *account for* —— 涵盖、把……考虑进去。accounts for all the information needed。
> - *take into account* —— 考虑进去。takes into account every single input。
