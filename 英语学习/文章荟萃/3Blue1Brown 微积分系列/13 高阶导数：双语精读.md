## 图像上的含义（Graphical meaning）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Given some function $f(x)$, the derivative can be interpreted as the slope of its graph above some input, right? A steep slope means a high value for the derivative, a downward slope means a negative derivative. | 给定某个函数 $f(x)$，导数可以解读为它的图像在某个输入点上方处的斜率，对吧？斜率陡意味着导数值大，斜率向下意味着导数为负。 |
| **Review question: what is the slope at $x=2$ and $x=4$?** | （译注：原文此处是一道互动选择题，选项为 $-2$ 和 $3$；$-1$ 和 $3$；$-1$ 和 $2$；$1$ 和 $-3$，正确答案为第三个，解析见下。）**复习题：$x=2$ 和 $x=4$ 处的斜率各是多少？** |
| Find the derivative of $f(x)$. | （译注：以下各行均为上题的答案解析。）求 $f(x)$ 的导数。 |

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{df}{dx} &= \frac{d}{dx} \left( \frac{x^3}{3}-2 x^2+3 x+1 \right) \\ \rule{0pt}{2.25em}
\frac{df}{dx} &= x^2 - 4x +3
\end{aligned}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Substitute $x=2$ into the derivative to calculate the slope at $x=2$. | 把 $x=2$ 代入导数，算出 $x=2$ 处的斜率。 |

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{df}{dx}(2) &= (2)^2 - 4(2) + 3 \\ \rule{0pt}{2.25em}
\frac{df}{dx}(2) &= 4 - 8 + 3 \\ \rule{0pt}{2.25em}
\frac{df}{dx}(2) &= -1
\end{aligned}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Substitute $x=4$ into the derivative to calculate the slope at $x=4$. | 把 $x=4$ 代入导数，算出 $x=4$ 处的斜率。 |

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{df}{dx}(4) &= (4)^2 - 4(4) + 3 \\ \rule{0pt}{2.25em}
\frac{df}{dx}(4) &= 16 - 16 + 3 \\ \rule{0pt}{2.25em}
\frac{df}{dx}(4) &= 3
\end{aligned}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| The slope at $x=2$ is $-1$ and the slope at $x=4$ is $3$. | $x=2$ 处的斜率是 $-1$，$x=4$ 处的斜率是 $3$。 |
| The second derivative, whose notation I'll explain in a moment, is the derivative of the derivative, meaning it tells you how that slope is changing. The way to see this at a glance is to think of how the graph of $f(x)$ curves. At points where it curves upward, the slope is increasing, so the second derivative is positive. At points where it curves downward, the slope is decreasing, so the second derivative is negative. | 二阶导数——记号我马上会解释——就是「导数的导数」，意思是它告诉你那条斜率本身在怎么变化。一眼看出的办法，是去想 $f(x)$ 的图像如何弯曲：图像向上弯的点，斜率在增大，所以二阶导数为正；图像向下弯的点，斜率在减小，所以二阶导数为负。 |
| For example, let's plot the derivative and second derivative alongside the original function. The plot tells us a lot about how the slope is changing: At $x=1$ the slope is decreasing at a rate of $-2$, at $x=2$ the slope isn't changing at all, and at $x=3$ the slope is increasing at a rate of $2$. | 举个例子，我们把导数和二阶导数与原函数画在一起。这张图能告诉我们很多关于斜率如何变化的信息：在 $x=1$ 处，斜率以 $-2$ 的速率减小；在 $x=2$ 处，斜率毫无变化；在 $x=3$ 处，斜率以 $2$ 的速率增大。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 图像上的含义
> - **interpret** /ɪnˈtɜːprət/ —— 解读、解释。can be interpreted as the slope of its graph。
> - **steep** /stiːp/ —— 陡的。A steep slope means a high value for the derivative。
> - **substitute** /ˈsʌbstɪtjuːt/ —— 代入。Substitute $x=2$ into the derivative。
> - **at a glance** —— 一眼望去。The way to see this at a glance。
> - **curve** /kɜːv/ —— 弯曲（动词）。At points where it curves upward。
> - **alongside** /əˌlɒŋˈsaɪd/ —— 与……并排。plot the derivative ... alongside the original function。
> - *in a moment* —— 马上、待会儿。whose notation I'll explain in a moment。

## 记号（Notation）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| As far as notation goes, you could try writing it like this: | 至于记号，你可以试着这样写： |

$$
\frac{d\left(\frac{d f}{d x}\right)}{d x}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| ... indicating some small change to the derivative function divided by some small change to $x$, where as always the use of that letter $d$ suggests that you really want to consider what this ratio approach as $dx$, both $dx$'s in this case, approach $0$. That's pretty awkward and clunky, so the standard is to abbreviate it as $\frac{d^{2}f}{dx^2}$. | ……意思是「导数函数的某个微小变化」除以「$x$ 的某个微小变化」；一如既往，字母 $d$ 的使用暗示你真正想考虑的是：当 $dx$——这里是两个 $dx$——趋近 0 时，这个比值会趋近于什么（译注：原文 this ratio approach 系 approaches 之笔误，照录）。这么写实在又笨拙又累赘，所以标准做法是把它缩写为 $\frac{d^{2}f}{dx^2}$。 |

$$
\frac{d^2 f}{d x^2}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| It's not terribly important for getting an intuition of the second derivative, but perhaps it's worth showing how you can read this notation. Think of starting at some input to your function, and taking two small steps to the right, each with a size $dx$. I'm choosing rather big steps here so that we'll better see what's going on, but in principle think of them as rather tiny. | 对建立二阶导数的直觉而言，这并不是特别要紧，但展示一下怎么读这个记号，或许还是值得的。想象从函数的某个输入出发，向右迈出两小步，每步大小都是 $dx$。这里我特意把步子选得比较大，好让大家看得更清楚，但原则上要把它们想成非常小的步子。 |
| The first step causes some change to the function, which I'll call $df_1$, and the second step causes some similar, but possibly slightly different change, which I'll call $df_2$. | 第一步引起函数的某个变化，我把它叫作 $df_1$；第二步引起一个相似、但可能略有不同的变化，我把它叫作 $df_2$。 |
| The difference between these; the change in how the function changes, is what we'll call $d(df)$. | 这两者之差——也就是「函数的变化量本身的变化」——就是我们所说的 $d(df)$。 |
| You should think of this as really small, typically proportional to the size of $(dx)^2$. So if your choice for $dx$ was $0.01$, you'd expect this $d(df)$ to be proportional to $0.0001$. | 你应该把它想成非常小的量，通常与 $(dx)^2$ 的大小成正比。所以如果你把 $dx$ 取成 0.01，那这个 $d(df)$ 就会与 0.0001 成正比。 |

$$
d x=0.01 \Rightarrow(d x)^2=0.0001
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| And the second derivative is the size of this change to the change, divide by the size of $(dx)^2$. Or, more precisely, it's whatever that ratio approaches as $dx$ approaches $0$. Even though it's not like the letter $d$ is a variable being multiplied by $f$, for the sake of more compact notation you write this as $\frac{d^2f}{dx^2}$, and you don't bother with any parentheses on the bottom. | 而二阶导数，就是这个「变化量的变化」的大小，除以 $(dx)^2$ 的大小（译注：原文 divide 系 divided 之笔误，照录）。更精确地说，它是当 $dx$ 趋近 0 时该比值趋近的值。尽管字母 $d$ 并不是一个乘在 $f$ 上的变量，但为了记号更紧凑，我们把它写成 $\frac{d^2f}{dx^2}$，而且分母上懒得写任何括号。 |
| For higher order derivatives, like the third derivative, fourth derivative and so are are written using the same compact notation. | 三阶导数、四阶导数等等更高阶的导数，也都用同样紧凑的记号书写。（译注：原文 so are are written 系笔误，照录。） |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 记号
> - **awkward** /ˈɔːkwəd/ —— 笨拙的、别扭的。That's pretty awkward and clunky。
> - **clunky** /ˈklʌŋki/ —— 笨重累赘的。与 awkward 连用强调「又难看又难用」。
> - **abbreviate** /əˈbriːvieɪt/ —— 缩写。abbreviate it as $\frac{d^2f}{dx^2}$。
> - **proportional to** —— 与……成正比。typically proportional to the size of $(dx)^2$。
> - **compact** /kəmˈpækt/ —— 紧凑的。for the sake of more compact notation。
> - **nudge** /nʌdʒ/ —— 轻推（此处指输入的小步）。taking two small steps to the right。
> - *not terribly important* —— 并不是特别要紧。It's not terribly important for getting an intuition。
> - *not bother with* —— 懒得管、不去费事。you don't bother with any parentheses。

## 物理含义（Physical meaning）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Maybe the most visceral understanding of the second derivative is that it represents acceleration. | 对二阶导数最切身的理解，也许是：它代表加速度（acceleration）。 |

$$
\frac{d^2 s}{d t^2}(t) \Leftrightarrow \text { Acceleration }
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Given some movement along a line, suppose you have some function that records distance traveled vs. time, and maybe its graph looks something like this, steadily increasing over time. | 设想沿一条直线的某个运动，假设你有一个记录「已行驶距离对时间」的函数，它的图像也许长这样——随时间稳步上升。 |
| Then its derivative tells you velocity at each point in time, right? For the example, the graph might look like this bump, increasing to some maximum, then decreasing back to $0$. | 那么它的导数告诉你在每个时刻的速度，对吧？就这个例子而言，速度图像也许长成这样一个弧丘：先升到某个最大值，然后又降回 0。 |
| Then its second derivative tells you the rate of change for velocity, the acceleration at each point in time. | 而它的二阶导数告诉你速度的变化率，也就是每个时刻的加速度。 |
| In the example, the second derivative is positive for the first half of the journey, which indicates indicates speeding up. That's sensation of being pushed back into your car seat with a constant force. Or rather, having the car seat push you with a constant force. | 在这个例子里，前半段行程的二阶导数为正，这表明表明汽车在加速。那就是被一个恒定的力压回汽车座椅的感觉。说得更准确些，是汽车座椅用一个恒定的力推你。（译注：原文 indicates 重复，照录。） |
| The third derivative, and this is not a joke, is called jerk. So if the jerk is not zero, it means the strength of the acceleration itself is changing. | 三阶导数——这可不是开玩笑——叫作加加速度（jerk）。所以如果 jerk 不为零，就意味着加速度本身的强度在变化。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 物理含义
> - **visceral** /ˈvɪsərəl/ —— 发自肺腑的、切身的。the most visceral understanding of the second derivative。
> - **represent** /ˌreprɪˈzent/ —— 代表。it represents acceleration。
> - **steadily** /ˈstedəli/ —— 稳步地。steadily increasing over time。
> - **bump** /bʌmp/ —— 弧丘、隆起。look like this bump。
> - **sensation** /senˈseɪʃn/ —— 感觉。That's sensation of being pushed back。
> - **jerk** /dʒɜːk/ —— 加加速度（三阶导数的正式名称）。The third derivative ... is called jerk。
> - **indicate** /ˈɪndɪkeɪt/ —— 表明。which indicates speeding up。

## 综合练习（Comprehensive Question）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| A Pi Creature is aboard a spaceship and is set to journey into space. The height $s(t)$ of the spaceship from the ground, in meters, as a function of time $t$, in seconds, is given by: | 一只 Pi 生物（Pi Creature）登上一艘即将启程飞向太空的飞船。飞船离地面的高度 $s(t)$（以米计）作为时间 $t$（以秒计）的函数由下式给出： |

$$
s(t)=t^4-4 t^3+6 t^2
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Based on the displacement function $s(t)$, determine the velocity function $v(t)$ of the spaceship. | （译注：原文此处是一道互动选择题，选项为 $v(t) = 4t^3 - 12t^2 + 12t$；$v(t) = 4t^3 - 12t^2 + 6t$；$v(t) = 4t^2 - 8t + 6$；$v(t) = t^3 - 3t^2 + 2t$，正确答案为第一个。）根据位移函数 $s(t)$，求出飞船的速度函数 $v(t)$。 |
| Given the velocity function from the previous question, what is the acceleration function $a(t)$ of the spaceship? | （译注：互动选择题，选项为 $a(t) = 12t - 24$；$a(t) = 12t^2 - 24t$；$a(t) = 6t - 12$；$a(t) = 12t^2 - 24t + 12$，正确答案为第四个。）沿用上一题的速度函数，飞船的加速度函数 $a(t)$ 是什么？ |
| Determine the jerk function $j(t)$ of the spaceship, which is the rate of change of acceleration. | （译注：互动选择题，选项为 $j(t) = 24t - 24$；$j(t) = 24t$；$j(t) = 12$；$j(t) = 24t + 24$，正确答案为第一个。）求出飞船的加加速度函数 $j(t)$，也就是加速度的变化率。 |
| If you plot the acceleration of the function $a(t)$, how would you interpret the motion of the spaceship at $t=1$ second? | （译注：此为官网原文的自由作答题，参考答案见下。）如果把函数 $a(t)$ 的加速度画成图，你会如何解读飞船在 $t=1$ 秒时的运动？ |
| If you plot the acceleration function $a(t)$, you'll notice that at $t=1$ second, the acceleration dips to be $0$ before increasing again. | （译注：上题参考答案。）如果把加速度函数 $a(t)$ 画成图，你会注意到在 $t=1$ 秒处，加速度先下探到 0，然后才重新增大。 |
| One explanation is that this as a two-stage rocket where the first stage completes its burn and then there's a momentary pause before the second stage ignites. The momentary standstill in the change of acceleration, or the point where the jerk becomes zero, is when the spaceship is making that transition. | 一种解释是，这是一个两级火箭：第一级完成燃烧之后，有片刻停顿，然后第二级才点火。加速度变化出现的这一瞬间停滞——也就是 jerk 变成 0 的那个点——正是飞船完成这次换级的时刻。（译注：原文 this as a two-stage rocket 系漏字，应为 this is..., 照录。） |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 综合练习
> - **aboard** /əˈbɔːd/ —— 在（船、飞船）上。A Pi Creature is aboard a spaceship。
> - **determine** /dɪˈtɜːmɪn/ —— 确定、求出。determine the velocity function $v(t)$。
> - **dip to** —— 下探到。the acceleration dips to be $0$。
> - **momentary** /ˈməʊməntri/ —— 短暂的、片刻的。a momentary pause。
> - **ignite** /ɪɡˈnaɪt/ —— 点火、点燃。before the second stage ignites。
> - **standstill** /ˈstændstɪl/ —— 停滞、停顿。The momentary standstill in the change of acceleration。
> - **transition** /trænˈzɪʃn/ —— 过渡、转换。making that transition。
