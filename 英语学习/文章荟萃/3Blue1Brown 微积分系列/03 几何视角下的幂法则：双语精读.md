## f(x) = x² 的导数（Derivative of f(x) = x²）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Let's start with a function like $f(x) = x^2$. What is its derivative? That is to say, if you look at some value of $x$, like $x = 2$, and compare it to a value slightly bigger, just $dx$ bigger, what's that corresponding change in the value of the function, $df$? | 我们从 $f(x) = x^2$ 这样的函数开始。它的导数是什么？也就是说，如果你盯着 $x$ 的某个值（比如 $x = 2$），拿它与一个稍微大一点、只大了 $dx$ 的值相比，函数值相应地变化了多少，也就是 $df$ 是多少？ |
| In particular, what is $df$ divided by $dx$? The _rate_ at which this function changes per unit change in $x$? As the first step for intuition, we know you can think of the ratio $\frac{df}{dx}$ as the slope of a tangent line to the graph of $x^2$. From that, we can see that the slope generally increases as $x$ increases. At $0$, the tangent line is flat, so the slope is $0$. At $x = 1$, it's something steeper, at $x=2$, it's steeper still. | 具体地说，$df$ 除以 $dx$ 是多少？也就是这个函数*每单位* $x$ 变化的变化*率*？作为建立直觉的第一步，我们知道可以把比值 $\frac{df}{dx}$ 想成 $x^2$ 图像上一条切线的斜率。由此可以看出，斜率大体上随 $x$ 增大而增大：在 0 处切线是平的，斜率为 0；在 $x = 1$ 处更陡一些；在 $x=2$ 处还要更陡。 |
| But looking at graphs isn't generally the best way to understand the _precise_ formula for a derivative. For that, it's best to take a more literal look at what $x^2$ actually means. In this case, let's picture a square whose side length is $x$. | 不过，看图像一般并不是理解导数*精确*公式的最佳途径。要得到精确公式，最好更贴着字面去看看 $x^2$ 实际是什么意思。在这个例子里，我们想象一个边长为 $x$ 的正方形。 |
| If you increase $x$ by a tiny nudge $dx$, what is the resulting change to the area of the square? That slight change in area represents $df$: the tiny increase in the value of $f(x)=x^2$ caused by increasing $x$ by a tiny nudge $dx$. | 如果你把 $x$ 增大一个小小的推动量 $dx$，正方形的面积会相应变化多少？面积的这点变化就代表 $df$：把 $x$ 增大一个小小的 $dx$ 所引起的 $f(x)=x^2$ 的微小增量。 |
| There's three new bits of area in this diagram, two thin rectangles, and a miniscule square. | 图里出现了三块新的面积：两个细长的矩形，和一个极小的正方形。 |
| The two thin rectangles have side lengths $x$ and $dx$, so together they account for $2 \cdot x \cdot dx$ units of new area. For example, if $x$ was $3$ and $dx$ was $0.01$, the new area from these thin rectangles would be $2 \cdot 3 \cdot 0.01$, which is $0.06$; About $6$ times the size of $dx$. | 两个细矩形的长短边分别是 $x$ 和 $dx$，所以它们合计贡献 $2 \cdot x \cdot dx$ 单位的新面积。比如，如果 $x$ 是 3、$dx$ 是 0.01，这两个细矩形带来的新面积就是 $2 \cdot 3 \cdot 0.01 = 0.06$，大约是 $dx$ 的 6 倍。 |
| **If $x=2$ and $dx$ was $0.01$, approximately how much bigger would the new area be relative to the size of $dx$?** | （译注：原文此处是一道互动选择题，选项为 6 / 2 / 4 / 3，正确答案为第三个，解析见下。）**如果 $x=2$、$dx$ 为 0.01，新面积大约是 $dx$ 的多少倍？** |
| If $x=2$ and $dx$ is $0.01$, the area of the thin rectangles would be $2 \cdot 2 \cdot 0.01$; About $4$ times the size of $dx$. We can check this answer by looking at the slope of the tangent line at $x=2$ on the plot of $f(x)=x^2$. Eyeballing the graph a slope, or rise over run, of about $4$ looks right. | （译注：上题的答案解析。）如果 $x=2$、$dx$ 为 0.01，细矩形的面积就是 $2 \cdot 2 \cdot 0.01$，大约是 $dx$ 的 4 倍。我们可以在 $f(x)=x^2$ 的图像上看 $x=2$ 处切线的斜率来验证这个答案：目测图像，斜率（纵变除以横变）约为 4，看上去没错。 |
| That little square has area $dx^2$, but you should think of this as being _really_ tiny; negligibly tiny. For example, if $dx$ was $0.01$, it would be $0.0001$. I'm drawing $dx$ with a fair bit of width here, so we can see it, but always remember in principle $dx$ should be thought of as a truly tiny amount. | 那个小正方形的面积是 $dx^2$，但你应当把它想得*非常*小——小到可以忽略。比如 $dx$ 是 0.01 时，它就是 0.0001。我这里把 $dx$ 画得比较宽，是为了让你能看见它；但永远记住，原则上 $dx$ 应当被想成一个真正微小的量。 |
| Phrased more precisely, our final consideration will always be what happens as the size of this $dx$ approaches $0$, and as that happens the proportion of this yellow area $df$ which is accounted for by the tiny $dx^2$ corner will go to $0$. | 说得更精确些，我们最终的考量永远是：当这个 $dx$ 的大小趋近 0 时会发生什么；而随着它趋近 0，黄色面积 $df$ 里由那个小小的 $dx^2$ 角所占据的比例将趋于 0。 |
| The full unapproximated change $df$ represented by all the yellow area above looks like $df = 2x \cdot dx + (dx)^2$. So you might begin thinking of the expression for the derivative like this: | 上面所有黄色面积所代表的、未做近似的完整变化量 $df$ 形如 $df = 2x \cdot dx + (dx)^2$。于是你可以开始这样写导数的表达式： |

$$
\frac{df}{dx} = \frac{2x \cdot dx + (dx)^2}{dx} = 2x + dx
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Remember, if we're using this "$d$" notation, the implicit meaning is that we consider what happens as $dx \to 0$. So in this case, our final expression would look like this. | 记住，一旦用上这种 "$d$" 记号，隐含的意思就是：我们要考虑 $dx \to 0$ 时会发生什么。所以在这个例子里，我们最终的表达式长这样： |

$$
\frac{df}{dx} = 2x
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Notice how we could have simply ignored the $(dx)^2$ term since it doesn't get fully canceled out when dividing by $dx$. A good rule of thumb is that you can ignore anything which includes a $dx$ raised to a power greater than one; that is, a tiny change squared is a negligible change. | 注意，我们其实本可以直接无视 $(dx)^2$ 这一项，因为它在除以 $dx$ 时不会被完全抵消掉。一条好用的经验法则是：凡是含有 $dx$ 的一次以上幂的东西都可以忽略；也就是说，微小变化的平方是可忽略的变化。 |
| **For example, if you were starting at $x=3$, as you slightly increase $x$, what is the rate in change of the area per unit of length added?** | （译注：原文此处是一道互动选择题，选项为 The rate of change in area would be 6 / 3 / 4 / 2，正确答案为第一个，解析见下。）**比如，如果你从 $x=3$ 出发，稍微增大 $x$，面积每增加一单位长度所对应的变化率是多少？** |
| The rate of change in the area per unit of length added, $\frac{d(x^2)}{dx}$, would be $2 \cdot 3$, or $6$. As another example, if you were starting at $x=5$, that rate of change would be $10$ units of area per change in $x$. | （译注：上题的答案解析。）面积每单位新增长度的变化率 $\frac{d(x^2)}{dx}$ 就是 $2 \cdot 3 = 6$。再举个例子，如果你从 $x=5$ 出发，这个变化率就是：$x$ 每变化 1，面积变化 10 个单位。 |

## f(x) = x³ 的导数（Derivative of f(x) = x³）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Let's try a different simple function, $f(x) = x^3$. This will be the geometric view of what you and I went through algebraically in the last chapter for the function $x^3$. We can think of $x^3$ geometrically as the volume of a cube with side lengths $x$. | 我们来试一个别的简单函数：$f(x) = x^3$。这是上一章里你我用代数处理过 $x^3$ 之后，补上的几何视角。几何上，我们可以把 $x^3$ 想成边长为 $x$ 的立方体的体积。 |
| When you increase $x$ by a tiny nudge, $dx$, the volume increases as shown in the figure below. That represents all the volume in a cube with side length $x + dx$ that's not already in the original cube with side length $x$. | 当你把 $x$ 增大一个小小的推动量 $dx$，体积就像下图那样增大。它代表的是：边长为 $x + dx$ 的立方体里，原本边长为 $x$ 的立方体中不曾包含的那部分体积。 |
| Remember that we are interested in what happens as $dx$ approaches $0$. The length of $dx$ is illustrated so big to demonstrate the change in volume it introduces. | （图注）记住，我们关心的是 $dx$ 趋近 0 时会发生什么。图中把 $dx$ 的长度画得特别大，只是为了演示它带来的体积变化。 |
| This figure shows the increase in volume of a cube when its side length $x$ is increased by a small nudge $dx$. It's nice to think of this new volume broken up into multiple components, but almost all of it comes from the three square faces; Or, said a little more precisely, as $dx$ approaches $0$, those three squares comprise a portion closer and closer to 100% of the new volume. | 这张图展示的是：当立方体的边长 $x$ 被小小的 $dx$ 推大时，体积增加了多少。把这份新体积拆成多个组成部分来想会很舒服，但几乎全部增量都来自那三个正方形的面；或者稍微精确一点说：随着 $dx$ 趋近 0，那三个正方形占新体积的比例会越来越接近 100%。 |
| Each of those thin squares has a volume of $x^2 \cdot dx$; the area of the face times the thickness of $dx$, so in total this gives us $3x^2dx$ of volume change. There are some other slivers of volume along the edges, and in the corner, but their volume will be proportional to $dx^2$, or $dx^3$, so they can be ignored. Again, this is because ultimately they will be divided by $dx$, and if there's still any $dx$ remaining, these terms won't survive the process of letting $dx$ approach $0$. | 每个薄正方形板的体积是 $x^2 \cdot dx$——面的面积乘以 $dx$ 的厚度——所以三块加起来给我们 $3x^2dx$ 的体积变化。还有一些别的体积碎片贴在棱边和角落上，但它们的体积与 $dx^2$ 或 $dx^3$ 成正比，因此可以忽略。重申一下，这是因为它们最终都要被除以 $dx$，而只要还残留任何 $dx$，这些项就活不到 $dx$ 趋近 0 的最后。 |

$$
(x + dx)^3 = x^3 + \color{orange} 3x^2 \color{currentColor} dx \color{gray} + 3xdx^2 + dx^3
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| So the derivative of $x^3$, the rate at which $x^3$ changes per unit of change in $x$, is $3x^2$. Looking at the graph, this means the slope of the graph of $x^3$ at each point $x$ is exactly $3x^2$. | 所以 $x^3$ 的导数——$x^3$ 每单位 $x$ 变化的变化率——就是 $3x^2$。看图像的话，这意味着 $x^3$ 的图像在每点 $x$ 处的斜率恰好是 $3x^2$。 |
| Graphical intuition with slope can tell us why this derivative is high on the left, $0$ at the origin, and high on the right, but just thinking in terms of graphs would not land us on the precise quantity $3x^2$. For that, we had to take a much more direct look at the actual meaning of the function. | 靠斜率的图像直觉能告诉我们：为什么这个导数左边大、原点处为 0、右边大；但只盯着图像想，是不会让我们落到 $3x^2$ 这个精确量上的。要做到这一点，我们必须更直接地去看这个函数的实际含义。 |

## f(x) = xⁿ 的导数（Derivative of f(x) = xⁿ）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| In practice, you wouldn't necessarily think of the square every time you're taking a derivative of $x^2$, nor would you necessarily think of the cube when taking a derivative of $x^3$. Instead, thinking like a mathematician, can you generalize this approach to see if a pattern emerges? Can you invent a tool to find the derivative of any polynomial? | 实践中，你未必每次给 $x^2$ 求导都要去想那个正方形，也未必每次给 $x^3$ 求导都要想那个立方体。不如像个数学家那样思考：能不能把这个做法推广开，看看会不会浮现出一个模式？你能不能发明一件工具，求出任何多项式的导数？ |
| Let's look at the pattern of the first three monomial functions, where I've included the monomial of degree one for completeness. So far, each of these monomial functions has had a nice geometric meaning.
