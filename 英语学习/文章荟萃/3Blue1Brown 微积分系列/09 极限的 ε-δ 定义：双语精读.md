\lim _{h \rightarrow 0} \frac{(2+h)^{3}-2^{3}}{h}

|---|---|

|---|---|

$$
\lim _{h \rightarrow 0} \frac{12 h + 6 h^2 + h^3}{h}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Since all of the terms in the numerator share the factor $h$, the fraction simplifies. | 由于分子里的所有项都共享因子 $h$，分式得以化简。 |

\lim _{h \rightarrow 0} 12 + 6 h + h^2
$$

|---|---|

$$

## 反例（Counter example）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| As a counter example, consider a function that looks like the one shown below, which is also not defined at $0$, but kind of jumps at that point. | 作为一个反例，来看一个长得像下面这样的函数：它在 $0$ 处同样没有定义，但在那个点上却有点「跳」。 |
| As you approach $h = 0$ from the right, the function approaches $2$, but as you come at $0$ from the left, it approaches $1$. Since there's not a clear, unambiguous value that this function approaches as $h$ approaches $0$, the limit is simply not defined at that point. | 当你从右边逼近 $h = 0$ 时，函数趋近于 2；而当你从左边逼近 $0$ 时，它趋近于 1。由于当 $h$ 趋近 $0$ 时，这个函数并没有一个清晰、毫无歧义的趋近值，极限在这一点上干脆就没有定义。 |
| One way to think of this is that when you look at any range of inputs around $0$, and the corresponding range of outputs, as you shrink that input range the corresponding outputs don't narrow in on any specific value. Instead those outputs straddle a range that never even shrinks smaller than $1$, no matter how small your input range. | 理解这一点的一个办法是：任取一个 $0$ 附近的输入范围，连同对应的输出范围，当你把这个输入范围收窄时，对应的输出并不会向任何特定值收拢。相反，不管你的输入范围多小，那些输出始终横跨在一个范围上，这个范围甚至永远不会缩到小于 1。 |
| This perspective of shrinking an input range around the limiting point, and seeing whether or not you're restricted in how much that shrinks the output range, leads to something called the "**epsilon delta**" definition of limits. | 这种视角——在极限点周围收缩输入范围，看看输出范围因此收缩的程度会不会受到限制——引出了所谓的极限「**epsilon delta**」定义。 |
| You've already seen the main idea: when a limit exists, you can make this output range as small as you want; but when the limit doesn't exist, that output range can't get smaller than some value, no matter how much you shrink the input range around the limiting input. | 你已经见过它的核心思想了：极限存在时，你可以让输出范围想要多小就多小；而极限不存在时，不管你把极限输入周围的输入范围缩得多小，输出范围都没法小于某个值。 |
| The limit $\displaystyle \lim _{h \rightarrow a} f(h)$ exists when the output range defined by $\epsilon$ (epsilon) can be made as small as you want and you can still find some $\delta$ (delta) which corresponds to the output range. | （图注）极限 $\displaystyle \lim _{h \rightarrow a} f(h)$ 存在，是指：由 $\epsilon$（epsilon）划定的输出范围可以想要多小就多小，而你仍然能找到某个与该输出范围相应的 $\delta$（delta）。 |
| Let's phrase that same idea, but a little more precisely. In the context of this example where the limiting value was $12$, think of any distance away from $12$, where it's common to use the greek letter $\epsilon$ "epsilon" to denote that distance. And the intent here is going to be that this distance $\epsilon$ can be as small as you want. | 让我们把同一个想法再表述得稍微精确一点。在这个极限值为 12 的例子里，设想离 12 任意远的一个距离；习惯上用希腊字母 $\epsilon$「epsilon」来表示这个距离。而这里的用意是：这个距离 $\epsilon$ 可以想要多小就多小。 |
| What it means for the limit to exist is that you will always be able to find a range of inputs around our limiting input, some distance delta away from $0$, so that any input within a distance delta of $0$ corresponds to an output within a distance epsilon of $12$. | 极限存在的含义是：你总能在我们的极限输入周围找到一个输入范围——离 $0$ 某个 delta 的距离之内——使得任何与 $0$ 的距离在 delta 以内的输入，都对应一个与 12 的距离在 epsilon 以内的输出。 |
| They key point is that this is true for any epsilon, no matter how small, you will always be able to find the corresponding delta. | （译注：原文 They key 系 The key 之笔误，照录。）关键在于：这对任何 epsilon 都成立——无论它多小，你总能找到与之相应的 delta。 |
| **What is the $\delta$ (delta) value corresponding to the output range $[11.994, 12.006]$ where $\epsilon$ (epsilon) is equal to $0.006$?** | （译注：原文此处是一道互动选择题，选项为 $1$、$0.1$、$0.01$、$0.001$，正确答案为第四个（$0.001$），解析见下。）**与输出范围 $[11.994, 12.006]$ 相应的 $\delta$（delta）值是多少？这里的 $\epsilon$（epsilon）等于 $0.006$。** |
| The output range of $[11.994, 12.006]$ is visually shown in the graph below. | （译注：上题的答案解析。）输出范围 $[11.994, 12.006]$ 在下图中直观地画了出来。 |
| Then we can see that an input range of $[-0.001, 0.001]$ corresponds to our output range of $[11.994, 12.006]$. Substituting $0$ plus or minus $\delta = 0.001$ into the function gives us the output. | 然后可以看到，输入范围 $[-0.001, 0.001]$ 对应我们的输出范围 $[11.994, 12.006]$。把 $0$ 加减 $\delta = 0.001$ 代入函数，就得到输出。 |

$$
f(-0.001) < f(0) < f(0.001)
$$

$$
\frac{(2+(-0.001))^{3}-2^{3}}{-0.001} < 12 < \frac{(2+(0.001))^{3}-2^{3}}{0.001}
$$

$$
11.994 < 12 < 12.006
$$

| In contrast, when a limit does _not_ exist, as in the example before, you can find a sufficiently small epsilon, like $0.4$, so that no matter how small you make your range around $0$, no matter how tiny delta is, the corresponding range of outputs is just always too big. | 相比之下，当一个极限*不*存在时——就像前面那个例子——你能找到一个足够小的 epsilon，比如 $0.4$，使得不管你把 $0$ 周围的范围取得多小、不管 delta 多么微小，对应的输出范围就是始终太大。 |
| There is no limiting output where everything is within a distance epsilon from that output. | 不存在这样一个极限输出，能让一切都落在与它相距 epsilon 的范围之内。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 反例
> - **straddle** /ˈstrædl/ —— 横跨、骑跨。those outputs straddle a range。
> - **shrink** /ʃrɪŋk/ —— 收缩、缩小。as you shrink that input range；过去式 shrank，过去分词 shrunk。
> - **denote** /dɪˈnəʊt/ —— 表示、指代。use the greek letter $\epsilon$ to denote that distance。
> - **sufficiently** /səˈfɪʃntli/ —— 足够地。a sufficiently small epsilon。
> - *narrow in on* —— 向……收拢、聚焦到。the corresponding outputs don't narrow in on any specific value。
> - *correspond to* —— 与……对应、相应。any input … corresponds to an output。
> - *in contrast* —— 相比之下、相反。In contrast, when a limit does _not_ exist…
> - 纯中文说明：注意角色分工——ε（epsilon）是「输出侧」的允许误差，δ（delta）是「输入侧」的相应范围，两者别混。
