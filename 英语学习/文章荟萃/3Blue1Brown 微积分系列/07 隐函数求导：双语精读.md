## 隐函数曲线（Implicit Curves）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Let's say you have a circle with radius $5$ centered at the origin of the $xy$-coordinate plane. This shape is defined using the equation $x^2 + y^2 = 5^2$. That is, all points on this circle are a distance $5$ from the origin, as encapsulated by the pythagorean theorem where the sum of the squares of the legs of this triangle equals the square of the hypotenuse, $5^2$. | 假设你有一个半径为 $5$、圆心在 $xy$ 坐标平面原点的圆。这个形状由方程 $x^2 + y^2 = 5^2$ 定义。也就是说，圆上所有点到原点的距离都是 $5$——正如勾股定理所概括的：这个三角形两条直角边的平方之和，等于斜边的平方 $5^2$。 |
| And suppose you want to find the slope of a tangent line to this circle, maybe at the point $(x, y) = (3, 4)$. | 再假设你想求这个圆的某条切线的斜率，比如在点 $(x, y) = (3, 4)$ 处。 |
| Now, if you're savvy with geometry, you might already know that this tangent line is perpendicular to the radius line touching that point. But let's say you don't already know that, or that you want a technique that generalizes to curves other than circles. | 此刻，如果你几何学得精通，可能已经知道这条切线垂直于接触该点的半径。但假设你并不知道这一点，或者你想要一种能推广到圆以外其他曲线的技术。 |
| As with other problems about the slopes of tangent lines to curves, they key thought here is to zoom in close enough that the curve basically looks just like its own tangent line, then ask about a tiny step along that curve. | 与其他关于曲线切线斜率的问题一样，这里的关键想法（原文 they key thought 系 the key thought 之笔误，照录）是：放大到足够近，让曲线看上去基本就像它自己的切线，然后问沿这条曲线的一小步会怎样。 |
| The $y$-component of that little step *along the curve* is what you might call $dy$, and the $x$-component is a little $dx$, so the slope that we want is the rise over run $dy/dx$. | （图注）这一小步_沿曲线方向_的 $y$ 分量，就是你可以叫作 $dy$ 的东西；$x$ 分量是一个小小的 $dx$，所以我们想要的斜率，就是「纵变除以横变」的 $dy/dx$。 |
| But unlike other tangent-slope problems in calculus, this curve is not the graph of a function. We cannot take a simple derivative, like we have in the past, by asking about the size of a tiny nudge to the output of a function caused by some tiny nudge to the input. In the equation $x$ is not an input and $y$ is not an output, they're both just interdependent values related by some equation. | 但与微积分里其他切线斜率问题不同的是，这条曲线不是一个函数的图像。我们无法像过去那样，通过问「输入的微小轻推引起函数输出的微小轻推有多大」来取一个简单的导数。在这个方程里，$x$ 不是输入，$y$ 也不是输出；它们只是由某个方程联系起来的、彼此依赖的两个值。 |
| This is called an "implicit curve"; it's just the set of all points $(x, y)$ that satisfy some property written in terms of the two variables $x$ and $y$. | 这叫作「隐函数曲线」（implicit curve）：它就是满足某个以两个变量 $x$ 和 $y$ 写出的性质的全体点 $(x, y)$ 的集合。 |
| The procedure for how you actually find $dy/dx$ for curves like this is the thing I found very weird as a calculus student. You take the derivative of both sides of this equation like this: | 对于这类曲线，你实际求出 $dy/dx$ 的操作流程，就是我当微积分学生时觉得非常怪的那件事。你像这样对方程两边取导数： |
| For the derivative of $x^2$ you write $2x \cdot dx$. | 求 $x^2$ 的导数，你写下 $2x \cdot dx$。 |
| Similarly, $y^2$ becomes $2y \cdot dy$. | 类似地，$y^2$ 变成 $2y \cdot dy$。 |
| The derivative of the constant $5^2$ on the right is just $0$. | 右边常数 $5^2$ 的导数就是 $0$。 |
| You can see why this feels strange, right? What does it mean to take a derivative of an expression with multiple variables? And why are we tacking on the little $dy$ and $dx$ in this way? Unlike with functions, there isn't clear flow from input to output. But if you just blindly move forward with what you get here, you can rearrange this equation to find an expression for $dy/dx$, which in this case comes out to $-x/y$. | 你能看出为什么这感觉奇怪，对吧？对一个含多个变量的表达式取导数，究竟是什么意思？我们为什么要在后面缀上小小的 $dy$ 和 $dx$？与函数不同，这里没有从输入到输出的清晰流向。但如果你就带着这里得到的东西盲目向前走，你可以重新整理这个方程，找出 $dy/dx$ 的表达式——在这个例子里它算出来是 $-x/y$。 |
| So at a point with coordinates $(x, y) = (3, 4)$, that slope would be $-3/4$, evidently. This strange process is called "implicit differentiation". Don't worry, I have an explanation for how you can interpret taking a derivative of an expression with two variables like this. But first, I want to set aside this particular problem, and show how this is related to a different type of calculus problem, something called a related rates problem. | 于是在坐标为 $(x, y) = (3, 4)$ 的点处，那个斜率显然就是 $-3/4$。这个奇怪的过程叫作「隐函数求导」（implicit differentiation）。别担心，对于「怎样理解像这样对含两个变量的表达式求导」，我有一个解释。但首先，我想把这个具体问题放到一边，展示它与另一类微积分问题的联系——那类问题叫作「相关变化率问题」（related rates problem）。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 隐函数曲线
> - **savvy** /ˈsævi/ —— 精通的、在行的。if you're savvy with geometry。
> - **perpendicular** /ˌpɜːpənˈdɪkjələ/ —— 垂直的。perpendicular to the radius line。
> - **encapsulate** /ɪnˈkæpsjuleɪt/ —— 概括、浓缩。as encapsulated by the pythagorean theorem。
> - **hypotenuse** /haɪˈpɒtənjuːz/ —— 斜边。the square of the hypotenuse。
> - **interdependent** /ˌɪntədɪˈpendənt/ —— 相互依赖的。they're both just interdependent values。
> - **generalize** /ˈdʒenrəlaɪz/ —— 推广。a technique that generalizes to curves other than circles。
> - *tack on* —— 缀上、附加。why are we tacking on the little $dy$ and $dx$。
> - *set aside* —— 放到一边、搁置。set aside this particular problem。

## 相关变化率（Related Rates）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Imagine a $5$ meter long ladder held up against a wall, where the top of the ladder starts off $4$ meters above the ground, which—by the pythagorean theorem—means the bottom is $3$ meters away from the wall. | 想象一架 $5$ 米长的梯子靠在墙上，梯子顶端起初离地 $4$ 米——由勾股定理，这意味着梯子底部离墙 $3$ 米。 |
| And say it's slipping down the wall in such a way that the top of the ladder is dropping at $1$ meter per second. In that initial moment, what is the rate at which the bottom of the ladder is moving away from the wall? | 再说梯子正沿墙下滑，下滑的方式使得梯子顶端以每秒 $1$ 米的速度下落。在起始那一刻，梯子底部离开墙的移动速度是多少？ |
| It's interesting, right? That distance from the bottom of the ladder to the wall is $100\%$ determined by the distance between the top of the ladder and the floor, so we should have enough information to figure out how the rates of change for each value depend on each other, but it might not be entirely clear how exactly you relate those two. | 很有意思，对吧？梯子底部到墙的距离 $100\%$ 由梯子顶端到地面的距离决定，所以我们手里的信息应该足以弄清这两个量的变化率如何相互依赖，但这两者到底该怎么联系起来，可能并不完全清楚。 |
| First thing's first, it's always nice to give names to the quantities we care about. So label the distance from the top of the ladder to the ground $y(t)$, written as a function of time because it's changing. Likewise, label the distance between the bottom of the ladder and the wall $x(t)$. They key equation that relates these terms is the pythagorean theorem: $x(t)^2 + y(t)^2 = 5^2$. What makes this equation powerful is that it's true at all points in time. | 要紧的事先办：给我们关心的量起名字总是好的。把梯子顶端到地面的距离记作 $y(t)$——写成时间的函数，因为它在变。同样，把梯子底部与墙之间的距离记作 $x(t)$。把这些项联系起来的关键方程（原文 They key equation 系 The key equation 之笔误，照录）是勾股定理：$x(t)^2 + y(t)^2 = 5^2$。这个方程之所以强大，是因为它在所有时刻都成立。 |
| One way to solve this would be to isolate $x(t)$, figure out what what $y(t)$ must be based this $1$ meter/second drop rate, then take a derivative of the resulting function; $dx/dt$, the rate at which $x$ is changing with respect to time. | 一种解法（原文 what what 与 based this 均系笔误，照录）是先把 $x(t)$ 解出来，基于这个每秒 $1$ 米的下落速率弄清 $y(t)$ 必须是多少，然后对得到的函数求导，得到 $dx/dt$——$x$ 随时间变化的速率。 |
| And that's fine; if you're willing to apply the chain rule a few times, this method will definitely work for you. But I want to show a different way to think about the same problem. | 那样做也挺好；只要你愿意把链式法则多用几次，这个方法绝对可行。但我想展示思考同一个问题的另一种方式。 |
| This left-hand side of the equation is a function of time, right? Which just so happens to equal a constant, meaning this value evidently doesn't change while time passes. | 方程的左边是时间的函数，对吧？它恰好等于一个常数，也就是说这个值显然不随时间流逝而改变。 |
| However, this expression is dependent on time which we can manipulate like any other function with $t$ as an input. In particular, we can take a derivative of the left hand side, which is a way of saying "If I let a little bit of time, $dt$, pass, which causes $y$ to slightly decrease, and $x$ to slightly increase, how much does this expression change?" | 不过，这个表达式依赖于时间，而我们可以像对待任何其他以 $t$ 为输入的函数一样摆弄它。特别地，我们可以对左边求导，这等于在说：「如果我让一点点时间 $dt$ 流逝——它使 $y$ 略微减小、$x$ 略微增大——这个表达式会变化多少？」 |
| On the one hand, we know that derivative should be $0$, since this expression is a constant, and constants don't care about your tiny nudge to time; they remain unchanged. But on the other hand, what do you get when you compute the derivative of this as a function of time? | 一方面，我们知道这个导数应该是 $0$，因为这个表达式是常数，而常数根本不在乎你对时间的小轻推；它们保持不变。但另一方面，当你把它当作时间的函数来计算导数时，你会得到什么？ |
| The derivative of $x(t)^2$ is $2 \cdot x(t)$ times the derivative of $x$. That's the chain rule in action. $2x \cdot dx$ represents the size of a change to $x^2$ caused by a change to $x$, and then we're dividing out by $dt$. Likewise, the rate at which $y(t)^2$ is changing is $2 \cdot y(t)$ times the derivative of $y$. | $x(t)^2$ 的导数是 $2 \cdot x(t)$ 乘以 $x$ 的导数。这就是链式法则在起作用：$2x \cdot dx$ 表示 $x$ 的变化给 $x^2$ 造成的变化的大小，然后我们再把它除以 $dt$。同样，$y(t)^2$ 的变化速率是 $2 \cdot y(t)$ 乘以 $y$ 的导数。 |
| Now, evidently, this whole expression must be zero, and that's an equivalent of saying $x^2+y^2$ must not change while the ladder moves. And at the very start, $t=0$, the height $y(t)$ is $4$ meters, the distance $x(t)$ is $3$ meters, and since the top of the ladder is dropping at a rate of $1$ meter per second, that derivative $dy/dt$ is $-1$ meters/second. | 现在，显然这整个表达式必须为零，这等价于说：梯子滑动时 $x^2+y^2$ 不能变。而在最开始的 $t=0$ 时刻，高度 $y(t)$ 是 $4$ 米，距离 $x(t)$ 是 $3$ 米；由于梯子顶端以每秒 $1$ 米的速率下落，导数 $dy/dt$ 就是 $-1$ 米/秒。 |
| Now this gives us enough information to isolate the derivative $dx/dt$, which, when you work it out, it comes out to be $4/3$ meters per second. | 这就给了我们足够的信息来解出导数 $dx/dt$——你算一下，它算出来是每秒 $4/3$ 米。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 相关变化率
> - **ladder** /ˈlædl/ —— 梯子。a $5$ meter long ladder held up against a wall。
> - **slip** /slɪp/ —— 滑落、打滑。it's slipping down the wall。
> - **isolate** /ˈaɪsəleɪt/ —— 孤立出来、解出。isolate $x(t)$；isolate the derivative $dx/dt$。
> - **manipulate** /məˈnɪpjuleɪt/ —— 摆弄、操作。we can manipulate like any other function。
> - **evidently** /ˈevɪdəntli/ —— 显然。this value evidently doesn't change。
> - **label** /ˈleɪbl/ —— 标记、记作。label the distance from the top of the ladder to the ground $y(t)$。
> - *first thing's first* —— 要紧的事先办。First thing's first, it's always nice to give names。

## 隐函数求导（Implicit Differentiation）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Now compare this to the problem of finding the slope of the tangent line to the circle. In both cases, we had the equation $x^2 + y^2 = 5^2$, and in both cases we ended up taking the derivative of each side of this expression. But for the ladder problem, these expressions were functions of time, so taking the derivative has a clear meaning: it's the rate at which this expression changes as time change. | 现在把这与求圆的切线斜率的问题对比一下。两种情形里，我们都有方程 $x^2 + y^2 = 5^2$，也都对这个表达式两边取了导数。但在梯子问题里，这些表达式是时间的函数，所以求导有明确的含义：它是这个表达式随时间变化的速率。 |
| What makes the circle situation strange is that rather than saying a small amount of time $dt$ has passed, which causes $x$ and $y$ to change, the derivative has the tiny nudges $dx$ and $dy$ both just floating free, not tied to some other common variable like time. | 圆的情形之所以奇怪，是因为我们并不是说「一小段时间 $dt$ 过去了，从而引起 $x$ 和 $y$ 变化」，而是导数里那两个微小轻推 $dx$ 和 $dy$ 都自由漂浮着，不与时间这样的其他公共变量绑在一起。 |
| Let me show you how you can think about this: Give this expression $x^2 + y^2$ a name, maybe $S$. $S$ is essentially a function of two variables; it takes every point $(x, y)$ on the plane and associates it with a number. | 让我给你展示可以怎么想这件事：给表达式 $x^2 + y^2$ 起个名字，比如 $S$。$S$ 本质上是一个二元函数；它取平面上的每个点 $(x, y)$，把它对应到一个数。 |
| For points on this circle, that number happens to be $25$. | 对这个圆上的点来说，那个数恰好是 $25$。 |
| If you step off that circle away from the center, that value would be bigger. For other points $(x, y)$ closer to the origin, that value is smaller. | （图注）如果你沿着离开圆心的方向走出这个圆，那个值会变大。而对更靠近原点的其他点 $(x, y)$，那个值会变小。 |
| What it means to take a derivative of this expression, a derivative of $S$, is to consider a tiny change to both these variables, some tiny change $dx$ to $x$, and some tiny change $dy$ to $y$ – and not necessarily one that keeps you on this circle, by the way, it's just some tiny step in any direction on the $xy$-plane. | 对这个表达式取导数——对 $S$ 取导数——意味着考虑这两个变量各自的微小变化：$x$ 的某个微小变化 $dx$，$y$ 的某个微小变化 $dy$——顺便说一句，不一定是让你留在这个圆上的那种变化，它只是 $xy$ 平面上任意方向的一小步。 |
| From there you ask how much the value of $S$ changes. That difference in the value of $S$, from the original point to the nudged point, is what we would call "$dS$" or "the change to the function $S$". | 然后你问 $S$ 的值变化了多少。$S$ 的值从原点到被轻推的点之间的差，就是我们会叫作「$dS$」或「函数 $S$ 的变化」的东西。 |
| As we saw in previous lessons, these $dx$ and $dy$ expressions raised to the second power are negligible as the nudges approaches zero. | （图注）正如前面几课所见，这些带上二次幂的 $dx$ 和 $dy$ 项，当轻推趋近零时可以忽略不计。 |
| For example, say we're starting at a point $(3,4)$, and let's just say that step $dx$ is $-0.02$, and that $dy$ is $-0.01$. Then the decrease to $S$, the amount that $x^2+y^2$ changes over that step, will be around $2(3)(-0.02) + 2(4)(-0.01)$. | 比如，假设我们从点 $(3,4)$ 出发，再假设这一步的 $dx$ 是 $-0.02$，$dy$ 是 $-0.01$。那么 $S$ 的减少量——$x^2+y^2$ 在这一步上的变化量——约为 $2(3)(-0.02) + 2(4)(-0.01)$。 |
| That's what this derivative expression $2x \cdot dx + 2y \cdot dy$ means. It tells you how much the value $x^2+y^2$ changes, as determined by the point $(x, y)$ where you started, and the tiny step $(dx, dy)$ that you take. | 这就是导数表达式 $2x \cdot dx + 2y \cdot dy$ 的含义。它告诉你 $x^2+y^2$ 的值会变化多少，这由你出发的点 $(x, y)$ 与你迈出的微小一步 $(dx, dy)$ 共同决定。 |
| As with all things derivative, this is only an approximation, but it's one that gets more and more true for smaller and smaller choices of $dx$ and $dy$. | 与一切导数相关的东西一样，这只是一个近似，但随着 $dx$ 与 $dy$ 的取值越来越小，它会越来越真。 |
| The key point is that when you restrict yourself to steps along this circle, you're essentially saying you want to ensure that this value $S$ doesn't change; it starts at a value of $25$, and you want to keep it at a value of $25$; that is, $dS$ should be $0$. So setting this expression $2x \cdot dx + 2y \cdot dy$ equal to $0$ is the condition under which a tiny step stays on the circle. | 关键点在于：当你把自己限制在沿这个圆的步子上时，你实质上是在说你想确保值 $S$ 不变；它从 $25$ 出发，你要让它保持为 $25$；也就是说，$dS$ 应该是 $0$。所以，令表达式 $2x \cdot dx + 2y \cdot dy$ 等于 $0$，就是微小的一步留在圆上的条件。 |
| Again, this is only an approximation. Speaking more precisely, that condition keeps you on a tangent line of the circle, not the circle itself, but for tiny enough steps those are essentially the same thing. | 再强调一次，这只是个近似。说得更精确些：那个条件让你留在圆的一条切线上，而不是圆本身；但对足够小的步子来说，两者本质上是一回事。 |

> - *float free* —— 自由漂浮、不受约束。the tiny nudges $dx$ and $dy$ both just floating free。
> - *by the way* —— 顺便说一句。and not necessarily one that keeps you on this circle, by the way。

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Of course, there's nothing special about the expression $x^2+y^2 = 5^2$ here. You could have some other expression involving $x$'s and $y$'s, representing some other curve, and taking the derivative of both sides like this would give you a way to relate $dx$ to $dy$ for tiny steps along that curve. | 当然，这里的表达式 $x^2+y^2 = 5^2$ 没有什么特别的。你完全可以有某个其他含 $x$ 和 $y$ 的表达式，代表另外某条曲线；像这样对两边求导，会给你一个办法，把沿那条曲线的微小步子的 $dx$ 与 $dy$ 联系起来。 |
| It's always nice to think through more examples, so consider the expression $\sin(x) \cdot y^2 = x$, which corresponds to many U-shaped curves on the plane. Those curves represent all the points $(x, y)$ of the plane where the value of $\sin(x) \cdot y^2$ equals the value of $x$. | 多想几个例子总是好的，所以考虑表达式 $\sin(x) \cdot y^2 = x$，它对应平面上许多 U 形曲线。这些曲线代表平面上所有满足「$\sin(x) \cdot y^2$ 的值等于 $x$ 的值」的点 $(x, y)$。 |
| Now imagine taking some tiny step with components $(dx, dy)$, and not necessarily one that keeps you on the curve. | 现在想象迈出某个分量为 $(dx, dy)$ 的微小一步——不一定是让你留在这条曲线上的那种。 |
| Taking the derivative of each side of the equation $\sin(x) \cdot y^2 = x$ will tell us how much the value of that side changes during this step. On the left side, the product rule tells us that this should be "left d-right plus right d-left": $\sin(x)$ times the change to $y^2$, which is $2y \cdot dy$, plus $y^2$ times the change to $\sin(x)$, which is $\cos(x) \cdot dx$. The right side is simply $x$, so the size of a change to the value is exactly $dx$, right? | 对方程 $\sin(x) \cdot y^2 = x$ 的每一边求导，会告诉我们这一步里每一边的值变化了多少。左边，乘积法则告诉我们应该是「左 d 右加右 d 左」：$\sin(x)$ 乘以 $y^2$ 的变化——即 $2y \cdot dy$——加上 $y^2$ 乘以 $\sin(x)$ 的变化——即 $\cos(x) \cdot dx$。右边就是 $x$，所以它的值变化的大小恰好是 $dx$，对吧？ |
| You might be thinking that the derivative of $x$ is just $1$, and yes the derivative of the function $f(x) = x$ is $df/dx = 1$. However, in this case. we are interested in how much the value of that side changes because of the small step which is analogous for calculating just $df$ in the case of the function $f(x) = x$. | （译注：原文脚注。「in this case.」之句点系原文笔误，照录。）你可能在想：$x$ 的导数不就是 $1$ 吗？是的，函数 $f(x) = x$ 的导数确实是 $df/dx = 1$。但在这个场景里，我们关心的是这一小步使那一边的值变化了多少——这类似于对函数 $f(x) = x$ 只计算 $df$。 |

That's the only way this top equation can remain true. | 让这两边彼此相等，等于在说：「无论你那个坐标为 $(dx, dy)$ 的微小一步是什么，只要它要让我们留在这条曲线上，左边与右边的值就必须变化相同的量。」这是上面这个方程能保持成立的唯一办法。 |
| From there, depending on what problem you're solving, you could manipulate further with algebra, where perhaps the most common goal is to find $dy$ divided by $dx$. | 从那里出发，取决于你在解什么问题，你可以继续用代数摆弄；其中最常见的目标，也许是求出 $dy$ 除以 $dx$。 |

## 求 ln(x) 的导数（Finding derivative of ln(x)）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| As one more example, let me show how you can use this technique to help find new derivative formulas. I've mentioned in a previous lesson that the derivative of $e^x$ is itself, but what about the derivative of its inverse function the natural log of $x$? | 再举一个例子，让我展示怎样用这个技巧帮助找到新的导数公式。我在前面某课提到过 $e^x$ 的导数是它自己，那它的反函数——$x$ 的自然对数——的导数呢？ |
| The graph of $\ln(x)$ can be thought of as an implicit curve; all the points on the $xy$-plane where $y = \ln(x)$, it just happens to be the case that the $x$'s and $y$'s of this equation aren't as intermingled as they were in other examples. The slope of this graph, $dy/dx$, should be the derivative of $\ln(x)$, right? | $\ln(x)$ 的图像可以想成一条隐函数曲线：$xy$ 平面上所有满足 $y = \ln(x)$ 的点，只不过这个方程里的 $x$ 和 $y$ 恰好不像其他例子里那样混缠在一起。这张图像的斜率 $dy/dx$，应该就是 $\ln(x)$ 的导数，对吧？ |
| Well, to find that, first rearrange this equation $y = \ln(x)$ to be $e^y = x$. This is exactly what the natural log of $x$ means; it's saying "$e$ to the what equals $x$?" | 好，为了求出它，先把方程 $y = \ln(x)$ 重排成 $e^y = x$。这正是 $x$ 的自然对数的含义：它在说「$e$ 的多少次方等于 $x$？」 |
| Graphically this should make sense too, since you can draw a diagonal line and reflect the functions to see that they are inverses of each other. | （图注）从图像上看这也应该讲得通：你可以画一条对角线，把两个函数翻折过去，就能看出它们互为反函数。 |
| Since we know the derivative of $e^y$, we can take the derivative of both sides of this equation $y = \ln(x)$, effectively asking how a tiny step with components $(dx, dy)$ changes the value of each side. | 由于我们知道 $e^y$ 的导数，我们就可以对方程 $y = \ln(x)$ 的两边取导数，实质上是在问：分量为 $(dx, dy)$ 的一小步会怎样改变每一边的值。 |
| To ensure the step stays on the curve, the change to the left side of the equation, which is $e^y \cdot dy$, must equal the change to the right side, which is $dx$. | 为了确保这一步留在曲线上，方程左边的变化——即 $e^y \cdot dy$——必须等于右边的变化，即 $dx$。 |
| Rearranging, this means $dy/dx$, the slope of our graph, equals $1/e^y$. | 重排一下，这意味着 $dy/dx$——我们图像的斜率——等于 $1/e^y$。 |
| And when we're on this curve, $e^y$ is by definition the same as $x$, so evidently the slope is $1/x$. | 而当我们在这条曲线上时，$e^y$ 按定义与 $x$ 相同，所以显然斜率是 $1/x$。 |
| An expression for the slope of the graph of a function in terms of $x$ like this is the derivative of that function, so the derivative of $\ln(x)$ is $1/x$. | 像这样以 $x$ 表出的函数图像斜率表达式，就是该函数的导数，所以 $\ln(x)$ 的导数是 $1/x$。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 求 ln(x) 的导数
> - **intermingled** /ˌɪntəˈmɪŋɡld/ —— 混缠在一起的。aren't as intermingled as they were in other examples。
> - **rearrange** /ˌriːəˈreɪndʒ/ —— 重排、整理。first rearrange this equation。
> - **effectively** /ɪˈfektɪvli/ —— 实质上、实际上。effectively asking how a tiny step...。
> - **ensure** /ɪnˈʃʊə/ —— 确保。To ensure the step stays on the curve。
> - **inverse function** —— 反函数。its inverse function the natural log of $x$。
> - **diagonal** /daɪˈæɡənl/ —— 对角线的。draw a diagonal line。
> - *by definition* —— 按定义。$e^y$ is by definition the same as $x$。

| **What is the slope of the tangent line at the point $(1, \sqrt{2})$ for the implicit curve defined by the equation $y^2 - x^2 = 1$? Compare this with the derivative at the point $x=1$ for the function $f(x) = \sqrt{1 + x^2}$.** | （译注：原文此处是一道互动选择题，选项为 $1$ / $\sqrt{2}$ / $\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ / $\frac{1}{2}$，正确答案为第三个，解析见下。）**对由方程 $y^2 - x^2 = 1$ 定义的隐函数曲线，在点 $(1, \sqrt{2})$ 处切线的斜率是多少？把它与函数 $f(x) = \sqrt{1 + x^2}$ 在 $x=1$ 处的导数比一比。** |
| Start by taking the implicit derivative of both sides of the equation $y^2 - x^2 = 1$. | （译注：以下为上题的答案解析。）先对方程 $y^2 - x^2 = 1$ 的两边做隐函数求导。 |

$$
2y \cdot dy - 2x \cdot dx = 0
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Rearrange the equation to solve for $dy/dx$. | 重排方程，解出 $dy/dx$。 |

$$
\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{x}{y}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Substitute the point $(1,\sqrt{2})$ into the expression. | 把点 $(1,\sqrt{2})$ 代入表达式。 |

$$
\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Let's check our work with the function $f(x) = \sqrt{1 + x^2}$ which forms the same curve as the implicit equation $y^2 - x^2 = 1$ in the first and second quadrants of the $xy$-plane. Start by taking the derivative of the function. | 让我们用函数 $f(x) = \sqrt{1 + x^2}$ 来检查自己的工作——在 $xy$ 平面的第一、第二象限里，它与隐函数方程 $y^2 - x^2 = 1$ 形成同一条曲线。先对这个函数求导。 |
