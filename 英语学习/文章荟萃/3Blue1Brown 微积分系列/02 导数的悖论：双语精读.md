## 变化率（Rates of Change）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| First let's talk about the real world, then we'll go into a purely mathematical one. Think about how you might build an actual speedometer for a car. | 我们先聊聊现实世界，然后再进入纯数学的世界。想想你会怎么给一辆车造一个真正的速度表。 |
| At some point, say $3$ seconds into the journey, the speedometer might measure how far the car goes in a very small amount of time, perhaps the distance traveled between $3$ seconds and $3.01$ seconds. Then it would compute the speed in meters per second as that tiny distance, in meters, divided by that tiny time, $0.01$ seconds. | 在某个时刻，比如行程进行到 3 秒时，速度表可以测量汽车在一小段时间里走了多远，比如 3 秒到 3.01 秒之间驶过的距离；然后用这段以米计的小距离，除以以秒计的小时间 0.01 秒，算出以米每秒计的速度。 |
| That is, a physical car can sidestep the paradox by not actually computing speed at a single point in time, and instead computing speed during _very_ small amounts of time. | 也就是说，一辆物理意义上的汽车可以绕开这个悖论：它并不真的去计算单一时间点上的速度，而是计算*很小*一段时间之内的速度。 |

$$
\text{Velocity} \implies \frac{\text { Change in distance }}{\text { Change in time }} \implies\frac{(20.21-20) \text { meters }}{(3.01-3) \text { seconds }}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Let's call that difference in time "$dt$", which you might think of as $0.01$ seconds, and call the resulting difference in distance traveled "$ds$". So the velocity at that point in time is $ds$ over $dt$, the tiny change in distance over the tiny change in time. | 我们把时间的差值叫作 "$dt$"，你可以把它想成 0.01 秒；把由此产生的行驶距离差值叫作 "$ds$"。于是该时刻的速度就是 $ds$ 除以 $dt$——距离的微小变化除以时间的微小变化。 |
| Graphically, imagine zooming in on the point of the distance vs. time graph above $t=3$. That $dt$ is a small step to the right, since time is on the horizontal axis, and that $ds$ is the resulting change in the height of the graph, since the vertical axis represents distance traveled. | 从图像上看，想象你放大「距离-时间」图像上 $t=3$ 上方的那个点：$dt$ 是向右迈出的一小步（时间在横轴上），$ds$ 则是图像高度随之产生的变化（纵轴代表行驶距离）。 |
| Zoomed in image of rise over run at $t=3$ | （图注）$t=3$ 处「纵变除以横变」的放大图。 |
| So $\frac{ds}{dt}$ is the rise-over-run slope between two _very_ close points on the graph. | 所以 $\frac{ds}{dt}$ 就是图像上两个*非常*接近的点之间的「纵变除以横变」斜率。 |

$$
\frac{d s}{d t}=\frac{\text { rise }}{\text{run}}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Of course, there's nothing special about the value $t=3$, we could apply this to any other point in time, so we consider this expression $\frac{ds}{dt}$ to be a function of $t$, something where I can give you some time $t$, and you can give back to me the value of this ratio at that time; the velocity as a function of time. | 当然，$t=3$ 这个值没有什么特别的，我们可以对任何其他时刻如法炮制。所以我们把表达式 $\frac{ds}{dt}$ 看成 $t$ 的函数：我给你一个时间 $t$，你还给我该时刻这个比值的取值——也就是作为时间函数的速度。 |
| Zoomed in image of rise over run at $t=6$ | （图注）$t=6$ 处「纵变除以横变」的放大图。 |
| So for example, when I had the computer draw this bump curve representing the velocity function, the one you can think of as the slope of this distance vs. time function at each point, here's what I had computer do: | 举个例子，当我让电脑画出这条代表速度函数的弧丘曲线——你可以把它想成「距离-时间」函数在每一点的斜率——我是这样让电脑做的： |
| First, I chose some small value for $dt$, like $0.01$. Then, I had the computer look at many times $t$ between $0$ and $10$, and compute the distance function $s$ at $(t + dt)$, minus the value of this function at $t$. That is, the difference in the distance traveled between the given time $t$, and the time $0.01$ seconds after that. Then divide that difference by the change in time $dt$, and this gives the velocity in meters per second around each point in time. | 首先，我给 $dt$ 选了一个较小的值，比如 0.01。然后，我让电脑在 0 到 10 之间取许多时刻 $t$，计算距离函数 $s$ 在 $(t + dt)$ 处的值减去它在 $t$ 处的值——也就是说，取从给定时刻 $t$ 到其后 0.01 秒之间行驶距离的差值。再把这一差值除以时间变化量 $dt$，就得到每个时刻附近以米每秒计的速度。 |

$$
\frac{d s}{d t}(t)=\frac{s(t+d t)-s(t)}{d t}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| With this formula, you can give the computer any curve representing the distance function $s(t)$, and it can figure out the curve representing the velocity $v(t)$. | 有了这个公式，你只要给电脑任何一条代表距离函数 $s(t)$ 的曲线，它就能算出代表速度 $v(t)$ 的那条曲线。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 变化率
> - **sidestep** /ˈsaɪdstep/ —— 绕开、回避。sidestep the paradox。
> - **zoom in** —— 放大。zooming in on the point of the graph。
> - **rise over run** —— 纵变除以横变（斜率的口语说法）。the rise-over-run slope。
> - **graphically** /ˈɡræfɪkli/ —— 从图像上看。Graphically, imagine zooming in...
> - **resulting** /rɪˈzʌltɪŋ/ —— 随之产生的。the resulting change in the height of the graph。
> - *around each point in time* —— 每个时刻附近。gives the velocity around each point in time。
> - *figure out* —— 算出、琢磨出。it can figure out the curve。

## 悖论（The Paradox）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| So now would be a good time to pause, reflect, make sure this idea of relating distance to velocity by looking at tiny changes in time $dt$ makes sense, because now we're going to tackle the paradox of the derivative head-on. This idea of $\frac{ds}{dt}$, a tiny change in the value of the function $s$ divided by a tiny change in the input $t$, is _almost_ what the derivative is. | 现在正是个暂停反思的好时机——确认一下「通过观察微小的时间变化 $dt$ 把距离和速度联系起来」这个想法讲得通——因为我们马上要正面迎击导数的悖论了。$\frac{ds}{dt}$ 这个想法——函数 $s$ 的值的微小变化除以输入 $t$ 的微小变化——已经*几乎*就是导数了。 |
| Even though our car's speedometer will look at an actual change in time like $0.01$ seconds to compute speed, and even though my program here for finding a velocity function given a position function also uses a concrete value of $dt$, in pure math, the derivative is not this ratio $\frac{ds}{dt}$ for any _specific_ choice of $dt$. It is whatever value that ratio _approaches_ as the choice for $dt$ approaches $0$. | 尽管我们汽车的速度表会去看一个实际的时间变化（比如 0.01 秒）来计算速度，尽管我这里根据位置函数求速度函数的程序也用了具体的 $dt$ 值，但在纯数学里，导数并不是对任何*特定* $dt$ 取值的比值 $\frac{ds}{dt}$；导数是当 $dt$ 的取值趋近 0 时，这个比值所*趋近*的那个值。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 悖论
> - **tackle** /ˈtækl/ —— 处理、迎击。tackle the paradox of the derivative head-on。
> - **head-on** /ˌhed ˈɒn/ —— 正面地、迎头地。
> - **approach** /əˈprəʊtʃ/ —— 趋近。whatever value that ratio approaches。
> - **concrete** /ˈkɒŋkriːt/ —— 具体的。a concrete value of dt。
> - **reflect** /rɪˈflekt/ —— 反思。pause, reflect。
> - *almost* —— 几乎（原文特意斜体强调）。is _almost_ what the derivative is。

## 切线（Tangent Lines）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Luckily, there is a really nice visual understanding for what it means to ask what this ratio approaches: For any specific choice of $dt$, this ratio $ds/dt$ is the slope of a line passing through two points on the graph, known as a secant line. | 幸运的是，对于「问这个比值趋近于什么」到底是什么意思，有一个非常漂亮的视觉理解：对任何特定的 $dt$ 取值，比值 $ds/dt$ 都是图像上一条穿过两点的直线的斜率，这条线叫作割线（secant line）。 |
| Well, as $dt$ approaches $0$, and those two points approach each other, the slope of that secant line approaches the slope of a line tangent to the graph at whatever point $t$ we're looking at. So the true, honest to goodness derivative, is not the rise-over-run slope between two nearby points on the graph; it's equal to the slope of a line _tangent_ to the graph at a single point. | 而随着 $dt$ 趋近 0、那两个点彼此靠拢，割线的斜率就趋近于一条与图像在我们所看的那点 $t$ 相切的直线的斜率。所以，真正的、地道十足的导数，并不是图像上两个邻近点之间的「纵变除以横变」斜率；它等于在单独一个点上与图像*相切*（tangent）的那条直线的斜率。 |
| **What does it mean if the slope of the tangent line is negative?** | （译注：原文此处是一道互动选择题，选项为 The car is slowing down. / The car is going backwards. / The car is upside down. / There is a hole in the road.，正确答案为第二个，解析见下。）**如果切线的斜率是负的，这意味着什么？** |
| A negative tangent line means that the slope is negative and the car is going backwards. | （译注：上题的答案解析。）切线为负意味着斜率为负，汽车正在倒退。 |
| Notice what I'm not saying: I'm _not_ saying that the derivative is whatever happens when $dt$ is infinitely small, whatever that may mean, nor am I saying that you plug in $0$ for $dt$. Following what is a general theme in calculus, the idea is a two step process where you first consider a finitely small change, some actual number like $0.0001$, and then you ask what your answer approaches as this number approaches $0$. | 注意我没有说的是什么：我*不是*在说导数就是 $dt$ 无穷小时发生的事（且不说那是什么意思），也不是说你该把 $dt$ 代成 0。遵循微积分的一个通用主题，这里的思路是两步走：先考虑一个有限小的变化——某个真实的数，比如 0.0001——然后再问：当这个数趋近 0 时，你的答案趋近于什么。 |
| Even though change in an instant makes no sense, it does make sense to ask what the rate of change across smaller and smaller amounts of time approaches. It's a sneaky backdoor way to talk reasonably about the rate of change at a single point in time. Isn't that neat? It's flirting with the paradox of change in an instant without ever needing to touch it. | 尽管「一瞬间里的变化」讲不通，但去问「越来越小的时间段上的变化率趋近于什么」却是完全讲得通的。这是一种迂回的后门办法，让我们能合理地谈论单个时间点上的变化率。是不是很妙？它与「瞬间的变化」这个悖论调情，却始终不必真的碰它。 |
| What's even more neat is how this potentially abstract notion of what many different rates of change approach ends up having such a clean and simple geometric meaning. Since the slope of the secant line between two nearby points approaches the slope of the tangent point at one of them as these points get closer together. | 更妙的是，「许许多多不同变化率各自趋近于什么」这个可能相当抽象的概念，最终竟有一个如此干净利落的几何含义：随着两个邻近点越靠越近，它们之间割线的斜率，趋近于其中一个点处切线的斜率。 |
| Since change in an instant still makes no sense, rather than interpreting the slope of this tangent line as an "instantaneous rate of change", an alternate notion is to think of it as the best constant approximation for rate of change _around_ a point. | 由于「瞬间的变化」仍然讲不通，与其把这条切线的斜率解读成「瞬时变化率」，不如换一种理解：把它想成这一点*附近*变化率的最佳常数近似。 |

## 零时刻的悖论（The Paradox at Time Zero）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| This example also sets the stage to think more concretely about why the notion of an instantaneous rate of change is paradoxical. Think about this car traveling according to this $t^3$ distance function, and consider its motion at moment $t=0$. Now ask yourself: Is the car moving at that time? | 这个例子也为我们更具体地思考「瞬时变化率这个概念为什么自相矛盾」搭好了舞台。想想这辆按 $t^3$ 距离函数行驶的汽车，考虑它在 $t=0$ 时刻的运动。现在问问自己：那一刻汽车在动吗？ |
| On the one hand, we can compute its speed at that point using the derivative of this function, $3t^2$, which is $0$ at time $t=0$. | 一方面，我们可以用这个函数的导数 $3t^2$ 算出它在那一刻的速度，而在 $t=0$ 时它等于 0。 |

$$
\frac{d s}{d t}(t)=3 t^{2}=3(0)^{2}=0 \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Visually, this means the tangent line to the graph at that point is perfectly flat, so the car's "instantaneous velocity" is $0$, which suggests it's not moving. But on the other hand, if it doesn't start moving at time $0$, when does it start moving? Really, pause and ponder this for a moment, is that car moving at $t=0$? | 从图像上看，这意味着图像在该点的切线完全水平，所以汽车的「瞬时速度」是 0，似乎它没有在动。可另一方面，如果它不在时刻 0 开始动，那它什么时候开始动？真的，停下来琢磨一会儿：那辆车在 $t=0$ 时到底动没动？ |
| Take a moment to unpack what it actually means for the derivative of the distance function to be $0$ at this point. It means the best constant approximation for the car's velocity _around_ that point is $0$ meters per second. For example, between $t=0$ and $t=0.1$ seconds, the car _does move_, it moves $0.001$ meters. That's very small, and importantly it's very small _compared_ to the change in time, an average speed of only $0.01$ meters per second. | 花点时间拆解一下：距离函数的导数在这个点是 0，究竟意味着什么。它意味着，在该点*附近*，汽车速度的最佳常数近似是 0 米每秒。比如，在 $t=0$ 与 $t=0.1$ 秒之间，汽车*确实动了*，它动了 0.001 米。这非常小，而且重要的是，它*相对于*时间的变化量来说非常小——平均速度只有 0.01 米每秒。 |
| For smaller and smaller nudges in time, this ratio of the change in distance over change in time approaches $0$, though in this case it never actually hits it. So would you say this qualifies as moving at the time $t=0$? I would argue the question makes no sense, movement is something which happens between two points in time, so has no meaning in a given instant. | 当时间的轻推越来越小，这个「距离变化除以时间变化」的比值趋近 0——不过在这个例子里它从未真正等于 0。那么你会说这够格算作「$t=0$ 时刻在运动」吗？我认为这个问题本身没有意义：运动是发生在两个时间点之间的东西，在给定瞬间毫无意义。 |
| It's tempting to say the derivative gives this notion meaning, and many people would happily say the car is not moving at $t=0$, but it is moving for all times $t > 0$. For my part, I'd recommend not taking the phrase "instantaneous rate of change" too seriously, instead thinking of it as a conceptual shorthand for "the best constant approximation for the rate of change". | 人们很想说是导数给了这个概念意义，许多人也会爽快地说：汽车在 $t=0$ 时没动，但对所有 $t > 0$ 的时刻都在动。就我个人而言，我建议别把「瞬时变化率」这个说法太当真，不如把它看作「变化率的最佳常数近似」的一个概念性速记。 |
