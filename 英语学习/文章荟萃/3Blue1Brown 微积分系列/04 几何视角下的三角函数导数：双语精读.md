## 引言：从单位圆看正弦（Introduction: sine from the unit circle）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Let's try to reason through what the derivatives of the functions sine and cosine should be. For background, you should be comfortable with how to think about both of these functions using the unit circle; that is, the circle with radius $1$ centered at the origin. | 我们来推一推：正弦函数和余弦函数的导数应该是什么。背景要求是：你要能熟练地用单位圆（unit circle）——也就是以原点为圆心、半径为 1 的圆——来思考这两个函数。 |
| （译注：原文此处有一条脚注：「To get up to speed on the unit circle or for a quick refresher see [this article](https://wumbo.net/concepts/unit-circle/).」） | （译注：脚注中译，脚注标记已按体例删除。）想补齐单位圆的功课或快速复习，可参见 wumbo.net 的「Unit circle」一文。 |
| For example, how would you interpret the value $\sin(0.8)$ if the value $\theta = 0.8$ is understood to be in radians? | 比如，如果 $\theta = 0.8$ 被理解为弧度（radian），你会怎么解读 $\sin(0.8)$ 这个值？ |
| You might imagine walking around a circle with a radius of $1$, starting from the rightmost point, until you've traversed the distance $0.8$ in arc length. | 你可以想象沿一个半径为 1 的圆行走：从最右端的点出发，一直走到弧长走了 0.8 的距离为止。 |
| This is the same thing as saying you've traversed an _angle_ of $0.8$ radians. Then $\sin(\theta)$ is your height above the $x$-axis at this point. | 这等价于说：你走过了 0.8 弧度的*角度*。此时 $\sin(\theta)$ 就是你在这个点高于 $x$ 轴的高度。 |
| As theta increases, and you walk around the circle, your height bobs up and down and up and down. | 随着 theta 增大、你绕着圆走，你的高度一起一伏、一起一伏。 |
| So the graph of $\sin(\theta)$ vs. $\theta$, which plots this height as a function of arc length, is a wave pattern. This is the _quintessential_ wave pattern. | 所以，把「这个高度作为弧长的函数」画出来——$\sin(\theta)$ 对 $\theta$ 的图像——就是一个波形（wave pattern）。而且是*最典型*的那种波形。 |

## 从猜测到精确：放大看 dθ（From guess to proof: zooming in on dθ）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Just from looking at this graph, we can get a feel for the shape of the derivative function. The slope at $0$ is something positive, then as $\sin(\theta)$ approaches its peak, the slope goes down to $0$. Then the slope is negative for a little while before coming back up to $0$ as the $\sin(\theta)$ graph levels out. If you're familiar with the graphs of trig functions, you might guess that this derivative graph should be exactly $\cos(\theta)$, whose graph is just a shifted-back copy of the sine graph. | 光看这条图像，我们就能对导数函数的形状有个感觉：在 0 处斜率是某个正值；随着 $\sin(\theta)$ 逼近峰顶，斜率降到 0；随后斜率在负值区间待一小会儿，等 $\sin(\theta)$ 图像走平，又回升到 0。如果你熟悉三角函数的图像，你可能会猜：这条导数图像应该恰好就是 $\cos(\theta)$——它的图像不过是正弦图像往回平移的副本。 |
| But all this tells us is that the peaks and valleys of the derivative graph seem to line up with the graph of cosine. How could we know that this derivative actually _is_ the cosine of theta, and not just some new function that looks similar to it? As with the previous examples of this video, a more exact understanding of the derivative requires looking at what the function itself represents, rather than the graph of the function. | 但这一切只告诉我们：导数图像的峰和谷似乎与余弦的图像对得上。我们怎么才能知道这个导数*就是* theta 的余弦，而不只是某个长得很像的新函数？和这支视频前面的例子一样，要更精确地理解导数，就得去看函数本身代表什么，而不是盯着函数的图像。 |
| Think back to the walk around the unit circle, having traversed an arc length of $\theta$, where $\sin(\theta)$ is the height of this point. Consider a slight nudge of $d$-theta along the circumference of the circle; a tiny step in your walk around the unit circle. How much does this change $\sin(\theta)$? How much does that step change your _height_ above the x-axis? This is best observed by zooming in on the point where you are on the circle. | 回想绕单位圆的那次行走：走过弧长 $\theta$ 之后，$\sin(\theta)$ 就是这个点的高度。现在考虑沿圆周的一个轻微推动 $d\theta$——你绕圈行走中的一小步。它会让 $\sin(\theta)$ 变化多少？这一步会让你高于 x 轴的*高度*变化多少？最好放大你在圆上所在的那个点来观察。 |
| Zoomed in close enough the circle basically looks like a straight line in this neighborhood. | 放得足够近时，圆在这个邻域里看上去基本就是一条直线。 |
| Consider the right triangle pictured below, where the hypotenuse represents a straight-line approximation of the nudge $d \theta$ along the circumference, and this left side represents the change in height; the resulting tiny nudge to $\sin(\theta)$. | 看下面画出的这个直角三角形：斜边代表沿圆周的推动量 $d \theta$ 的直线近似，左边这条边代表高度的变化——也就是 $\sin(\theta)$ 随之产生的微小变化量。 |
| This tiny triangle is actually similar to this larger triangle with the defining angle theta, and whose hypotenuse is the radius of the circle with length $1$. | 这个小三角形其实与那个大的三角形相似（similar）——大三角形带有定义角 theta，斜边是长度为 1 的圆的半径。 |
| Specifically, the angle between its height $d(\sin(\theta))$ and its hypotenuse $d\theta$ is precisely equal to $\theta$. | 具体地说，小三角形的高度 $d(\sin(\theta))$ 与斜边 $d\theta$ 之间的夹角，恰好等于 $\theta$。 |
| Similar triangle explanation | （译注：此为官网原文一个可折叠面板的标题，以下为面板展开后的内容。）相似三角形的解释。 |
| We know these right triangles must be similar by doing some angle-chasing geometry. Start by drawing a line parallel to the $x$-axis. From geometry, we know that the angle formed between the radius and this parallel line is equal to theta. | 通过一点「追角」几何，我们就知道这两个直角三角形必然相似。先画一条平行于 $x$ 轴的直线。由几何知识可知，半径与这条平行线形成的角等于 theta。 |
| Then we can draw a line tangent to the point formed by theta as shown below. Because the line is tangent, we know that the angle formed between the radius and the tangent line is perpendicular, so we can calculate the right-most angle of this tiny triangle formed by $d\theta$ to be $\pi/2 - \theta$. | 然后，如下图那样，在 theta 对应的那个点画一条切线。因为这条线是切线，它与半径形成的角是垂直的，于是可以算出由 $d\theta$ 构成的这个小三角形最右侧的角为 $\pi/2 - \theta$。 |
| Finally, we know that the angles of a triangle sum to a half-rotation ($\pi$ radians or $180 \degree$), so the top angle must be equal to $\theta$. | 最后，三角形内角和为半圈（$\pi$ 弧度，即 $180°$），所以顶部的那个角必等于 $\theta$。 |
| Since these two triangles share the same angles, they must be similar triangles, which allows us to continue forward and find the derivative of the trigonometric function. | 既然这两个三角形的角完全相同，它们必然是相似三角形——这让我们得以继续推进，求出这个三角函数的导数。 |
| Think about what the derivative of sine is supposed to mean. It's the ratio between that $d\left(\sin(\theta)\right)$, the tiny change to the output of sine, divided by $d \theta$, the tiny change to the input of the function. From the picture, that's the ratio between the length of the side adjacent to this little right triangle divided by the hypotenuse. Well, let's see, adjacent divided by hypotenuse; that's exactly what $\cos(\theta)$ means! | 想想正弦的导数本来该是什么意思：它是 $d\left(\sin(\theta)\right)$——正弦输出的微小变化——除以 $d \theta$（函数输入的微小变化）的比值。从图上看，这就是这个小直角三角形的邻边长度除以斜边。来瞧瞧：邻边除以斜边——这恰恰就是 $\cos(\theta)$ 的定义！ |
| Notice, by considering the slope of the graph, we can get a quick intuitive feel for the rough shape that the derivative of $\sin(\theta)$ should have, which is enough to make an educated guess. But to more to understand why this derivative is _precisely_ $\cos(\theta)$, we had to begin our line of reasoning with the defining features of $\sin(\theta)$. | 注意，通过考虑图像的斜率，我们能快速直观地感受到 $\sin(\theta)$ 的导数大致该是什么形状，这足以做出一个有根据的猜测；但要理解这个导数为什么*恰恰*是 $\cos(\theta)$，我们就必须从 $\sin(\theta)$ 的定义特征出发来展开推理。 |
| For those of you who enjoy pausing and pondering, take a moment to find a similar line of reasoning that explains what the derivative of $\cos(\theta)$ should be. | 喜欢停下来琢磨的读者，请花点时间找一条类似的推理路线，解释 $\cos(\theta)$ 的导数应该是什么。 |

> - **level out** —— 走平、趋平。as the sin(θ) graph levels out。
> - **educated guess** —— 有根据的猜测。enough to make an educated guess。

## 余弦的导数（Derivative of Cosine）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Derivative of Cosine | （译注：此为官网原文一个可折叠面板的标题，以下为面板展开后的内容。）余弦的导数。 |
| To find the derivative of the cosine function, let's look at the unit circle definition of cosine. Imagine walking around the unit circle starting from the right-most point to an arc-length distance of $\theta$ (theta) unit. The cosine function returns the distance from the $y$-axis and the end-point corresponding the angle. | 为求余弦函数的导数，来看余弦的单位圆定义：想象绕单位圆行走，从最右端的点出发，走过弧长 $\theta$。余弦函数返回的，是该角度对应终点到 $y$ 轴的距离。 |
| For example, given the angle $\theta = 0.8$ the cosine function returns the distance from the $y$-axis and the point corresponding to the angle which is about equal to $0.697...$ | 比如，给定角度 $\theta = 0.8$，余弦函数返回该角度对应终点到 $y$ 轴的距离，约等于 $0.697...$。 |
| Just as with sine, imagine nudging the input $\theta$ some small amount $d\theta$. This gives us the change in cosine $d(\cos (\theta))$ labeled on the diagram below. | 和正弦一样，想象把输入 $\theta$ 轻推一个小量 $d\theta$。这就给了我们余弦的变化量 $d(\cos (\theta))$，标在下图中。 |
| For angles in the first quadrant a small nudge $d\theta$ **decreases** the output of cosine, so this length is negative. | （图注）对第一象限的角度，小小的推动量 $d\theta$ 会让余弦的输出**减小**，所以这段长度是负的。 |
| Just as before, we can observe that this nudge forms a _similar_ right triangle with a hypotenuse of length $d \theta$. | 和之前一样，可以观察到这个推动量构成了一个*相似*的直角三角形，斜边长度为 $d \theta$。 |
| Applying the definition of the sine function to this right triangle, we know that the length of the opposite side is equal to $d\theta \cdot \sin(\theta)$. However, we need to be careful to observe that this small nudge $d\theta$ that we introduced **decreases** the length of cosine on the unit circle, so this value is negative. | 把正弦函数的定义用在这个直角三角形上，我们知道对边的长度等于 $d\theta \cdot \sin(\theta)$。不过要小心地注意到：我们引入的这个微小推动量 $d\theta$ 是在**缩短**单位圆上余弦的长度，所以这个值是负的。 |
| This gives us the change of the function as $d(\cos (\theta)) = d \theta \cdot(-\sin (\theta))$ which we can rearrange to find the derivative. | 由此得到函数的变化量 $d(\cos (\theta)) = d \theta \cdot(-\sin (\theta))$，稍作整理即可求出导数。 |

$$
\frac{d(\cos (\theta))}{d\theta} = -\sin (\theta)
$$

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 余弦的导数
> - **adjacent** /əˈdʒeɪsnt/ —— 邻边的。the side adjacent to this little right triangle。
> - **opposite** /ˈɒpəzɪt/ —— 对边的。the length of the opposite side。
> - **perpendicular** /ˌpɜːpənˈdɪkjələ/ —— 垂直的。the angle formed between the radius and the tangent line is perpendicular。
> - **half-rotation** /hɑːf rəʊˈteɪʃn/ —— 半圈。the angles of a triangle sum to a half-rotation。
> - **rearrange** /ˌriːəˈreɪndʒ/ —— 整理、移项。which we can rearrange to find the derivative。
> - **quadrant** /ˈkwɒdrənt/ —— 象限。angles in the first quadrant。
> - *parallel to* —— 平行于。a line parallel to the x-axis。
