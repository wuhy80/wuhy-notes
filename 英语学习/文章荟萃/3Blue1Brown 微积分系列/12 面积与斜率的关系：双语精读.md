## 正弦函数的平均值（Average of sine function）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Take a look at the graph of $\sin(x)$ between $0$ and $\pi$, which is half its period. | 来看 $\sin(x)$ 在 $0$ 到 $\pi$ 之间的图像，这是它周期的一半。 |
| What is the average height of this graph on that interval? It's not a useless question. All sorts of cyclic phenomena in the world are modeled with sine waves: For example, the number of hours the sun is up per day as a function of which-day-of-the-year-it-is follows a sine wave pattern. | 这条图像在那个区间上的平均高度是多少？这可不是个无用的问题。世界上形形色色的周期现象都用正弦波建模：比如，每天太阳照几个小时，作为「一年中第几天」的函数，就遵循正弦波的模式。 |
| Hours of Daylight | （图注）日照时数。 |
| If you wanted to predict, say, the average effectiveness of solar panels in summer months vs. winter months, you'd want to be able to answer a question like this: What's the average value of that sine function over half its period. | 如果你想预测，比如说，太阳能板在夏季月份与冬季月份的平均效能，你就想能回答这样的问题：那个正弦函数在半个周期上的平均值是多少。 |
| Hours of Daylight Summer and Winter | （图注）夏季与冬季的日照时数。 |
| Whereas a case like that will have all sorts of constants mucking up the function, we'll just focus on an unencumbered $\sin(x)$ function, but the substance of the approach would be the same in any application. | 那样的实际情形里，会有各种常数把函数搅得乱糟糟；我们只聚焦一个不受拖累的 $\sin(x)$ 函数，但这个方法的实质在任何应用里都一样。 |
| It's kind of a weird thing to think about, isn't it, the average of a continuous variable. Usually, with averages, we think of a finite number of values, where you add all of them up and divide that sum by how many values there are. But there are infinitely many values of $\sin(x)$ between $0$ and $\pi$, and it's not like we can add all those numbers and divide by infinity. | 连续变量的平均值，想起来是件有点怪的事，不是吗？通常说到平均，我们想的是有限多个值：把它们全加起来，再用那个和除以值的个数。但 $\sin(x)$ 在 $0$ 到 $\pi$ 之间有无穷多个值，而我们总不能把那些数全加起来再除以无穷吧。 |
| This sensation actually comes up a lot in math, and is worth remembering, where you have this vague sense that you want to add together infinitely many values associated with a continuum, even though that doesn't really make sense. Almost always, when you get this sense, the key will be to use an integral somehow. And to think through exactly how, a good first step is usually to approximate your situation with some kind of finite sum. | 这种感觉在数学里其实出现得非常频繁，值得记住：你隐约觉得想把与某个连续统相伴的无穷多个值加在一起，尽管那并不真正讲得通。几乎每次你有这种感觉时，关键都会是设法用一个积分。而为了想清楚究竟怎么用，一个好的第一步，通常是用某种有限和来近似你的处境。 |
| In this case, imagine sampling a finite number of points, evenly spaced in this range. | 在这个例子里，想象在这个范围内等间距地采样有限多个点。 |
| Since it's a finite sample, you can find the average by adding up all the heights, $\sin(x)$, at each one, and divide that sum by the number of points you sampled, right? | 既然是有限样本，你就可以把每个点上的高度 $\sin(x)$ 全部加起来，再把那个和除以你采样点的个数，从而得到平均值，对吧？ |
| The summation notation here makes explicit what we are doing here. | （图注）这里的求和记号把我们正在做的事明确写了出来。 |
| And presumably, if the idea of an average height among all infinitely many points is going to make any sense at all, the more points we sample, which would involve adding up more heights, the closer the average of that sample should be to the actual average of the continuous variable. | 而可以想见，如果「在无穷多个点上取平均高度」这个想法还想有任何意义，那么我们采的样越多——也就是加总的高度越多——那个样本的平均值就应该越接近这个连续变量真正的平均值。 |
| This should feel at least somewhat related to taking an integral of $\sin(x)$ between $0$ and $\pi$, even if it might not be clear at first exactly how the two ideas will match up. | 这至少应该让你觉得，与对 $\sin(x)$ 在 $0$ 到 $\pi$ 之间取积分多少有些关系，即便一开始可能还不清楚这两个想法究竟如何对上号。 |

$$
\int_0^\pi \sin (x) d x
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| For that integral, you also think of a sample of inputs on this continuum, but instead of adding the height $\sin(x)$ at each one, and dividing by how many there are, you add up $\sin(x) \cdot dx$ where $dx$ is the spacing between the samples; that is, you're adding little areas, not heights. | 对那个积分来说，你同样在连续统上取一批输入样本；但不是把每个点上的高度 $\sin(x)$ 加起来再除以个数，而是把 $\sin(x) \cdot dx$ 加起来，其中 $dx$ 是样本之间的间距。也就是说，你加的是小块的面积，而不是高度。 |
| Technically, the integral is not quite this sum, it's whatever that sum approaches as $dx$ approaches $0$. But it's helpful to reason with respect to one of these finite iterations, where you're adding the areas of some specific number of rectangles. So what you want to do is reframe this expression for the average, this sum of the heights divided by the number of sampled points, in terms of $dx$, the spacing between samples. | 严格说来，积分并不完全等于这个和，而是当 $dx$ 趋近 $0$ 时那个和所趋近的值。但借助这些有限迭代中的某一个来推理是很有帮助的——此时你加的是特定个数矩形的面积。所以你要做的，是把「平均值」这个表达式——高度之和除以采样点数——用 $dx$，也就是样本间距，重新表述出来。 |

$$
\text { (Number of samples) } \approx \frac{x_2 - x_1 }{dx}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| For example, if the spacing between these points is $0.1$, and you know that they range from $0$ to $\pi$, then you can take the length of the interval, $\pi$, and divide it by the length of the space between each sample. If it doesn't go in evenly, you'd round down to the nearest integer, but as an approximation this is fine. | 比如，如果这些点之间的间距是 $0.1$，而你知道它们的范围是 $0$ 到 $\pi$，那你就可以拿区间的长度 $\pi$，除以每个样本之间空隙的长度。如果除不尽，你会向下取整到最近的整数，但作为近似这就够了。 |

$$
\text { (Number of samples }) \approx \frac{\pi}{0.1} \approx 31
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| If we write the spacing between samples as $dx$, the number of samples is $\frac{\pi}{dx}$. So replacing the denominator with $\frac{\pi}{dx}$ here, you can rearrange, putting the $dx$ up top and distributing. | 如果把样本间距写成 $dx$，样本数就是 $\frac{\pi}{dx}$。于是把这里的分母换成 $\frac{\pi}{dx}$ 之后，你可以重新整理，把 $dx$ 挪到上面，再分配进去。 |
| But, think about what it means to distribute that $dx$ up top; it means the terms you're adding all look like $\sin(x) \cdot dx$ for the various inputs $x$ that you're sampling, so that numerator looks exactly like an integral expression. And for larger and larger samples of points, this average approaches the actual integral of $\sin(x)$ between $0$ and $\pi$, all divided by the length of that interval, $\pi$. | 但是，想想把 $dx$ 分配到上面那些项意味着什么：它意味着你加的每一项，对你采样的各个输入 $x$ 来说，全都长得像 $\sin(x) \cdot dx$，于是那个分子看起来恰好就是一个积分表达式。而随着采样点越来越多，这个平均值就趋近于 $\sin(x)$ 在 $0$ 到 $\pi$ 之间的真实积分，再除以那个区间的长度 $\pi$。 |
| In other words, the average height of this graph is this area divided by its width. On an intuitive level, and just thinking in terms of units, that feels pretty reasonable, doesn't it? Area divided by width gives you average height. | 换句话说，这条图像的平均高度，就是这块面积除以它的宽度。从直觉上讲，单从单位来想，这也相当合理，不是吗？面积除以宽度，给你的就是平均高度。 |

## 算出结果（Computing a result）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Let's actually compute this integral expression. As we saw, last lesson, to compute an integral you need to find an antiderivative of the function inside the integral; some function whose derivative is $\sin(x)$. And, if you're comfortable with trig derivatives, you know the derivative of $\cos(x)$ is $-\sin(x)$. | 让我们实际算一算这个积分表达式。正如上一课所见，要计算积分，你需要找到积分号里面那个函数的反导数；某个以 $\sin(x)$ 为导数的函数。而如果你对三角函数的导数足够熟，你知道 $\cos(x)$ 的导数是 $-\sin(x)$。 |
| If you negate that, $-\cos(x)$ is the antiderivative of $\sin(x)$. | 把它取个负，$-\cos(x)$ 就是 $\sin(x)$ 的反导数。 |
| To gut check yourself on that, look at this graph of $-\cos(x)$. At $0$, the slope is $0$, then it increases to some maximum slope at $\pi/2$, then it goes back down to $0$ at $\pi$, and in general its slope does indeed seem to match the height of the sine graph. | 要直觉自检一下，看看这条 $-\cos(x)$ 的图像：在 $0$ 处斜率是 $0$，然后在 $\pi/2$ 处升到某个最大斜率，接着在 $\pi$ 处又落回 $0$；总体上看，它的斜率确实和正弦图像的高度相匹配。 |
| To evaluate the integral of $\sin(x)$ between $0$ and $\pi$, take that antiderivative at the upper bound, and subtract its value at the lower bound. | 要计算 $\sin(x)$ 在 $0$ 到 $\pi$ 之间的积分，就把那个反导数在上界处取值，再减去它在下界处的值。 |

$$
\int_0^\pi \sin (x) d x=(-\cos (\pi))-(-\cos (0))
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| More visually, that's the difference in the height of this $-\cos(x)$ graph above $\pi$, and its height at $0$, and as you can see, that change in height is exactly $2$. | 更直观地说，这就是这条 $-\cos(x)$ 图像在 $\pi$ 上方的高度与它在 $0$ 处的高度之差；如你所见，这个高度变化恰好是 $2$。 |
| That's kind of interesting, isn't it? That the area under this sine graph turns out to be exactly $2$. | 这有点意思，不是吗？这条正弦图像下的面积竟然恰好是 $2$。 |
| The answer to our average height problem, this integral divided by the width of the region, evidently turns out to be $\frac{2}{\pi}$, which is around $0.64$. | 而我们平均高度问题的答案——这个积分除以区域宽度——显然就是 $\frac{2}{\pi}$，大约 $0.64$。 |

$$
\frac{\int_0^\pi \sin (x) d x}{\pi-0}=\frac{(-\cos (\pi))-(-\cos (0))}{\pi-0}=\frac{2}{\pi} \approx 0.64
$$

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 算出结果
> - **trig** /trɪɡ/ —— 三角函数的（口语缩写）。if you're comfortable with trig derivatives。
> - **negate** /nɪˈɡeɪt/ —— 取负、否定。If you negate that。
> - **visually** /ˈvɪʒuəli/ —— 直观地、视觉上。More visually, that's the difference…。
> - *gut check* —— 直觉检验、凭本能核对。To gut check yourself on that。
> - *comfortable with* —— 对……熟、驾驭自如。if you're comfortable with trig derivatives。
> - 纯中文说明：本节核心结果——$\int_0^\pi \sin(x)\,dx = 2$，平均高度 $= \frac{2}{\pi} \approx 0.64$。

## 综合练习（Comprehensive Question）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Now is a good time to try your hand at calculating the average value of a function over a range. For example, given the function $f(x)=-x^2+4 x-3$, what is the average value of the function between $1$ and $3$? | 现在正是个好时机，亲手算一算函数在某个范围上的平均值。比如，给定函数 $f(x)=-x^2+4 x-3$，它在 $1$ 到 $3$ 之间的平均值是多少？ |
| **What is the average height of the function $f(x)=-x^2+4 x-3$ from $1$ to $3$?** | （译注：原文此处是一道互动选择题，选项为 $0.66\bar{6}$、$0.5$、$0.75$、$1$，正确答案为第一个（$0.66\bar{6}$，即 $\frac{2}{3}$），解析见下。）**函数 $f(x)=-x^2+4 x-3$ 从 $1$ 到 $3$ 的平均高度是多少？** |
| We can start with the idea that we've been building up throughout the lesson that the average height is equal to the area under a range divided by the width of the range. | （译注：上题的答案解析。）我们可以从整节课一直在铺垫的那个想法出发：平均高度等于某个范围下的面积除以该范围的宽度。 |

$$
\text { Average height }=\frac{\text { Area }}{\text { Width }}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| We know that the area is equal to the integral over that range. | 我们知道，面积就等于该范围上的积分。 |

$$
\text { Average height } = \frac{\displaystyle \int_{x_1}^{x_2} f(x)dx}{x_2 - x_1}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| And substituting the function and the range into the expression we get the following. | （译注：官网原文此处及下文多处把被积函数写成 $x^2+4x-3$，漏了首项的负号，照录；实际函数是 $f(x)=-x^2+4x-3$。）把函数和范围代入表达式，我们得到下面这个。 |

$$
\text { Average height }= \frac{\displaystyle \int_{1}^{3} (x^2+4 x-3) dx}{3 -1}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| To evaluate the integral of $x^2+4 x-3$ from $1$ and $3$, take the antiderivative at the upper bound and subtract its value at the lower bound. | 要计算 $x^2+4 x-3$ 从 $1$ 到 $3$ 的积分，就把反导数在上界处取值，再减去它在下界处的值。 |

$$
\int_{1}^{3} (x^2+4 x-3) dx = F(3) - F(1)
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Where we know the antiderivative is $F(x)=-\frac{x^3}{3}+2 x^2-3 x$ plus some arbitrary constant that will get cancelled in the subtraction. | 其中我们知道，反导数是 $F(x)=-\frac{x^3}{3}+2 x^2-3 x$，再加上某个任意常数——它会在相减时被抵消。 |

$$
\begin{align}
\int_{1}^{3} (x^2+4 x-3) dx &= \left(-\frac{(3)^3}{3}+2(3)^2-3(3)\right)-\left(-\frac{(1)^3}{3}+2(1)^2-3(1)\right) \\[1em]
&= (-9+18-9)-\left(-\frac{1}{3}+2-3\right) \\[1em]
&=0-\left(-\frac{4}{3}\right) \\[1em]
&=\frac{4}{3}
\end{align}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| This means that the area under the function $x^2+4 x-3$ from $1$ and $3$ is equal to $\frac{4}{3}$. | 这说明函数 $x^2+4 x-3$ 从 $1$ 到 $3$ 之下的面积等于 $\frac{4}{3}$。 |
| And to calculate the average height of this function, we need to divide by the width of the range, and $\frac{4}{3}$ divided by $2$ is equal to $\frac{2}{3}$. | 而要计算这个函数的平均高度，需要除以范围的宽度：$\frac{4}{3}$ 除以 $2$ 等于 $\frac{2}{3}$。 |
| This is the answer to our question! The average height of the function $x^2+4 x-3$ from $1$ to $3$ is equal to $\frac{2}{3}$. | 这就是我们问题的答案！函数 $x^2+4 x-3$ 从 $1$ 到 $3$ 的平均高度等于 $\frac{2}{3}$。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 综合练习
> - **arbitrary** /ˈɑːbɪtrəri/ —— 任意的。plus some arbitrary constant。
> - **cancelled** /ˈkænsld/ —— 被抵消的。will get cancelled in the subtraction；动词 cancel。
> - *try your hand at* —— 试身手、练一练。try your hand at calculating…。
> - 纯中文说明：本例答案 $\frac{2}{3} \approx 0.67$；原文公式的负号排印问题见前文译注。

## 另一个视角（Another perspective）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| I promised at the start that this question of finding the average value of a function offers an alternate perspective on on why integrals and derivatives are inverses of one and other; why the area under one graph is related to the slope of another. | （译注：原文 on on 系 on 之重复、one and other 系 one another 之笔误，均照录。）我在开头承诺过：求函数平均值这个问题，为「积分与导数为什么互为逆运算」「一张图下的面积为什么与另一张图的斜率相关」提供了另一个视角。 |
| Notice how finding this average value $\frac{2}{\pi}$ came down to looking at the change in the antiderivative $-\cos(x)$ over the input range, divided by the length of that input range. Another way to think about that fraction is as the rise-over-run slope between the point of the antiderivative graph below zero, and the point of that graph above $\pi$. | 注意，求出这个平均值 $\frac{2}{\pi}$，归根结底是看反导数 $-\cos(x)$ 在输入范围上的变化量，除以那个输入范围的长度。换个角度理解这个分式：它是反导数图像在 $0$ 下方的点与在 $\pi$ 上方的点之间的「纵变除以横变」斜率。 |
| Now think about why it might make sense that this slope represents the average value of $\sin(x)$ on that region. By definition, $\sin(x)$ is the derivative of this antiderivative graph; it gives the slope of $-\cos(x)$ at every input. So another way to think about the average value $\sin(x)$ is as the average slope over all tangent lines here between $0$ and $\pi$. And from that view, doesn't it make a lot of sense that the average slope of a graph over all its point in a certain range should equal the total slope between the start and end point? | 现在想想，为什么这个斜率代表 $\sin(x)$ 在那个区域上的平均值会有道理。按定义，$\sin(x)$ 就是这张反导数图像的导数；它给出 $-\cos(x)$ 在每个输入处的斜率。于是，理解 $\sin(x)$ 平均值的另一种方式，是把它当成 $0$ 到 $\pi$ 之间所有切线的平均斜率。从这个视角看，一张图在某个范围内所有点上的平均斜率，应当等于起点与终点之间的总斜率——这不是很说得通吗？ |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 另一个视角
> - **alternate** /ˈɔːltənət/ —— 另一种的、替代的。an alternate perspective。
> - **tangent** /ˈtændʒənt/ —— 切线的、相切的。the average slope over all tangent lines。
> - *rise over run* —— 纵变除以横变（斜率的口语说法）。the rise-over-run slope。
> - *come down to* —— 归结为。came down to looking at the change…。

## 把这个视角推广（Generalize this view）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| To digest this idea, it helps to see what it looks like for a general function. For any function $f(x)$, if you want to find its average value on some interval, say between $a$ and $b$, what you do is take the integral of $f$ on that interval, divided by the width of the interval. | 要消化这个想法，看看它对一般函数长什么样会很有帮助。对任何函数 $f(x)$，如果你想求它在某个区间（比如 $a$ 到 $b$ 之间）上的平均值，你要做的就是取 $f$ 在那个区间上的积分，除以区间的宽度。 |
| You can think of this as taking the area under the graph divided by the width. Or more accurately, it's the signed area of that graph, since area below the x-axis is counted as negative. | 你可以把它想成图像下的面积除以宽度；或者更准确地说，是那张图的带符号面积，因为 $x$ 轴以下的面积记为负。 |
| And take a moment to remember the connection between this idea of a continuous average and the usual finite notion of an average, where you add up many numbers and divide by how many there are. When you take some sample of points spaced out by $dx$, the number of samples is about the length of the interval divided by $dx$. | 再花点时间回想一下：这种连续平均的想法，与通常的有限平均——把许多数加起来除以个数——之间是如何联系起来的。当你取一些以 $dx$ 为间距铺开的样本点时，样本数约等于区间长度除以 $dx$。 |
| If you add up the value of $f(x)$ at each sample and divide by the total number of samples, it's the same as adding up the products $f(x) \cdot dx$ and dividing by the width of the entire interval. | 如果你把每个样本处的 $f(x)$ 值加起来再除以样本总数，那就等价于把乘积 $f(x) \cdot dx$ 加起来，再除以整个区间的宽度。 |
| The only difference between that and the integral expression is that the integral asks what happens as $dx$ approaches $0$, but that just corresponds with samples of more and more points that approximate the true average increasingly well. Like any integral, evaluating this comes down to finding an antiderivative of $f(x)$, commonly denoted capital $F(x)$. | 这跟积分表达式的唯一区别在于：积分问的是当 $dx$ 趋近 $0$ 时会发生什么——而这只不过对应着越来越多的样本点，它们对真实平均的近似越来越好。像任何积分一样，求它的值归根结底是找 $f(x)$ 的一个反导数，通常记作大写 $F(x)$。 |
| In particular, what we want is the change to this antiderivative between $a$ and $b$, $F(b) - F(a)$, which you can think of as the change in the height of this new graph between the two bounds. | 具体地说，我们想要的是这个反导数在 $a$ 与 $b$ 之间的变化量 $F(b) - F(a)$，你可以把它想成这张新图在两个界之间的高度变化。 |
| I've conveniently chosen an antiderivative which passes through $0$ at the lower bound here, but keep in mind that you could freely shift this up and down, adding whatever constant you want to it, and it would still be a valid antiderivative. So the solution to the average problem is the change in the height of this new graph divided by the change to its $x$ value between $a$ and $b$. | 这里我图方便，选了一个在下界处穿过 $0$ 的反导数；但请记住，你完全可以把它上下平移，加上你想要的任何常数，它仍然是一个合法的反导数。于是平均问题的解，就是这张新图在 $a$ 与 $b$ 之间的高度变化，除以它的 $x$ 值的变化。 |

$$
\frac{\displaystyle \int_a^b f(x) d x}{b-a}=\frac{F(b)-F(a)}{b-a}
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| In other words, it's the slope of the antiderivative graph between these endpoints. | 换句话说，它就是反导数图像在这两个端点之间的斜率。 |
| And again, that should make a lot of sense, because little $f(x)$ gives the slope of a tangent line to this graph at each point, after all it is by definition the derivative of capital $F$. | 再说一遍，这应该非常说得通，因为小写的 $f(x)$ 给出的正是这张图在每个点上切线的斜率——毕竟按定义，它就是大写 $F$ 的导数。 |
| Why are antiderivative the key to solving integrals? Well, my favorite intuition is still the one I showed last lesson, but a second perspective is that when you reframe the question of finding the average of a continuous value as finding the average slope of bunch of tangent lines, it lets you see the answer just by the comparing endpoints, rather than having to actually tally up all points in between. | 为什么反导数是解积分的关键？嗯，我最喜欢的直觉仍然是上一课展示的那个，但还有第二个视角：当你把「求连续值的平均」重新框定为「求一堆切线的平均斜率」时，你只需比较两个端点就能看出答案，而不必真的把中间所有点逐一累加。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 把这个视角推广
> - **digest** /daɪˈdʒest/ —— 消化、领会。To digest this idea。
> - **denote** /dɪˈnəʊt/ —— 记作、表示。commonly denoted capital $F(x)$。
> - **valid** /ˈvælɪd/ —— 成立的、合法的。it would still be a valid antiderivative。
> - **conveniently** /kənˈviːniəntli/ —— 图方便地、恰好。I've conveniently chosen an antiderivative…。
> - *tally up* —— 逐项累加、清点。having to actually tally up all points in between。
> - 纯中文说明：平均值的完整公式 $\frac{1}{b-a}\int_a^b f(x)\,dx = \frac{F(b)-F(a)}{b-a}$——右端即反导数图像两端点间的斜率。

## 总结（Summary）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| In the last video, I described a sensation that should bring integrals to your mind. Namely, if you feel like the problem you're solving could be approximated by breaking it up somehow, and adding up a large number of small things. Here I want you to come away recognizing a second sensation that should bring integrals to your mind. If there's some idea that you understand in a finite context, and which involves adding up multiple values, like taking the average of a bunch of numbers, and if you want to generalize that idea to apply to an infinite continuous range of values, try seeing if you can phrase things in terms of an integral. It's a feeling that comes up enough that's it's definitely worth remembering. | （译注：原文 that's it's 系 it's 之笔误，照录。）在上一集里，我描述过一种应当让你联想到积分的感觉：如果你觉得自己正在解决的问题，可以通过某种拆分、再把大量小东西加总来近似。这一篇，我想让你带着对第二种感觉的认识离开——它同样应当让你想到积分。如果有个想法你在有限情境里已经理解，它涉及把多个值加总，比如求一堆数的平均；而你想把这个想法推广到适用于无穷连续的值域，那就试试看，能不能把事情用积分的语言表述出来。这种感觉出现的次数足够多，绝对值得记住。 |
