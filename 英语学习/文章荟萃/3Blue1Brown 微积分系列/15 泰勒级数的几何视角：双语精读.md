## 几何视角（Geometric View）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| In the spirit of showing you just how connected the topics of calculus are, let me turn to a completely different way to understand this second order term geometrically. It's related to the fundamental theorem of calculus, which I talked about in chapters 1 and 8. | 为了让你看看微积分的各个主题之间是多么环环相扣，让我换一个完全不同的几何方式来理解这个二阶项。它与微积分基本定理（fundamental theorem of calculus）有关，我在第 1 章和第 8 章讲过它。 |
| Like we did in those videos, consider a function that gives the area under some graph between a fixed left point and a variable right point. What we're going to do is think about how to approximate this area function, not the function for the graph like we were doing before. Focusing on that area is what will make the second order term pop out. | 像在那两课里做的那样，考虑一个函数：它给出某张图像在「固定左端点与可变右端点之间」围出的面积。我们要做的，是想办法近似这个面积函数——而不是像之前那样去近似图像所对应的函数。正是把注意力放在面积上，二阶项才会自己蹦出来。 |
| Remember, the fundamental theorem of calculus is that this graph itself represents the derivative of the area function, and as a reminder it's because a slight nudge dx to the right bound on the area gives a new bit of area approximately equal to the height of the graph times dx, in a way that's increasingly accurate for smaller choice of dx. | 记住，微积分基本定理说的是：这张图像本身就代表面积函数的导数。再提醒一句，原因在于：把面积的右端点轻轻推一下 dx，会添出一小块新面积，它近似等于图像的高度乘以 dx，而且 dx 选得越小，这个说法就越准。 |
| So df over dx, the change in area divided by that nudge dx, approaches the height of the graph as dx approaches 0. | 于是 df/dx——面积的变化除以那一推 dx——在 dx 趋近 0 时趋近于图像的高度。 |
| But if you wanted to be more accurate about the change to the area given some change to x that isn't mean to approach 0, you would take into account this portion right here, which is approximately a triangle. | 可如果你想更准确地算出面积的变化——这里的 x 变化并不打算趋近 0——你就得把这里这一块也考虑进来，它近似是一个三角形。（译注：原文 isn't mean to 系 isn't meant to 之笔误，照录。） |
| Let's call the starting input a, and the nudged input above it x, so that this change is (x-a). | 把起始输入叫作 a，它上方被推过的输入叫作 x，于是这个变化就是 (x-a)。 |
| The base of that little triangle is that change (x-a), and its height is the slope of the graph times (x-a). Since this graph is the derivative of the area function, that slope is the second derivative of the area function, evaluated at the input a. | 那个小三角形的底就是变化量 (x-a)，它的高是图像的斜率乘以 (x-a)。由于这张图像就是面积函数的导数，这个斜率就是面积函数在输入 a 处取值的二阶导数。 |
| So the area of that triangle, ½ base times height, is one half times the second derivative of the area function, evaluated at a, multiplied by (x-a)2. | 所以那个三角形的面积——½ 乘底乘高——就等于：½ 乘以面积函数在 a 处的二阶导数，再乘以 (x-a)2。（译注：原文 (x-a)2 如此，指 $(x-a)^2$，照录。） |
| And this is exactly what you see with Taylor polynomials. If you knew the various derivative information about the area function at the point a, you would approximate this area at x to be the area up to a, f(a), plus the area of this rectangle, which is the first derivative times (x-a), plus the area of this triangle, which is ½ (the second derivative) * (x - a)2. | 而这正是你在泰勒多项式里看到的。如果你掌握了面积函数在点 a 处的各阶导数信息，你会把 x 处的面积近似为：到 a 为止的面积 f(a)，加上这个矩形的面积——一阶导数乘以 (x-a)——再加上这个三角形的面积，即 ½（二阶导数）* (x - a)2。（译注：原文 (x - a)2 如此，指 $(x-a)^2$，照录。） |
| I like this, because even though it looks a bit messy all written out, each term has a clear meaning you can point to on the diagram. | 我很喜欢这个视角：整个式子全写出来虽然看着有点乱，但每一项都有清晰的含义，你都能在图上指出来。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 几何视角
> - **pop out** —— 蹦出来、凸显出来。Focusing on that area is what will make the second order term pop out。
> - **nudge** /nʌdʒ/ —— 轻推。a slight nudge dx to the right bound。
> - **portion** /ˈpɔːʃn/ —— 一部分。this portion right here。
> - **messy** /ˈmesi/ —— 乱的、凌乱的。it looks a bit messy all written out。
> - **bound** /baʊnd/ —— 界、端点。the right bound on the area。
> - *in the spirit of* —— 本着……的精神。In the spirit of showing you just how connected...。
> - *take into account* —— 把……考虑进来。you would take into account this portion。
> - *evaluate at* —— 在……处取值。evaluated at the input a。
