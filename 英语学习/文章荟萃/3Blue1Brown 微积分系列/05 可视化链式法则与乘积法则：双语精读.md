## 求和法则（Sum rule）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Derivative Sum Rule for the function $f(x) = g(x) + h(x)$ | （图注）函数 $f(x) = g(x) + h(x)$ 的导数求和法则。 |
| The sum rule is the easiest, if somewhat tongue-twisting to say out loud: The derivative of a sum of two functions is the sum of their derivatives. But it's worth warming up with an example and _really_ thinking through what it means to take a derivative of a sum of two functions, since the derivative patterns for products and function composition won't be so straight forward, and will require this kind of deeper thinking. | 求和法则最容易——虽然大声念出来有点拗口：两个函数之和的导数，等于它们导数之和。但值得先用一个例子热身，_真正_想透「对两个函数之和求导」是什么意思，因为乘积与函数复合的求导模式可不会这么直截了当，它们需要这种更深入的思考。 |
| For example, let's think about this function $f(x) = \sin(x) + x^2$. It's a function where, for every input, you add together the values of $\sin(x)$ and $x^2$ at that point. | 比如，我们来想想这个函数 $f(x) = \sin(x) + x^2$：对每一个输入，你都要把该点上 $\sin(x)$ 与 $x^2$ 的值加在一起。 |
| Given the input $x = 0.5$, the output of the function is the height of the sine graph represented by the blue bar **plus** the height of the $x^2$ parabola represented by the green bar. | 给定输入 $x = 0.5$，函数的输出就是蓝色柱代表的正弦图像的高度**加上**绿色柱代表的 $x^2$ 抛物线的高度。 |
| For the derivative, you ask what happens as you nudge the input slightly, maybe increasing it to $0.5 + dx$. The difference in the value of $f$ between these two values is what we call $df$. | 至于导数，你问的是：把输入轻轻推动一点——比如增加到 $0.5 + dx$——会发生什么。$f$ 在这两个取值之间的差，就是我们所说的 $df$。 |
| Well, pictured like this, I think you'll agree that the total change in height is whatever the change to the sine graph is, what we might call $d\left(\sin(x)\right)$, plus whatever the change to $x^2$ is, $d(x^2)$. | 这么画出来，我想你会同意：高度的总变化，就是正弦图像的变化——我们可以记作 $d\left(\sin(x)\right)$——加上 $x^2$ 的变化，即 $d(x^2)$。 |
| This gives us $df$ as the sum of change of the two functions. | 这就给了我们 $df$：两个函数的变化之和。 |
| We know the derivative of sine is cosine, and what that means is that this little change $d\left(\sin(x)\right)$ would be about $\cos(x) \cdot dx$. It's proportional to the size of $dx$, with a proportionality constant equal to cosine of whatever input we started at. Similarly, because the derivative of $x^2$ is $2x$, the change in the height of the $x^2$ graph is about $2x \cdot dx$. | 我们知道正弦的导数是余弦，这意味着这个微小变化 $d\left(\sin(x)\right)$ 大约是 $\cos(x) \cdot dx$：它与 $dx$ 的大小成正比，比例常数等于我们出发的那个输入处的余弦值。同样，由于 $x^2$ 的导数是 $2x$，$x^2$ 图像高度的变化大约是 $2x \cdot dx$。 |
| So, $\frac{df}{dx}$, the ratio of the tiny change to the sum function to the tiny change in $x$ that caused it, is indeed $\cos(x)+2x$, the sum of the derivatives of its parts. | 于是，$\frac{df}{dx}$——和函数的微小变化与引起它的 $x$ 的微小变化之比——确实就是 $\cos(x)+2x$：各部分导数之和。 |
| **What is the derivative of the function $f(x) = x^4 + \cos(x)$?** | （译注：原文此处是一道互动选择题，选项为 $4x^3 - \sin(x)$ / $4x^3 + \sin(x)$，正确答案为第一个，解析见下。）**函数 $f(x) = x^4 + \cos(x)$ 的导数是什么？** |
| Start by setting up the derivative of the function. | （译注：以下为上题的答案解析。）先把这个函数的导数搭出来。 |

$$
\frac{d}{dx }\left( f(x) \right) = \frac{d}{dx }\left(  x^4 + \cos(x) \right)
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Apply the sum rule. | 应用求和法则。 |

$$
\frac{df}{dx} = \frac{d}{dx }\left(  x^4 \right) + \frac{d}{dx }\left(  \cos(x) \right)
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Take the derivative of the two functions. | 对两个函数分别求导。 |

$$
\frac{df}{dx} = 4x^3 + (- \sin(x))
$$

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Simplify. | 化简。 |

$$
\frac{df}{dx} = 4x^3 - \sin(x)
$$

## 乘积法则（Product rule）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Things are a bit different for the product of two functions. Let's think through why, in terms of tiny nudges. In this case, I don't think graphs are our best bet for visualizing things. Pretty commonly in math, all levels of math really, if you're dealing with a product of two things, it helps to try to understand it as some form of area. | 对两个函数的乘积，事情就有点不一样了。我们用「微小轻推」的思路想想这是为什么。在这个问题上，我觉得图像并不是可视化的最佳选择。在数学里——真的是各个层次的数学里——一个很常见的做法是：如果你在处理两个东西的乘积，把它理解成某种形式的面积会很有帮助。 |
| For example, for the function $\sin(x) \cdot x^2$, you might try to configure some mental setup of a box whose side-lengths are $\sin(x)$ and $x^2$. | 比如，对函数 $\sin(x) \cdot x^2$，你可以在脑海里搭一个盒子，让它的两条边长分别是 $\sin(x)$ 和 $x^2$。 |
| What would that mean? Well, since these are functions, you might think of these sides as adjustable; dependent on the value of $x$, which you might think of as a number that you can freely adjust. | 这意味着什么？嗯，既然这些是函数，你可以把这些边想成可调节的——依赖于 $x$ 的取值，而 $x$ 你可以想成一个能随意调动的数。 |
| So, just getting the feel for this, focus on that top side, whose changes as the function $\sin(x)$. As you change the value of $x$ up from $0$, it increases up to a length of $1$ as $\sin(x)$ moves towards its peak. After that, it starts decreasing as $\sin(x)$ comes down from $1$. And likewise, that height changes as $x^2$. | 好，先来找找感觉：盯住上面那条边，它随着函数 $\sin(x)$ 变化。当你把 $x$ 的值从 $0$ 往上调，随着 $\sin(x)$ 走向峰值，这条边会变长到 $1$；过了那一点，随着 $\sin(x)$ 从 $1$ 降下来，它又开始变短。同样，那条高随着 $x^2$ 变化。 |
| So $f(x)$, defined as this product, is the area of this box. For the derivative, think about how a tiny change to $x$ by $dx$ influences this area; that resulting change in area is $df$. That nudge to $x$ causes the width to change by some small $d\left( \sin(x)\right)$, and the height to change by some $d(x^2)$. | 于是，定义为这个乘积的 $f(x)$ 就是这个盒子的面积。至于导数，想想把 $x$ 轻推一个微小的 $dx$ 会如何影响这个面积；由此产生的面积变化就是 $df$。对 $x$ 的这一轻推，让宽度变化了某个小的 $d\left( \sin(x)\right)$，让高度变化了某个 $d(x^2)$。 |
| This gives us three little snippets of new area: A thin rectangle on the bottom, whose area is its width, $\sin(x)$, times its thin height, $d(x^2)$; there's a thin rectangle on the right, whose area is its height, $x^2$, times its thin width, $d\left( \sin(x)\right)$. And there's also a bit in the corner. But we can ignore it, since its area will ultimately be proportional to $dx^2$, which becomes negligible as $dx$ goes to $0$. | 这给了我们三小块新增的面积：底下有一条细长矩形，它的面积是宽度 $\sin(x)$ 乘以细长的高 $d(x^2)$；右边有一条细长矩形，它的面积是高 $x^2$ 乘以细长的宽 $d\left( \sin(x)\right)$。角落里还有一小块，但我们可以无视它，因为它的面积正比于 $dx^2$，当 $dx$ 趋近 $0$ 时可以忽略不计。 |
| This is very similar to what I showed in the last chapter, with the $x^2$ diagram. Just like then, keep in mind that I'm using somewhat beefy changes to draw things, so we can see them, but in principle think of $dx$ as _very_ small, meaning $d(x^2)$ and $d\left(\sin(x)\right)$ are also _very_ small. | 这和上一章 $x^2$ 的那张图非常相似。和那时一样，请记住：为了让变化看得见，我画图时用的是偏大的变化量，但原则上要把 $dx$ 想得_非常_小，这意味着 $d(x^2)$ 和 $d\left(\sin(x)\right)$ 也_非常_小。 |
| So we are interested in finding the change to the **area** of this rectangle represented by the two smaller rectangles highlighted in yellow. | 我们关心的，是这两个黄色高亮小矩形所代表的这个矩形**面积**的变化。 |
| Applying what we know about the derivative of sine and $x^2$, that tiny change $d(x^2)$ is $2x \cdot dx$, and that tiny change $d\left(\sin(x)\right)$ is $\cos(x)dx$. | 用上我们对正弦和 $x^2$ 导数的已有知识：那个微小变化 $d(x^2)$ 是 $2x \cdot dx$，那个微小变化 $d\left(\sin(x)\right)$ 是 $\cos(x)dx$。 |
| Dividing out by that $dx$, the derivative $\frac{df}{dx}$ is $\sin(x)$ by the derivative of $x^2$, plus $x^2$ by the derivative of sine. | 把那个 $dx$ 除掉，导数 $\frac{df}{dx}$ 就是 $\sin(x)$ 乘 $x^2$ 的导数，加上 $x^2$ 乘正弦的导数。 |
| This line of reasoning works for any two functions. | 这条推理思路对任何两个函数都适用。 |
| Generic Product Rule where $f(x) = g(x)h(x)$. | （图注）一般形式的乘积法则，其中 $f(x) = g(x)h(x)$。 |
| A common mnemonic for the product rule is to say in your head "left d right, right d left". In this example, $\sin(x) \cdot x^2$, "left d right" means you take the left function, in this case $g(x) = \sin(x)$, times the derivative of the right, $h(x) = x^2$, which gives $2x$. Then you add "right d left": the right function, $x^2$, times the derivative of the left, $\cos(x)$. | 乘积法则一个常见的口诀，是在心里默念「左 d 右，右 d 左」（left d right, right d left）。在这个例子 $\sin(x) \cdot x^2$ 里，「左 d 右」指的是：取左边的函数——这里是 $g(x) = \sin(x)$——乘以右边函数的导数，$h(x) = x^2$ 的导数，得到 $2x$。然后再加上「右 d 左」：右边的函数 $x^2$ 乘以左边函数的导数 $\cos(x)$。 |
| Out of context, this feels like kind of a strange rule, but when you think of this adjustable box you can actually see how those terms represent slivers of area. "Left d right" is the area of this bottom rectangle, and "right d left" is the area of this rectangle on the right. | 抽离语境看，这条法则感觉有点怪；但当你想到这个可调节的盒子，你就能真真切切看到这些项代表着一片片细长的面积。「左 d 右」是底下那条矩形的面积，「右 d 左」是右边那条矩形的面积。 |

## 常数乘法（Constant multiplication）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| By the way, I should mention that if you multiply by a _constant_, say $2 \cdot \sin(x)$, things end up much simpler. The derivative is just that same constant times the derivative of the function, in this case $2 \cdot \cos(x)$. I'll leave it to you to pause and ponder to verify that this makes sense. | 顺带一提，如果你乘的是一个_常数_，比如 $2 \cdot \sin(x)$，事情就简单多了：导数就是同一个常数乘以函数的导数，在这个例子里是 $2 \cdot \cos(x)$。至于停下来琢磨琢磨、验证这为什么讲得通，就交给你了。 |
| Explanation | （译注：此为官网原文一个可折叠面板的标题，面板内是常数乘法的解释，需到官网原文页查看。）解释。 |
| One way to verify that this is the case is to visualize a constant multiplied by a function as the area of the rectangle with width equal to the function and height equal to the constant. | 验证这一点的一种办法，是把「常数乘函数」可视化为一个矩形的面积：宽等于函数值，高等于常数。 |
| Then, introducing a small nudge $dx$ to the input of the function, you can measure the change in the area highlighted in yellow below. | 然后，给函数的输入引入一个小的轻推 $dx$，你就可以量出下面黄色高亮部分的面积变化。 |

Solving for $\frac{df}{dx}$ we get that the derivative is $2\cos(x)$. | 在 $g(x) = \sin(x)$、$c = 2$ 的情形里，我们知道 $d(g(x)) = \cos(x) \cdot dx$。这给出面积变化 $df = 2 \cdot \cos(x) \cdot dx$。解出 $\frac{df}{dx}$，就得到导数是 $2\cos(x)$。 |

## 链式法则（Chain rule）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Aside from addition and multiplication, the other common way to combine functions that comes up all the time is function composition. For example, let's say we take the function $x^2$, and shove it inside $\sin(x)$ to get a new function, $\sin(x^2)$. Or, in other words, the output of the function $x^2$ gets fed as input to sine function. | 除了加法和乘法，另一种随时都会冒出来的常见组合方式是函数复合。比如，取函数 $x^2$，把它塞进 $\sin(x)$ 里，得到新函数 $\sin(x^2)$；换句话说，函数 $x^2$ 的输出被当作输入喂给了正弦函数。 |
| What's the derivative of this new function? | 这个新函数的导数是什么？ |
| Here I'll choose yet another way to visualize things, just to emphasize that in creative math, we have lots of options. I'll put up three number lines. The top one will hold the value of $x$, the second one will represent the value of $x^2$, and the third line will hold the value of $\sin(x^2)$. | 这里我会再换一种可视化方式，就是要强调：在创造性的数学里，我们有很多种选择。我摆出三条数轴：最上面一条放 $x$ 的值，第二条表示 $x^2$ 的值，第三条放 $\sin(x^2)$ 的值。 |
| That is, the function $x^2$ gets you from line $1$ to line $2$, and the function sine gets you from line $2$ to line $3$. In the image, I'm showing an $x$ value of $0.5$ on the first number line. So the second number line, which just displays $x^2$, is showing the output of the inner function, $0.25$. The third number line shows $\sin(x^2)$, which is really just the sine of the previous value, so $\sin(0.25) \approx 0.247$. | 也就是说，函数 $x^2$ 带你从第 1 条线走到第 2 条线，正弦函数带你从第 2 条线走到第 3 条线。图里第一条数轴上显示的 $x$ 值是 $0.5$，所以只显示 $x^2$ 的第二条数轴展示的是内层函数的输出 $0.25$；第三条数轴显示 $\sin(x^2)$——其实就是前一个值的正弦，所以 $\sin(0.25) \approx 0.247$。 |
| **What is the value of the composed function given the input $x=2$? To get a hang of this visualization technique, go from the first line to the second line to the third line.** | （译注：原文此处是一道互动选择题，选项为 $\sin(2) \approx 0.909$ / $\sin(4) \approx -0.757$，正确答案为第二个，解析见下。）**给定输入 $x=2$，复合函数的值是多少？为了摸到这个可视化技巧的门道，请从第一条线走到第二条线、再走到第三条线。** |
| Using the number line visual, start on $x=2$ and move to the next line using the function $g(x) = x^2$. Plugging in $x=2$ into the function we get $(2)^2 = 4$ on the next numberline which we then feed into the outer function $h(x) = \sin(x)$. | （译注：上题的答案解析。原文 numberline 系连写，照录。）利用数轴图像，从 $x=2$ 出发，用函数 $g(x) = x^2$ 走到下一条线。把 $x=2$ 代入函数，得到 $(2)^2 = 4$ 显示在下一条数轴上，然后再把它喂给外层函数 $h(x) = \sin(x)$。 |
| As I shift that value of $x$, maybe up to the value $3$, then the value on the second shifts to whatever $x^2$ is, in this case $9$. And that bottom value, being the $\sin(x^2)$, will go over to whatever $\sin(9)$ is. | 当我挪动 $x$ 的值——比如挪到 $3$——第二条线上的值就挪到 $x^2$ 所对应的值，这里是 $9$；而最底下那个值，也就是 $\sin(x^2)$，会挪到 $\sin(9)$ 所对应的值。 |
| So for the derivative, let's again think of nudging that $x$-value by some little $dx$. I find it helpful to imagine $x$ starting out as some actual number, maybe $1.5$, and $dx$ as some small number approaching zero, like $0.1$. | 至于导数，我们再来想一遍：把那个 $x$ 值轻推某个小小的 $dx$。我发现一个有用的想象方式：把 $x$ 想成从某个真实的数出发，比如 $1.5$，而 $dx$ 是某个趋近零的小数，比如 $0.1$。 |
| The resulting nudge to this second value, the change to $x^2$ caused by such a $dx$, is what we might call $d(x^2)$. You can expand this as $2x \cdot dx$. For our specific input that length would be $2(1.5)dx$, but it helps to keep it written as $d(x^2)$ for now. In fact let me go one step further and give a new name to $x^2$, maybe $h$, so this nudge $d(x^2)$ is just $dh$. | 由此给第二个值带来的轻推——这样的 $dx$ 造成的 $x^2$ 的变化——我们可以叫它 $d(x^2)$。你可以把它展开成 $2x \cdot dx$；对我们这个具体输入来说，那个长度是 $2(1.5)dx$，但眼下把它保持写作 $d(x^2)$ 会更有帮助。实际上，让我再进一步，给 $x^2$ 起个新名字，比如 $h$，于是这个轻推 $d(x^2)$ 就干脆叫 $dh$。 |
| The fact that $\sin(h)$ is moving left while the $dh$ bump is to the right just means that this change $d\left(\sin(h)\right)$ is some negative number. | （图注）$\sin(h)$ 向左移动、而 $dh$ 那一小块在右边，这个事实只是意味着变化 $d\left(\sin(h)\right)$ 是某个负数。 |
| Now think of that third value, which is pegged at $\sin(h)$. Its change is $d\left(\sin(h)\right)$, the tiny change _caused_ by the nudge $dh$. Well, we know the derivative of sine, so we can expand $d\left(\sin(h)\right)$ as $\cos(h) \cdot dh$; that's what it means for the derivative of sine to be cosine. | 现在想第三个值，它被钉在 $\sin(h)$ 上。它的变化是 $d\left(\sin(h)\right)$——由轻推 $dh$ _引起_的微小变化。而我们知道正弦的导数，所以可以把 $d\left(\sin(h)\right)$ 展开成 $\cos(h) \cdot dh$；这就是「正弦的导数是余弦」的含义。 |
| Now we can unfold the transformation, replacing $h$ with $x^2$ and $dh$ with $d(x^2)$. So the bottom nudge becomes $\cos(x^2)d(x^2)$ and the middle nudge becomes $d(x^2)$. Of course, we also know that $d(x^2) = 2x \cdot dx$ and so we can substitute that into the diagram as well. | 现在我们可以把这个变换重新展开：把 $h$ 换回 $x^2$，把 $dh$ 换回 $d(x^2)$。于是最底下的轻推变成 $\cos(x^2)d(x^2)$，中间的轻推变成 $d(x^2)$。当然，我们还知道 $d(x^2) = 2x \cdot dx$，所以也可以把它代进图里。 |
| The derivative of $f(x) = \sin(x^2)$ is reframed by using the transformation $h=x^2$. | （图注）利用变换 $h=x^2$，重新框定 $f(x) = \sin(x^2)$ 的导数。 |
| It's always good to remind yourself of what this all actually means. In this case where we started at $x = 1.5$ up top, this means that the size of that nudge on the third line is about $\cos(1.5^2) \cdot 2(1.5)$ multiplied by the size of $dx$; proportional to the size of $dx$, where the derivative here gives us that proportionality constant. | 时时提醒自己这一切究竟意味着什么，总是好的。在我们从顶上的 $x = 1.5$ 出发的情形里，这意味着第三条线上那个轻推的大小约为 $\cos(1.5^2) \cdot 2(1.5)$ 乘以 $dx$ 的大小：与 $dx$ 的大小成正比，而这里的导数给出的正是那个比例常数。 |
| Since the nudge on the third line represents the change to our initial function $df$ when we introduced the small nudge $dx$, we can rearrange the expression and this gives us the derivative of the function. | 由于第三条线上的轻推代表我们引入微小轻推 $dx$ 时初始函数的变化 $df$，我们可以把表达式重新排列，这就得到了函数的导数。 |
| Notice what we have here, we have the derivative of the outside function, still taking in the unaltered inside function, and we multiply it by the derivative of the inside function. | 注意我们这里有什么：外层函数的导数——它仍然吃进未经改动的内层函数——再乘以内层函数的导数。 |
| Again, there's nothing special about $\sin(x)$ and $x^2$. If you have two functions $g(x)$ and $h(x)$, the derivative of their composition function $g\left(h(x)\right)$ is the derivative of $g$, evaluated at $h(x)$, times the derivative of $h$. This is what we call the "chain rule". | 再说一遍，$\sin(x)$ 和 $x^2$ 没有什么特别的。如果你有两个函数 $g(x)$ 和 $h(x)$，它们的复合函数 $g\left(h(x)\right)$ 的导数，就是 $g$ 的导数——在 $h(x)$ 处取值——乘以 $h$ 的导数。这就是我们所说的「链式法则」（chain rule）。 |
| The chain rule for the function $f(x) = g(h(x))$ | （图注）函数 $f(x) = g(h(x))$ 的链式法则。 |
| Notice, for the derivative of $g$, I'm writing it as $\frac{d}{dh}$ instead of $\frac{d}{dx}$. On the symbolic level, this serves as a reminder that you still plug in the inner function to this derivative. But it's also an important reflection of what this derivative of the outer function actually represents. | 注意，对于 $g$ 的导数，我写作 $\frac{d}{dh}$ 而不是 $\frac{d}{dx}$。在记号层面上，这是在提醒你：这个导数里仍然要代入内层函数。但它也是对「外层函数的导数究竟代表什么」的一个重要反映。 |
| Remember, in our three-lines setup, when we took the derivative of sine on the bottom, we expanded the size of the nudge $d(\sin)$ as $\cos(h) \cdot dh$. This was because we didn't immediately know how the size of that bottom nudge depended on $x$, that's kind of the whole thing we're trying to figure out, but we could take the derivative with respect to the intermediate variable $h$. That is, figure out how to express the size of that nudge as multiple of $dh$. Then it unfolded by figuring out what $dh$ was. | 回想一下，在我们的三条数轴布局里，当我们在最底下对正弦求导时，我们把轻推 $d(\sin)$ 的大小展开成了 $\cos(h) \cdot dh$。这是因为我们并不能立刻知道最底下那个轻推的大小如何依赖于 $x$——那差不多正是我们想搞清楚的全部问题——但我们可以对中间变量 $h$ 求导。也就是说，搞清楚怎样把那个轻推的大小表示成 $dh$ 的倍数。然后再通过搞清楚 $dh$ 是什么，把它展开回来。 |
| So in this chain rule expression we're saying to look at the ratio between the tiny change in $g$ and the tiny change in $h$ that caused it, where $h$ is the value that we're plugging into $g$. Then multiply that by the tiny change in $h$ divided by the tiny change in $x$ that caused it. | 所以在这个链式法则表达式里，我们说的是：看 $g$ 的微小变化与引起它的 $h$ 的微小变化之比——$h$ 是我们代入 $g$ 的那个值；然后再乘上 $h$ 的微小变化除以引起它的 $x$ 的微小变化。 |
| The $dh$'s cancel to give the ratio between a tiny change in the final output, and the tiny change to the input that, through a certain chain of events, brought it about. That cancellation of $dh$ is more than just a notational trick, it's a genuine reflection of the tiny nudges that underpin calculus. | $dh$ 互相约掉，给出「最终输出的微小变化」与「输入的微小变化」之比——后者经过一连串事件导致了前者。$dh$ 的这种约掉不只是一个记号把戏，它真实反映着支撑起微积分的那些微小轻推。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 链式法则

> - **peg** /peɡ/ —— 钉住、固定于。pegged at $\sin(h)$。

> - **underpin** /ˌʌndəˈpɪn/ —— 支撑、构成……的基础。the tiny nudges that underpin calculus。

> - *with respect to* —— 对（某个变量）。take the derivative with respect to the intermediate variable $h$。
