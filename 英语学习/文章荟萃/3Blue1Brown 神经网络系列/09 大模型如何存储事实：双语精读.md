## 事实存放在 LLM 的哪里？（Where are facts in LLMs stored?）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| In December of 2023, a few researchers from Google DeepMind [posted work](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/iGuwZTHWb6DFY3sKB/fact-finding-attempting-to-reverse-engineer-factual-recall) on this question, using this same specific example of matching athletes to their sports. | 2023 年 12 月，来自 Google DeepMind 的几位研究者围绕这个问题[发布了一项工作](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/iGuwZTHWb6DFY3sKB/fact-finding-attempting-to-reverse-engineer-factual-recall)，用的正是这种「运动员配运动」的具体例子。 |

| In the last few chapters, you and I have been digging into the details of a transformer–the architecture underlying large language models. | 在之前几章里，你我一直都在深挖 transformer（变换器）——也就是大语言模型底层架构——的种种细节。 |

| Compared to attention, the computations in the MLPs are relatively simple, consisting essentially of a pair of matrix multiplications with a simple function in between. | 与注意力相比，MLP 里的计算相对简单：本质上就是两次矩阵乘法，中间夹一个简单的函数。 |

| Our main goal in this lesson will be to step through the computations and make them memorable, but we'll do it in the context of showing a specific example of how one of these blocks could store a concrete fact. | 本课的主要目标，是把这套计算一步步走一遍，让它们变得好记；我们会借着一个具体例子来做这件事——看看其中一个块如何存储一条具体的事实。 |

## 前情回顾（Recap）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| In the previous chapters, we've been studying a model that's trained to take in a piece of text and predict what text comes next. | 在前几章里，我们一直在研究这样一种模型：它被训练来读入一段文本，并预测接下来会出现什么文本。 |
| First, the input text is broken up into a bunch of **tokens**, which are little chunks of the initial input text, typically words or little pieces of words. | 首先，输入文本会被切成一堆**词元（tokens）**——也就是初始输入文本的小碎块，通常是一个个词，或是词的小碎片。 |
| Then each token is associated with a high-dimensional vector, which is to say a long list of numbers, called its **embedding**. | 随后，每个词元都会关联上一个高维向量（high-dimensional vector）——说白了就是一个长长的数字列表——称为它的**嵌入（embedding）**。 |
| This sequence of vectors is then repeatedly passed through two kinds of operations, the first being the attention, which allows the vectors to pass information between one another, followed by the multilayer perceptron, the thing that we're going to focus on today. | 这串向量接着会被反复送过两类运算：第一类是注意力（attention），让向量彼此之间传递信息；紧随其后的是多层感知机——正是我们今天要聚焦的主角。 |
| There is also a certain normalization step in between, but we'll leave that for another lesson. | 两者之间还夹着某个归一化（normalization）步骤，不过我们把它留给以后的一课。 |
| After this sequence of vectors has flowed through _many, many_ different iterations of both of these blocks, the hope is that each vector has soaked up enough information, both from the context as well as from the general knowledge that was encoded into the model's weights through training, that it can be used to make a prediction of what token comes next. | 当这串向量流经这两种块的 _许多许多_ 次迭代之后，理想情况是：每个向量都已吸饱了足够的信息——既来自上下文，也来自通过训练编码进模型权重里的一般知识——足以用它来预测下一个词元是什么。 |
| As we dive into this MLP block, one of the key ideas that we **need** to have in mind is that all of these vectors live in a very, very high-dimensional space. | 在深入这个 MLP 块之前，有一个**必须**记在心里的关键思想：所有这些向量都生活在一个非常、非常高维的空间里。 |
| And when we think about this space, we need to conceptualize that different directions can encode different kinds of meaning. | 而当我们思考这个空间时，需要建立起这样一种观念：不同的方向，可以编码不同种类的含义。 |
| A very classic example is how if the embedding of _man_ is subtracted from the embedding of _woman_, and then that little step is added to another masculine noun, like uncle, it will lead to a vector that is very close to the corresponding feminine noun. | 一个非常经典的例子是：如果把 _man_ 的嵌入减去 _woman_ 的嵌入，再把这一小步加到另一个阳性名词上——比如 uncle——得到的向量会非常接近与之对应的阴性名词。 |
| In this case, this particular direction would encode _gender information_. | 在这个例子里，这个特定方向编码的就是 _性别信息（gender information）_。 |
| However, in the context of a transformer, these vectors don't merely encode the meaning of a single word. | 然而，在 transformer 的语境下，这些向量编码的绝不只是单个词的含义。 |
| As they flow through the network, they imbibe a much richer meaning based on all the context around them as well as the model's knowledge. | 随着它们流过网络，它们会汲取一种丰富得多的含义——依据是它们周围的全部上下文，以及模型自身的知识。 |
| Ultimately, each vector needs to encode something far, far beyond the meaning of a single word, since it needs to be sufficient to predict what will come next. | 归根结底，每个向量需要编码的东西，远远、远远超出单个词的含义——因为它必须足以支撑对「接下来会是什么」的预测。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 前情回顾
> - **token** /ˈtəʊkən/ —— 词元。LLM 处理文本的最小单位，可以是词或词的碎片。
> - **embedding** /ɪmˈbedɪŋ/ —— 嵌入（向量）。token 映射成向量的结果。
> - *soak up* —— 吸收、吸饱。soak up enough information 吸足信息，画面感很强。
> - **iteration** /ˌɪtəˈreɪʃn/ —— 迭代、一轮重复。many iterations of these blocks。
> - **conceptualize** /kənˈseptʃuəlaɪz/ —— 在头脑中建立概念、从概念上把握。
> - *subtract A from B* —— 从 B 中减去 A。注意语序，别读反。
> - **imbibe** /ɪmˈbaɪb/ —— 喝下、汲取。imbibe a richer meaning 汲取更丰富的含义，书面语。
> - **masculine** /ˈmæskjəlɪn/ —— 阳性的；反义 feminine /ˈfemənɪn/ 阴性的（语法性别）。

## 假设（Assumptions）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| In the last chapter, we've already covered how attention blocks let us incorporate context, but a majority of the model parameters live inside the MLP blocks. | 上一章我们已经讲过，注意力块如何让我们纳入上下文；但模型的多数参数，其实住在 MLP 块里。 |
| A common thought for what these MLP blocks might be doing is that they offer _an extra capacity to store facts_. | 关于这些 MLP 块可能在做什么，一个常见的猜想是：它们提供了 _存储事实的额外容量_。 |
| Before we dive into this example of how exactly the MLP could store the fact that Michael Jordan plays basketball, we're going to have to make a couple of assumptions about the high-dimensional space that we're working in. | 在深入「MLP 究竟如何存储迈克尔·乔丹打篮球这条事实」这个例子之前，我们得先对所处的高维空间做几条假设。 |
| First, we'll suppose that one of the directions in this high-dimension space represents the idea of a first name _Michael_; another nearly perpendicular direction represents the idea of the last name _Jordan_; and a third, also nearly perpendicular, direction that represents the idea of _basketball_: | 首先，我们假设这个高维空间中的某个方向代表名（first name）_Michael_ 这一概念；另一个近乎垂直的方向代表姓（last name）_Jordan_；还有第三个方向——同样近乎垂直——代表 _basketball_（篮球）这个概念： |
| What this means is that, if we looked into the network and took out one of the vectors being processed, the dot product between one of the directions in this high-dimensional space and the vector will represent how well it aligns with the direction. | 这意味着：假如我们钻进网络、取出一个正在被处理的向量，那么高维空间中某个方向与这个向量的点积（dot product），就代表它跟这个方向对齐（align）的程度。 |
| For example, if the dot product of the direction _Michael_ and the vector is one, that would mean the vector encodes the idea of a person with the first name Michael. | 举例来说，如果方向 _Michael_ 与该向量的点积为 1，就说明这个向量编码了「某人名叫 Michael」这一概念。 |
| Otherwise, if the dot product were zero or negative, it would mean the vector doesn't really align with the _Michael_ direction. | 反之，如果点积为 0 或负数，就说明这个向量与 _Michael_ 方向并不真正对齐。 |
| This $\vec{E}$ vector aligns with the _Micheal_ direction | 这个 $\vec{E}$ 向量与 _Micheal_ 方向对齐 |
| This $\vec{E}$ vector doesn't align with the _Micheal_ direction | 这个 $\vec{E}$ 向量与 _Micheal_ 方向不对齐 |
| Similarly, its dot product with the other directions _Jordan_ or _basketball_ would tell you how well it aligns with those directions. | 类似地，它与 _Jordan_、_basketball_ 这两个方向的点积，则告诉你它与这些方向的对齐程度。 |
| For simplicity, we'll completely ignore the very reasonable question of what it might mean if that dot product was bigger than one. | 为简单起见，「如果那个点积大于 1 会是什么意思」——这个非常合理的问题，我们就完全置之不理了。 |
| Now, let's think of a vector that is meant to represent the full name, Michael Jordan. | 现在，来想一个打算用来表示全名 Michael Jordan 的向量。 |
| Its dot product with both the directions _Michael_ and _Jordan_ would have to be one. | 它与 _Michael_、_Jordan_ 两个方向的点积都必须是 1。 |
| Since the text Michael Jordan spans two different tokens, _Michael_ and _Jordan_, this would also mean we have to assume that an earlier attention block has successfully passed information to the second of these two vectors, Jordan, to ensure that it can encode both names. | 由于文本 Michael Jordan 横跨两个不同的词元——_Michael_ 与 _Jordan_——这也意味着我们必须假设：更早的一个注意力块已成功地把信息传递给了这两个向量中的第二个（Jordan），以确保它能同时编码两个名字。 |
| With all that as the assumptions, let's now dive into the core of the lesson. What happens inside a multilayer perceptron? | 以上就是全部前提假设。现在来进入本课的核心：多层感知机内部究竟会发生什么？ |

## MLP 内部（Inside an MLP）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| When entering the MLP block, each vector that was originally associated with one of the tokens from the input text is going to go through a short series of operations that will result in another vector of the same dimension. | 进入 MLP 块时，原本与输入文本中某个词元相关联的每一个向量，都会经过一小串运算，其结果是得到另一个同维度的向量。 |
| This sequence of operations is applied to every vector in the sequence and all happens in parallel. | 这串运算会施加给序列里的每一个向量，而且全部并行发生。 |
| We'll go over these operations in a moment, but after the operations, that resulting vector will be added to the original vector that flowed in, and that sum will be the result flowing out of the MLP. | 这些运算我们马上细讲；运算过后，所得的向量会加到流入的原始向量上，这个和就是流出 MLP 的结果。 |
| What this means is that the vectors don't exchange information with each other in this step; they're all kind of doing their own thing. | 这意味着：在这一步里，向量们彼此不交换信息，都在各干各的。 |
| For us, this actually makes it a lot simpler to understand, as understanding what happens to just one of the vectors through this block will effectively help us understand what happens to all the vectors. | 对我们来说，这反而让事情好懂得多：只要弄懂其中一个向量在这个块里经历了什么，实际上就等于弄懂了所有向量。 |
| This sequence of operations is applied to every vector in the sequence and all happens in parallel. | 这串运算会施加给序列里的每一个向量，而且全部并行发生。 |
| Vectors that pass through the MLP go through the operation in parallel, and they do not communicate and exchange information with each other. | 穿过 MLP 的向量是并行地经历这套运算的，它们互相之间不通信、不交换信息。 |
| When we say that this MLP block is going to encode the fact that Michael Jordan plays basketball, what we mean is that, if a vector that encodes the first name Michael and last name Jordan flows in the block, then we hope that the sequence of computations that exist in this block will produce an output, one that includes the direction basketball, to be added on to the vector encoding Michael Jordan. | 当我们说这个 MLP 块要编码「迈克尔·乔丹打篮球」这条事实时，意思就是：如果一个编码了名 Michael、姓 Jordan 的向量流进这个块，那么我们希望，这个块里存在的那一串计算会产出一个输出——一个包含 basketball 方向的输出——把它加到那个编码 Michael Jordan 的向量上去。 |

> - **dimension** /daɪˈmenʃn/ —— 维度。a vector of the same dimension 同维度的向量。
> - *in parallel* —— 并行地。计算语境的高频搭配。
> - *result in ...* —— 导致、产生（某结果）。
> - *go over ...* —— 过一遍、细讲。We'll go over these operations in a moment。
> - *exchange information* —— 交换信息。
> - *do one's own thing* —— 各干各的、自顾自。口语。
> - **effectively** /ɪˈfektɪvli/ —— 实际上、等同于。will effectively help 实际上就等于帮……。
> - *add on to ...* —— 附加到……之上。

## 第一步（Step 1）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| The first step of this MLP process looks like multiplying the vector that flowed into the MLP block by a very big matrix, which is no surprise when studying deep learning. | MLP 过程的第一步，看上去是把流入 MLP 块的向量乘以一个非常大的矩阵——学深度学习，这早已见怪不怪。 |
| This matrix, like all of the other ones we've covered, is filled with model parameters that are learned from data. | 这个矩阵和我们讲过的所有矩阵一样，塞满了从数据中学来的模型参数。 |
| These parameters can be thought of as a bunch of knobs and dials that get tweaked and tuned to determine what the model behavior is. | 这些参数可以想成一大堆旋钮和刻度盘，经过反复拧动调校，最终决定了模型的行为。 |
| A nice way to think about matrix multiplication is to imagine each row of that matrix as being its own vector and taking a bunch of dot products between those rows and the vector being processed, which we'll label as $\vec{E}$. | 思考矩阵乘法的一个好方式：把矩阵的每一行想象成它自己就是一个向量，然后在这些行与正在被处理的向量之间做一串点积；被处理的向量，我们记作 $\vec{E}$。 |
| For our example, let's suppose that the very first row happened to equal this _first name Michael_ direction that we're presuming exists. | 就我们的例子来说，不妨假设第一行恰好等于我们预设存在的 _first name Michael_ 方向。 |
| That would mean that the first component in this output, the dot product $R_0 \cdot \vec{E}$, would equal **one** if that vector encodes the first name Michael, and **zero or negative** otherwise. | 那就意味着：输出中的第一个分量，也就是点积 $R_0 \cdot \vec{E}$，若该向量编码了名 Michael，就等于 **1**；否则为 **0 或负数**。 |
| Even better, take a moment to think about what it would mean if that first row was this _first name Michael_ plus _last name Jordan_ direction, which for simplicity we'll write as $\vec{M}+\vec{J}$. | 更妙的是，不妨花点时间想想：如果第一行是 _first name Michael_ 加上 _last name Jordan_ 的方向——为简便起见，我们把它写作 $\vec{M}+\vec{J}$——那又意味着什么？ |

|---|---|
| The next step in this MLP process looks very similar to the first one. The vector leaving the ReLU would be multiplied by a very large matrix, followed by adding a certain bias vector. | MLP 过程的下一步，看上去和第一步非常像：离开 ReLU 的向量会乘上一个非常大的矩阵，随后再加上某个偏置向量。 |

| Another way to conceptually think of matrix multiplication is by taking each column of the matrix and multiplying it by the corresponding term in the vector that it's processing, and then adding together all of those rescaled columns. | 从概念上理解矩阵乘法还有另一种方式：取出矩阵的每一列，乘上它正在处理的向量中对应的分量，再把所有这些重新缩放过的列加总起来。 |

| For instance, let's imagine that the model has learned to make that first column of this matrix into this _basketball_ direction that we suppose exists. | 举例来说，不妨想象模型已经学会了把这个矩阵的第一列做成我们假设存在的 _basketball_ 方向。 |
| What that would mean is that when the relevant neuron in that first position is active, we would be adding this _Basketball_ column to the final result. | 这意味着：当第一个位置上的相关神经元处于激活状态时，我们就会把这一 _Basketball_ 列加进最终结果。 |
| But if that neuron was inactive, if that number was zero, then this would have no effect. | 而如果那个神经元未激活——也就是那个数为 0——它就不会产生任何影响。 |
| It doesn't just have to be basketball. ## 叠加（Superposition）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| The basic idea is that if you have an n-dimensional space and you want to represent a bunch of different features using directions that are all perpendicular to one another in that space (that way if you add a component in one direction it won't influence any of the other directions) then the maximum number of vectors you can fit is only n, the number of dimensions. | 基本思想是：假如你有一个 n 维空间，想用「在这个空间里彼此全部垂直」的方向来表示一堆不同的特征（这样，你在某个方向上添加分量时，不会影响其他任何方向），那么你能装下的向量最多只有 n 个——也就是维度的数目。 |
| To a mathematician this is the definition of dimension! | 在数学家看来，这就是维度的定义！ |
| However, what if you relax that constraint a little bit and tolerate some noise? | 不过，如果你把这条约束稍稍放宽、容忍一点噪声呢？ |
| Say you allow those features to be represented by vectors that aren't exactly perpendicular; they're just nearly perpendicular, maybe between _85_ and _95_ degrees apart. | 比如说，允许这些特征由并不严格垂直的向量来表示——只是近乎垂直，彼此夹角大概在 _85_ 到 _95_ 度之间。 |
| For smaller dimensions, this hardly gives you any extra wiggle room to fit more vectors in, which makes it all the more counterintuitive that, for higher dimensions, the answer changes dramatically. | 在低维度下，这几乎不给任何额外的腾挪空间去多塞向量——这也使得高维情形更加反直觉：到了更高维度，答案会发生剧变。 |
| In general, a consequence of something known as the **Johnson-Lindenstrauss lemma** is that the number of vectors that can be crammed into a space that are _nearly perpendicular_ grows exponentially with the number of dimensions. | 一般而言，有一个叫**约翰逊–林登施特劳斯引理（Johnson-Lindenstrauss lemma）**的东西，它的一个推论是：能够塞进一个空间、且 _近乎垂直_ 的向量数量，会随维度数指数级增长。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 叠加
> - **relax** /rɪˈlæks/ —— 放宽（约束、规则）。relax that constraint 把那条约束放宽。
> - **tolerate** /ˈtɒləreɪt/ —— 容忍、容许（误差、噪声）。
> - *wiggle room* —— 腾挪空间、回旋余地。口语习语。
> - **counterintuitive** /ˌkaʊntərɪnˈtjuːɪtɪv/ —— 反直觉的。counter-（相反）+ intuitive（直觉的）。
> - **dramatically** /drəˈmætɪkli/ —— 剧烈地、戏剧性地。changes dramatically 发生剧变。
> - **lemma** /ˈlemə/ —— 引理（比定理低一级的数学命题）。
> - **cram** /kræm/ —— 把……硬塞进。cram vectors into a space 把向量塞进空间。
> - **exponentially** /ˌekspəˈnenʃəli/ —— 指数级地。grows exponentially 指数级增长。

| The broader point made at that point in the video is true, though, which is that the number of nearly orthogonal vectors you can cram in will scale exponentially. | 不过，视频里那一刻想表达的更宏观的要点是对的：你能塞进去的近正交向量的数量，会以指数级增长。 |
| But when that makes for an appreciable effect is an interesting question. The viewer Nick Yoder wrote in with more explorations on this, with these as a few highlights: | 但这个效应从什么时候开始变得显著，是个有趣的问题。观众 Nick Yoder 写信分享了他对此更多的探究，以下是几个亮点： |
| 1. Forcing pairs of vectors into the range (89°, 91°) is rather tight. At this angle, things don't get interesting until dimensions > 250,000 | 1. 把向量对强行压进 (89°, 91°) 这个范围相当苛刻。在这个夹角下，要到维度超过 25 万，事情才开始变得有趣。 |

| 3. 85 degrees and 12,288 dimensions (GPT3 embedding space) holds well over 40 Billion vectors (likely more than $10^{17}$) | 3. 85 度加 12288 维（GPT-3 的嵌入空间）能容纳远超 400 亿个向量（很可能超过 $10^{17}$）。 |
| 4. 85 degrees and 116,000 dimensions (within reason for GPT4, GPT-o1, -o3 or similar newer models) holds well over a Googol ($10^{100}$) vectors and likely over $10^{160}$ | 4. 85 度加 116000 维（对 GPT-4、GPT-o1、GPT-o3 或类似的新模型来说是合理量级）能容纳远超一个古戈尔（$10^{100}$）的向量，甚至很可能超过 $10^{160}$。 |
| This point is very significant for large language models, which benefit from associating independent ideas with nearly perpendicular directions. | 这一点对大语言模型意义重大：它们受益于「把相互独立的概念关联到近乎垂直的方向上」。 |
| It means that it's possible for the models to store many, many more ideas than there are dimensions in the space that it's allotted. | 这意味着，模型能存储的概念，可以比分配到的空间维度多出许多许多倍。 |
| This might explain why model performance seems to scale so well with size. | 这或许能解释：为什么模型性能随规模扩大而提升得如此之好。 |
| A space that has 10 times as many dimensions can store way, way more than 10 times as many independent ideas. | 一个维度数是 10 倍的空间，能存储的独立概念远远不止 10 倍。 |
| And this is relevant not just to the embedding space of the model, but also to that vector full of neurons in the middle of that multilayer perceptron that we just studied. | 这不仅与模型的嵌入空间有关，也与我们刚研究过的、多层感知机中间那个塞满神经元的向量有关。 |
| In our case, at the sizes of GPT-3, it might not just be probing at 50,000 features. | 就我们的情形而言，以 GPT-3 的规模，它探查的可能就不只是 5 万个特征。 |
| If it instead leveraged this enormous added capacity by using nearly perpendicular directions of the space, it could be probing at orders of magnitude more features of the vector being processed. | 如果它转而利用近垂直方向带来的这份巨大的额外容量，那么它探查的被处理向量的特征，可能会多出好几个数量级。 |
| In doing so, it means that individual features aren't going to be visible as a single neuron lighting up. | 这样一来就意味着：单个特征将不会以「某一个神经元亮起」的形式显形。 |
| It would have to look like some specific combination of neurons instead, hence **a superposition**. | 它只能表现为某种特定的神经元组合——故名**叠加（superposition）**。 |
| In general, a consequence of something known as the Johnson-Lindenstrauss lemma is that the number of vectors that can be crammed into a space that are nearly perpendicular grows exponentially with the number of dimensions. | 一般而言，有一个叫约翰逊–林登施特劳斯引理（Johnson-Lindenstrauss lemma）的结论，它的一个推论是：能够塞进一个空间、且近乎垂直的向量数量，会随维度数指数级增长。 |
