# 17 庆典准备与成长挑战

> [!abstract] 文章信息
> - **系列**：及时雨高考单词 · 重复阅读短文（17/30）
> - **词汇来源**：《及时雨高考英语词汇手册》
> - **本文重点词**：celebrate · ceremony · challenge · champion · characteristic · charity · charm
> - **说明**：左栏原文、右栏译文逐段对照；各节末尾附词汇注释，文末有总词汇表。重点词在原文中以下划线标注并有意高频重复。

## 一、阅读月：重复成就记忆

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| During reading month at school, I began a long reading practice around a reading festival becomes a challenge for shy students. At first I did not fully understand why <u>celebrate</u>, <u>ceremony</u>, and <u>challenge</u> were repeated so often, but the language teacher said that repeated words become remembered words. The teacher explained that if one article is too short, a student may see a word once and then lose it. If one article is longer, the same word can return in a new line, a new detail, and a new feeling. So our class started a longer passage built around preparing a ceremony, sharing books, and leading charity work. In that passage, the key words did not appear once. They appeared again and again until the page itself became a place of review. | 在学校读书月期间，我开启了一项长期的阅读练习，主题是一场阅读节如何成为害羞学生的挑战。起初我不太明白为什么 <u>celebrate</u>、<u>ceremony</u> 和 <u>challenge</u> 这几个词反复出现，但语言老师说：重复的单词，才会变成记得住的单词。老师解释说，如果一篇文章太短，一个词可能只被看见一次，然后就丢掉了；如果文章更长，同一个词就能在新的一行、新的细节、新的感受里再次回来。于是我们班开始学习一篇更长的篇章，围绕筹备一场典礼、分享图书和主持慈善活动展开。在那篇文章里，重点词不是出现一次，而是一次又一次地出现，直到这一页纸本身变成一个复习的场所。 |
| On the first day, I copied <u>celebrate</u> into my notebook several times and read <u>celebrate</u> aloud several times. Then I did the same with <u>ceremony</u> and <u>challenge</u>. This was not empty copying. Every time I wrote <u>celebrate</u>, I linked it to the main scene. Every time I wrote <u>ceremony</u>, I linked it to a clear action. Every time I wrote <u>challenge</u>, I linked it to a standard that could be checked. When I went home, I met <u>celebrate</u>, <u>ceremony</u>, and <u>challenge</u> again in the original passage, in my notes, and in the questions from class. That early repetition felt slow, but it began to give the words weight. | 第一天，我把 <u>celebrate</u> 抄进笔记本好几遍，又大声朗读了好几遍。然后对 <u>ceremony</u> 和 <u>challenge</u> 也如法炮制。这不是空洞的抄写。每写一次 <u>celebrate</u>，我就把它联系到主场景；每写一次 <u>ceremony</u>，我就把它联系到一个清晰的行动；每写一次 <u>challenge</u>，我就把它联系到一条可以检验的标准。回到家后，我又在原文、笔记和课堂练习题里遇见了 <u>celebrate</u>、<u>ceremony</u> 和 <u>challenge</u>。这种早期的重复感觉很慢，却开始让这些词有了分量。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 一
> - **celebrate** /ˈselɪbreɪt/ —— 庆祝。celebrate a festival / a victory 庆祝节日 / 庆祝胜利；名词形式 celebration。
> - **ceremony** /ˈserəməni/ —— 仪式，典礼。hold / attend a ceremony 举行 / 参加典礼；opening ceremony 开幕式。
> - **challenge** /ˈtʃælɪndʒ/ —— 挑战（名词 / 动词）。face / take on a challenge 面对 / 接受挑战；challenging 形容词「有挑战性的」。
> - *a place of review* —— 复习的场所。把「一页纸」比作复习发生的地方，呼应全文「重复」的主题。

## 二、慢下来：看见词与词的联系

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| The second stage became more demanding because students fear speaking before others. I wanted to move quickly, but the language teacher asked us to slow down and notice how <u>champion</u> worked with <u>characteristic</u> and how <u>charity</u> changed the whole situation. At first I thought one reading would be enough. It was not enough. On the first reading, I only saw the main story. On the second reading, I finally saw where <u>champion</u> was needed. On the third reading, I noticed that <u>characteristic</u> did not stand alone; it led toward <u>charity</u>. Repetition made these links visible. Without repetition, the links would have stayed hidden. | 第二个阶段变得更加吃力，因为学生们害怕在别人面前发言。我想快点往前赶，语言老师却让我们慢下来，去留意 <u>champion</u> 如何与 <u>characteristic</u> 搭配、<u>charity</u> 如何改变整个局面。起初我以为读一遍就够了，其实远远不够。第一遍我只看到了故事主线；第二遍我终于看清 <u>champion</u> 该用在哪儿；第三遍我发现 <u>characteristic</u> 从不孤立出现，它指向 <u>charity</u>。重复让这些联系显形；没有重复，这些联系就会一直隐藏着。 |
| A few days later, the key words began to feel less cold. <u>celebrate</u> no longer looked like a word from a distant list. <u>celebrate</u> had a place in the passage, a tone in my voice, and a role in my memory. The same thing happened to <u>ceremony</u> and <u>challenge</u>. Because the passage was long, I met these words in calm description, in a small problem, in a teacher’s reminder, and in my own reflection. That variety mattered. Repetition is useful, but repetition inside changing sentences is even more useful. It lets a learner understand not only the word itself, but also how the word behaves when the meaning becomes deeper. | 几天之后，重点词开始不再冷冰冰。<u>celebrate</u> 不再像遥远词表里的一个词：它在文章里有位置，在我的声音里有语气，在我的记忆里有角色。<u>ceremony</u> 和 <u>challenge</u> 也是一样。因为文章足够长，我是在平静的叙述里、在小问题里、在老师的提醒里、在我自己的反思里遇见这些词的。这种多样性很重要。重复有用，但在不断变化的句子里重复更有用——它让学习者不仅理解这个词本身，还理解当含义变深时，这个词会如何表现。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 二
> - **champion** /ˈtʃæmpiən/ —— 冠军；优秀者。win the championship 赢得冠军；champion 也可作动词，表示「拥护、为……辩护」。
> - **characteristic** /ˌkærəktəˈrɪstɪk/ —— 特征（名词）；典型的（形容词）。be characteristic of... 是……的典型特征。
> - **charity** /ˈtʃærəti/ —— 慈善；慈善机构。charity work 慈善工作；donate money to charity 向慈善机构捐款。
> - *make these links visible / stay hidden* —— 让联系显形 / 一直隐藏。本节的一对反义表达。

## 三、复述：从「认识」到「会用」

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Our teacher then added a speaking round. We had to close the book and retell the passage with the same key words. When I retold the first half, I made sure to use <u>celebrate</u>, <u>ceremony</u>, and <u>challenge</u> again. When I retold the second half, I used <u>champion</u>, <u>characteristic</u>, and <u>charity</u> again. At first the retelling was not smooth, but that difficulty was useful. It showed me which words I had only recognized and which words I could really use. After two more rounds, the retelling became stronger. The repeated article helped me because the same structure returned each time, and the same words returned inside that structure. | 老师随后加了一轮口语练习：我们要合上书，用同样的重点词复述课文。复述前半部分时，我特意再把 <u>celebrate</u>、<u>ceremony</u> 和 <u>challenge</u> 用了一遍；复述后半部分时，又用了一遍 <u>champion</u>、<u>characteristic</u> 和 <u>charity</u>。起初复述得并不顺畅，但这种困难是有用的——它让我看清，哪些词我只是认识，哪些词是我真正能用的。再多练两轮之后，复述变得熟练起来。那篇反复读的文章帮了我：同样的结构每次都会回来，同样的词也在这个结构里一次次回来。 |
| I also noticed that repeated reading changed my confidence in class. Before this practice, I often understood a word for one minute and forgot it the next day. After this practice, I could explain why <u>celebrate</u> mattered in the scene, why <u>ceremony</u> remained central, and why <u>challenge</u> was not just decoration. I could also explain how <u>champion</u> supported <u>characteristic</u> and how <u>charity</u> marked an important turning point. Because the passage was long enough, I was not memorizing a disconnected list. I was following one complete line of thought. That line of thought gave each repeated word a stable home. | 我还注意到，重复阅读改变了我在课堂上的信心。在这项练习之前，我常常是一分钟认识了一个词，第二天就忘了；练习之后，我能讲清楚 <u>celebrate</u> 为什么在这个场景里重要、<u>ceremony</u> 为什么始终处于中心、<u>challenge</u> 为什么不只是装饰。我还能讲清楚 <u>champion</u> 如何支撑 <u>characteristic</u>、<u>charity</u> 如何标志一个重要的转折点。因为文章足够长，我记住的不是一张互不相干的清单，而是沿着一条完整的思路走下来。这条思路让每个反复出现的词都有一个稳定的家。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 三
> - 本节不引入新重点词，六个已学词在不同句式里复现——注意观察它们的新搭配。
> - *retell* —— 复述。re-（再）+ tell；同前缀高频词：replay、rewrite、review。
> - *not just decoration* —— 不只是装饰。decoration 由 decorate（装饰）派生，意为「点缀品」。
> - *a stable home* —— 稳定的家。把「记忆中的位置」具象化，写作可借用。

## 四、复习循环：让单词自然回归

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| At home, I continued with a simple review cycle. In the morning, I read the article once for general meaning. At noon, I read it again and marked every line containing <u>celebrate</u> or <u>ceremony</u>. In the evening, I read it once more and focused on <u>challenge</u>, <u>champion</u>, and <u>characteristic</u>. On the next day, I covered the Chinese meanings and tried to understand the page only from context. This long cycle taught me something important. A longer article gives repeated words enough room to breathe. A longer article lets <u>celebrate</u> return naturally, lets <u>ceremony</u> return naturally, and lets <u>challenge</u> return naturally until the reader stops feeling surprise and starts feeling familiarity. | 在家里，我继续一个简单的复习循环：早上通读一遍，把握大意；中午再读一遍，标出每一个含 <u>celebrate</u> 或 <u>ceremony</u> 的句子；晚上再读一遍，集中攻克 <u>challenge</u>、<u>champion</u> 和 <u>characteristic</u>。第二天，我盖住中文意思，试着只靠上下文读懂这一页。这个长循环教会我一件重要的事：更长的文章给反复出现的词足够的呼吸空间。它让 <u>celebrate</u> 自然地回来，让 <u>ceremony</u> 自然地回来，让 <u>challenge</u> 自然地回来，直到读者不再感到陌生，而是开始感到熟悉。 |
| Another advantage of the long passage was emotional memory. Because the text stayed with one clear situation, I could remember not only the meaning of <u>celebrate</u> but also the feeling around <u>celebrate</u>. I remembered when <u>ceremony</u> became necessary and when <u>challenge</u> became useful. I remembered how <u>champion</u> led to <u>characteristic</u> and how <u>charity</u> helped solve the harder part. Emotional memory is not magic. It grows when the same words appear in a meaningful sequence. That is why repeated reading worked so much better than a quick word list for me. The article gave the words a story, and the story gave the words force. | 长文章的另一个好处是情绪记忆。因为全文始终停留在一个清晰的情境里，我记住的不只是 <u>celebrate</u> 的意思，还有 <u>celebrate</u> 周围的氛围：我记得 <u>ceremony</u> 是什么时候变得必要的，<u>challenge</u> 是什么时候变得有用的；我记得 <u>champion</u> 如何引出 <u>characteristic</u>，<u>charity</u> 如何帮助解决更难的部分。情绪记忆不是魔法，它生长在「同样的词出现在有意义的先后顺序里」的时候。这就是为什么对我来说，反复阅读比快速过一遍词表有效得多：文章给了这些词一个故事，故事又给了这些词力量。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 四
> - *review cycle* —— 复习循环。cycle 与文中的 order（顺序）、stage（阶段）构成「流程」词汇场。
> - *cover the Chinese meanings* —— 盖住中文意思。cover 熟词生义：遮住、盖住。
> - *give ... enough room to breathe* —— 给……足够的呼吸空间，即「留出余地」。
> - *emotional memory / a meaningful sequence* —— 情绪记忆 / 有意义的先后顺序。本节解释「为什么重复阅读有效」。

## 五、收尾：服务与阅读让自信看得见

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| By the end of the practice cycle, the result was clear: service and reading make confidence visible. I still could not say that every line was easy, but I could say that the words were no longer strangers. <u>celebrate</u>, <u>ceremony</u>, and <u>challenge</u> had become active parts of one usable memory. <u>champion</u>, <u>characteristic</u>, and <u>charity</u> had become tools rather than decorations. When the teacher asked what helped most, I did not mention speed or clever tricks. I mentioned time, repetition, and context. I mentioned seeing the same word many times until it stayed in mind without force. That was the change I wanted from the beginning. | 到练习周期结束时，结果已经很清楚：服务与阅读让自信变得看得见。我还是不敢说每一行都轻松，但我敢说这些词不再是陌生人。<u>celebrate</u>、<u>ceremony</u> 和 <u>challenge</u> 已经成为一段可用的记忆里活跃的组成部分；<u>champion</u>、<u>characteristic</u> 和 <u>charity</u> 已经成为工具，而不是摆设。当老师问什么帮助最大时，我没有提速度，也没有提什么聪明的技巧；我提到的是时间、重复和语境——是看着同一个词出现很多次，直到它毫不费力地留在脑海里。这就是我从一开始就想要的改变。 |
| Now, when I review this article again, I still follow the same order. First, I read for general meaning. Second, I read only for the repeated key words. Third, I retell the main part and use <u>celebrate</u>, <u>ceremony</u>, <u>challenge</u>, <u>champion</u>, and <u>characteristic</u> on purpose. Finally, I try to write two new sentences with <u>charity</u> and <u>charm</u>. This final step shows whether repetition has become real knowledge. For me, the answer is often yes, and that is why I wanted a longer article rather than a short one. A longer article offers more returns, more reminders, and more chances for a difficult word to stay long enough to become familiar. | 现在，当我再次复习这篇文章时，仍然按同样的顺序：第一遍读大意；第二遍只读反复出现的重点词；第三遍复述主体部分，并有意识地用上 <u>celebrate</u>、<u>ceremony</u>、<u>challenge</u>、<u>champion</u> 和 <u>characteristic</u>；最后，试着用 <u>charity</u> 和 <u>charm</u> 各写两个新句子。最后这一步能检验重复是否已经变成真正的知识。对我来说，答案常常是肯定的——这也正是当初我想要一篇长文章而不是短文章的原因：长文章提供更多的回报、更多的提醒，也给一个难词更多停留的时间，让它来得及变得熟悉。 |
| In my final repetition note, I circled <u>charm</u>, read <u>charm</u> aloud, wrote <u>charm</u> in a new sentence, and reviewed <u>charm</u> once more before sleep. | 在最后的重复笔记里，我把 <u>charm</u> 圈出来，大声读一遍，用它造一个新句子，睡前再复习一遍。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 五
> - **charm** /tʃɑːm/ —— 魅力；使着迷（动词）。a woman of great charm 颇有魅力的女子；charming 形容词「迷人的」。
> - *service and reading make confidence visible* —— 服务与阅读让自信变得看得见。make + 宾语 + 形容词的使役结构。
> - *tools rather than decorations* —— 是工具，不是摆设。rather than 表取舍，写作高频。
