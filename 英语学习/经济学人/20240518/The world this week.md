# The world this week · 商业：经济学人双语精读

> [!abstract] 文章信息
> - **标题**：Business（商业）——“The world this week” 一周要闻
> - **出处**：The Economist · The world this week · 2024-05-16（印刷版 2024-05-18）
> - **主题**：对华关税、英美资源拆分、GPT-4o、腾讯与软银财报、美日英增长、迷因股回潮、树莓派上市
> - **说明**：左栏原文、右栏译文逐段对照；原栏目按地域与主题编排，这里按原文顺序分六节，各节附词汇注释，文末有总词汇表。

## 贸易：对华关税再加码

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Joe Biden imposed another round of tariffs on Chinese exports to America, most notably increasing the duties on **electric vehicles** to 100%. America imports relatively few Chinese EVs but Mr Biden suggested his steps were pre-emptive. Chinese carmakers are expanding in Europe, but “We’re not going to let China flood our market,” said the president, with one eye on votes in Michigan, the centre of America’s car industry and a crucial state in November’s election. Tariffs are also going up on semiconductors, solar cells and critical minerals. A furious China described the decision as “political manipulation” and vowed to retaliate. | 乔·拜登对中国输美商品加征新一轮关税，最引人注目的是把**电动汽车**的关税提高到 100%。美国进口的中国电动车其实不多，但拜登表示此举是先发制人。中国车企正在欧洲扩张，“我们不会让中国淹没我们的市场，”总统说——说这话时他一只眼睛盯着密歇根州的选票：那里是美国汽车工业的中心，也是 11 月大选的关键州。半导体、太阳能电池和关键矿产的关税也在上调。愤怒的中国称这一决定是“政治操纵”，并誓言报复。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 关税
> - **tariff** /ˈtærɪf/ —— 关税。impose/raise/cut tariffs on sth，报道贸易战的必备词。
> - **duty** /ˈdjuːti/ —— 税、关税（常用复数 duties）。与 tariff 近义，duty 更强调“应缴税款”。
> - **pre-emptive** /prɪˈemptɪv/ —— 先发制人的、预防性的。pre-empt = 抢先行动。
> - **flood the market** —— 向市场大量倾销、淹没市场。flood 作动词表“涌入、泛滥”。
> - **with one eye on** —— 一边做事一边惦记着……。本文暗指关税有选举考量，属记者式点评。
> - **manipulation** /məˌnɪpjuˈleɪʃn/ —— 操纵。动词 manipulate；political manipulation 是外交抗议的惯用语。

## 企业与交易：英美资源拆分自保

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| **Anglo American** decided to break up its business to fend off a revised takeover bid from **BHP**, valued at £34bn (\$43bn). Anglo will divest its coking coal assets and spin off its platinum and De Beers diamonds divisions. It is also considering the options for its nickel business in order to focus on copper, iron ore and crop nutrients. The mining giant’s headquarters are in London but it employs 45,000 people in South Africa, where it was founded in 1917; the government there will want a say in any demerger or sale process. | **英美资源集团**决定拆分业务，以抵御**必和必拓**修改后价值 340 亿英镑（430 亿美元）的收购要约。英美资源将剥离炼焦煤资产，并把铂金和戴比尔斯钻石业务分拆出去；它还在评估镍业务的选项，以便专注于铜、铁矿石和作物营养。这家矿业巨头总部在伦敦，但在其 1917 年的创立地南非雇有 4.5 万人；南非政府希望在分拆或出售过程中拥有发言权。 |
| The Brazilian government replaced Jean Paul Prates as chief executive of **Petrobras**, the state oil company, with Magda Chambriard, the former head of the energy regulator. Markets took a dim view. The move will be cheered by left-wing politicians who want Petrobras to lower prices and invest more to create jobs. | 巴西政府撤换国家石油公司**巴西石油**的首席执行官让·保罗·普拉特斯，由前能源监管机构负责人玛格达·尚布里亚尔接任。市场反应消极。此举将受到左翼政客的欢迎——他们希望巴西石油降价，并加大投资以创造就业。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 企业
> - **fend off** —— 挡开、抵御。fend off a takeover bid = 抵御收购，商战报道高频搭配。
> - **divest** /daɪˈvest/ —— 剥离（资产、业务）。反义 acquire；名词 divestment。
> - **spin off** —— 分拆（子公司独立出去）。名词 spin-off，指“衍生公司/副产品”。
> - **coking coal** —— 炼焦煤。coke 是“焦炭”，炼钢用。
> - **crop nutrients** —— 作物营养（指钾肥、磷肥等农化业务）。
> - **have a say in** —— 在……中有发言权。say 作名词表“发言权”，如 have no say。
> - **demerger** /diːˈmɜːdʒə/ —— 分拆、拆分（与 merger 相对）。英式商业术语。
> - **take a dim view of sth** —— 对……不看好、持否定态度。固定表达，比 disapprove 更含蓄。

## AI 与算力：GPT-4o、Project Astra 与碳账

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| The competition accelerated in **artificial-intelligence** services. OpenAI updated its chatbot model to GPT-4o (the “o” stands for omni, or all-encompassing). The latest version is able to interpret voice commands to hold a “conversation”. And Alphabet unveiled Project Astra, an AI assistant that also responds to voice commands. | **人工智能**服务的竞争加速。OpenAI 把聊天机器人模型升级为 GPT-4o（“o”代表 omni，即“全能”）。最新版本能理解语音指令，进行“对话”。Alphabet 则发布了 Project Astra——一个同样能响应语音指令的 AI 助手。 |
| All the energy used to power data centres for AI is causing tech companies’ carbon emissions to rise. **Microsoft** reported a 31% increase in its indirect (Scope 3) emissions last year from building more data centres (including the carbon found in construction materials) as well as from semiconductors, servers and racks. | 为 AI 数据中心供电所耗的能源，正推高科技公司的碳排放。**微软**报告称，去年其间接（范围三）排放增加 31%，原因是新建了更多数据中心（包括建筑材料中的碳），以及半导体、服务器和机架带来的排放。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · AI
> - **all-encompassing** —— 包罗万象的、全面的。encompass = 包含，比 include 更书面。
> - **interpret** /ɪnˈtɜːprɪt/ —— 理解、解读（指令、语言、数据）。interpret voice commands = 解析语音指令。
> - **unveil** /ʌnˈveɪl/ —— 发布、揭幕。原义“揭开面纱”，科技发布会常用。
> - **Scope 3 emissions** —— 范围三排放。温室气体核算体系中的“价值链间接排放”，与 Scope 1（直接）、Scope 2（外购电力）并列。
> - **rack** /ræk/ —— 机架、机柜。服务器机架的 rack 与 rack up（累积）同形不同义。

## 财报：腾讯的游戏与广告

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| **Tencent** announced a sharp increase in quarterly net profit to 42bn yuan (\$5.8bn), boosted by advertising and e-commerce. The Chinese tech giant’s gaming business also showed improvement, which Tencent hopes will be boosted by the forthcoming release in China of “Dungeon & Fighter”, a popular mobile game. | **腾讯**宣布季度净利润大幅增长至 420 亿元人民币（58 亿美元），受广告和电商业务提振。这家中国科技巨头的游戏业务也有所改善，腾讯希望即将在中国上线的热门手游《地下城与勇士》能进一步拉动增长。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 财报
> - **net profit** —— 净利润。对应 gross profit（毛利）、operating profit（营业利润）。
> - **boost** /buːst/ —— 提振、促进。名词/动词同形，boosted by = 受……提振。
> - **forthcoming** /ˌfɔːθˈkʌmɪŋ/ —— 即将到来的、即将发布的。forthcoming release = 即将上线。
> - **mobile game** —— 手游。注意与 console game（主机游戏）、PC game 区分。

## 宏观：美国通胀、日本与英国增长

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| The annual **inflation** rate in America dipped to 3.4% in April, following two consecutive monthly rises. Investors are poring over every detail of the monthly consumer-price indices for clues about when the Federal Reserve might cut interest rates. The slightly lower inflation data delighted markets, propelling the S&P 500, NASDAQ Composite and Dow Jones Industrial Average to record levels. | 美国 4 月年通胀率降至 3.4%，此前已连续两个月上升。投资者仔细研读每月消费者价格指数的每个细节，寻找美联储何时降息的线索。略低的通胀数据令市场欣喜，推动标普 500、纳斯达克综合指数和道琼斯工业平均指数创下新高。 |
| Higher prices in **Japan** that are causing consumers to tighten their belts were the main factor behind the economy shrinking by 0.5% in the first three months of the year, quarter on quarter. An earthquake at the start of 2024 also dented output. | **日本**物价上涨迫使消费者收紧开支，是今年前三个月经济环比萎缩 0.5% 的主要原因。2024 年初的地震也打击了产出。 |
| **Britain’s economy** pulled out of recession in the first quarter of 2024, growing by 0.6% compared with the last quarter of 2023. That was a faster pace of growth than America’s 0.4% and the euro zone’s 0.3% on the same basis. But compared with the first quarter of 2023, GDP rose by only 0.2%. | **英国经济**在 2024 年第一季度走出衰退，环比增长 0.6%（与 2023 年第四季度相比）。按同一口径，这快于美国的 0.4% 和欧元区的 0.3%。但与 2023 年第一季度相比，GDP 仅增长 0.2%。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 宏观
> - **dip to** —— 降至。dip 指小幅下降，比 fall 更轻，常用于数据播报。
> - **consecutive** /kənˈsekjətɪv/ —— 连续的。two consecutive monthly rises = 连续两个月上涨。
> - **pore over** /pɔː/ —— 仔细研读。注意与 pour（倒）区分，pore over documents/data 是高频搭配。
> - **propel** /prəˈpel/ —— 推动、驱使。propel markets to record levels = 把市场推至新高。
> - **tighten one’s belt** —— 勒紧裤腰带、紧缩开支。形象化习语，经济报道常用。
> - **quarter on quarter** —— 环比。同比是 year on year；写作 QoQ / YoY。
> - **pull out of recession** —— 走出衰退。recession 的技术定义常为“连续两季度负增长”。
> - **dent** /dent/ —— 打击、削弱（动词）。dent output/confidence = 打击产出/信心。

## 市场与新股：迷因股回潮、软银减亏、树莓派上市

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| The **meme stocks** at the centre of a day-traders’ frenzy during the pandemic came roaring back. GameStop’s share price surged by 74% and AMC Entertainment’s by 78% in a day. The cause was a sudden burst of activity on the Roaring Kitty account on X, which had been dormant since June 2021. Roaring Kitty is the brainchild of Keith Gill, a small trader who posts video clips and images as clues about his investment tips without mentioning the name of the company. | 疫情期间散户狂潮的中心——**迷因股**——呼啸而归。GameStop 股价单日暴涨 74%，AMC 娱乐上涨 78%。起因是 X 上 Roaring Kitty 账号突然活跃——该账号自 2021 年 6 月起一直沉寂。Roaring Kitty 是基思·吉尔的化身，这位小散户发布视频片段和图片，作为其投资建议的线索，却从不点出公司名字。 |
| Despite turning a profit in the first three months of 2024, **SoftBank** reported another annual net loss, of ¥228bn (\$1.4bn) for the 12 months ending March 31st. The Japanese tech investment company is banking on AI for its future growth, based on the performance of Arm, a chip designer that floated on the stockmarket in September and in which SoftBank owns a 90% stake. | 尽管 2024 年第一季度实现盈利，**软银**仍报告截至 3 月 31 日的 12 个月净亏损 2280 亿日元（14 亿美元）。这家日本科技投资公司把未来增长押在 AI 上，依据是 Arm 的表现——这家芯片设计公司 9 月上市，软银持有其 90% 的股份。 |
| **Raspberry Pi** announced its intention to list shares on the London Stock Exchange. The firm, based in Cambridge, has sold over 60m low-cost single-board computers and modules since it began trading in 2012. It grew out of a charitable foundation to promote the study of computing, allowing children (and their parents) to build their own PC and learn coding. It has a dedicated fan base. | **树莓派**宣布拟在伦敦证券交易所上市。这家总部位于剑桥的公司自 2012 年开始销售以来，已卖出逾 6000 万台低成本单板计算机和模块。它脱胎于一个推广计算学习的慈善基金会，让孩子们（以及他们的父母）自己组装电脑、学习编程。它拥有一批忠实粉丝。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 市场与新股
> - **meme stock** —— 迷因股。指因网络社区炒作而非基本面暴涨的股票，meme 本义“模因/梗”。
> - **come roaring back** —— 呼啸而归、强势回归。roaring 强化“猛烈”的语气。
> - **surge** /sɜːdʒ/ —— 暴涨、激增。名词/动词同形，surge by 74% = 暴涨 74%。
> - **dormant** /ˈdɔːmənt/ —— 休眠的、暂停活动的。账户、火山、病毒都可用。
> - **brainchild** —— 某人的创意、心血结晶。the brainchild of sb = 某人的点子。
> - **bank on** —— 指望、押注于。bank on AI = 把宝押在 AI 上。
> - **float** /fləʊt/ —— （公司）上市、发行股票。英式说法，同义 go public / list。
> - **single-board computer** —— 单板计算机。树莓派的品类定义，整台电脑集成在一块电路板上。
> - **dedicated fan base** —— 忠实的粉丝群体。dedicated 此处是“忠诚的、专一的”。
