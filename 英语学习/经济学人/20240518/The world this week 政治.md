## 俄罗斯与乌克兰：换将、访华、新攻势

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| In a surprise shake-up of **Russia’s** security apparatus, Vladimir Putin moved Sergei Shoigu from his job as minister of defence, a role he has held since 2012, to the national security council. His replacement is Andrei Belousov, an economist with no experience of security matters but who has worked extensively on military budgets. The changing of the guard will have little effect on operations in Ukraine. Mr Putin prefers to speak directly to Valery Gerasimov, his senior general in the field. Mr Belousov said his task was to achieve victory, but “with minimal human losses”, presumably just on the Russian side. | **俄罗斯**安全机构出人意料地大改组：普京把自 2012 年起担任国防部长的绍伊古调往国家安全委员会，接任者是经济学家别洛乌索夫——他没有安全事务经验，但长期研究军费预算。这次换将不会对乌克兰战事产生多大影响，普京更愿意直接与前线的总参谋长格拉西莫夫沟通。别洛乌索夫说，他的任务是取得胜利，但要“把人员损失降到最低”——想必只是指俄方。 |
| Mr Putin met his Chinese counterpart, **Xi Jinping**, in Beijing. It was his first trip abroad since winning a sham election in March. The leaders reaffirmed their support for each other. Mr Xi has been accused by Western officials of propping up Mr Putin’s war machine in Ukraine. | 普京在北京会晤中国国家主席**习近平**。这是他 3 月赢得一场虚假选举后首次出访。两位领导人重申相互支持。西方官员指责习近平支撑普京在乌克兰的战争机器。 |
| Antony Blinken, America’s secretary of state, visited **Ukraine**, where he held talks with Volodymyr Zelensky, the president, about the arms that are being sent to the country following Congress’s passage of a military-aid bill. Mr Zelensky said more missile-defence systems were needed. Mr Blinken showed off his guitar skills in Kyiv, joining a local rock band to play Neil Young’s “Rockin’ in the Free World”. | 美国国务卿布林肯访问**乌克兰**，与泽连斯基总统会谈，讨论国会通过军援法案后正运往该国的武器。泽连斯基说还需要更多防空系统。布林肯在基辅秀了把吉他，加入当地一支摇滚乐队，演奏尼尔·杨的《Rockin’ in the Free World》。 |
| The Russians said they had entered Vovchansk, a border town in Ukraine’s north-east close to **Kharkiv**, the country’s second-biggest city and where many civilians have sought refuge. Russia has deployed five battalions in its advance into the area, one of its biggest ground attacks since the start of the war. | 俄方称已进入沃夫昌斯克——乌克兰东北部靠近**哈尔科夫**的边境城镇；哈尔科夫是乌克兰第二大城市，许多平民已逃往那里避难。俄军在该方向投入五个营，这是开战以来规模最大的地面进攻之一。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 俄罗斯与乌克兰
> - **shake-up** —— 大改组、人事大调整。shake up 动词表“重组”，报道政府与企业人事变动常用。
> - **security apparatus** —— 安全机构、安全机器。apparatus 指“一整套机构/体系”，不是单件设备。
> - **changing of the guard** —— 换岗、新老交替。原指白金汉宫卫兵换岗，引申为领导层更替。
> - **counterpart** /ˈkaʊntəpɑːt/ —— 对应职位的同僚（此处指中国国家主席对俄罗斯总统）。不是“对手”。
> - **sham** /ʃæm/ —— 虚假的、伪装的。a sham election = 走过场的选举。
> - **reaffirm** /ˌriːəˈfɜːm/ —— 重申。re-（再）+ affirm（确认）。
> - **prop up** —— 支撑、扶持（常含贬义）。prop up a war machine = 撑起战争机器。
> - **secretary of state** —— 国务卿（美国）。注意不是“国务秘书”。
> - **show off** —— 炫耀、展示。show off one’s guitar skills = 露一手吉他。
> - **battalion** /bəˈtæliən/ —— 营（军事编制）。五个营约数千人。

## 欧洲：遇刺、外国代理人法与加泰罗尼亚

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Robert Fico, the prime minister of **Slovakia**, was shot and seriously wounded while visiting the town of Handlova. The interior minister described the attack as politically motivated. A suspect was arrested. Mr Fico, a populist-nationalist, has sought closer ties with Russia and wants to defang the country’s public broadcaster. | **斯洛伐克**总理菲佐在访问汉德洛瓦镇时遭枪击，伤势严重。内政部长称袭击出于政治动机，一名嫌疑人已被捕。菲佐是民粹民族主义者，寻求与俄罗斯拉近关系，还想削弱该国的公共广播机构。 |
| **Georgia’s** parliament passed a law that will require NGOs and media groups that receive at least 20% of their funding from abroad to register as foreign agents. Fighting broke out among MPs as the vote was held. Called the “Russia law” by critics because of its similarity to rules that stifle dissent in Russia, the legislation has brought tens of thousands of protesters out onto the streets. The European Union has warned that the measures threaten the country’s chances of joining the bloc. | **格鲁吉亚**议会通过一项法律：凡从国外获得至少 20% 资金的非政府组织和媒体机构，须登记为“外国代理人”。表决时议员之间爆发肢体冲突。批评者称之为“俄罗斯法”，因为它与俄罗斯压制异见的规则相似；该法已让数万抗议者走上街头。欧盟警告，这些措施将危及该国加入欧盟的前景。 |
| Pro-independence parties lost their majority in Catalonia’s regional election, which for the first time gave the most seats to the Socialists. The result ends more than a decade in power by parties seeking to secede from **Spain**. The conservative People’s Party increased its number of seats from three to 15. The Socialists will try to form a coalition government with other parties. | 在加泰罗尼亚地区选举中，独立派政党失去多数席位，社会党首次赢得最多席位。这一结果终结了寻求脱离**西班牙**的政党十多年的执政。保守派人民党席位从 3 席增至 15 席。社会党将尝试与其他政党组建联合政府。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 欧洲
> - **politically motivated** —— 出于政治动机的。motivated 表“有……动机”，如 profit-motivated。
> - **populist-nationalist** —— 民粹民族主义者。两个标签叠加，是近年欧洲政党的常见画像。
> - **defang** /diːˈfæŋ/ —— 拔去獠牙、削弱（威胁性）。原义“去掉毒牙”，形象化动词。
> - **public broadcaster** —— 公共广播机构（如 BBC、斯洛伐克 RTVS）。
> - **foreign agents** —— 外国代理人。源自美国 FARA 的法律概念，格鲁吉亚版本被指仿俄。
> - **stifle dissent** —— 压制异见。stifle = 扼杀、憋住；dissent = 不同意见。
> - **secede** /sɪˈsiːd/ —— 脱离、独立（from）。名词 secession，美国南北战争语境常见。
> - **coalition government** —— 联合政府。coalition 指多党联合执政。

## 中东：拉法、援助与建国日

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| The UN said that 600,000 people had fled Rafah in south **Gaza**, as **Israel** stepped up its incursion into the area. Fighting also intensified in north Gaza, where Israel had been winding down its operations. Meanwhile America’s State Department said it was “reasonable to assess” that Israel may have used American weapons in a way that is “inconsistent” with its obligations under international law, but that there was “no direct indication of Israel intentionally targeting civilians”. The White House moved forward with a plan to send another \$1bn-worth of arms to Israel. | 联合国称，随着**以色列**加大对**加沙**南部拉法的进攻，已有 60 万人逃离该地。加沙北部战斗也在加剧，而以色列此前一直在收缩那里的行动。与此同时，美国国务院表示，“有理由评估”以色列可能以“不符合”其国际法义务的方式使用了美国武器，但“没有直接迹象表明以色列故意针对平民”。白宫则推进向以色列再提供价值 10 亿美元武器的计划。 |
| A small group of Israeli protesters vandalised **food-aid** packages that were en route to Gaza from Jordan. America condemned the “looting”. Meanwhile Israel opened a new aid crossing at Erez that the UN hopes will enable a constant stream of supplies into Gaza. | 一小群以色列抗议者破坏了从约旦运往加沙的**食品援助**包。美国谴责这种“抢劫”行为。同时，以色列在埃雷兹开放了一个新的援助过境点，联合国希望这能让物资持续流入加沙。 |
| Celebrations to mark **Israel’s founding** in 1948 were muted. Fireworks were cancelled and the families of the hostages being held in Gaza organised an alternative event to protest against the government’s failure to bring the captives home. Binyamin Netanyahu, the prime minister, was heckled during a speech. | 纪念以色列 1948 年建国的活动气氛低沉。烟花取消，被扣在加沙的人质家属组织了另一场活动，抗议政府未能把人质带回家。总理内塔尼亚胡演讲时被人起哄。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 中东
> - **incursion** /ɪnˈkɜːʃn/ —— 侵入、突入。比 invasion 轻，报道以军行动时的惯用词。
> - **wind down** —— 逐步收缩、逐渐结束。wind down operations = 收缩行动。
> - **inconsistent with** —— 与……不一致、相抵触。外交辞令中表示“违反”的委婉说法。
> - **vandalise** /ˈvændəlaɪz/ —— 故意破坏（公物）。名词 vandalism。
> - **en route to** —— 在前往……的途中。法语借词，读作 /ɒn ˈruːt/。
> - **muted** /ˈmjuːtɪd/ —— 低调的、克制的（庆祝、反应）。原义“静音”。
> - **hostage** /ˈhɒstɪdʒ/ —— 人质。take/hold sb hostage。
> - **heckle** /ˈhekl/ —— 起哄、打断（演讲者）。名词 heckler。

## 非洲与亚洲：普遍管辖权与新加坡交班

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| A Swiss court convicted Ousman Sonko, who was interior minister under **The Gambia’s** former dictator, Yahya Jammeh, of crimes against humanity between 2000 and 2016, including murder and torture. Mr Sonko is the highest-ranking official to be convicted in Europe under the principle of universal jurisdiction, whereby a person who commits a crime in one country can be tried and jailed in another. | 瑞士法院判定**冈比亚**前独裁者贾梅政府的内政部长乌斯曼·松科犯有 2000 至 2016 年间的反人类罪，包括谋杀和酷刑。松科是在欧洲依“普遍管辖权”原则被定罪的最高级别官员——该原则允许一人在某国犯罪、在另一国受审服刑。 |
| Lawrence Wong was sworn in as **Singapore’s** prime minister in a carefully crafted handover of power arranged by Lee Hsien Loong, who had held the job for 20 years. Mr Wong retains his role as finance minister, but has relinquished his chairmanship of the central bank to Gan Kim Yong, who is also deputy prime minister. | 黄循财宣誓就任**新加坡**总理，这是李显龙精心安排的权力交接——他担任该职 20 年。黄循财保留财政部长职务，但把央行主席一职交给同时担任副总理的颜金勇。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 非洲与亚洲
> - **convict** /kənˈvɪkt/ —— 判定……有罪。名词 convict 重音在前，指“罪犯”。
> - **crimes against humanity** —— 反人类罪。国际刑法三大罪名之一（另有种族灭绝罪、战争罪）。
> - **universal jurisdiction** —— 普遍管辖权。无论犯罪地在哪里，任何国家都可起诉严重国际罪行。
> - **whereby** /weəˈbaɪ/ —— 据此、凭此（关系副词）。法律文本中解释机制时常用。
> - **handover of power** —— 权力交接。handover 来自 hand over（移交）。
> - **relinquish** /rɪˈlɪŋkwɪʃ/ —— 放弃、交出（职位、权力）。比 give up 更正式。

## 亚太：新喀里多尼亚、克什米尔与洪灾

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Four people were killed during rioting in **New Caledonia**, a French Pacific-island territory, after the French National Assembly passed a law allowing French citizens who have lived there for ten years to vote in elections. Locals fear the law will dilute the political power of the Kanak people. | 法属太平洋岛屿领地**新喀里多尼亚**发生骚乱，四人死亡。起因是法国国民议会通过法律，允许在当地居住满十年的法国公民参加选举。当地人担心该法会稀释卡纳克人的政治权力。 |
| Several days of protests against rising food and electricity prices left four people dead in Pakistan-administered **Kashmir**. The violence ended after the Pakistani government promised to provide subsidies. | 在巴基斯坦管辖的**克什米尔**，数日来反对食品和电价上涨的抗议造成四人死亡。巴基斯坦政府承诺提供补贴后，暴力才结束。 |
| The UN said that Flash flooding in northern **Afghanistan** had killed more than 300 people. An exceptionally dry winter has hardened the soil, which takes longer to absorb heavy rainfall. In **Indonesia** scores of people were killed in floods in the province of West Sumatra. | 联合国称，**阿富汗**北部的突发洪水已造成 300 多人死亡。异常干燥的冬季使土壤板结，吸收强降雨更慢。**印度尼西亚**西苏门答腊省的洪灾也造成数十人死亡。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 亚太
> - **rioting** /ˈraɪətɪŋ/ —— 骚乱、暴动。riot 名词/动词，run riot = 失控。
> - **territory** /ˈterətri/ —— 领地、属地。法国海外领地（overseas territory）的正式说法。
> - **dilute** /daɪˈluːt/ —— 稀释、削弱。dilute political power = 削弱政治影响力。
> - **administer** /ədˈmɪnɪstə/ —— 管辖、管理。Pakistan-administered = 巴方管辖的。
> - **subsidy** /ˈsʌbsədi/ —— 补贴。复数 subsidies，动词 subsidise。
> - **flash flooding** —— 突发洪水、山洪。flash flood 强调来得快。
> - **scores of** —— 数十个、许多。score = 20，scores of = 大量。

## 安全、拉美与美国大选

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| The director of Britain’s GCHQ, which handles signals intelligence, said that China, more than Russia or Iran, poses the biggest risk to **cyber-security**. Anne Keast-Butler criticised China’s “irresponsible actions”. Meanwhile, three men were charged in London with spying for Hong Kong’s intelligence agency. | 英国负责信号情报的政府通信总部（GCHQ）负责人表示，**网络安全**的最大风险来自中国，而非俄罗斯或伊朗。安妮·基斯特-巴特勒批评中国的“不负责任行为”。与此同时，三名男子在伦敦被控为香港情报机构从事间谍活动。 |
| The IMF’s staff praised **Argentina’s** “better-than-expected performance” and recommended the country receive the next tranche of loans under a bail-out agreement. President Javier Milei has undertaken a number of painful economic reforms and spending cuts; unions recently held a general strike. Argentina’s annual inflation rate stood at 289% in April, though the month-on-month rate has eased, allowing the central bank to cut its key interest rate from 50% to 40%. | 国际货币基金组织工作人员称赞**阿根廷**“表现好于预期”，建议在该救助协议下向其发放下一批贷款。总统米莱推行了一系列痛苦的经济改革和开支削减，工会最近举行了大罢工。阿根廷 4 月年通胀率为 289%，但环比涨幅已放缓，使央行得以将关键利率从 50% 下调至 40%。 |
| Joe Biden and Donald Trump agreed to hold two presidential televised **debates**, on June 27th and September 10th. The dates are much earlier than normal in the election cycle and have been arranged outside the auspices of the commission that decides the timings. The candidates may want early debates so that they can recover in the polls if they trip up. | 拜登与特朗普同意举行两场总统电视**辩论**，分别在 6 月 27 日和 9 月 10 日。这两个日期比大选周期的通常安排早得多，而且绕开了决定辩论时间的委员会。候选人或许希望早点辩论，这样万一失手，还能在民调中恢复过来。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 安全与拉美
> - **signals intelligence** —— 信号情报（SIGINT）。指截获通信与电子信号的情报门类。
> - **cyber-security** —— 网络安全。cyber- 前缀表“网络的”。
> - **charge sb with** —— 指控某人（犯罪）。be charged with spying = 被控从事间谍活动。
> - **tranche** /trɑːnʃ/ —— （贷款、资金的）一批、一期。国际金融报道高频词。
> - **bail-out** —— 纾困、救助（资金）。bail out 动词表“救助（企业/国家）”。
> - **general strike** —— 大罢工、总罢工。区别于行业性罢工。
> - **month-on-month** —— 环比的。此处指月度通胀较上月的变化。
> - **auspices** /ˈɔːspɪsɪz/ —— 主持、赞助（under the auspices of）。表示“由……主办/授权”。
> - **trip up** —— 失足、出错。口语化，指辩论中口误或露怯。
