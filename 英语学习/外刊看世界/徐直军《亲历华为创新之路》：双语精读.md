# Inside Huawei's innovation journey：双语精读

## 入职：一张整版招聘广告

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Back in May 1993, as I was wrapping up my PhD, I took a trip to Shenzhen to look for work. While at a friend's house, I was flipping through the *Shenzhen Special Zone Daily* and was immediately drawn to a full-page recruitment ad from Huawei. It specifically highlighted that they were looking for people with a master's degree or doctorate. It was odd. As the frontier of China's reform and opening up, Shenzhen was a hub for processing and trade, and very few companies were hiring people with higher education. Out of curiosity, the next day I went for an interview and ended up getting offered a job. | 1993 年 5 月，我博士学业即将结束，去深圳找工作。在朋友家里，我随手翻看《深圳特区报》，一下子被华为一整版的招聘广告吸引。广告特别写明，要招硕士和博士。这有点反常。作为中国改革开放的前沿，深圳当时是加工贸易的聚集地，很少有企业招揽高学历人才。出于好奇，第二天我就去面试了，结果拿到了录用通知。 |
| As the day of my graduation neared, I had already received several offers but the one from Huawei stood out. Their HR manager would call me every week, saying everything was ready and they were expecting me. The respect they showed me, the urgency of their calls, and the promise of a salary five times more than my PhD supervisor's made the decision easy. I chose Huawei. | 临近毕业，我手里已经有好几个录用通知，但华为那份与众不同。他们的 HR 经理每周都给我打电话，说一切都准备好了，就等我过去。这份尊重、这种急切，再加上承诺的薪水是我博士生导师的五倍，让选择变得毫无悬念。我选了华为。 |
| When I came onboard in August, I realized that, of the company's 600 employees, more than 200 were R&D engineers, as well as a ton of postgraduates. Five PhDs joined that same year. It was unimaginable anywhere in China, especially in Shenzhen, because the entire country was producing only about 2,000 PhD graduates a year. Huawei's huge bets on talent in its early years were a clear sign of its commitment to innovation and forward-looking investment. | 8 月入职后我才发现，公司 600 名员工中，有 200 多人是研发工程师，还有一大批研究生。同年入职的博士就有五位。这在中国任何地方都难以想象，深圳尤其如此，因为当时全国每年毕业的博士也只有约 2000 人。华为在创业初期就在人才上下如此重注，清楚表明了它对创新的执着和面向未来的投入。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 入职
> - **wrap up** /ræp ʌp/ —— 收尾、结束。wrap up a PhD = 完成博士学业；会议收尾也说 wrap up。
> - **flip through** —— 随手翻看。比 read 更轻，强调随便翻翻。
> - **recruitment ad** —— 招聘广告。ad 是 advertisement 的口语缩写，advert 为英式。
> - **stand out** /stænd aʊt/ —— 突出、显眼。the one from Huawei stood out = 华为那份格外扎眼。
> - **urgency** /ˈɜːdʒənsi/ —— 紧迫感、急切。the urgency of their calls 电话里透出的急切。
> - **come onboard** —— 入职、加入。onboard 可作动词，比 join 更强调"上船"的融入感。
> - **a ton of** —— 一大堆、大量。口语量词，比 a lot of 更随意。
> - **bet on** /bet ɒn/ —— 押注于、把赌注下在……上。huge bets on talent 在人才上下重注。
> - **forward-looking** /ˈfɔːwəd lʊkɪŋ/ —— 面向未来的、有远见的。

## 数字程控交换机：从农村包围城市

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Back then, the R&D team was working on digital stored program control switches. As China's economy soared, demand for household and office phones far outstripped supply. Getting a phone line installed was difficult and expensive for years. Switches from Western vendors were available in China, but they used a wide range of incompatible standards and went for about 3,000 Chinese yuan per line. A fortune at the time. | 当时，研发团队正在做数字程控交换机。随着中国经济高速增长，家庭和办公电话的需求远远超过供给。有好些年，装一部电话既难又贵。西方厂商的交换机在中国也能买到，但标准五花八门、互不兼容，每线价格约 3000 元人民币。这在当时是一笔巨款。 |
| This presented an opportunity. At the time, major urban centers were surrounded by geographically dispersed townships and villages. Setting up a complete switch for each location didn't make economic sense, and connecting distant communities with copper was both expensive and prone to signal loss. So we came up with a fiber-connected remote switching module, which allowed for a single centralized system to provide network coverage for an entire county. | 这里就藏着机会。当时，中心城市周边散布着大量地理上分散的乡镇和村庄。每个点都建一套完整的交换机，在经济上不划算；用铜缆连接偏远地区，不仅成本高，信号还容易衰减。于是我们做出了光纤连接的远端交换模块，用一套集中式系统就能为整个县提供网络覆盖。 |
| Our customers liked it. And that's how we managed to rapidly grow our business in a head-to-head race with established Western vendors, all of which had joint ventures with our customers. Our product was cheaper, around 1,000 yuan per line, with a profit margin higher than 20%. This helped sustain Huawei's rapid growth and gave us much-needed cash flow. In a short span of ten years (1993–2002), our headcount climbed to 22,000, including over 10,000 R&D engineers, and revenue was closing in on 4 billion USD. | 客户很喜欢这个方案。我们就是这样在与西方老牌厂商的正面竞争中快速做大了业务，而这些厂商无一例外都与我们的客户建有合资企业。我们的产品更便宜，每线约 1000 元，毛利率还超过 20%。这支撑了华为的高速发展，也带来了急需的现金流。短短十年间（1993—2002），员工规模增至 2.2 万人，其中研发工程师超过 1 万名，营收接近 40 亿美元。 |
| Many leading US companies started out small. They struck out on their own, probing forward one step at a time, selling products to make money, and investing it back into R&D. The whole "pulling yourself up by your bootstraps" ethos. And the same can be said for Huawei. | 许多美国领军企业也是从小做起。它们独自闯荡，一步一步摸索向前，卖产品赚钱，再把钱投回研发。就是那种"拽着自己的鞋带把自己提起来"的劲头。华为也是如此。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 交换机
> - **stored program control** /stɔːd ˈprəʊɡræm kənˈtrəʊl/ —— 存储程序控制，简称程控。digital stored program control switches = 数字程控交换机。
> - **outstrip** /ˌaʊtˈstrɪp/ —— 超过、胜过。demand far outstripped supply 需求远超供给。
> - **incompatible** /ˌɪnkəmˈpætəbl/ —— 不兼容的。反义 compatible；incompatible standards 互不兼容的标准。
> - **vendor** /ˈvendə/ —— 供应商、厂商。Western vendors 西方厂商。
> - **dispersed** /dɪˈspɜːst/ —— 分散的、散布的。geographically dispersed 地理上分散的。
> - **make economic sense** —— 在经济上说得通、划算。didn't make economic sense 不划算。
> - **prone to** /prəʊn tuː/ —— 易于、容易（发生坏事）。prone to signal loss 容易信号衰减。
> - **head-to-head** /ˌhed tə ˈhed/ —— 正面的、一对一地。head-to-head race 正面竞争。
> - **cash flow** /kæʃ fləʊ/ —— 现金流。much-needed cash flow 急需的现金流。
> - **close in on** /kləʊz ɪn ɒn/ —— 逼近、接近。revenue was closing in on 4 billion USD 营收逼近 40 亿美元。
> - **bootstrap** /ˈbuːtstræp/ —— 鞋带；pull yourself up by your bootstraps = 白手起家、自力更生。ethos 指精神气质。

## IPD 变革：用六亿美元把"偶然"变成"必然"

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| In 2002, I was unexpectedly put in charge of a major transformation program called Integrated Product Development (IPD). Then in 2005, I was appointed the Chair of the Investment Review Board (IRB), a role that oversees all of the company's product investments. When I became Rotating CEO in 2011, I handed over the IPD-related responsibilities, but even now I still devote a lot of my time to keeping IPD up-to-date and driving Huawei's business development. | 2002 年，我意外地被安排负责一项名为"集成产品开发（IPD）"的重大变革项目。2005 年，我被任命为投资评审委员会（IRB）主任，负责审视公司所有的产品投资。2011 年我出任轮值 CEO 时，才卸下与 IPD 相关的工作，但直到现在，我仍然投入大量时间维护 IPD 体系迭代，推动华为业务向前发展。 |
| IPD was a game changer in how we developed products and made R&D investment decisions. It also completely reshaped our R&D structure. Prior to this transformation, our R&D successes were highly dependent on individual outperformers, and high-quality products were a matter of chance. The IPD transformation put systems in place for us to reliably deliver competitive, quality products that meet our customers' needs. | IPD 彻底改变了我们开发产品和做研发投资决策的方式，也重塑了整个研发组织架构。变革之前，华为的研发成功高度依赖个别顶尖人才，高质量产品的产出充满偶然性。IPD 变革搭建起一套体系，让我们能够稳定交付满足客户需求、有市场竞争力的高质量产品。 |
| IPD was the brainchild of our founder Ren Zhengfei. In 1997, he visited several US tech companies and was inspired by the way they developed their products. Following this visit, he brought on IBM as a consultant to help us design and execute a sweeping transformation across our R&D investment decision processes, organization, and culture. | IPD 是我们创始人任正非的构想。1997 年，他走访了多家美国科技公司，深受其产品开发方式的启发。访问归来后，公司聘请 IBM 担任咨询顾问，推动研发投资决策流程、组织架构和企业文化的全面变革。 |
| The transformation kicked off in 1999. I was one of many participants in the program until 2002, when I was given the reins to work alongside IBM consultants and drive the transformation forward, rolling out the IPD process across all of R&D. | 变革于 1999 年启动。起初我只是项目的众多参与者之一，直到 2002 年，我被委以主导权，与 IBM 顾问并肩推进变革，把 IPD 流程推广到整个研发体系。 |
| Over the course of a decade, we developed a more efficient decision-making process for R&D investment, a process for integrated product development, and the corresponding organizational structures, capabilities, and IT systems – collectively called the IPD Management System. It's this system that has enabled us to not only manage an ever-expanding R&D team (from 10,000 to 110,000 members today), but keep delivering innovative products that meet our customers' evolving needs. | 十年间，我们建立了更高效的研发投资决策流程、集成产品开发流程，以及相应的组织架构、能力体系和 IT 系统——统称为 IPD 管理体系。正是这套体系，让我们不仅能管理一支不断壮大的研发队伍（从 1 万人增长到今天的 11 万人），还能持续推出创新产品，匹配客户不断变化的需求。 |
| From 1997 to 2006, Huawei paid IBM nearly 600 million USD in consulting fees. This may very well be the largest single corporate consulting engagement in China's history. | 1997 年到 2006 年，华为向 IBM 支付了近 6 亿美元的咨询费。这很可能是中国企业历史上规模最大的单笔企业咨询合作。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · IPD
> - **game changer** /ˈɡeɪm tʃeɪndʒə/ —— 彻底改变格局的人或事、颠覆性变量。
> - **outperformer** /ˌaʊtpəˈfɔːmə/ —— 表现突出者、佼佼者。依赖个别天才的"英雄驱动"。
> - **a matter of chance** —— 碰运气的事、偶然。与 a matter of time（迟早的事）结构相同。
> - **brainchild** /ˈbreɪntʃaɪld/ —— 独创的构想、智慧结晶。常与 the brainchild of sb 连用。
> - **bring on** —— 聘请、邀请（某人加入项目）。brought on IBM as a consultant 请 IBM 做顾问。
> - **sweeping** /ˈswiːpɪŋ/ —— 全面的、大规模的。a sweeping transformation 一场全面变革。
> - **kick off** /kɪk ɒf/ —— 启动、开球。项目启动的口语表达。
> - **given the reins** /reɪnz/ —— 被授予主导权、掌缰绳。reins 本义缰绳，喻控制权。
> - **roll out** /rəʊl aʊt/ —— 推广、铺开。roll out the process across R&D 把流程推广到整个研发体系。
> - **engagement** /ɪnˈɡeɪdʒmənt/ —— （咨询、服务类）项目、合约。单笔咨询合作。

## 无线产品线：分布式基站与 SingleRAN

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| In 2003, I was appointed President of the Wireless Product Line. At the time, the division had just over 2,000 people, and was working on 3G products. My mission was to transform Huawei from a fixed-network equipment supplier to a provider of both fixed and mobile network equipment. As China kept delaying the issuance of 3G licenses, we were forced to pursue market expansion overseas. | 2003 年，我被任命为无线产品线总裁。当时这个部门只有 2000 多人，正在做 3G 产品。我的使命，是让华为从固定网络设备供应商，转变为同时提供固定和移动网络设备的供应商。由于中国一再推迟发放 3G 牌照，我们被迫到海外拓展市场。 |
| Back then, Richard Yu was responsible for WCDMA products, one of the main technologies that powers 3G networks. While visiting a base station equipment room with the Dutch carrier Telfort, he discovered that site acquisition costs in Europe were exceptionally high, and there wasn't really any room for new base stations anyway. He called me from Europe to discuss whether we could split a base station into two parts – one part placed on the cell tower, and the other into a small box that could be installed inside the existing base station's cabinet. We both thought it was a plausible solution, so we decided to create a distributed base station. | 当时，余承东负责 WCDMA 产品，这是支撑 3G 网络的主要技术之一。与荷兰运营商 Telfort 一起巡检基站机房时，他发现欧洲的站点获取成本高得惊人，而且根本没有空间再新建基站。他从欧洲打电话给我，商量能不能把基站拆成两部分：一部分装在铁塔上，另一部分装进一个小盒子，可以塞进现有基站的机柜里。我们都觉得这个方案可行，于是决定做分布式基站。 |
| The solution was met with resistance from many carriers and equipment vendors, but it eventually became standard for the 3G, 4G, and 5G base stations we see today. Richard also proposed integrating 2G and 3G into a single base station during a discussion with Telefónica Germany. Later, in collaboration with Vodafone, we launched SingleRAN, a solution that physically integrates 2G, 3G, 4G, and 5G into a single base station. This, too, became the de facto standard for base stations around the world. These two groundbreaking solutions – the distributed base station and SingleRAN – established Huawei's global leadership in mobile communications. | 这个方案遭到许多运营商和设备商的抵触，但最终成为今天 3G、4G、5G 基站的标准形态。余承东还在与西班牙电信德国公司讨论时提出，把 2G 和 3G 集成到一个基站里。后来，我们与沃达丰合作推出了 SingleRAN，把 2G、3G、4G、5G 在物理上集成到同一个基站中。这同样成为全球基站的事实标准。分布式基站和 SingleRAN 这两个开创性方案，确立了华为在移动通信领域的全球领先地位。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 无线
> - **product line** /ˈprɒdʌkt laɪn/ —— 产品线。Preside over a product line 掌管一条产品线。
> - **issuance** /ˈɪʃuəns/ —— 发放、签发。the issuance of 3G licenses 3G 牌照的发放。
> - **site acquisition** /saɪt ˌækwɪˈzɪʃn/ —— 站点获取（选址、征地、租用）。欧洲建站的头号成本。
> - **exceptionally** /ɪkˈsepʃənəli/ —— 格外地、异常地。比 very 更书面。
> - **plausible** /ˈplɔːzəbl/ —— 看似可行的、说得通的。plausible solution 可行的方案。
> - **be met with resistance** —— 遭到抵制、遇到阻力。meet with 表"遭遇（反应）"。
> - **de facto** /deɪ ˈfæktəʊ/ —— 事实上的（拉丁语）。de facto standard 事实标准。
> - **groundbreaking** /ˈɡraʊndbreɪkɪŋ/ —— 开创性的、破土动工的。

## 2012 实验室：为明天留一扇门

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| To find our Noah's Ark amid the coming flood of digital information, we strengthened our basic research efforts. In 2011, we consolidated the company's research teams into a new organization – 2012 Labs, named after the film *2012: Doomsday*. It would serve as a platform for investment in sustained innovation, including basic research, core technologies, and engineering capabilities. At the same time, we changed our R&D investment model: Part of 2012 Labs' funding would come from the company, and the rest from its business units. From day one, I have been shaping the Labs' organization, team, capabilities, and future-oriented research projects. 5G, autonomous driving, and Ascend were all incubated there. | 为了在即将到来的数字信息洪流中找到我们的"诺亚方舟"，我们加强了基础研究。2011 年，我们将公司的研究团队整合为一个新组织——2012 实验室，名字取自电影《2012》。它将作为持续创新投入的平台，涵盖基础研究、核心技术和工程能力。与此同时，我们改变了研发投入模式：2012 实验室的资金一部分来自公司，其余来自各业务单元。从实验室成立之初，我就参与规划它的组织、团队、能力建设以及面向未来的科研项目。5G、自动驾驶、昇腾芯片，都在这里完成孵化。 |
| We began researching 5G as early as 2009, ultimately becoming a major contributor to 5G standards and one of the world's largest holders of 5G patents, cementing Huawei's global leadership in the field. In 2012, I led the establishment of the Internet of Vehicles Lab under 2012 Labs to conduct research on future automotive technologies, a move that led to our autonomous driving solutions, laying the foundation for our leadership in this domain as well. In 2016, the Labs began researching AI, securing us a place in the race to come. | 我们早在 2009 年就启动了 5G 研究，最终成为 5G 标准的主要贡献者和全球最大的 5G 专利持有者之一，巩固了华为在该领域的全球领先地位。2012 年，我主导在 2012 实验室下成立了车联网实验室，研究未来汽车技术。这一举措催生了我们的自动驾驶解决方案，也为我们在这一领域的领先奠定了基础。2016 年，实验室开启人工智能方向的研究，让华为在人工智能赛道抢占先机。 |
| Thanks to the Labs' pioneering basic research, technology readiness, and engineering capabilities, Huawei has been able to continuously launch innovative products and solutions that keep ahead of market demand. | 得益于 2012 实验室在基础研究、技术准备和工程能力上的开创性工作，华为能够持续推出领先于市场需求的创新产品和解决方案。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 2012 实验室
> - **Noah's Ark** /ˈnəʊəz ɑːk/ —— 诺亚方舟。喻"避难的方舟、保全火种之地"。
> - **amid** /əˈmɪd/ —— 在……之中、 amidst。amid the coming flood 在即将到来的洪流之中。
> - **consolidate** /kənˈsɒlɪdeɪt/ —— 整合、合并、巩固。把分散的研究团队并成一个组织。
> - **sustained** /səˈsteɪnd/ —— 持续的、持久的。sustained innovation 持续创新。
> - **incubate** /ˈɪŋkjubeɪt/ —— 孵化（项目、企业）。incubator 孵化器。
> - **cement** /sɪˈment/ —— 巩固、确立（地位）。cement leadership 巩固领先地位。
> - **Internet of Vehicles** /ˈɪntənet əv ˈviːəklz/ —— 车联网（IoV）。
> - **technology readiness** /tekˈnɒlədʒi ˈredinəs/ —— 技术就绪度、技术成熟度。
> - **keep ahead of** —— 领先于、跑在……前面。keep ahead of market demand 领先市场需求。

| 原文 | 译文 |

| I joined Huawei in 1993 to work in R&D, and have spent the vast majority of my career witnessing, taking part in, and leading Huawei's innovation journey – one shared by hundreds of thousands of people in our R&D team. | 1993 年，我加入华为做研发，职业生涯的绝大部分时间都在见证、参与和引领华为的创新之路——这也是我们研发团队数十万人共同走过的路。 |

> - **utmost** /ˈʌtməʊst/ —— 极度的、最大的。utmost respect 极度尊重；do one's utmost 竭尽全力。
> - **intellectual property** /ˌɪntəˈlektʃuəl ˈprɒpəti/ —— 知识产权（IP）。

> - **a large swathe of** /swɑːð/ —— 一大片、一大批。swathe 本义"一刈之草、一条地带"。
