## 导语：高斯消元法已死

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| LOS ANGELES — Terence Tao, considered by many the greatest living mathematician, began a recent lecture here with a rundown of what A.I. has already accomplished in his field: proofs confirmed, papers auto-generated, insights into the Riemann hypothesis, a famously unsolved problem about the distribution of prime numbers. | 洛杉矶——特伦斯·陶（Terence Tao）被许多人视为在世最伟大的数学家。最近在这里的一场讲座上，他开场先盘点了一遍 AI 在数学领域已经取得的成果：得到确认的证明、自动生成的论文、对黎曼猜想（一个关于素数分布的著名未解难题）的新见解。 |
| Then he flashed a slide with a striking phrase: "Gaussian elimination is dead," a reference to the 200-year-old method that generations of students learn for solving systems of equations. He paused for dramatic effect. Gaussian elimination, he said, is indeed dead — killed by A.I., which has essentially taken over that task. | 接着他打出一张幻灯片，上面是一句惊人的话："**高斯消元法已死。**"高斯消元法是有两百年历史的方法，一代代学生都靠它解方程组。他故意停顿了一下以制造戏剧效果，然后说：高斯消元法确实死了——被 AI 杀死的，这项活儿如今实际上已被 AI 接管。 |
| The audience of several hundred students and professors, packed into an auditorium at the University of California, Los Angeles, burst into knowing laughter. | 几百名学生和教授挤在加州大学洛杉矶分校（UCLA）的礼堂里，爆发出会心的笑声。 |
| For Dr. Tao, 50, the joke was not entirely a joke. He is on what he calls a war footing, a kind of academic battle stations, over what artificial intelligence means for the future of mathematics — and what mathematics means for the rest of us. | 对 50 岁的陶博士来说，这个玩笑并不只是玩笑。对于人工智能意味着数学的怎样的未来——以及数学又意味着我们其余人的什么——他正处于自己所说的"战时状态"，某种学术意义上的战斗准备。 |
| In talks, podcasts, interviews and a rush of posts on Mastodon, the social media platform he uses, Dr. Tao has become the surprise leading voice on whether the arrival of A.I. in mathematics is a blessing, a curse or something in between — and on what the rest of us should be watching for as it spreads. | 通过演讲、播客、访谈，以及他在用的社交平台 Mastodon 上接连不断的帖子，陶博士出人意料地成了这个问题的头号发声者：AI 降临数学，究竟是福、是祸、还是介于两者之间——以及当它扩散开来，我们其余人应该关注什么。 |
| For now, his position is that A.I. is a fantastic assistant, but just that. "It's very humbling," he said. "There are things that A.I. is much better at than me." | 就目前而言，他的立场是：AI 是一个极好的助手，但仅此而已。"这很让人清醒，"他说，"有些事情，AI 比我强得多。" |
| In his mind, the potential of A.I. has yet to be fully grasped, which explains his war-footing metaphors. "If there's an actual possibility that A.I. could dramatically accelerate science and mathematics within a few years, we should be preparing for that now," he said. "We should be devoting some fraction of our efforts to planning for this scenario." | 在他看来，AI 的潜力还没有被完全认识到——这解释了他那些"战时状态"的比喻。"如果确实存在一种可能：AI 能在几年之内极大地加速科学与数学，那我们现在就该开始为此做准备，"他说，"我们应该投入一部分精力，为这种情景做规划。" |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 导语
> - **war footing** /wɔːr ˈfʊtɪŋ/ —— 战时状态、战备状态。footing 指"立足状态"，on a war footing 即"按打仗的标准运转"，这里是比喻：学术研究进入紧急备战。
> - **a rundown of ...** —— 对……的简要盘点、概览。run down 动词短语"匆匆过一遍"，名词 rundown = summary/overview。
> - **Riemann hypothesis** —— 黎曼猜想，素数分布的核心未解难题，千禧年七大数学问题之一。
> - **Gaussian elimination** —— 高斯消元法，线性代数解方程组的基础方法。elimination 此处取"消去"（消元）之意。
> - **systems of equations** —— 方程组。注意 system 在数学语境是"方程组"而非"系统"。
> - **for dramatic effect** —— 为了戏剧效果。写作高频搭配。
> - **burst into knowing laughter** —— 爆发出会心的笑。knowing = 心照不宣的（形容词，≠ 知道的）。
> - **a blessing, a curse or something in between** —— 是福、是祸、还是介于两者之间。议论文经典三分句式。
> - **humbling** /ˈhʌmblɪŋ/ —— 令人自惭的、使人清醒的。来自 humble（使谦卑），"It's humbling" 是高频口语表达。
> - **devote some fraction of our efforts to ...** —— 投入一部分精力做……。fraction = 一小部分。

## 风暴前夕（转场段）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| His sudden prominence in the A.I. debate is unexpected in part because mathematics has traditionally been one of the more inward-looking of academic fields. Mathematicians toil in abstract realms that the broader public rarely sees and sometimes struggles to understand. And while many have been skeptical of A.I., wondering if its proofs can be trusted, most have not been especially vocal about it. | 他突然站上 AI 辩论的前台，多少有些出人意料——部分原因是数学历来是学术界里较为"向内看"的领域之一。数学家在公众几乎看不见、有时也难以理解的抽象世界里耕耘。而尽管许多同行对 AI 持怀疑态度，怀疑它的证明是否可信，大多数人并没有特别公开地发声。 |
| Dr. Tao — widely known as Terry, a rarefied star in a rarefied world, arguably the most famous mathematician alive — has become the exception. | 陶博士——人们普遍叫他 Terry，这个精英世界里的精英明星，可以说是在世最有名的数学家——成了例外。 |
| The pressures on his field are intensifying. Corporate investment and the drive for new products are pushing into every sector, and mathematicians, whose work has long seemed immune from such forces, are now confronting the same questions about obsolescence that have already hit writers, lawyers, radiologists and other white-collar workers. | 这个领域承受的压力正在加剧。企业投资和追逐新产品的动力正涌入每个行业，而数学家——他们的工作长期以来似乎对这些力量免疫——如今也开始直面同一个关于"过时"的问题；这个问题此前已经砸到了作家、律师、放射科医生和其他白领头上。 |
| Dr. Tao said that mathematicians have to accept that their field will be disrupted, but that they have the power to shape it. | 陶博士说，数学家必须接受这个领域将被颠覆的现实，但他们也有能力去塑造这种颠覆。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 转场段
> - **prominence** /ˈprɒmɪnəns/ —— 突出地位、显赫。rise to prominence = 崭露头角。
> - **inward-looking** —— 向内看的、不关心外界的。复合形容词，-looking 系列：outward-looking（外向型）、forward-looking（有前瞻性的）。
> - **toil** /tɔɪl/ —— 长时间苦干。文学化动词，比 work 更有画面感。
> - **abstract realms** —— 抽象的领域/王国。realm 比 field 更文学化。
> - **vocal** /ˈvəʊkəl/ —— 直言不讳的、大声表态的。be vocal about sth = 对某事公开发声。
> - **rarefied** /ˈreərɪfaɪd/ —— （层级/氛围）精英化的、曲高和寡的。原义"稀薄的"（空气），引申为"高处不胜寒的"。
> - **immune from / to ...** —— 对……免疫、不受影响。
> - **obsolescence** /ˌɒbsəˈlesns/ —— 过时、报废。形容词 obsolete。科技报道高频词。
> - **radiologist** —— 放射科医生。radi(o) = 放射。
> - **disrupt / disruption** —— 颠覆。商业与科技报道的核心词汇（disruptive innovation 颠覆式创新）。

## As Much Art as Science（既是科学，也是艺术）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| In April, at a conference on "Progress in Mathematics" in Heidelberg, Germany, Dr. Tao drew laughs by flashing a photograph of himself, an apparent casualty of A.I.: it showed him pinned under a fallen structure labeled "computer-assisted proof." | 今年 4 月，在德国海德堡一场名为"数学的进展"的会议上，陶博士放出一张自己的照片引来哄笑——照片里他俨然是 AI 的受害者：被一座倒塌的、标着"计算机辅助证明"字样的结构压在下面。 |
| A month later he was on the popular science podcast "The Joy of Why," where host Steve Strogatz, a Cornell mathematician and Dr. Tao's friend, asked whether A.I. would eventually replace mathematicians. | 一个月后，他上了热门科普播客《The Joy of Why》。主持人史蒂夫·斯托加茨是康奈尔大学的数学家，也是陶的朋友。他问：AI 最终会取代数学家吗？ |
| It was a version of the question he gets everywhere. | 这是他走到哪儿都会被问到的那个问题的一个版本。 |
| His answer: A.I. is already changing how mathematics gets done, but mathematical research is a combination of intuition and analysis, art as well as science, and human creativity and taste still matter. | 他的回答是：AI 已经在改变数学的研究方式，但数学研究是直觉与分析的结合，既是科学也是艺术——人类的创造力与品味依然重要。 |
| Dr. Tao (pronounced "tow") speaks in clear, conversational sentences, and in his telling, mathematics is the study of patterns: "The point is to get some appreciation for how these patterns fit together." | 陶博士（Tao 发音同 "tow"）说话清晰、像聊天一样自然。按他的讲法，数学是研究模式的学问："关键在于体会这些模式是如何拼合在一起的。" |
| The A.I. tools that have most impressed him are from OpenAI and Google DeepMind. They are, he said, like "an eager but somewhat mediocre graduate student" who can help by brute-force number crunching, testing and double-checking to ensure that proofs hold up. | 最让他印象深刻的 AI 工具来自 OpenAI 和 Google DeepMind。他说，它们就像"一个热情但资质平平的研究生"，能靠蛮力算数、测试和反复核对来帮忙，确保证明站得住脚。 |
| But he added that the current A.I. models have real limitations: They make mistakes, "hallucinate" incorrect answers and often have trouble with complex proofs that require building on techniques developed over decades. | 但他也补充说，当前的 AI 模型有真实的局限：它们会犯错、会"幻觉"出错误答案，而且常常搞不定那种需要建立在数十年积累的技术之上的复杂证明。 |
| "Mathematics as an art form isn't going anywhere," he said in an interview at U.C.L.A., his home for more than 25 years. "But the way it's practiced is going to change. That's what I'm trying to prepare my students for." | "数学作为一种艺术形式不会消失，"他在 UCLA——他待了 25 年多的家——接受采访时说，"但数学的实践方式会变。这就是我努力让学生们做好准备的原因。" |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · As Much Art as Science
> - **as much A as B** —— 既是 B 也是 A（同等程度）。标题句式：数学既是科学，也是艺术。
> - **draw laughs** —— 引来笑声。draw = 吸引（反应），类似 draw applause / draw criticism。
> - **flash a slide / photograph** —— 迅速打出（幻灯片/照片）。flash 强调"一闪而出"。
> - **casualty** /ˈkæʒuəli/ —— 伤亡者、受害者。casualty of A.I. = AI 的受害者。
> - **pin (sb) under ...** —— 把（某人）压在……下面动弹不得。
> - **a version of the question** —— 那个问题的一种变体。version 的灵活用法：同一问题的不同问法。
> - **in his telling** —— 按他的说法。telling = 讲述（名词），比 in his words 更文学。
> - **eager but somewhat mediocre** —— 热情但资质平平。mediocre /ˌmiːdiˈəʊkə(r)/ = 平庸的，形容能力"不上不下"。
> - **brute-force** —— 蛮力的、靠堆算力的。brute force 本义"蛮力"，在计算语境常译"暴力（计算）"。
> - **number crunching** —— 大量数字计算、算数苦力。crunch = 嘎吱嘎吱地嚼，引申"啃数字"。
> - **hold up** —— 站得住脚、经得起检验。proof holds up = 证明成立。
> - **hallucinate** /həˈluːsɪneɪt/ —— 产生幻觉。AI 术语"幻觉"的动词原形。
> - **build on ...** —— 在……的基础上构建。build on techniques developed over decades = 建立在数十年技术积累之上。
> - **isn't going anywhere** —— 不会消失、哪儿也去不了。口语化表达，此处带幽默感。
> - **the way it's practiced** —— 它的实践方式。practice = 实践/执业，math as practiced = 数学的研究方式。

## 'Screaming for Help'（" screaming 求救"——无人准备的冲击）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| One impetus for his war footing was GPT-5, released by OpenAI in October, which he says has jaw-dropping abilities: it could pass math Ph.D. qualifying exams at many universities. | 让他进入"战时状态"的一个动因是 OpenAI 十月发布的 GPT-5。他说这代模型的能力令人瞠目：它能通过许多大学的数学博士资格考试。 |
| "GPT-5 is the first one where you actually can start having pretty serious mathematical conversations with it," Dr. Tao said. | "GPT-5 是第一个你能真正开始和它进行相当严肃的数学对话的模型，"陶博士说。 |
| He worries that people have become numbed to the pace of change, and no longer appreciate how extraordinary the new systems are. He compared the moment to the early years of the internet, and to 2012, when a neural network taught itself to recognize cats in YouTube videos, an advance that set off the deep learning boom. | 他担心人们已经对变化的速度感到麻木，不再意识到新系统有多不寻常。他把当下比作互联网的早期，也比作 2012 年——那年一个神经网络自己学会了在 YouTube 视频里识别猫，那项进展引爆了深度学习浪潮。 |
| To mathematicians, he has a different message: You are not prepared. The math A.I. could do five years ago, he said, was "high school level." Now, he said, it is at the level of a strong graduate student. At that pace, he asked, what will it be doing in five more years? | 而对数学家，他有一句不同的信息：你们没有准备好。他说，五年前 AI 能做的数学是"高中水平"；现在，已经是优秀研究生的水平。他问：照这个速度，再过五年它会做到什么程度？ |
| The Oct. 6 release of GPT-5 "sort of triggered a bit of PTSD for me," Dr. Tao said. "I'd kind of written off the field" of A.I. ever matching human mathematicians, he said, before the current wave of progress. | GPT-5 在 10 月 6 日发布，"多少触发了我一点 PTSD（创伤后应激反应），"陶博士说。"我原本已经有点不看好"AI 能匹敌人类数学家这件事了——在这波进展之前，他说。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · Screaming for Help
> - **impetus** /ˈɪmpɪtəs/ —— 推动力、动因。an impetus for ... = ……的推动因素。
> - **jaw-dropping** —— 令人瞠目结舌的（惊到下巴掉下来）。drop one's jaw 的复合形容词。
> - **qualifying exam** —— 资格考试。Ph.D. qualifying exam = 博士资格考（博士生第一道关口）。
> - **numbed to ...** —— 对……麻木。numb /nʌm/ 麻木的（牙医打麻药那个"麻"）。
> - **set off ...** —— 引爆、触发。set off the deep learning boom = 引爆深度学习热潮。
> - **You are not prepared.** —— 你们没有准备好。短句的修辞力量，可背诵。
> - **PTSD** —— 创伤后应激障碍（post-traumatic stress disorder）缩写，口语里戏谑化使用="心理阴影"。
> - **write off ...** —— 认定……没戏、不看好。write off 本义"（会计）核销"，引申"放弃对……的期待"。

## A 760 on the Math SAT at Age 8（8 岁数学 SAT 760 分）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Terence Tao was born in July 1975 in Adelaide, Australia. His parents were Chinese immigrants who had met at Hong Kong University; his father was a pediatrician, his mother a teacher of mathematics and physics. | 特伦斯·陶 1975 年 7 月生于澳大利亚阿德莱德。父母是在香港大学相识的中国移民；父亲是儿科医生，母亲教数学和物理。 |
| By age 2 he was performing arithmetic for his father's patients. At 8 he scored 760 out of 800 on the math SAT. At 10 he became the youngest participant in the International Mathematical Olympiad, where he won a bronze, then silver and gold medals in the following years. He was the youngest ever to win gold, at age 13. | 两岁时他就在给父亲的病人们表演算术。8 岁，他在数学 SAT 中考出 800 分里的 760 分。10 岁成为国际数学奥林匹克最年轻的参赛者，先拿铜牌，随后几年接连银牌、金牌——13 岁夺金，是史上最年轻的金牌得主。 |
| He went to college at Flinders University in Adelaide, earned a Ph.D. from Princeton at 20 and joined U.C.L.A.'s faculty at 24. At 31 he won the Fields Medal, mathematics' highest honor, awarded only to mathematicians 40 and under. | 他在阿德莱德的弗林德斯大学读本科，20 岁在普林斯顿拿到博士，24 岁加入 UCLA 教职。31 岁斩获菲尔兹奖——数学界最高荣誉，只授予 40 岁及以下的数学家。 |
| Fame has followed him his whole life. Newspapers in Australia wrote about the "tormented genius" of his childhood, and his parents, in a 1984 book, described his early years as a kind of experiment in how to raise a genius. (Dr. Tao, asked about this, said his childhood was happy.) | 名气跟了他一辈子。澳大利亚的报纸写过他童年"备受煎熬的天才"，他的父母在 1984 年出版的一本书里，把他的幼年描述成一场"如何养育天才"的实验。（被问及此事时，陶博士说自己的童年是快乐的。） |
| His major results include a famous proof, with Ben Green, of the Green-Tao theorem, which shows that arbitrarily long sequences of prime numbers are in arithmetic progression. He also played a key role in developing compressed sensing, a signal processing technique with applications in medical imaging and other fields. | 他的代表性成果包括与本·格林合作的著名证明——格林-陶定理：素数中存在任意长的等差数列。他还在压缩感知的发展中扮演了关键角色，这是一种信号处理技术，应用于医学成像等领域。 |
| Dr. Tao, who became a United States citizen in 2000, is married to an engineer, Laura Tao, and they have two children. His son, he said, once described him as "a happy nerd." | 陶博士 2000 年成为美国公民，妻子劳拉是工程师，两人育有两个孩子。他说，儿子曾形容他是"一个快乐的呆子（nerd）"。 |
| With Dr. Tao's fame has come criticism of another sort: Some researchers fault him for spreading himself too thin, arguing that he has contributed broadly but not deeply. It is a charge he takes seriously. In recent years, by his account, he has narrowed his focus. | 名气之外，针对陶博士的批评也有另一种：一些研究者指责他铺得太开，贡献"广而不深"。对这种指控他很当回事。按他自己的说法，近年来他已经收窄了研究方向。 |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · 天才成长史
> - **pediatrician** /ˌpiːdiəˈtrɪʃn/ —— 儿科医生。pediatric = 儿科的（英式 paediatric）。
> - **score 760 out of 800** —— 满分 800 中考了 760。score 作动词"考得（分数）"。
> - **the youngest ever to ...** —— 有史以来最年轻的……。ever 强调纪录。
> - **faculty** —— 全体教员/教职。join the faculty = 加入教师队伍（≠ 能力 faculty）。
> - **Fields Medal** —— 菲尔兹奖，数学界最高荣誉，四年一颁、限 40 岁以下，被称为"数学界的诺贝尔奖"。
> - **tormented genius** —— 备受折磨/煎熬的天才。torment /tɔːˈment/ 折磨。
> - **Green-Tao theorem** —— 格林-陶定理。⚠️ 注意文末 NYT 更正：定理说的是素数包含**任意长**（arbitrarily long）的等差数列，不是**无限长**（infinitely long）——"任意长"与"无限长"在数学上是两回事，这本身就是个精确语言的经典案例。
> - **arbitrarily long** —— 任意长的。arbitrary = 任意的（arbitrarily random）。
> - **arithmetic progression** —— 等差数列（英式常用 arithmetic sequence）。
> - **compressed sensing** —— 压缩感知。信号处理术语，MRI 加速成像的数学基础。
> - **a happy nerd** —— 一个快乐的呆子。nerd 自嘲式褒义用法。
> - **spread oneself too thin** —— 摊得太开、精力分散。高频习语。
> - **broadly but not deeply** —— 广而不深。对称句式。
> - **by his account** —— 据他所说。account = 叙述（give an account of）。

## 'The Elephant in the Room'（房间里的大象）

| 原文 | 译文 |
|---|---|
| Dr. Tao's status gives him outsize influence — and a platform for his views on how A.I. could remake his field. Over coffee with a reporter in his office, he discussed his fear that A.I. will create "a lost generation" of mathematicians. | 陶博士的地位给了他超常的影响力——也给了他一个平台，去宣讲 AI 可能如何重塑这个领域。在办公室里与记者喝咖啡时，他谈到了自己的担忧：AI 会制造出数学家的"迷失的一代"。 |
| A.I. systems, he said, "can now do a lot of the routine tasks that we used to give to students" — the repetitive work of calculation, checking and literature review. "So how do you train the next generation?" | 他说，AI 系统"如今能干很多我们过去交给学生做的例行任务"——计算、核对、文献综述这些重复性劳动。"那下一代人怎么训练？" |
| His other worry: an erosion of human skills and intuition. "If A.I. does more and more of the work, at some point you become a slave to it, because you can't actually check what it's doing," he said. "You lose the ability to know whether it's giving you something correct or not." | 他的另一个担忧：人类技能与直觉的流失。"如果 AI 干的活越来越多，到某个时刻你就成了它的奴隶，因为你实际上没法核查它在做什么，"他说，"你会失去判断它给你的东西对不对的能力。" |
| The risk, he said, is that mathematicians become "irrelevant, obsolete" — reduced to the equivalent of A.I. janitors, cleaning up after the machines. He is determined not to let that happen. | 他说，风险在于数学家变得"无关紧要、被时代淘汰"——沦落到相当于"AI 清洁工"的地步，跟在机器后面收拾残局。他决心不让这一幕发生。 |
| At the Heidelberg conference, and again in the podcast interview, he posed the same provocative question: Should mathematicians let A.I. work on the most famous unsolved problems, like the Riemann hypothesis or Navier-Stokes equations, whose solutions carry million-dollar bounties from the Clay Mathematics Institute? | 在海德堡会议上，以及后来那期播客里，他抛出了同一个挑衅性问题：数学家应该让 AI 去攻那些最著名的未解难题吗？比如黎曼猜想、纳维-斯托克斯方程——解出它们能从克雷数学研究所领走百万美元悬赏。 |
| Navier-Stokes is one of the seven millennium problems. It is a set of equations describing the motion of fluids. Dr. Tao, who has spent years on the problem, joked that trying to crack it was like "living in a toxic waste site" — the risk of failure is high, but so is the reward. "We live in hope," he said. | 纳维-斯托克斯是七大千禧年问题之一，是一组描述流体运动的方程。在这个问题上花了多年的陶博士开玩笑说，试图攻克它就像"住在有毒废料场里"——失败的风险极高，回报也极高。"我们活在希望里，"他说。 |
| In a talk titled "AI and Mathematics" posted online in October, Dr. Tao laid out scenarios for how A.I. might tackle such problems. One possibility, he said, is that A.I. produces long, dense proofs that humans can't fully verify — "proofs that no one can check." Another is what he called "spoiler" risk: A.I. solves a problem that a human mathematician has spent a career on, at essentially zero cost, in minutes. (In an online discussion, he used the phrase "the elephant in the room" for the profession-wide anxiety about this.) | 十月他上网发布了一场题为《AI 与数学》的演讲，列出了 AI 可能攻克这类问题的几种情景。一种可能：AI 产出冗长而艰深、人类无法完全验证的证明——"没有人能检查的证明"。另一种是他所说的"剧透"风险：某位人类数学家倾注整个职业生涯的难题，AI 几乎零成本、几分钟就解掉了。（在一次线上讨论中，他用"房间里的大象"来形容整个行业对此的集体焦虑。） |
| An extreme scenario, he said, would be A.I. delivering a century of mathematical progress in a single year, "which would be epiphany-level, euphoric, but also destabilizing." | 而极端情景，他说，是 AI 在一年之内交付一个世纪的数学进展——"那将是顿悟级别的、令人狂喜的，但也是颠覆稳定的。" |
| Even now, he said, he has moments of soul-searching. "If A.I. can do everything, then what do we do?" he asked. "What's our value?" | 他说，即使是现在，他也有陷入深刻自省的时刻。"如果 AI 什么都能做，那我们做什么？"他问，"我们的价值是什么？" |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · Elephant in the Room
> - **the elephant in the room** —— 房间里的大象：人人看得见、却都回避不谈的显眼问题。英语十大习语之一，标题党常用。
> - **outsize influence** —— 超常的影响力。outsize = 特大的（out- + size）。
> - **remake** /ˌriːˈmeɪk/ —— 重塑。re- 前缀"重新"。
> - **a lost generation** —— 迷失的一代。海明威时代的文学典故（The Lost Generation），此处指被 AI 跳过的训练期数学人。
> - **routine tasks** —— 例行任务。routine = 日常的、例行的。
> - **literature review** —— 文献综述。学术标准搭配。
> - **erosion** /ɪˈrəʊʒn/ —— 侵蚀、流失。an erosion of skills = 技能流失。
> - **become a slave to ...** —— 沦为……的奴隶。become a slave to routine/alcohol/A.I. 皆可。
> - **irrelevant, obsolete** —— 无关紧要的、过时的。两个"被淘汰"近义词并列，语气递进。
> - **be reduced to ...** —— 沦落到……的地步。reduce 的"贬降"用法，后接身份/惨状。
> - **janitor** /ˈdʒænɪtə(r)/ —— 清洁工、门房。clean up after the machines 跟在机器后面打扫。
> - **pose a provocative question** —— 抛出挑衅性问题。pose a question = 提出问题（正式搭配）。
> - **bounty** /ˈbaʊnti/ —— 悬赏金。million-dollar bounties = 百万美元悬赏。
> - **millennium problems** —— 千禧年大奖难题，克雷研究所 2000 年设立的七大问题，每题百万美元。
> - **crack** /kræk/ —— 攻克、破解。crack a problem = 解开难题。
> - **toxic waste site** —— 有毒废料场。toxic /ˈtɒksɪk/ 有毒的。
> - **lay out scenarios** —— 列出（各种）情景。lay out = 铺陈、展开。
> - **spoiler** /ˈspɔɪlə(r)/ —— 剧透。此处喻指"AI 把结局提前揭晓"——你一生的悬念被别人剧透了。
> - **epiphany** /ɪˈpɪfəni/ —— 顿悟、灵光乍现。epiphany-level = 顿悟级别的。
> - **euphoric** /juːˈfɒrɪk/ —— 极度兴奋的、狂喜的。名词 euphoria。
> - **destabilizing** —— 使失稳的、颠覆稳定的。de- + stabilize（使稳定）+ -ing。
> - **soul-searching** —— 深刻的自我反省、扪心自问。复合名词，have moments of soul-searching。

| For Dr. Tao, the answer to that question begins with a story about doing therapy — with a machine. | 对陶博士来说，这个问题的答案，始于一个"做心理治疗"的故事——和一台机器。 |

"We can plow ahead and use these tools to do the same mathematics we've always done, but faster and cheaper. Or we can use them to do mathematics we've never been able to do before." | "我喜欢的说法是：我们应该用 AI 去做**更多**的数学，而不只是**更便宜**的数学，"他说。"我们可以闷头往前走，用这些工具把过去一直在做的数学做得更快更便宜；也可以用它们去做我们**从来做不到**的数学。" |

> [!note]- 词汇注释 · Therapy Sessions
> - **therapy / therapist** /ˈθerəpi/ —— 心理治疗 / 心理治疗师。do therapy = 做心理治疗。
> - **mourning** /ˈmɔːnɪŋ/ —— 哀悼、守丧。动词 mourn。
> - **grief** /ɡriːf/ —— 悲伤、丧亲之痛。grieve 动词、grievous 形容词。
> - **process one's feelings** —— 消化、疏解自己的情绪。process 此处是心理学术语"处理/加工"。
> - **It's not like I had a choice of ...** —— 口语句型：我又不是有……的选项（暗示别无选择）。
> - **A.I. or nothing** —— 要么 AI，要么什么都没有。全文的文眼句式，重复两次，值得背诵。
> - **parable** /ˈpærəbl/ —— 寓言、比喻故事。
> - **access** /ˈækses/ —— 可及性、获取渠道。此处不是"进入"，而是"资源分配"意义上的 access。
> - **curricula** /kəˈrɪkjələ/ —— 课程体系（curriculum 的复数）。
> - **brainstorm** —— 头脑风暴（动词）。
> - **rise to the challenge** —— 迎接并战胜挑战。rise to the occasion/challenge 固定搭配。
> - **be run over (by ...)** —— 被……碾过、碾压。
> - **plow ahead** /plaʊ/ —— 埋头猛进（美式拼法，英式 plough）。plow 本义"犁地"。
> - **Don't panic.** —— 别慌。《银河系漫游指南》封面那句名言，英国式的冷静幽默。
