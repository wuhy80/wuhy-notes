## 如何在 Linux 下安装 Ollama
http://192.0.2.104:3210/
安装的 Lobo 工具使用本地的 ollama，配置跨域的需求

```
root@inspur:/etc/systemd/system# cat ollama.service 
[Unit]
Description=Ollama Service
After=network-online.target

[Service]
ExecStart=/usr/local/bin/ollama serve
User=ollama
Group=ollama
Restart=always
RestartSec=3
Environment="PATH=/root/anaconda3/bin:/root/anaconda3/condabin:/root/anaconda3/bin:/usr/local/sbin:/usr/local/bin:/usr/sbin:/usr/bin:/sbin:/bin:/usr/games:/usr/local/games:/snap/bin"
Environment="OLLAMA_HOST=0.0.0.0"
Environment="OLLAMA_ORIGINS=*"

[Install]
WantedBy=default.target
```

systemctl reload-daemon
systemctl restart ollama.service
